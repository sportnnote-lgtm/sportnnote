## Post text (post exactly)
```
At a school badminton or table tennis tournament, the scorer is often a student volunteer with a phone at the side of the court. If the two point buttons look the same and sit one above the other, a point goes to the wrong side sooner or later.

So we rebuilt how points go in for all six racket sports: tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel and pickleball.

• Two big buttons side by side, in each team's colour and named for the side. The server's button says SERVING.
• Nothing starts until the scorer picks who serves first. The app no longer assumes the home side serves.
• Picked the wrong server? "Fix who served first" corrects it without touching the score.
• A chip under the board calls MATCH POINT, SET POINT or BREAK POINT, so the scorer and the players agree on what's at stake.
• Undo says exactly what it will undo: "Undo: point to Asha Rao (30-15)", not just "Undo".
• Fast4 tennis tiebreaks go to sudden death at 4-4, as the format intends.

One tap per point is still the whole job. It's just much harder to get wrong now.

Scoring badminton, step by step: https://sportnnote.in/guides/score-badminton/
```
