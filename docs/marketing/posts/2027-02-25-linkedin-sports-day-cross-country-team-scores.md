## Post text (post exactly)
```
At most school cross-country races, the finish line is chaos. Forty runners in two minutes, one teacher with a clipboard, and the house totals added up by hand after lunch.

The race is simple. The paperwork isn't. So we built it for that finish line:

• Record the finish by bib. Type 214, press Enter, and the box is ready for the next runner. A bib that has already finished can never be placed twice. One official calls out bibs while the other types.
• Times are optional. Places come from the finish order. Add a time for everyone, for some runners, or for no one.
• House team scores, worked out as each runner finishes. The first four from each house score their places (you choose 3 to 6), and the lowest total wins: 2 + 4 + 9 + 10 = 25.
• The usual school rule, built in. Only complete teams score, and their 5th and 6th runners still push other houses' scorers back. A tie goes to the house whose last scorer finished higher. A house with too few finishers is listed, not ranked.
• Individual and team medals both go on the house table, next to every other event of the day.

The same screen runs a road race (5 km to the marathon) and a race walk, with red cards and the disqualification on the third. Road times count as personal bests on any course. Meet and school records count only on a certified course with gun timing, as World Athletics requires.

The step-by-step guide: https://sportnnote.in/guides/run-a-cross-country-or-road-race/
```
