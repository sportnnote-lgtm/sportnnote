UK schools and the bodies that buy for them ask about accessibility early: public bodies work to WCAG 2.1 AA. Audience: heads of PE, SENCOs, school business managers and IT leads. The angle is everyone on the team, on match day: a scorer using a screen reader, a parent who needs larger text, a teacher at a laptop with a keyboard. The post makes no compliance claim; it says what's built and points to the page that lists what isn't done yet. The founder should edit it into their own voice.

## Post text (post exactly)
```
On sports day, the person keeping score might use a screen reader. The parent following from work might need text at twice the size. The teacher entering results might be at a laptop, working by keyboard.

We built SportnNote's scoring screens to WCAG 2.1 AA, the accessibility standard public bodies in the UK work to:

• Screen readers read every button on the scoring screens, and announce each score as it changes.
• The web app works by keyboard, with a clear focus ring you can always see.
• Text grows to 200% without cutting off, and buttons are big enough to hit on a cold touchline.
• Colour is never the only signal. Team buttons carry the team's name, results carry W, D or L.
• Motion calms down when your phone's "reduce motion" setting is on.

We also publish an accessibility page that says honestly what's done and what isn't yet, criterion by criterion. If something gets in your way, tell us: blocking issues on the scoring screens get fixed first.

Read it here: https://sportnnote.in/accessibility/

#Accessibility #InclusiveSport #SchoolSport #SEND #PhysicalEducation
```
