Gulf outdoor sports days run early, before the heat, and any delay pushes events towards midday. This carousel shows the timetable tools organisers use when the day slips. Audience: PE departments and sports coordinators planning spring sports days before the outdoor season winds down in May. It states what the app does; it gives no heat-safety advice and makes no health claims.

Sports day running late, and the sun's climbing? Don't rewrite the programme by hand ☀️
