First of three first-season spotlights for the newest team sports (rugby 15 Feb, ice hockey 13 Mar). They're numbered "N of 3", apart from the "Four more sports, N of 4" series (archery, rowing, canoe sprint, cycling). Netball goes first: it's the sport girls' school teams play most in the Gulf, the UK and Singapore, and it has a school following in India too, so the post works for the India launch and later for the international track. It takes the free Saturday Instagram slot in "fix anything" week (the only new Instagram post that week). The YouTube how-to follows on Thu 28 Jan.

Audience: PE teachers and netball coaches, mostly of girls' teams, and the players themselves. The Reel shows phone screens only: no players, no school names.
