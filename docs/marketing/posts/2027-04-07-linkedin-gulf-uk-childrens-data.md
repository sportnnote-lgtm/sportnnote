## Post text (post exactly)
```
Before a school puts its students on any app, someone asks: what happens to the children's data? Here's our answer for families outside India.

• A parent confirms. When someone under 18 signs up, their parent or guardian gets an email and confirms from their own inbox. Until then the account stays limited: nobody can follow the child, and only their own teams and tournaments see their full profile.
• Strangers see initials. People outside a child's teams, tournaments and school don't see a child's full profile.
• No direct messages to children. Coaches and organisers can never message an under-18 player directly; messages go to the parent.
• Contacts belong to the parent. When a school imports a class list, phone numbers and emails are saved on the parent, never the child.
• Analytics is a choice. In the UK and Europe, we ask before counting anything. Everywhere, a child's activity is never linked to them. No ads, and we never sell data.
• The parent stays in charge. From their own account, a parent can view, download or delete their child's data, or withdraw consent.

If your school has questions about how this works, my messages are open.

#Safeguarding #SchoolSport #ChildrensPrivacy #UKSchools #UAESchools
```
