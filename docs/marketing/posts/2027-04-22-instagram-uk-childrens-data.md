What changes for the UK: the analytics choice is asked first (slide 2), the parent confirms from their own email (slide 3), and there's no India consent-box line. Audience: parents and guardians, and the PE teachers who forward it. Lunchtime slot, so parents see it on a break.

**Tone rule (founder):** how we protect children, said plainly and positively. No compliance or certification claims (the international privacy policy and terms are a lawyer's draft until reviewed), no "now", "new" or "we fixed", no security-change talk, no fear words.

## Alt text
An eight-slide carousel titled "Your child's data, your choice". The slides explain that in the UK the app asks before counting anything for analytics, that a parent confirms an under-18's account from their own email, that people outside a child's teams see initials and a shirt number instead of the full profile, that coaches cannot message children directly, that parent contacts stay on the parent, and that parents can view, download or delete their child's data.
