## Post text (post exactly)
```
SportnNote now scores 29 sports. Here's the rule we hold every one of them to.

A sport only goes live when it's scored the way its own federation scores it, and when it gets the same depth as every other sport:

• Its own rulebook. FIFA for football, FIH for hockey, IHF for handball, FIBA for basketball, FIVB for volleyball, BWF, ITTF and the ITF for racket sports, FIDE for chess, World Athletics, World Aquatics, IWF for weightlifting, ISSF for shooting, World Archery, World Rowing, the ICF for canoe sprint, the UCI for cycling, World Netball, World Rugby, the IIHF for ice hockey, the WBSC for baseball and softball, and IFAF for flag football.
• Its own scoring screen, built for one person with one phone, not a generic "+1" button.
• Its own tables and tie-breaks, the way that sport breaks a tie.
• A career page for every player, with that sport's own stats: a keeper's save %, a raider's super raids, a lifter's make rate, a shooter's inner tens, an archer's 10 + X rate.
• Corrections that are public, so both sides can trust the result.

Our newest sports went through the same test. Handball times every 2-minute suspension. Weightlifting keeps the lifting order. Shooting runs the elimination final, shoot-offs included. Archery seeds its bracket from the ranking round and settles 5–5 with one arrow. Rowing and canoe sprint send every boat through heats, a repechage or semi-final, and Finals A and B. Cycling times a time trial to the hundredth and runs the track sprint as a bracket. Netball alternates the centre pass and times every suspension. Rugby runs the sin-bin on playing time and the bonus-point table. Ice hockey runs the penalty box, power plays included. Baseball and softball take one tap per pitch and build a real line score. Flag football keeps the downs to midfield and the conversions from the 5 or the 12.

Cricket isn't the lead. Neither is any other sport. A school that runs kabaddi, chess, a swim meet and a regatta deserves the same records for all of them.

The 29: cricket, football, hockey, handball, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi, tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball, carrom, chess, golf, athletics, swimming, weightlifting, shooting, archery, rowing, canoe sprint, cycling, netball, rugby, ice hockey, baseball and softball, and American football.

We open to everyone on 11 January. The last three sports are revealed this week and next.

#SchoolSports #PhysicalEducation #SportsEducation
```
