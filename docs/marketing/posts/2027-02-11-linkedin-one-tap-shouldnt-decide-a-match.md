## Post text (post exactly)
```
In most sports a match ends after a long build-up. In chess, golf match play and carrom, one tap can decide it. Tap the wrong result and the game closes, the table moves and every follower gets an alert.

So in SportnNote the deciding taps now ask first, in plain words.

♟ Chess: results read the way an arbiter writes them, White first: 1-0, ½-½ or 0-1, with the names underneath. Before saving, the app says it as a sentence: "Record 0-1: Kabir beat Dev by Resignation?"
⛳ Golf: conceding a match-play match, or picking up in stroke play (which makes the whole card a no return), both confirm before they happen.
⚪ Carrom: no default for the coins left on the board. The Record button tells you when a board wins the game or the match.
🏑 Hockey: Full time stays locked until the last period has started.
🏀 Basketball: Eject sits apart from the foul buttons and asks first.

Small changes. They're the difference between a scorer trusting the app at a final and keeping a paper sheet "just in case".

Recording a chess result, step by step: https://sportnnote.in/guides/score-chess/

```
