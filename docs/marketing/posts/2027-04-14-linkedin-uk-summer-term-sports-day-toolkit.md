## Post text (post exactly)
```
Summer term has started, which means sports day is on the calendar. And with it: entry sheets from every house, a timetable on a spreadsheet nobody else can open, start lists printed at 7.30 on the morning, a record board last updated years ago, and someone at the finish line asking "so who's the Inter Girls champion?"

SportnNote is a scoring and results app for school sport. Here's the part we built for sports day:

• Year groups and ages done for you. Pick "United Kingdom" as your school system and the classes run Reception to Year 13, ages count on 31 August, and Junior, Inter and Senior are ready to use.
• Entries on one grid: every athlete in an age group against every event. Set your school's limit (say 3 events + 1 relay); it warns, it never blocks. Paste a house's list straight from a spreadsheet.
• Late entries and scratches on the day: the late runner gets a free outside lane, the injured one is listed DNS with the reason.
• A timetable by session, with what's on NOW and NEXT. Running late? Move the whole afternoon back 15 minutes in one tap. It flags anyone doubling up.
• A4 sheets: start lists with signature lines, field cards with trial boxes, results and the house table.
• Your record board, typed or pasted in once. A result is only marked as a record when it really beats it.
• Champions for every age group: the champion house and the individual champion, shared as one message for assembly.

Seven slides below. Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).

Start here: https://sportnnote.in/guides/manage-meet-entries/

#SportsDay #PhysicalEducation #SchoolSport #UKSchools #PEteachers
```
