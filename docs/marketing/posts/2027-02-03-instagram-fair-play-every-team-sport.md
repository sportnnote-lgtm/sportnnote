## Alt text
An eight-slide carousel about fair play in SportnNote team sports:
1. Fair play, sport by sport: cards, bans and walkover scores.
2. A discipline table listing each player's cards and who is suspended for the next match.
3. Hockey and kabaddi: green, yellow and red cards; a red card is one match out by default.
4. Handball: warnings, two-minute suspensions, red and blue cards.
5. Basketball: unsportsmanlike, technical and disqualifying fouls; fouling out is never a ban.
6. Picking a suspended player for the matchday squad: the app asks first but never blocks.
7. Walkover scores: football 3-0, basketball 20-0, volleyball 25-0 in each set, counted in the table only.
8. Every default can be changed per tournament.
