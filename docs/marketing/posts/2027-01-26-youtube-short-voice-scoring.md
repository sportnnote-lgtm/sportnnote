Republic Day, in "Republic Day inter-house meets" week: the day a lot of scorers are standing at a pitch with a whistle in one hand. Free Tuesday Shorts slot. It's the second standalone Short (after double elimination, 4 Feb), so it has its own file. Hockey is the demo sport because its voice calls are the richest (goals with assists, penalty corners, cards) and it isn't cricket. Audience: scorers and PE teachers.

Why now: on APK 37, phones could only type voice calls. APK 38 adds real speech recognition on Android, so this is the first time "say it" is true on the phone.

## Title
Hands busy? Say the score: voice scoring on SportnNote #shorts

## Description (post exactly)
```text
Say "goal Rao" and SportnNote logs it, with the scorer and the team. Every call shows what the app heard and waits for your tap, unless you switch Auto on. Ending a quarter or the match always asks first, even in Auto. Works in the Android app (latest version) and in Chrome or Edge on the web, for hockey, football, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi, handball, racket sports, carrom and more.

SportnNote never records or keeps your audio: only the words your phone recognised come back to the app.

Guide: https://sportnnote.in/guides/score-by-voice/
Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).

AI-generated voice; real app screens with demo data.

#SportnNote #Hockey #SchoolSports
```

## Shot list and demo data
- **Account:** the marketing demo account, tournament **SportnNote Demo Meet 2026**, hockey friendly Red House v Blue House, Open category.
- **Players:** fictional adults: Red House includes **Asha Rao**; Blue House includes **Meera Khan**. Only one Rao and one Khan in the match, so the calls are unambiguous.
- **Phone:** an Android phone on APK 38 with the English speech pack installed (on-device recognition where available). Record in a quiet room, then lay a light crowd bed under it in the edit; don't fake the recognition.
- **Order:** start in **✋ Confirm**; show the **⚡ Auto ON** switch without turning it on.
- A small "Demo data" label for the whole clip. Do Not Disturb on. Phone screen and hands only.

## Disclosure
YouTube Studio → "Altered or synthetic content" = **Yes** (synthetic voice-over). The description carries the line. The spoken calls are a real person's voice.
