## Post text (post exactly)
```
In school and club tournaments, discipline usually lives in someone's memory. "Wasn't he booked in the group game?" Nobody's sure, so he plays, or he doesn't, and someone feels cheated either way.

Fair play needs a record. Here's what SportnNote keeps now:

• Football: a discipline table for the whole tournament. Yellows, second-yellow reds and direct reds per player, and a red line under the team: "Suspended for next match", with the reason. You set the rule (for example 2 yellows = 1 match out) and when the count resets. Pick a suspended player for the next match and the app asks first. It never blocks you, because the referee's report and your rules have the final say.
• Kabaddi: green, yellow and red cards as the rulebook has them. A yellow takes the player off the mat for 2 minutes of playing time, and the mat count, the bonus line, super tackles and all outs follow it. Technical points and line-outs are recorded too, with the reason.
• Tennis, padel, table tennis, badminton, squash and pickleball: warnings, penalty points and game penalties, suggested step by step from each sport's own schedule. A point penalty is scored like a point, so the game and set follow.

Every card and penalty goes on the timeline, and on the player's profile when the match ends. Wrong player? Correct it, and the table and any ban are worked out again.

Football discipline guide: https://sportnnote.in/guides/football-discipline-and-suspensions/
```
