## Description (post exactly)
```text
Score a baseball or softball game on one phone, the WBSC way. Tap each pitch: ball, strike, swinging, foul. When the ball is put in play, pick what happened, and the app moves the runners for you. Steals, wild pitches, pinch hitters and pitching changes are a tap or two. Innings, the run rule, walk-offs and extra innings end the game by themselves. You get the line score, batting and pitching boxes, AVG, OBP, SLG and OPS, ERA and WHIP, and WBSC standings.

Formats: Baseball 9 innings, Softball fast pitch 7, Slow pitch 7, Youth 6 and School 7, each one editable.
```
