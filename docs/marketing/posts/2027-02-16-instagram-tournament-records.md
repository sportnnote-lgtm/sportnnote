## Shot list and demo data
- **Account:** the marketing demo account, tournament **SportnNote Demo Meet 2026**, with finished, live-scored demo matches in football, basketball, kabaddi, volleyball (or badminton), cricket and chess. Records only come from matches scored live, so build the demo set by scoring them (a walkover sets none).
- **Players and teams:** the four houses and fictional adults only (Ravi, Arjun, Kabir, Dev, Veer, Rahul, Imran, Sameer, Karan, Vikram, Asha Rao, Priya, Meera). The guide's example names aren't the marketing demo cast: don't use them.
- **Cricket partnerships:** score at least two cricket matches ball by ball in the demo tournament so **Highest partnerships** shows.
- **Volleyball comeback:** script one match to finish 3–2 from 0–2 down.
- **Chess:** enter the number of moves on at least two decisive games.
- A small "Demo data" label on every screenshot slide. No photos of people.

## Pre-post checks
- **Build:** recheck in the frozen launch build: **Stats** tab, **📊 By sport**, **📜 … records** (heading names the sport), the chip labels **Fastest goal**, **Hat-tricks**, **30-point games**, **Triple-doubles**, **Super 10s**, **High 5s**, **Comebacks**, **Longest tiebreaks**, **Highest partnerships**, **Fastest wins (moves)**.
- **Scope:** golf, athletics, swimming and the other timed or measured sports keep records on their own results pages, not here. The caption names only the sports on the slides; don't add "every sport".
- **Older matches:** if the founder wants pre-launch pilot matches to show records, the admin backfill (`backfillMatchRecords`) must have run. Not needed for the demo account.
- **Minors:** demo adults only.
