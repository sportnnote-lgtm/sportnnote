## Post text (post exactly)
```
If you've ever built a term of school fixtures by hand, you know the drill. One round a week, every Wednesday afternoon. Except half-term. And the INSET day. And the week the minibus is booked for the trip.

SportnNote lays out a whole league in one go:

• Pick your match day, say Wednesday, or two days, like Tuesday and Friday.
• Set the term dates and the kick-off time, 15:30 or 3:30 pm.
• List the days with no matches: half-term as one range, odd days one per line.
• Preview it: "6 rounds: Wed 14 Oct, Wed 21 Oct, Wed 4 Nov…". Half-term is skipped, and it warns you if the rounds don't fit the term.
• Then run it like a season. Every phone shows the venue's time, and 15:30 stays 15:30 when the clocks change.

Running qualifiers into a final? Link the league to the next round, choose "top 2 of each group", and the qualifiers join the final in one tap, each marked "Qualified from" their league. No retyping teams.

Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).

How to set it up: https://sportnnote.in/guides/weekly-fixtures-across-a-term/
```
