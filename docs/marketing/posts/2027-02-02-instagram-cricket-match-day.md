## Alt text
A screen recording of SportnNote's cricket scoring. A fixed line above the run buttons shows the striker and bowler and turns green with FREE HIT after a no-ball. A run out is recorded by picking the fielder, then one chip for who is out and at which end. In a short squad, the wicket panel offers Confirm wicket, innings closed. A 5-run penalty is armed to ride on the next ball, and the line turns amber. Text reads: Cricket, without the panic.
