## Post text (post exactly)
```
At a Gulf inter-school meet, the schools on the start list often don't agree on what to call a class.

One runs KG1 to Grade 12 on an April school year. The British-curriculum school across town runs FS1 to Year 13 and counts ages on 31 August. The American-curriculum school counts in grades, from 1 September. All three send teams to the same fixtures.

We built SportnNote so that isn't the organiser's problem:

• Each school picks its system: Gulf (Indian curriculum), Gulf (British curriculum) or US. Class names, the graduating class, age groups and the age cut-off start right, and "Promote year" never invents a Year 14.
• The host school's system sets the meet's age cut-off, and the organiser can change it in one place.
• Fixtures keep the venue's time zone, so a family in Doha sees "Sat, 4:00 pm GST" for a game in the UAE.
• 29 sports, each scored by its own international rules: netball, swimming, athletics, football, basketball, badminton, flag football and the rest, with house tables, records and a career page for every player.
• A parent confirms a child's account from their own email, and coaches never message children directly.

If you run PE or school sport in the UAE or Qatar, I'd like to hear how your inter-school season works.

Open it at https://app.sportnnote.in. Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).

#SchoolSport #PhysicalEducation #UAESchools #QatarSchools #InternationalSchools
```
