## Post text (post exactly)
```
SportnNote went live on Monday with 24 sports. Four of them didn't make our reveal series, so here they are: archery, rowing, canoe sprint and cycling.

They're not "a team against a team" sports. A field of athletes shoots, rows or rides, and rounds, heats and finals decide the medals. Each one got its federation's format, not a stopwatch and a spreadsheet:

• Archery (World Archery): a ranking round entered end by end on a keypad (X, 10 to 1, M), then a seeded bracket. Recurve sets go to 6, and 5–5 is settled with one arrow.
• Rowing (World Rowing): crews picked seat by seat, bow to stroke plus cox. Heats, a repechage, then Finals A and B, with photo-finish thousandths.
• Canoe sprint (ICF): K1 to C4 over 200, 500 or 1000 m on nine lanes. The best-ranked boats get the centre lanes from round two.
• Cycling (UCI): time trials to the hundredth, road races by the order on the line (same group, same time), and track pursuit, sprint, keirin, points and elimination races.

Every one feeds the same house table, meet records and career pages as the other 20. A rower's best 2000 m sits on the same profile as their football goals.

The 24: cricket, football, hockey, handball, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi, tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball, carrom, chess, golf, athletics, swimming, weightlifting, shooting, archery, rowing, canoe sprint and cycling.

Open it at https://app.sportnnote.in. Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).
```
