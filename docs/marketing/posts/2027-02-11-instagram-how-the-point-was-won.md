## Caption (post exactly)
```
Not just who won the point: how. 🎾🏸🏓

Turn on 🔎 Point detail and, after each point, add one optional tap:
• Winner (forehand, backhand, smash, drop, nick, bandeja…)
• Forced error
• Unforced error (net or out)
• Ace, service winner or service fault

It's in tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel and pickleball. Skip it any time; the score never waits.

The result: winners and unforced errors on every player's career page, like the pros.

Tag your doubles partner (and count their unforced errors 😅)

#SportnNote #Tennis #Badminton #TableTennis #Squash #Padel #Pickleball
```
