## Post text (post exactly)
```
One wish for school sport in 2027: that every result is kept.

Here's what changes when it is:

Selections get fairer. A coach picking the U16 team can see who actually scored, saved, raided or ran fastest across the season, not just who had a good final.

Personal bests become real. A student who knocks half a second off their 200 m knows it, and so does their PE teacher, a parent, and next year's coach.

House tables stop being argued about. Every point comes from a scored match, with the federation's tie-breakers, and any correction is listed publicly with a name and a time.

Every sport counts the same. The chess Swiss and the carrom doubles get the same records as the cricket final.

That's what SportnNote does, for 24 sports, from 11 January. Ten schools are running their January sports days on it with us. If your school would like to be next, my messages are open.

Happy new year to everyone who runs, coaches, scores and organises school sport. You make it happen.

#SchoolSports #PhysicalEducation #SportsEducation
```
