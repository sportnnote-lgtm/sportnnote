## Post text (post exactly)
```
A golf day never goes exactly to plan. A player hurts a wrist on the 9th. Someone never turns up. A handicap was typed as 21.4 instead of 12.4. Two players tie for first.

On SportnNote, the organiser handles each from the scorecard:

• WD, DQ or DNS, with the reason shown on the leaderboard ("WD — Injury"). Under the R&A rules a withdrawal returns no score, so the total shows –, but the hole scores stay on the card.
• Fix a Handicap Index at any time, even after the round. Net scores and Stableford points are worked out again for the whole card.
• A leaderboard that reads like the pros': position, total, Thru (F when finished), today, R1 to R4 and the stroke total.
• Best gross and best net side by side. Choose "can win both", or one prize each, gross first, the usual club rule.
• Ties for first? Set "Playoff for 1st", then tap the winner when it's played.

And if markers want it, an optional stats row per hole: fairway left, hit or right, greenside bunker and penalty strokes. From that the app works out greens in regulation, scrambling, sand saves and putts per GIR. Off by default, so a quick round stays quick. Older rounds count as "not tracked", never as 0%.

The step-by-step guide: https://sportnnote.in/guides/golf-entry-admin-and-stats/
```
