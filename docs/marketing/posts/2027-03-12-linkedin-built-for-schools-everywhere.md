## Post text (post exactly)
```
SportnNote launched in India in January. From day one we built it so a school anywhere could run its sport on it without fighting the app.

School sport looks the same everywhere, but the details don't. So the details are settings, not workarounds:

• Your clock and your calendar. Pick your country and time zone, and kickoffs, typed dates (05/03 is 5 March or May 3), 12- or 24-hour times and the first day of the week follow it. A tournament keeps its venue's time zone, so an away team sees "4:00 pm GST".
• Your school system. Grade 10, Year 13, Secondary 4 or Form 5: choose India, Gulf (Indian or British curriculum), UK, Singapore, Malaysia, Australia or the US, and classes, the graduating year and the age cut-off date (31 August for a UK school, for example) start right. "Promote year" never invents a Year 14.
• Your units. Swim meets in 25-yard pools with their own records, never converted. Long jump in feet and inches if you want it, stored in metres so records stay comparable.
• Your season. Inter-school leagues laid out one round a week on your match day, skipping half-term and holidays. A league can feed its qualifiers into the next tournament.
• Your class list. Import a whole year group or squad from a spreadsheet or your school system's CSV, with parents' contacts saved on the parent, never the child.
• Everyone on the team. Scoring screens built to WCAG 2.1 AA: screen readers read every button and announce each score, the web app works by keyboard, and text grows without cutting off. Our accessibility page says honestly what's done and what isn't yet.

27 sports, one standard, any school calendar.

Open it at https://app.sportnnote.in. Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).

#SchoolSports #PhysicalEducation #InternationalSchools #EdTech
```
