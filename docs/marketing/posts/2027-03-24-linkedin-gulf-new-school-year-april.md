Spring break in the UAE (≈ late Mar–early Apr) means fewer readers that week; it's a planning post they can come back to. ## Post text (post exactly)
```
New school year in April? Here's the bit of school sport nobody enjoys: moving every student up a class, and getting next year's squads into a system before the first fixture.

On SportnNote it's three steps:

1. Pick your school system. "Gulf (Indian curriculum)" gives KG1, KG2 and Grade 1 to 12, graduating after Grade 12. "Gulf (British curriculum)" gives FS1 to Year 13 with ages on 31 August.
2. Promote the year. Every student moves up one class, Grade 9 → Grade 10. The graduating class shows "Graduate", nobody is moved into a class that doesn't exist, and you can hold anyone back or keep them on.
3. Import the new intake. Paste a class list or upload the CSV from your school system: name, date of birth, class, house and a parent's contact. The preview shows what's new, what's already there and any date it can't read.

Parents' phone numbers and emails are saved on the parent, never on the child, and only the parent, the school's admins and coaches can see them.

Open it at https://app.sportnnote.in. Free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance (30 days' notice).
```
