## Post text (post exactly)
```
SportnNote now scores 20 sports. Here's the rule we hold every one of them to.

A sport only goes live when it's scored the way its own federation scores it, and when it gets the same depth as every other sport:

• Its own rulebook. FIFA for football, FIH for hockey, IHF for handball, FIBA for basketball, FIVB for volleyball, BWF, ITTF and the ITF for racket sports, FIDE for chess, World Athletics, World Aquatics, IWF for weightlifting, ISSF for shooting.
• Its own scoring screen, built for one person with one phone, not a generic "+1" button.
• Its own tables and tie-breaks, the way that sport breaks a tie.
• A career page for every player, with that sport's own stats: a keeper's save %, a raider's super raids, a lifter's make rate, a shooter's inner tens.
• Corrections that are public, so both sides can trust the result.

Our three newest sports went through the same test. Handball runs the clock, times every 2-minute suspension and enforces the time-out limits. Weightlifting keeps the lifting order and the "never lighter" rule for declarations. Shooting ranks series with the countback rules and runs the elimination final, shoot-offs included.

Cricket isn't the lead. Neither is any other sport. A school that runs kabaddi, chess, a swim meet and a shooting range deserves the same records for all of them.

The 20: cricket, football, hockey, handball, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi, tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball, carrom, chess, golf, athletics, swimming, weightlifting and shooting.

We open to everyone on 11 January. The last three sports are revealed this week and next.

#SchoolSports #PhysicalEducation #SportsEducation
```
