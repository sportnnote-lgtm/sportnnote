1. Who's your best attacker? 15 kills from 50, or 12 from 25?
2. Turn on Detailed stats; it is off by default.
3. Every attack swing counts: kill, error, blocked or in play.
4. An attack kept in play is logged without changing the score.
5. Attack efficiency equals kills minus errors minus blocked attacks, divided by attempts.
6. A box score with attempts and efficiency columns, and a Best attacker award.
7. Turn it on for the big matches; guide at sportnnote.in/guides.
