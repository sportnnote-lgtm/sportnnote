# SportnNote pilot — guide for players and organisers

*For the Hyderabad open-tournament pilot. Forward the message below, or share this page.*

## Message to forward

> Hey! I'm piloting **SportnNote**, an app to score our matches, run tournaments, and keep
> everyone's stats in one place. It's free. Would love you to try it this week:
>
> **iPhone:** open **app.sportnnote.in** in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
> **Android:** install the app from <APK link>, or use app.sportnnote.in
>
> 1. **Create account:** email, mobile and date of birth (it's used for age groups).
> 2. **Profile → your number → Verify:** you'll get an SMS code.
> 3. **Next game:** Organize → **🤝 Start a friendly**, pick the sport and score it live.
>    Everyone following sees the score update.
>
> Something broken or confusing? **Settings → Send feedback.** It comes straight to me.

## Getting started (2 minutes)

1. **Sign up.**
   - Use your real date of birth; age-group tournaments check it.
   - Your mobile and email stay **private** unless you choose to show them (Edit profile).
2. **Verify your number:** Profile → your phone → **Verify** → enter the SMS code.
3. **Set up your profile:** Edit profile → add your sports, city and a photo.

## Scoring a match

1. **Organize → 🤝 Start a friendly:** pick the sport, then the players or teams. For
   singles sports, search people by name.
2. Tap **Start** and score as you play. Every tap is saved, even offline; it syncs when
   you're back online.
3. Made a mistake? **Undo** removes the last action.
4. Most sports end on their own when the match is won (sets, games, frames). Timed sports
   have **🏁 End Match**. Results go to both players' stats and to any tournament standings.

**Golf:** Organize → 🤝 **Start a friendly** → **Golf**.
- Pick or quick-create the course (par and stroke index), add your group, and enter each
  hole with − / + from par.
- The leaderboard updates live for everyone.

## Running a tournament

1. **Organize → 🏆 New tournament:** sport, format (league, knockout, groups + knockout,
   and more) and dates.
2. **Teams/players:** add them yourself, or share the invite link so they register.
3. **Fixtures:** open the tournament and auto-generate fixtures, then set venues and
   times.
4. Standings, brackets and stats update automatically as matches are scored.

## Following and messaging

- **Follow** players, teams and tournaments to see their live scores on Home.
- **Message** a player from their profile. For under-18 players, messages go to their
  parent or guardian.

## Privacy

- Phone and email are hidden by default.
- Under-18s need a guardian's consent, and messages about them go to the guardian.
- **Settings → Delete my account** removes your personal data.
- Policy: Settings → Privacy Policy.

## What to tell us

**Settings → Send feedback.** We want especially:
- anything that **didn't work** or lost a score;
- anything that took **too many taps** during a live game;
- a **sport, format or rule** you play that the app can't handle yet.
