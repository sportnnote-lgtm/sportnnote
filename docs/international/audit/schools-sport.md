# International audit: schools sport

Area: the school/org model, age groups and cut-offs, the school year, how school competitions are organised abroad, rule presets, school terminology, and what the platform needs for the 5 new sports.
Scope: read-only audit of the `feat/sport-formats-and-scoring` working tree, 2026-10-11. Markets in founder order: **Gulf** (UAE, Qatar; British / Indian / IB / American curricula) → **UK** → **SG-MY** → **AU** → **US**. India stays unchanged.
Details of foreign school bodies and rules (ESAA/ESFA, NFHS, School Sport Australia, NSG, BSME, CBSE) come from general knowledge and are marked **verify** wherever the exact rule matters.

## Summary

The school model is mostly free text already. Class labels, Houses, academic-year dates, contingents and custom division labels are all typed by the organiser, so a UK or Gulf school can use it today. The India-only parts are in the **defaults**:
- schools graduate after "Grade 10" by default;
- the age bands are U11/U14/U16/U19;
- age-group eligibility only reads `U<n>` labels;
- the "board's date" cut-off is a fixed date, not a recurring rule;
- the help text mentions SGFI/CBSE "circulars";
- academic-year labels assume a year that spans two calendar years.

Nothing here blocks a non-India user outright. Only the graduation default is close to P0, because rollover silently gets it wrong. The bigger work is three things:
1. **Market presets**: one switch that sets the class naming, graduating class, academic-year dates, age bands and cut-off, and the sport rule presets.
2. **Term-long league fixtures** and **school team levels** (Varsity/JV, A/B teams).
3. **The 5 new sports.** The platform covers about 60–70 % of what each needs: clocks, timed suspensions, shoot-outs, lineups, loss bonus points and win %. The scoring engines themselves are new.

## 2. The 5 new sports: what the platform already supports and what each needs

Adding a sport means one plugin (`src/sports/types.ts:1-14`, contract registered in `src/sports/registry.ts`). The database stores `sport` as free text, with no CHECK constraint (no `check (sport in …)` found in `supabase/migrations`), so a new sport needs no migration. About 46 `Record<SportId, …>` maps (e.g. `src/core/matchSafety.ts`, `src/data/teamStats.ts`, `src/data/walkover.ts`, `src/sports/discipline.ts`, `src/screens/CreateTournamentScreen.tsx`) need an entry per sport. TypeScript will flag every missing entry.

**Building blocks already in the platform:**
- stoppable game clocks: basketball, futsal, handball;
- timed suspensions on game time: field hockey green/yellow cards (`src/sports/hockey/engine.ts:10-16`), handball 2-minute suspensions (`src/sports/handball/engine.ts:18-20,107`);
- shoot-out order: `src/sports/shootoutOrder.ts`;
- on-field time tracking: `src/sports/football/fieldTime.ts`, basketball;
- lineups with x/y positions, formations and captain (`LineupSlot`, `src/core/types.ts:766-774`);
- an innings / batting-order / outs model (cricket);
- stat schemas with leaders, careers and awards (`src/sports/statSchema.ts`);
- standings kit: `win/draw/loss`, `lossBonus{margin,points}`, `shootout{win,loss}`, `rankBy:'winPct'`, `forfeitLoss` (`src/data/standings.ts:170-215`);
- series and best-of-X ties; voice scoring hooks; walkovers.

## 4. Can do now vs needs company/store accounts

**Can do now (code only, no accounts):** every item in the gap table, #1–#23. That includes the market presets, the sport-name overrides, the cut-off basis, eligibility bands, team levels, the weekly fixture rhythm, tournament qualification links, NFHS/9v9/pairs-cricket presets, and the copy fixes.
The new-sport engines are also code-only, but the founder has scheduled them as a separate build.

**Needs company/store accounts:** nothing in this area directly. Indirectly:
- Shipping the presets to iOS/Android users needs an OTA or store build, which is already possible on the Indian Play account. Listing in Gulf/UK/US stores needs the new company account.
- An MIS roster integration (#13; Wonde/Arbor/iSAMS/PowerSchool APIs) would need a contracting company and a data-processing agreement. A CSV import does not.

## 5. Top 5 for this area

1. **Graduation default (#1, P0):** stop defaulting every school to "Grade 10". Ask when the school is created, prefill from the market, and warn at rollover.
2. **Market presets (§3, closes #4–6, #14–17, #21):** one `market` setting on the community that drives class ladder, school year, cut-off, age bands, sport names and terms. India stays unchanged.
3. **Age eligibility beyond "U14" (#2, #3, #12):** min/max age or grade per division, a 31 Aug school-year cut-off in TS and SQL, and US grade eligibility.
4. **Real inter-school competition shape (#7, #8, #9):** school team levels (Varsity/JV, A/B), a weekly term fixture rhythm, and tournament-to-tournament qualification (county → national, zonal → national, state playoffs).
5. **Make room for the new sports now (#10, §2):** add per-market sport display names (Soccer / Field hockey) and reserve `ice_hockey` / `american_football` IDs. The new engines then plug into the existing clock, suspension, shoot-out, lineup, `lossBonus`, `shootout` and `winPct` blocks. Add a try-bonus and an OT-loss standings option. Prioritise netball for the Gulf, UK and SG girls' school market, and flag football as the first step toward American football.
