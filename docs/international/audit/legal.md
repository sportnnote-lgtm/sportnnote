# International readiness audit: Legal and privacy

_Audit date 2026-10-11. Read-only. **This is not legal advice.** Statutes are cited from general knowledge and marked **(verify)** where the exact article, threshold or commencement date must be confirmed by a lawyer in that market. Items marked **LAWYER** need qualified local counsel before launch._

Scope: privacy policy and terms (`src/data/legal.ts`), consent for minors, cross-border transfer (one Supabase DB in ap-south-1 Mumbai), analytics/cookies, data subject rights, retention, breach, DPO/representatives, age gates, public profiles of minors, messaging, photos, school data agreements.

Market keys: **Gulf** = UAE + Qatar · **UK** · **SG-MY** = Singapore + Malaysia · **AU** · **US**.

---

## 0. What is already in good shape (keep it)

- **Guardian routing for messages.** Only adults can send. Messages about an under-18 go to the guardian, never the child (`src/data/messages.ts:4-8`, `supabase/migrations/20261008120000_messaging.sql:231,266,551`). This beats most markets' baseline.
- **No per-user analytics for minors or unknown ages** (`supabase/migrations/20261011120000_account_deletion_minor_analytics.sql:10-12`). First-party only, allow-listed events, no PII (`src/core/telemetry.ts:1-16`). The 13-month purge is implemented (`supabase/migrations/20261010120000_analytics.sql:220-221`).
- **Account deletion.** It runs end to end: DB wipe, storage removal and auth deletion (`supabase/functions/delete-account/index.ts:9-19,62,70`; migration 0030). Sporting records are kept but pseudonymised as "Deleted player".
- **Private-by-default contact details.** DOB, guardian and verification data are hidden by the view (`supabase/migrations/20261007120000_private_contact_details.sql:68-96`). Contact discovery is adults only (`20261015120000_discover_by_contact.sql:6,67`).
- **Guardian consent is enforced server-side for phone sign-up** (`supabase/functions/phone-login/index.ts:90-98`). The accepted legal version is recorded (`:110`).
- **No ads, no sale of data** (`legal.ts:41`). This removes most US-state "sale/share" and COPPA third-party-disclosure exposure.

## 4. Top 5 (legal)
1. **L3**: Close the anon API read of minors' name, photo, city, school and age, and the public photo bucket. The "members only" promise in the policy currently isn't true at the data layer.
2. **L4 + L11**: Make guardian consent verifiable (the guardian confirms from their own email or phone; FTC-approved VPC for US under-13s) and give guardians rights over the child's account (review, export, delete, withdraw).
3. **L1 + L2 + L7 + L8**: Rewrite the policy and terms as global plus regional sections. Name the legal entity, disclose that data is stored in Mumbai with a transfer basis per market, and list every subprocessor (add Anthropic and Cloudflare).
4. **L5 + L6 + L16**: Get scope opinions on UK Online Safety Act and AU under-16 rules, and ship Children's Code defaults (DPIA, minors visible to their teams only, no stranger follows or match-venue alerts, L15).
5. **L19 + L13**: Master school DPA (with UK IDTA/Addendum, US FERPA/SOPIPA/NY 2-d riders) plus DPO/UK representative appointments. Schools abroad will ask for these first.
