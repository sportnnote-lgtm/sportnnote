# Episode 9 — Your profile, stats & verification

- **Length:** ~75s
- **KB articles:** `edit-profile`, `verification`
- **YouTube title:** Your player profile, stats & getting verified
- **Description:** Your Sportfolio profile is your sporting CV — matches, wins,
  per-sport stats and the teams you've played for. Plus how age & guardian
  verification works for under-18 players.
- **Thumbnail:** a player profile with the coloured avatar ring and big stat tiles.

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Profile tab: avatar ringed in the house colour, name, sport pills, three stat tiles (Matches, Wins, Win rate). | Your profile is your sporting CV — every match, win and stat, in one place. |
