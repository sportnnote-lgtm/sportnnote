# Episode 1 — What is Sportfolio?

- **Length:** ~60s
- **KB article:** `what-is-sportfolio`
- **YouTube title:** What is Sportfolio? Live scoring for every sport
- **Description:** Sportfolio lets anyone score a match live and share it — like
  CricHeroes, but for every sport. Score football, cricket, basketball, kabaddi,
  tennis and more, run a full tournament, and follow your players. Works offline.
- **Thumbnail:** the app's live "Live now" card with a bold "2 – 1", plus the
  word "EVERY SPORT".

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | App opens on Home — the "Live now" list, a football card reading Red House 2 – 1 Blue House. | If you've ever kept score on a scrap of paper and lost it by full time — this is for you. |
| 2 | Finger taps between sport cards: football, cricket, basketball, kabaddi, tennis. | Sportfolio is live scoring for every sport. Football, cricket, basketball, kabaddi, tennis — one app. |
| 3 | Tap a live match; the scoring controls appear; tap a couple, the score ticks up. | Tap a match, and score it live, ball by ball or point by point. Everyone following sees it update. |
