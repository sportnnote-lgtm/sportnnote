# Episode 7 — Follow players & get match reminders

- **Length:** ~60s
- **KB articles:** `follow-players`, `match-reminders`
- **YouTube title:** Never miss a match: follow players & set reminders
- **Description:** Follow the players and teams you care about so their matches
  land in your feed, and set reminders so you're pinged before kickoff.
- **Thumbnail:** a "★ Following" button and a bell with "1 day · 1 hour · 15 min".

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | A player profile; tap "+ Follow player"; it becomes "✓ Following". | Want to keep up with a player or a team? Open their profile and tap Follow. |
| 2 | Cut to Home; a followed team's match appears in the "Live now" / "Up next" feed. | Now their matches show up right on your Home feed — live and upcoming. |
| 3 | Go to Settings → Match reminders. | To get a nudge before kickoff, open Settings — Match reminders. |
| 4 | Tap lead-time chips: 1 day, 1 hour, 15 min; the summary updates. | Pick when you want reminding — a day before, an hour, fifteen minutes. Choose as many as you like. |
| 5 | Cut to a notification preview: "🔴 Red House vs Blue House is live". | You'll get reminders for matches you're playing in and for anyone you follow. |
| 6 | Back on Settings, clear the selection to show "Off". | Want quiet? Clear them all and reminders are off. You're in control. |
| 7 | Cut to Sportfolio wordmark. | Follow your game. Never miss the moment. |

**On-screen CTA (last 3s):** "Follow from any profile · reminders in Settings"
