# Episode 3 — Fix a mistake & score offline

- **Length:** ~75s
- **KB articles:** `undo-fix-mistake`, `offline-scoring`
- **YouTube title:** Never lose a score: undo mistakes & score offline
- **Description:** Two things that make Sportfolio safe to score with in the real
  world — step-by-step Undo to fix any mistake, and full offline scoring that
  syncs automatically when you reconnect.
- **Thumbnail:** the offline banner ("Offline — scoring saved on this device")
  with a green tick.

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | A live match being scored; the score reads 2 – 1. | Real matches are messy. Someone bumps your arm, the wifi drops. Here's why that never costs you the score. |
| 2 | Tap a wrong button — score jumps to 3 – 1. A beat. | First: mistakes. Say you tap the wrong thing — score goes to three. |
| 3 | Tap the "↶ Undo" row once; score returns to 2 – 1. | Tap Undo. Back to two. It also reverses whatever stat that tap credited, so the player records stay right. |
| 4 | Tap Undo two more times; earlier actions unwind. | Each tap steps back one more action — so you can rewind all the way to any earlier point and re-enter it correctly. |
| 5 | Toggle airplane mode on. The banner appears: "Offline — scoring saved on this device." | Now the connection. Flip to airplane mode — Sportfolio notices, and tells you it's got you. |
| 6 | Keep scoring while offline; the banner shows "3 changes saved here…". | Keep scoring. Every tap is written to your phone first — nothing is waiting on the network. |
| 7 | Toggle airplane mode off; banner switches to "Syncing…", then disappears. | Back online, and it syncs on its own. No button to press, nothing lost. |
| 8 | Brief flash of the "Can't sync right now — Retry" state. | And if syncing ever genuinely fails, it says so plainly and gives you Retry — it never pretends everything's fine. |
| 9 | Cut to Sportfolio wordmark. | Score with confidence, wherever the match is. |

**On-screen CTA (last 3s):** "Undo is on the scoring tab"
