# Episode 8 — Find teams & players on Discover

- **Length:** ~60s
- **KB articles:** `join-team-code` (+ the Connect noticeboard)
- **YouTube title:** Find a team, or fill your squad — Discover on Sportfolio
- **Description:** The Discover tab connects the community: search players and
  teams, post to the noticeboard when you need a player, opponent or ground, and
  join a team with an invite code.
- **Thumbnail:** the Connect board with a "Player wanted" listing and a WhatsApp button.

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Discover tab opens on the Connect board — listings like "Available player", "Player wanted". | Need a player? Looking for a team, an opponent, or a ground? That's the Discover tab. |
