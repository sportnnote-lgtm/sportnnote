# Episode 2 — Score your first match

- **Length:** ~90s
- **KB article:** `score-first-match`
- **YouTube title:** Score your first match on Sportfolio (90 seconds)
- **Description:** A quick walkthrough: open a match, start the clock, tap to
  score as play happens, and undo a mistake. No setup required — it works offline.
- **Thumbnail:** the scoring controls mid-tap with a big "TAP TO SCORE".

| # | Visual (on screen) | Voiceover |
|---|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Home screen, "Live now" list. Finger hovers a card marked "tap to score ›". | Let's score a match, start to finish, in about a minute. On Home, any match that says "tap to score" is ready to go. |
| 2 | Tap the card → the match opens on the scoreboard, big "0 – 0". | Tap it, and you're in. Up top is the scoreboard — that's what everyone following the match sees. |
| 3 | Tap the green "▶ Start the match" button; the clock starts. | Tap "Start the match" to begin. Now the clock's running. |
| 4 | The sport's scoring controls fill the screen; tap a goal button; the score becomes 1 – 0. | These buttons are tailored to your sport — goals, runs, points, raids. Tap what happens, and the score updates instantly. |
| 5 | Tap two or three more actions; assign a scorer/player from the prompt. | Keep tapping as play unfolds. If it asks who scored, pick the player — that's how their stats build up. |
| 6 | Deliberately tap a wrong button, then tap the "↶ Undo" row; the score steps back. | Made a mistake? Tap Undo — it steps back one action. Tap again to keep rewinding to any point. |
| 7 | Show the airplane icon toggling on; the "saved on this device" banner appears; scoring continues. | Lose signal mid-match? No problem. Every tap is saved on your phone and syncs when you're back online. |
| 8 | Match ends; the final score shows; cut to Sportfolio wordmark. | That's it — you just scored a live match. Nothing to set up, nothing to lose. Your turn. |

**On-screen CTA (last 3s):** "Open a match and tap Start"
