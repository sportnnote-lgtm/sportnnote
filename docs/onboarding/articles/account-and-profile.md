# Account & profile

*Full Help-centre guide. Covers KB topics: `edit-profile`, `verification`,
`change-timezone`.*

## Your profile & stats

Your profile is your sporting CV — every match, win, and per-sport stat in one
place.

- The header shows your avatar (ringed in your house/team colour), name, the
  sports you play, and headline tiles: **Matches**, **Wins**, **Win rate**.
- Use the **All / Official / Friendly** chips to switch scope — see everything,
  or split competitive tournament matches from casual friendlies.
- The **By sport** section breaks your record down per sport. Tap one to open its
  detailed profile: sport-specific stats and a match history where **wins carry a
  green edge and a "WON" tag** so results read at a glance.

## Edit your profile

1. On your profile, tap **✎ Edit profile**.
2. Update your name, city, jersey number, photo, and the sports you play.
3. Save — changes show immediately.

Keeping your city and sports current helps teammates and organizers find you in
**Discover → People**.

## Age & guardian verification

Verification unlocks verified play and, for under-18s, confirms a parent or
guardian.

1. On your profile, open **🛡️ Age & guardian verification**.
2. Upload a proof of age — birth certificate, school ID, or passport.
3. The status changes to **⏳ Pending review** while our support team checks it.
4. Under-18 players confirm a parent/guardian here as well.
5. Once approved, your status shows **☑️ Verified** with a tick beside your name.

**Your privacy:** the document is only ever seen by the support team for review —
it is **never** shown on your public profile.

## Change your timezone

Match times show in your timezone so kickoffs and reminders line up with your
clock.

1. Go to **Settings → Preferences → Timezone**.
2. Pick your timezone from the inline list.

Set this if you travel or if your matches are in a different region — it keeps
schedules and reminders accurate.
