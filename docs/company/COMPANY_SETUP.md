# Company setup plan — SportnNote

*Prepared 2026-10-06 for the founder.*

**Situation:**
- Indian citizen, based in Hyderabad, Telangana.
- Wife and father available as directors.
- No investors for 18 months; no revenue for 12 months.
- Currently employed at Salesforce.

> ⚠️ This plan is research and guidance, not legal or tax advice.
> - Have a **Chartered Accountant (CA) or Company Secretary (CS)** handle the filings and
>   confirm fees.
> - Have an **employment lawyer** review Step 0.
> - Government fees and stamp duty change; the figures below are approximate.

---

## Decision: Private Limited company

**Why:**
- **Limited liability.** This matters because you hold minors' data under the DPDP Act.
- **One structure for the long term.** It is the standard vehicle for investors, ESOPs,
  employees and partnerships, so there's no conversion later.
- **Accounts are set up once.** Meta/WhatsApp, Apple, Google, SMS (DLT), bank and GST
  accounts all sit with the company from day one.
- **Startup India (DPIIT) recognition** is available.

**Why not the alternatives:**
- **Sole proprietorship:** unlimited personal liability; no shares; moving the accounts
  later would repeat the slowest setup work.
- **One Person Company:** unnecessary now that two directors are available.
- **LLP:** poor fit for future equity investment and ESOPs.

**Suggested name:** *SportnNote Technologies Private Limited*. Check availability on the
MCA portal and in a trademark search first.

**Suggested shareholding:**
- You hold the large majority (e.g. 90–99%). A family member holds the rest.
- Shareholders appoint and remove directors, so a majority holding keeps you in control even
  if you aren't a director yet (see Step 0).

## Step 0 — Clear your Salesforce employment (do this FIRST)

This is the one thing that can genuinely hurt the company later. Investors, acquirers and app
stores look at who owns the code.

1. **Read your employment agreement**, especially the "Proprietary Information and Inventions
   Assignment" section or similar, and the **Code of Conduct / Outside Business Activities**
   and **outside board service** policies. Look for:
   - **IP assignment:** does it claim inventions made during employment that relate to
     Salesforce's business, or that use its equipment, time or confidential information? A
     sports-scoring consumer app is far from enterprise CRM, which helps, but wording varies.
   - **Approval requirements** for starting, owning or being a director of another company,
     and for "competing" or "conflicting" activities.
   - **Moonlighting** restrictions.
2. **If approval is required, request it in writing** (manager / HR / Ethics office). Describe
   SportnNote accurately: a consumer sports-scoring and tournament app, built on personal time
   and personal equipment, not competing with Salesforce. Keep the approval.
3. **From now on, keep a clean separation:**
   - never use Salesforce laptops, accounts, networks, paid tools, code or confidential
     information for SportnNote;
   - work outside Salesforce working hours.
4. **If approval for a directorship is slow or refused:**
   - your **wife and father serve as the two directors**;
   - you hold the majority shares;
   - the board appoints you as an authorised signatory only if your policy allows that;
   - you join the board when you're permitted or when you leave Salesforce.

   Confirm this arrangement with the lawyer, because owning or advising can itself be an
   "outside business activity".
5. **Have a lawyer review it (paid, worth it).** A one-hour review of your agreement against
   this plan typically costs about ₹5,000–15,000.

**Output of Step 0:** written approval (or a lawyer's written view that none is needed), and a
decision on whether you're a director at incorporation.

## Step 1 — Prepare (1–3 days)

**Name:**
- Check the MCA name-availability service and the IPO India trademark search for
  "SportnNote" in:
  - Class 9 (app/software);
  - Class 41 (sports/entertainment services);
  - Class 42 (SaaS/platform).
- Shortlist 2 names. Name reservation allows two.

**Registered office (Hyderabad):**
- A home address is fine.
- You need a utility bill (not older than 2 months).
- You need a no-objection certificate from the owner, plus rent agreement or ownership proof.

**Documents for each director and shareholder (wife, father, you):**
- PAN;
- Aadhaar;
- address proof;
- photo;
- email and mobile (each person needs their own).

**Pick a CA/CS or a reputable incorporation service.** They file the forms and handle
post-incorporation compliance.

## Step 2 — Digital Signature Certificates (1–2 days)

- **Class 3 DSC** for each director (and for the subscribers to the founding documents).
- About **₹1,000–2,000 per person**.
- Director Identification Numbers (DINs) are allotted within the incorporation form for up to
  3 directors.

## Step 3 — Incorporate via SPICe+ (about 7–15 working days)

**What gets filed (one integrated online process on the MCA portal):**
- **SPICe+ Part A:** name reservation.
- **SPICe+ Part B:** incorporation.
- **e-MoA / e-AoA:** constitution documents.
- **AGILE-PRO-S**, which bundles:
  - PAN and TAN;
  - EPFO and ESIC registration;
  - bank account opening;
  - optional GSTIN — **skip GST for now** (see Step 7).

**Suggested capital:**
- Authorised capital about ₹1–10 lakh.
- Paid-up capital about ₹10,000–1,00,000.
- MCA government filing fees are **nil for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh**.

**Costs:**
- **Stamp duty (Telangana):** a few thousand rupees.
- **Professional fees:** about ₹5,000–15,000.
- **Total incorporation:** typically **₹10,000–25,000 including DSCs**.

**Output:**
- Certificate of Incorporation;
- CIN, PAN and TAN;
- the company's DINs.

## Step 5 — Recognitions and identifiers (weeks 2–6, in parallel)

**DPIIT Startup India recognition:**
- Free, online, typically about 2–4 weeks.
- **Benefits:** self-certification under some labour/environment laws; faster and cheaper
  trademark and patent filing; eligibility for the income-tax holiday (Section 80-IAC, on a
  separate application); easier government tenders. Schools and the government sometimes
  procure this way.

**Udyam (MSME) registration:**
- Free.
- Helps with some vendor terms and bank schemes.

**D-U-N-S number** (Dun & Bradstreet):
- Free through Apple's lookup; about 1–2 weeks.
- **Needed for the Apple Developer organisation account**, and used by Google Play
  organisation accounts.

**Telangana startup ecosystem (T-Hub, the state innovation programmes):** worth registering
for incubation, mentoring and events. Not mandatory.

---

## Step 6 — Trademark (file early, weeks 1–3)

- File **"SportnNote"** (word mark; add the logo later) in **Classes 9, 41 and 42**.
- **Government fee:** about **₹4,500 per class** for startups/MSMEs/individuals on e-filing
  (₹9,000 otherwise), plus attorney fees if used.
- You can use ™ immediately after filing. Registration takes many months.

## Step 7 — Tax registrations: what can wait

| Registration | Needed now? | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **GST** | **No.** | Mandatory once service turnover crosses **₹20 lakh/year** in Telangana, or when selling inter-state in certain cases. Register voluntarily only if a vendor or partner requires it. |
| **Professional Tax (Telangana)** | Not yet | When the company pays salaries (including director remuneration). |
| **Shops & Establishments (Telangana)** | Not yet | When you have premises or employees. |
| **TDS filings** | When you pay contractors or vendors above the thresholds | TAN arrives with incorporation. |
| **Import/export code** | Only for foreign payments for services/exports | Later. |

## Ongoing annual compliance (from year 1)

**Filings:**
- **Statutory audit** of the accounts.
- **AOC-4** (financial statements) and **MGT-7A** (annual return for small companies).
- **ITR-6** (company income tax return).
- **DIR-3 KYC** for each director, every year.

**Cost:** about **₹20,000–50,000/year** with a CA at our size.

**Penalties:** late filing penalties accrue per day, so put this calendar into the Finance
agent's schedule.
