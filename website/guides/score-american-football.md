Score a flag or tackle game on one phone. By default you tap only the scoring plays; play by play also tracks the downs, the ball and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: flag (IFAF 2 × 20, school 2 × 15) or tackle (NFL, College, High school). It sets the periods, timeouts and overtime.
- **Scoring detail**: **Scores only** (the fast default) or **Play by play**.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Kickoff**. The clock counts down.
2. Tap **⏸ Stop** and **▶ Resume** whenever the officials stop it.
3. When a period ends, tap **⏭ End Q1** (**⏭ End H1** in flag) and confirm.
4. Timeouts: tap **⏸ Lions TO · 3 left**. The clock stops; the count resets at half-time.
5. NFL format: the clock stops by itself at 2:00 of the 2nd and 4th quarters (two-minute warning).
6. Clock wrong? Tap **⏱ Set clock** and type the time left (`4:12`).

## Step by step: score a touchdown
1. Tap **🏈 Touchdown** under the team that scored.
2. Choose how: **Run**, **Pass**, **Interception return** or **Fumble return** (tackle adds punt and kickoff returns). Typing the yards feeds the longest-touchdown record.
3. Tap the scorer. For a pass, also tap the passer (or **Skip passer**).
4. The try comes up next. Tackle: **✓ Kick good** or **✓ 2-pt good** (or the misses). Flag: **✓ 1 pt (5 yd)** or **✓ 2 pts (12 yd)** (or the misses). Then tap the kicker or scorer.
5. Defence returned the try? Tap **↩ … return it (2)**. None taken? **No try**.

## Field goals, safeties and ejections
- **🥅 Field goal** (tackle): type the distance if you know it, tap **✓ Good** or **✗ No good**, then the kicker.
- **🛡️ Safety (2)**: tap it under the team that scores the 2 points, then the defender (or **Skip player**).
- **🚫 Ejection**: the player can't be picked again; discipline settings decide any ban (1 game by default).

## Play by play
A drive panel shows who has the ball, the down and distance, and the spot ("Lions · 2nd & 6", ball on Own 35).
1. Start each half: tap the team with the ball. Flag possessions start on the team's own 5; in tackle, tap who receives the kickoff, then the touchback or spot.
2. Tap the play: **🏃 Run**, **🎯 Pass ✓**, **✗ Incomplete**, **💥 Sack**, **🔄 Interception**, **🏈 Fumble lost**, **🦶 Punt**, **🥅 Field goal**, **🚩 Penalty** or **⛔ Turnover on downs**.
3. Pick the player, then the yards: tap a chip such as **+4**, or type any number. The app remembers the last passer, so a completion is just receiver and yards.
4. Downs move on their own: tackle 4 downs for 10 yards; flag 3 downs to cross midfield, then 3 to score (a failed series gives the other team the ball on its own 5). Reaching the goal line is a touchdown; tackled in your own end zone is a safety.
5. Penalties: pick the foul. It shows the usual yards, automatic first down or loss of down; you can change the yards. Don't record declined penalties.
6. Ball in the wrong place? Tap **📍 Set** to choose the team, down, spot and yards to go.

> **Tip:** **Ask who made the tackle / flag pull** adds a tap after every run and catch: turn it on only with a helper.

## Overtime
- Level after regulation? Pick who has the ball first after the coin toss.
- **Flag**: one conversion try each per round, until one team leads.
- **Kansas** (NFHS, college): one possession each from the 10 (college 25), repeated while level. A possession without a score: tap **⛔ … no score — possession over**.
- **NFL**: both teams get the ball, then the next score wins; regular-season games can end tied.
- Knockout games always get the standard overtime for their rules.

## Fix a mistake
- **↶ Undo** names what it will remove, for example "Undo Touchdown · Asha Rao (13-7)".
- **✎ Correct the timeline**: **✕** removes a moment after asking; **✎ Edit** re-enters a score at its original time (**Cancel** keeps the original).

## What you get
- The score by quarter, a scoring summary and a box score (TD, FG, XP, PTS). Play by play adds passing, rushing, receiving and defence, plus first downs, total yards, turnovers, penalties, time of possession and 3rd downs.
- Careers and leaderboards: touchdowns, yards, passer rating (NFL formula, 20 attempts to rank), field goal %, Top touchdown scorer and Top passer.
- Tables rank by win % (a tie counts half), then head-to-head, points difference and points scored.
- A walkover counts 1-0 (NFHS, college); the organiser can change it.

## Common questions
### Can I switch to play by play during a game?
Yes. Open **⚙️ Game settings** and turn on play by play, then tap **📍 Set** to place the ball. The stats count from then on.

### Do I have to name the players?
No. Every picker can be skipped; the score is right either way.
