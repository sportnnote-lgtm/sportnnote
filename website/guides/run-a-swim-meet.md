A swim meet is a list of events (say 50 m freestyle U12 Girls), each swum in one or more heats. You enter times on a phone; the app seeds heats the World Aquatics way, ranks swimmers, adds house points and keeps records and personal bests per pool length.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter results.
- Create the tournament with **Swimming** as a sport. Under **Swimming basics**, pick the **Pool** (50 m or 25 m), the number of **Lanes**, the position points (8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 by default) and whether you use **Manual timing** (stopwatches, no touchpads).
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Swimmers and relay teams score for them.

## Step by step: add an event
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Swimming — events**, and tap **＋ Add an event**.
2. **Category:** pick the age group and Boys, Girls or **Mixed (relays)**. The pool chip can switch one event to the other pool.
3. **Event:** pick the race. Longer races appear for older groups; 100 m individual medley is 25 m pool only.
4. **Swimmers:** tap a house or search any player, and tap each one to enter them.
5. **Entry marks (optional):** type entry times, or tap **Fill from PB** or **Fill from SB**. Digits fill from the right: 3245 is 32.45, 11520 is 1:15.20. Swimmers without a time are seeded slowest.
6. **Rounds:** if everyone fits in the pool, it is a straight final. Otherwise choose **Timed final (heats ranked on time)**, **Heats → final** or **Heats → semi-finals → final**.
7. Tap **Create event & start list**.

## How the heats and lanes are seeded
- In a timed final, the fastest entries swim in the last heat, the next fastest in the heat before, and so on.
- With heats before a final, the fastest are spread over the last three heats (two in 400 m and longer), fastest in the last.
- The first heat always has at least three swimmers.
- In each heat the fastest gets lane 4 (lane 3 in a 6-lane pool), then the others alternate left and right. A 10-lane pool uses lanes 0 to 9.

## Relays
- Pick a relay, then tap a house to add its team. Tap the team and pick its 4 swimmers in order, plus up to 2 reserves.
- Medley relay legs are labelled backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, freestyle.
- A mixed relay must be 2 boys and 2 girls; the app enforces this.

## Step by step: enter results
1. Open the event and tap **Enter results**. Use the **Heat 1**, **Heat 2** chips to switch heats.
2. Type each time with the number keys (3310 is 33.10, to the hundredth). Tap **Next ›** to save and move on, and **Next heat → Heat 2** when the heat is done.
3. A time far outside the usual range for the event and pool, such as a 200 m typed as 2153 (21.53), asks **Check this mark** first; tap **No, re-enter it** to fix it. An unconfirmed time never shows **PB** or **MR**.
4. For races over 50 m, tap **＋ Splits** for cumulative 50 m splits (37.20, then 1:20.10). The results sheet shows each lap.
5. With manual timing, type up to three times in **Watches** (e.g. 3245 3251 3248). The **Official time** above is read-only: the time two watches agree on, else the middle one; with two watches, their average.
6. Tap **DNS** for a swimmer who didn't start. Tap **DQ**, then the reason, such as **SW 7.6 · One-hand or non-simultaneous touch**. For a relay, pick the leg first (an early take-off is **SW 10.13 · Early take-off**). A disqualified swimmer gets no time or place. Equal times share the place and points.
7. With heats and a final, if two swimmers tie for the last place in the final, the app asks for a **Swim-off place**. Enter it after the swim-off, or leave it and both go through.
8. Close the heats to seed the final, then tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. Each check counts swimmers with no time, and the finish lists any new meet record. Medals, house points, records and profiles then update.
9. Mistake after locking? An organiser can tap **↺ Reopen heats** (until the final has a time) or **↺ Reopen final**, which returns any record it set to the previous holder and removes its house points until you lock it again.

> **Tip:** Tap **📤 Share** to send the start list or results, with a live-results link, to WhatsApp.

## What everyone sees
- **House table:** on the Swimming page and the tournament's **Stats** tab. Relays can score double (**⚙ Pool, points & timing**).
- **Best by event**, **Best swimmers (points)** from individual finals, and **Meet records**, marked LC (50 m pool) or SC (25 m pool), kept apart as World Aquatics does. When a school hosts, **School records** show its earlier meets' best times.
- **Swimmer profiles:** races, medals, and a PB and SB per event and pool length.

## Common questions
### Is a timed final fair?
It is common at school meets: places come from times across all heats, so a swimmer in heat 1 can win a medal.
