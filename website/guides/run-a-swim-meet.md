A swim meet is a list of events (say 50 m freestyle U12 Girls), each swum as one or more heats. You enter times on a phone; the app seeds the heats the way World Aquatics does, ranks every swimmer, adds points to the house table and keeps meet records and personal bests, separately for 25 m and 50 m pools.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter results. Everyone else sees results as view only.
- Create the tournament with **Swimming** as a sport. Under **Swimming basics**, pick the **Pool** (50 m or 25 m), the number of **Lanes**, the position points (8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 by default) and whether you use **Manual timing** (stopwatches, no touchpads).
- Add your houses or schools as the tournament's teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Swimmers score for them, and relay teams are made from them.

## Step by step: add an event
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Swimming — events**.
2. Tap **＋ Add an event**.
3. **Category:** pick the age group and Boys, Girls or **Mixed (relays)**. The pool chip can switch one event to the other pool.
4. **Event:** pick the race. Longer races appear for older groups, and 100 m individual medley appears only in a 25 m pool.
5. **Swimmers:** tap a house to see its swimmers, or search any player, and tap each one to enter them.
6. **Entry marks (optional):** type each swimmer's entry time, or tap **Fill from PB** or **Fill from SB**. Digits fill from the right, so 3245 means 32.45 and 11520 means 1:15.20. Swimmers without a time are seeded slowest.
7. **Rounds:** if everyone fits in the pool, it is a straight final. Otherwise choose **Timed final (heats ranked on time)**, **Heats → final**, or, for a big field, **Heats → semi-finals → final**.
8. Tap **Create event & start list**.

## How the heats and lanes are seeded
- In a timed final, the fastest entry times swim in the last heat, the next fastest in the heat before, and so on.
- With heats before a final, the fastest swimmers are spread over the last three heats (the last two in 400 m and longer races), fastest in the last heat.
- The first heat always has at least three swimmers.
- In each heat the fastest swimmer gets lane 4 (lane 3 in a 6-lane pool), the next fastest the lane on their left, then right and left. A 10-lane pool uses lanes 0 to 9.

## Relays
- Pick a relay, then tap a house to add its team. Tap the team and pick its 4 swimmers in order, plus up to 2 reserves.
- In a medley relay the order is backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, freestyle, and the legs are labelled that way.
- A mixed relay must be 2 boys and 2 girls; the app stops you if a team breaks this.

## Step by step: enter results
1. Open the event and tap **Enter results**. Use the **Heat 1**, **Heat 2** chips to switch heats.
2. Type each time with the number keys: 3310 is 33.10. Times are to the hundredth.
3. For races longer than 50 m, type the 50 m splits under the time (they are cumulative, for example 37.20, then 1:20.10). The results sheet shows each 50 m lap.
4. With manual timing, type up to three watch times in **Watches** (for example 3245 3251 3248). The official time is the one two watches agree on, else the middle one; with only two watches it is their average.
5. Tap **DNS** for a swimmer who didn't start. Tap **DQ**, then the reason, such as **SW 7.6 · One-hand or non-simultaneous touch**. For a relay, pick the leg first; an early take-off is **SW 10.13 · Early take-off**. A disqualified swimmer gets no time or place.
6. Equal times share the place and the points.
7. With heats and a final, if two swimmers tie for the last place in the final, the app asks for a **Swim-off place**. Enter it after the swim-off, or leave it and both go through.
8. Close the heats to seed the final, then tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. Medals, house points, meet records and swimmer profiles update.

> **Tip:** Tap **📤 Share** to send the start list before the race, or the results after it, to a WhatsApp group. The message has a link to the live results page.

## What everyone sees
- **House table:** on the Swimming page and the tournament's **Stats** tab. Relays can score double (**⚙ Pool, points & timing**).
- **Best by event**, **Best swimmers (points)** from individual finals, and **Meet records**, marked LC (50 m pool) or SC (25 m pool). When a school hosts, **School records** show the best times from its earlier meets.
- **Swimmer profiles:** races, finals, medals, and a personal best and season best for each event and pool length.

## Common questions
### Is a timed final fair?
It is common at school meets: every heat counts, and places come from the times across all heats. A swimmer in heat 1 can win a medal.

### Do 25 m pool times count towards my 50 m pool best?
No. Records and personal bests are kept separately for each pool length, as World Aquatics does.
