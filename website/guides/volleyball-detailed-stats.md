Points alone don't show who attacks best. A hitter with 15 kills from 50 swings did less than one with 12 kills from 25. **Detailed stats** counts every attack swing, so the app can work out attack efficiency the way coaches and the big leagues do. It is optional and off by default: the normal point buttons stay one or two taps.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [How to score a volleyball match](/guides/score-volleyball/).
- Add both squads. Detailed stats needs player names; it doesn't show for a match without squads.
- It is extra work. Use it when you have a second person helping, or for the matches that matter most.

## Step by step: turn it on
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under the two point panels, find **🔎 Detailed stats (optional)** and tap **Off** so it reads **On**.
3. It applies from the next point. Points already scored keep what they had.
4. To stop, tap **On** again. Rallies after that are not counted.

## What counts as an attack attempt
Every swing at the ball to attack it is one attempt. It ends one of four ways:
- **Kill**: the attack wins the point. Record it as usual: **Attack**, then the attacker.
- **Error**: the attacker hits out or into the net. Record **🚩 Opp. fault** for the other team, pick **Attack out** and tap the attacker.
- **Blocked**: the other team blocks it for a point. Record **Block** and the blocker, then answer **🧱 Who was blocked?** with the attacker. You can skip it.
- **In play**: the attack is dug or kept alive and the rally goes on. Tap **⚡ Attack in play · team**, then the attacker. The score doesn't change.

So a kill and an error need no extra taps. Only blocked and in-play attacks need one more tap.

## Reception (optional)
When a team receives a serve, tap **📥 Reception · team**, pick a grade and then the receiver:
- **# Perfect**: the setter can run any play.
- **+ Good**: a positive pass, but not perfect.
- **− Poor**: the team could only send a free ball or a weak attack.
A missed reception is the other team's ace, so record that as an **Ace** instead.

## How the figures work
- **Attack attempts** = kills + attack errors + attacks blocked + attacks in play.
- **Attack efficiency** = (kills − attack errors − attacks blocked) ÷ attempts. A blocked attack counts against the attacker, as in the NCAA hitting percentage and FIVB attack efficiency. It can be negative.
- **Attack success %** = kills ÷ attempts, the figure the FIVB uses for its Best Attacker.
- **Positive reception %** = (perfect + good) ÷ receptions, and **Perfect reception %** the same for perfect passes only.

## Where you see them
- **Box score**: the Score and Summary tabs add **ATT** (attempts) and **EFF** (efficiency) for each player and the team totals, with **SE** (serve errors) and **ERR** (errors) when players were named. A set filter works as usual.
- **Player profile**: the **Attack** section adds attack attempts, kills, attack errors, attacks blocked, efficiency and success %. A **Reception** section shows receptions and the two percentages.
- **Tournament leaders** add **Attack efficiency**, for players with at least 10 attempts.
- **Awards**: the tournament awards include **Best attacker**, ranked by attack efficiency. **Most attack points** stays for organisers who don't use detailed stats.

Older matches, and matches scored without detailed stats, read "not tracked" for these figures, never 0. A player's efficiency only uses the rallies that were scored in detail.

> **Tip:** Organisers can change the 10-attempt minimum: under the tournament's leaders, tap **Minimums**. See [Tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong player for an attack in play or a reception? Tap **↶ Undo**. It removes the last thing you logged.
- Wrong blocked attacker? Tap the right player under **Who was blocked?**. It replaces the first pick.
- Correcting an older point in the timeline keeps the detail you logged.

## Common questions
### Do I have to record every attack in play?
Try to. Missing in-play attacks makes efficiency look better than it is. If you can't keep up, leave detailed stats off for that match.

### Why don't I see Detailed stats?
Add the squads for both teams. Without player names there is nobody to count attempts for.
