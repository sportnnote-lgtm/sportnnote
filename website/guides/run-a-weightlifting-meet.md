A weightlifting meet is a list of bodyweight categories (say Senior Men 79 kg), each lifted in one session: three snatch attempts per lifter, then three clean & jerk attempts. You tap each decision on a phone; the app keeps the IWF lifting order, adds up totals, and updates medals, house points, records and profiles.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add categories and enter attempts.
- Create the tournament with **Weightlifting** as a sport. Under **Weightlifting basics**, pick the **Position points** and whether to give **Medals for snatch and C&J too** (as World Championships do; the default is the total only, as at the Olympic Games).
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Lifters score for them.

## Step by step: add a bodyweight category
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Weightlifting — events**, and tap **＋ Add a bodyweight category**.
2. **Category:** pick **Youth**, **Junior**, **Senior** or **School**, then the gender and the bodyweight category. Senior, junior and youth use the IWF categories in force from 1 June 2025. **School** categories are a house choice: check them against your school board.
3. **Lifters:** tap a house or search any player, and tap each lifter to add them.
4. **Opening weights (optional):** first snatch and clean & jerk in whole kilograms, or enter them at the weigh-in.
5. Tap **Create event & weigh-in list**. Each lifter gets a drawn lot number: it sets the weigh-in order and breaks ties in the lifting order.

## Step by step: weigh-in
1. Open the category. The **⚖️ Weigh-in** card lists lifters in lot order.
2. Type each **Bw** (bodyweight) to 0.01 kg, and the opening **Sn** and **C&J** if you haven't yet.
3. A bodyweight outside the category gets a warning naming the right category, and the app asks before keeping it (an IWF meet wouldn't; a school meet may).

## Step by step: run the session
1. The card under the weigh-in shows who is up, e.g. **Next: Asha Rao · 87 kg · 2nd attempt**, and the next three.
2. Tap **✓ Good lift** or **✗ No lift**. To record the lights, tap **3 lights** and set each referee to white or red; two whites is a good lift.
3. The next attempt is set for you: 1 kg more after a good lift, the same weight after a no lift. For another weight, type it and tap **Change kg**.
4. A lifter who gives up an attempt: tap **Declined**. It counts as a no lift.
5. The order is the IWF one: lightest bar first; at the same weight, the earlier attempt, then the bigger jump, then whoever lifted before, then the lower lot.
6. After the last snatch, the card moves to the clean & jerk.
7. To fix a mistake, tap the attempt in the lifter's row and change the weight, or tap **✓ Good**, **✗ No lift** or **Clear**. **Undo** at the bottom takes back the last decision.
8. When all attempts are taken, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The check lists lifters with no attempts and any new meet record.

> **Important:** The app refuses half kilos, a weight lighter than the last attempt, or less than 1 kg more after a good lift. Unusual weights, like 850 kg or a jump of over 20 kg, ask **Check this weight** or **Big jump** first.

## How places are decided
- **Total** = best good snatch + best good clean & jerk.
- No good lift in either means **no total**, shown as "—". The lifter can still rank in the other lift.
- **Equal totals:** whoever reached the total first ranks higher. Bodyweight no longer breaks ties (IWF, since 2017).
- **Equal snatch or clean & jerk:** whoever lifted it first, then the lower lot number.
- During the snatch, **Live — snatch standings** shows the order; after that, the live ranking is by total.

## What everyone sees
- **Results sheet:** the total table plus **Snatch** and **Clean & jerk** tables, every attempt shown with no lifts in brackets: 85 (88) 88.
- **House table:** on the Weightlifting page and the tournament's **Stats** tab.
- **Best total by category** and **Meet records** for the snatch, the clean & jerk and the total per category; **School records** when a school hosts.
- **Best lifters (Sinclair):** turn on **Sinclair totals** in **⚙ Medals & points** to compare across categories (IWF 2021–2024 coefficients).
- **Lifter profiles:** competitions, medals, make rate (good lifts out of attempts), and personal and season bests per lift and total.

## Common questions
### Can a lifter repeat a weight?
Yes, after a no lift. After a good lift, the next attempt must be at least 1 kg heavier.

### We locked the results and found a mistake.
An organiser can tap **↺ Reopen final**. Its records go back to the previous holders and its points leave the house table until you lock it again.

### Does the app run the clock or the bar loading chart?
Not yet. Use the platform clock and loading chart; the app keeps the order, attempts and results.
