Score a baseball or softball game on one phone. Tap each pitch; when the ball is put in play, pick what happened from a short list. The app keeps the count, outs, bases and line score, and builds every batter's and pitcher's stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: **Baseball 9 inn (WBSC)**, **Softball fast pitch 7 inn (WBSC)**, **Slow pitch 7 inn**, **Youth 6 inn**, **School 7 inn** or **Custom**. **⚙ Customize this format** changes the innings, **Run rule (mercy)**, **Extra innings**, designated hitter, batters in the order, the 1-1 count and the slow-pitch foul-out.
- Add both teams' players. A team with no roster still works: its batters are numbered.

## Step by step: line-ups
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. The **Line-ups** card shows each team's batting order and pitcher from the team sheet. The visitors bat first.
3. To change an order, tap **Set batting order**, tap the players in order, then pick the starting pitcher. **Pitcher** changes only the pitcher.
4. The first pitch locks the line-ups. After that, changes are substitutions.

## Step by step: score each pitch
1. Tap **Ball**, **Strike** (called), **Swinging** or **Foul**. The count shows as dots next to the diamond. A foul with two strikes doesn't add a strike.
2. Ball four is a walk and strike three a strikeout; the next batter comes up by himself. Tap **HBP** for a hit batter.
3. When the ball is put in play, tap **In play** and pick the outcome: **Single**, **Double**, **Triple**, **Home run**, **Groundout**, **Fly / pop out**, **Lineout**, **Double play**, **Sac fly**, **Sac bunt**, **Fielder's choice** or **Error**.
4. With nobody on base, that tap is the whole play. With runners on, each runner and the batter is shown with the usual advance already picked (on a single, everyone moves up one base). Change any of them to **Out**, a base or **Score**, check the **RBI**, then tap **✓ Confirm**.
5. For an error, tap the fielder's number (6 is the shortstop). Turn on **Ask for fielders (6-3)** in the match settings to record the fielders on every out.

> **Tip:** Turn off **Pitch-by-pitch (balls & strikes)** in the match settings to tap outcomes only.

## Runners, subs and ejections
1. Tap **Runners** for a steal, caught stealing, wild pitch, passed ball, balk, pick-off or other advance. A steal asks which runner; a wild pitch, passed ball or balk moves every runner up one.
2. Tap **Subs** for a pinch hitter, pinch runner, pitching change or defensive change. A run scored by a pinch runner still counts against the pitcher who let the original runner on.
3. Tap **More** for an **Intentional walk** or an **Ejection**.

## How the game ends
Three outs end the half-inning; you never end one. The game ends by itself:
- when the home team leads after the top of the last inning (its last turn shows as X on the line score);
- on a walk-off, when the home team goes ahead in the last inning. Only the winning run counts, unless it's a home run;
- on the run rule, for example a 10-run lead after 4 innings in the youth format;
- after extra innings. With the WBSC tie-break each inning starts with runners on base; their runs are unearned.

If time runs out or the weather stops play, tap **🏁 Call game (time / weather)** at the bottom. It asks before saving the score.

## Fix a mistake
1. Tap **↶ Undo**; it names what it removes.
2. For an older play, tap **✎ Correct the plays**. Tap **✕** to remove a play (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to enter it again in the same place. **▶ Back to live** or **Cancel** keeps the play as it was. Later plays keep their runner moves, so check the bases afterwards.
3. Tap **ER** / **UER** on a play to mark its runs earned or unearned (the scorer's call).

## What you get
- The line score (runs per inning, hits, errors) with the diamond, count and outs.
- **Score** tab: play-by-play, a batting box (AB, R, H, RBI, BB, SO, AVG) and a pitching box (IP, H, R, ER, BB, SO, pitches, ERA, with W, L and SV).
- Player profiles: AVG, OBP, SLG, OPS, home runs, RBI and steals; for pitchers W-L, IP, ERA, WHIP and strikeouts. ERA uses each format's innings (7 in softball, 9 in baseball).
- Tournament tables use **WBSC (win %, TQB)**: winning percentage, then head-to-head, team quality balance (runs per inning scored minus allowed, between the tied teams), earned-run TQB and batting average. Or pick **Win % · run difference**. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).
- Tournament Records: no-hitters, perfect games, hitting for the cycle, grand slams and the most strikeouts in a game.

## Common questions

### What does a walkover count as?
9-0 in baseball and 7-0 in softball for the table by default, never for a player. Organisers can change it. See [Walkover and forfeit scores](/guides/walkover-and-forfeit-scores/).

### Can a knockout game end tied?
No. A league can allow ties (**A tied game may stand**); a knockout always plays extra innings.
