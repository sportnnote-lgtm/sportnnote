A school meet can have 30 to 60 house and class teams. You can add them yourself, or share one link on WhatsApp so each captain enters their own team. This page covers both, plus fixing a misspelt team name and taking a team out.

## Before you start
- To add or remove teams, you must be a host of the tournament.
- To enter your own team with a link or code, you must manage that team (captain, vice-captain, team admin or its creator). See [Who can edit a squad](/guides/who-can-edit-a-squad/).

## Step by step: add teams yourself
1. Open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → **Participating teams**.
2. In a multi-sport meet, pick the **Sport** at the top. If the tournament has divisions, pick the **Division** too.
3. Choose how teams come in. With **Add directly**, the team is in straight away. With **Invite (captain accepts)**, the captain gets an alert and accepts from the tournament page.
4. Type in the search box to find a team, then tap its name to select it. Tap again to unselect. **Select all** picks every team shown.
5. If a team doesn't exist yet, tap **＋ New team**. Enter the **Name**, a **Short** name such as `RED`, a colour, and the manager's name and phone if you have them. Then tap **Add & select**.
6. Tap the save button at the bottom. It shows how many teams you're saving, for example "Save 8 participants". If you're inviting, it reads **Send invites & save**.

> **Tip:** For an inter-house meet hosted by a school, tap **🏠 Enter the school’s Houses** to add every house in one go.

## Step by step: let captains join with one link
1. On **Participating teams**, turn on **🔗 Teams can join by link**.
2. A join code appears, such as `T-ABC123`. Tap **💬 WhatsApp** to send the invite to a group, or **Copy link** to paste it anywhere.
3. Each captain who opens the link picks their team, and it's in the tournament at once.
4. When every team is in, turn the switch off. Anyone who opens the old link then sees "This link is turned off".

## Step by step: enter your team as a captain
1. Open the link the organiser sent. Or go to **Settings** → **Join a team with a code** and type the `T-` code. The same box takes squad invite codes too.
2. On the **Enter a tournament** screen, check the tournament name. If you're asked, sign in. The app brings you back here.
3. Under **Your teams**, tap the team you want to enter. If you haven't made it yet, tap **＋ New team**, fill in the **Team name** and **Short**, and tap **Create team**.
4. If the tournament has divisions, pick yours.
5. Tap the **Enter** button. It shows your team's name.

You'll see "You're in!" and the tournament's **Teams** tab opens.

## Fix a team's name or logo
1. On **Participating teams**, find the team under **In the tournament**.
2. Tap **Edit** under its name. You only see **Edit** for teams you manage.
3. Change the **Team name**, the **Short (2–4)** name, the colour, the **City / town** or the logo, then tap **Save**.

> **Note:** A team that's part of a club takes its name and logo from the club page. **Edit** shows a link to it.

## Take a team out
There are two ways, and the difference matters:
- **Withdraw** (under **In the tournament**) keeps the team on record but takes it out of the fixtures. Use this once a team has played. You can **Reinstate** it later.
- **Remove** drops the team from the list completely. To remove a team you just added by mistake, tap its name to unselect it and save. You'll be asked to confirm. If the team already has fixtures, the message suggests Withdraw instead.

Removing a team from a tournament never deletes the team itself.

## Requests and invites
If registration is open, or you invited teams, a card shows:
- **Requests to join**: tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Invited**: teams still waiting for their captain. Tap **Confirm** to put them in yourself, or **Cancel** to withdraw the invite.

## Common questions

### Can a captain join after the deadline?
No. Once the registration deadline has passed, or the tournament has reached its maximum number of teams, the link stops letting teams in. The captain sees "Registration has closed" or "This tournament is full". A host can still add the team directly.

### A withdrawn team tried the link. What happens?
They're told to ask the organiser. Tap **Reinstate** to bring them back.

### A captain says their team isn't listed under "Your teams". Why?
They don't manage that team in the app, or it's for a different sport. The team's captain or an admin must enter it, or they can make a new team on that screen.

### What does Check in do?
On match day, tap **Check in** as each team arrives. The list shows how many are here.
