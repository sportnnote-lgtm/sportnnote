Score a netball match on one phone. Set the seven positions once and every goal is one tap on the shooter's name. The app runs the clock, shows whose centre pass it is, times suspensions and builds the stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: **International 4 × 15**, **School 4 × 10**, **Halves 2 × 20** or **Custom**, and **If level at full time**: **Draw stands (league)** or **Extra time, then play on (World Netball)**.
- Add both teams' players. Under **⚙ Customize this format** you can turn on **Ask for feeds & rebounds**, change the suspension length, or allow team time-outs if your league uses them.

## Step by step: set up and start
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Under "Who has the first centre pass?", tap the team that won the toss. **▶ Start** unlocks.
3. Tap **More** → **⇄ Positions** for each team. Tap a player, then her position (GS, GA, WA, C, WD, GD, GK). Each row also reminds you where that position may go.
4. Tap **▶ Start**. The clock counts down the quarter, like a netball scoreboard.

## Step by step: score the play
1. A goal: tap **🥅 GS · Name** or **🥅 GA · Name** for that team. Only the GS and GA can score.
2. A missed shot: tap **↗ Miss GS** or **↗ Miss GA**. Shooting % comes from goals and attempts.
3. The **⚪ Centre pass** strip alternates after every goal, whoever scored; a quarter starts with the centre that is due. If the umpire's order differs, tap **⇄ Swap**.
4. Tap **More** for **🖐 Intercept**, **☝️ Deflection**, **⤴️ Rebound**, **⚠️ Turnover**, **✋ Penalty** (contact or obstruction), **🟨 Sanction**, **🔁 Sub** and **⇄ Positions**.
5. With **Ask for feeds & rebounds** on, a goal then asks "Goal assist — who fed the circle?", and a miss asks for the feed and the rebound.

> **Tip:** Hands busy? Tap **🎤 Voice scoring** and say "goal GS home", "miss GA away" or "centre away". See [Score by voice](/guides/score-by-voice/).

## Held time, subs and breaks
1. When the umpires hold time, tap **⏸ Hold** and pick **🩹 Injury / illness**, **🩸 Blood**, **⇄ Tactical change** or **Other**. The clock stops; tap **▶ Resume** when play restarts.
2. Substitutions are allowed at any time under the 2024 World Netball rules. Tap **🔁 Sub**, then who comes off and who comes on. The new player takes the same position.
3. At the end of a quarter, tap **⏭ End Q1** and confirm. During the break, make position changes from **More**. Then tap **▶ Start Q2**.
4. **🏁 Full time** sits at the bottom of the controls and stays off until the last quarter has started.

## Sanctions
1. Tap **🟨 Sanction**, then **🟨 Warning**, **⏱ Suspend 2 min** or **🟥 Order off**, then the player. Bench players and team officials can be sanctioned too.
2. A suspension stops the clock. The team plays a player short on playing time (her button shows "GS off 1:24"), then she returns to her position automatically.
3. An ordered-off player takes no further part and isn't replaced. Her position stays empty.

## Extra time in knockouts
Group matches keep a draw. In a knockout, a level score at the end of the fourth quarter goes to extra time: two halves of 7 minutes. **⏭ End Q4** says so before you tap it.
1. Still level after extra time? The match plays on with no clock limit ("Play on · 2-goal lead").
2. The match ends by itself the moment a team leads by two goals, or on the first goal if your organiser chose **Golden goal**.

## Fix a mistake
1. Tap **↶ Undo**. It names what it removes, for example "Goal · GS · Priya Nair (12-10)".
2. For an older moment, tap **✎ Correct the timeline**. Tap **✕** to remove it (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to enter it again at its time. **▶ Back to live** or Cancel keeps it as it was.
3. After the match, use [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## What you get
- **Score** tab: the timeline, goals per quarter, team stats (attempts, shooting %, centre passes, centre-pass goals, goals from gains, intercepts, penalties, sanctions) and each player's line.
- **Summary**: Player of the match and the top shooter.
- Player profiles: goals, attempts, shooting %, goals per game, intercepts and quarters played at each position.
- Tournament tables use **World Netball 2-1-0** (ties on goal percentage, then goal difference, then head-to-head) or **Goal difference**. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).
- Tournament Records: highest scores, biggest wins, most goals by a player, perfect shooting (10+ attempts).

## Common questions

### Can I score without setting positions?
Yes. Tapping a goal button then asks who shot. Positions make it one tap and add quarters per position to profiles.

### What does a walkover count as?
20-0 for the table by default (never for a player); organisers can change it. See [Walkover and forfeit scores](/guides/walkover-and-forfeit-scores/).
