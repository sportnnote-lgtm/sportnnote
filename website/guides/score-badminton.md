Every rally scores, and the rally winner serves next. You tap who won each rally; the app keeps the score and shows who serves from which court.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- **Format**: **BWF · 21 rally**, **5×11 (rally, cap 15)**, **15-point**, **Single game to 21** or **Custom**.
- In BWF a game is won at 21 by two. From 20-all play goes on until someone leads by two; at 29-all the next point wins, so no game passes 30.
- **⚙ Customize this format** changes **Points per game**, the **Deuce cap**, **Match length**, or **At the cap**: **Golden point (next wins)** or **Win by 2 (no golden point)**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**.

## Step by step: score a rally
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the side serving first. Until you do, the point buttons stay grey.
3. Read the serving line, e.g. "Serving: Asha Rao · right court". The serving side's button shows a **SERVING** tag.
4. After each rally, tap the big button of the side that won it. Each is in the team colour and named for the side (singles: the player's name).
5. Singles credits the player automatically. In doubles the tap scores for the pair; to credit one player (optional), tap **credit a player** or long-press a side, then pick the name (**Cancel** closes it).
6. Repeat. Each new game starts at 0-0.

## How the app follows the serve
- The rally winner serves next: from the right court on an even score, the left on odd (BWF rule).
- Singles names the server; doubles names the pair, since which partner serves depends on where they stand.
- Wrong first server? After the first rally, tap **Fix who served first** under the serving line and pick the right side. Only the serve changes; the score stays, and serve stats re-work themselves.

## Intervals and change of ends
The app works out the breaks from the score and shows them above the point buttons and under the scoreboard:
- "⏸ Interval at 11 (60 s)" when the leading score first reaches 11 in a 21-point game.
- "↔ Change ends · interval at 11 in the deciding game (60 s)" in the deciding game.
- "↔ Change ends · 120 s between games" after each game.

The same cues appear as markers in the rally log.

## Reading the board
GAMES won and a points column per game, with a 🏸 dot by the serving side. The result reads like "2–1 · 21-18, 19-21, 21-15".

A chip under the board shows **GAME POINT** or **MATCH POINT** with the side's name. Both sides can show one at once, e.g. at 29-all with the golden point.

## Match stats
The **Score** tab's "Match stats" (per match or game) show points won in total, on serve and on receive; streaks; biggest lead; and game and match points saved. Corrections update them. In doubles serve figures are for the pair.

## Point detail (optional)
Off by default, so a rally stays one tap. Turn it on with **On** in the "🔎 Point detail (optional)" row under the buttons.

After each rally, "How was it won? · Asha Rao" appears. Tap a chip or **Skip**; nothing is required.
- **Winner** + **Smash**, **Net kill**, **Drop**, **Clear**, **Drive**, **Push / lift**.
- **Forced error**, or **Unforced error** + **Net**, **Out**, **Fault** (touch or double hit).
- **Service fault** (receiver won).

Winners count for the rally winner; errors and service faults against the opponent (doubles: pick who under **By:**). The box score adds **W**, **UE**, **FE**.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? The Undo bar names what it removes, e.g. "↶ Undo: point to Asha Rao (20-18)". Each tap undoes one step.
- Older rally: tap **Edit** next to **Correct the timeline** on the **Scoring** tab.
1. Tap ✎ on a point to change the winner, player (or **Team (no player)**) or detail. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to remove a point; the app asks you to confirm first.
3. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it, pick the side and tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match, so score, games, server and stats follow, even if a game changes hands. After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends itself when a side wins enough games. If a player can't go on, tap **🏁 End match…** → **Retired** or **Default**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
The player's badminton page adds **W-L**, **Win %**, **Match play**, **Serve & return** (singles only) and **Shot making** (from point-detail matches). Doubles players get **Partners**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Do I need to tap who served?
No. The app knows the server from the last rally. You only choose the first server.

### What is the golden point?
At 29-all in a 21-point game, the next rally wins 30-29. Choose **Win by 2 (no golden point)** under **At the cap** if your event plays on.
