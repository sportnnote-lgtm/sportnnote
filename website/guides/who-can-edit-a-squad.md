A team's squad is public, but not everyone can change it. A parent who opens their child's house team sees the players and their roles, but none of the edit buttons. This page explains who can manage a squad, how to make someone a team admin, and what to do if you see a squad you can't edit.

## Who can manage a squad
A squad can be managed by:
- the person who created the team
- the captain and the vice-captain
- any team admin (a team can have several)
- for a team that belongs to a club, that club's admins
- for a team that belongs to a school or other organisation, its Owner, Admins and Organizers

Managing a squad means you can add and remove players, make someone captain, vice-captain or admin, set squad roles, edit the team's name and logo, and share the squad's invite link.

> **Important:** Running a tournament does not make you a manager of every team in it. Organisers can enter, remove and withdraw teams in their tournament, but each team's squad belongs to that team. If an organiser needs to fix a squad, the captain can send them the squad's invite link (see below), which lets them manage it.

Everyone else sees the squad read-only, with the note "Only the captain, vice-captain or team admins can edit this squad."

## Step by step: open a squad
1. Open the team (from a match, a tournament's **Teams** tab, or search).
2. Tap **👥 Squad · ＋ Add**. If you can't manage the team, the button just reads **👥 Squad**.
3. Managers see **＋ Add more players** at the top, with buttons under each player.

## Step by step: make someone captain, vice-captain or admin
1. Open the squad.
2. Under the player's name, tap **Make captain**, **Make vice-captain** or **Make admin**.
3. If the team already has a captain, you're asked "Change captain?". Tap **Make captain** to confirm. The old captain loses the role.
4. Tap the same button again (now **★ Captain**, **★ Vice-captain** or **★ Admin**) to take the role away.

Rules worth knowing:
- One person can't be both captain and vice-captain. Picking one clears the other.
- Captain and admin can go together. A captain can also be an admin.
- A team can have only one captain and one vice-captain, but any number of admins.
- For club teams, admins are set on the club page, not in the squad. The squad shows "Admins are set on the club page ›".

> **Tip:** Make a second person admin before match day, such as the PE teacher or the vice-captain's parent. Then someone can still fix the squad if the captain's phone is off.

## Step by step: set squad roles
For cricket, football, basketball, volleyball and kabaddi, managers see role chips under each player. Examples are **Wicketkeeper**, **Batter** and **Bowler** for cricket, or **Goalkeeper** and **Forward** for football.
1. Open the squad.
2. Tap a role chip to turn it on, and tap it again to turn it off. A player can hold more than one role.

Viewers see the roles as text next to the player's name.

## Stepping down
If you remove your own captaincy, vice-captaincy or admin role, you're asked to confirm: "You'll stop managing this team unless you're also an admin." Once you confirm, the edit buttons disappear for you. Make sure someone else is still a captain or admin first.

## Common questions

### I tapped "Make captain" and it went back. Why?
The server checked and found you're not allowed to change this team. The squad goes back to how it was and a message explains why. This can happen if someone removed your role a moment ago. Go back, open the squad again, and ask the captain if you still can't edit it.

### Where is the squad's invite link?
At the bottom of the squad, managers see **📨 Invite the captain / coach** with **Generate invite link**. The person you send it to enters the code under **Settings** → **Join a team with a code**, and becomes able to manage the squad.

### How do I invite teammates to my team with a QR code?
On the team's page, under **Members**, a team admin taps **🔗 Invite**. A QR code and an invite code such as `JOIN-ZCK8AH5KUA` appear, and the share sheet opens with the QR picture and this message:

> Join Team L&H on SportnNote! Hrudhay invited you 🛡️
> Open this to join: https://app.sportnnote.in/join-club/JOIN-ZCK8AH5KUA
> Or in the app, go to Join a team and enter code: JOIN-ZCK8AH5KUA

Pick WhatsApp to send both. Tap **📤 Share QR + message** to send them again. If your browser can't share pictures, the QR is saved to your downloads so you can attach it yourself. Teammates scan the QR with their phone camera, or open **Join a team** in the app and type the code.

> **Note:** In the Android app, sending the QR picture needs the next app update. Until then the app sends the message on its own, and teammates can still scan the QR from your screen.

### Can I fix a player's name or shirt number?
Yes, for players who haven't joined the app themselves. See [Fix a player's details](/guides/fix-a-players-details/).

### Who can add the team logo?
Any squad manager, from **✎ Edit team**. See [Add a player photo, team logo or tournament banner](/guides/photos-and-logos/).
