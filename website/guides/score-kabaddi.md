In kabaddi, one raid can score for both teams. The raider earns points for touches, and the defenders earn a point for catching the raider. You record each raid once, and the app works out the points, who is out, all outs and do-or-die.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Rule set** when scheduling. **Standard / Pro (7 · 2×20)** turns on do-or-die and super tackles. **Circle style (7 · 2×15)** and **School (7 · 2×10)** leave them off. **Custom** lets you set each rule, including **Revival style** and **If level at full time**.
- Add both squads first. Names are optional, but only named players appear in the player stats.
- There is no toss step. Start with whichever team raids first.

## Step by step: score a raid
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start match**. The clock starts.
2. Tap the raiding team's button, for example "🤼 Red House raiding".
3. Under **Raider (optional)**, tap the raider.
4. Under "Defenders touched (they go out)", tap 0 to 5. Each touch is a point, and each touched defender goes out.
5. If the raider crossed the bonus line, tap the chip so it reads **Bonus point: Yes**.
6. If the raider was caught, tap the chip so it reads **Raider tackled: Yes**, then tap the defender under "Who made the tackle?".
7. Check the "Scores:" line. It shows exactly what will count, for example "Scores: raid +0 · tackle +1 to Blue House".
8. Tap **✓ Record raid**, or **Cancel** to throw it away.

## What each raid scores
- Empty raid: no touches, bonus or tackle. Nothing scores.
- Touch points: one per defender touched, credited to the raider.
- Bonus: one point for the raider, but only when the defending team has 6 or more players on the mat. Otherwise the "Scores:" line says "(bonus void: under 6 defenders)".
- Tackle: the point goes to the defending team, because they put the raider out.
- Super raid: a raid worth 3 or more points.
- Super tackle: with Pro rules on, a tackle made with 3 or fewer defenders on the mat is worth 2 instead of 1.
- All out: when a whole team is out, the other team gets 2 extra points ("All out +2"). With Sanjeevani revival, the whole team comes back on.

> **Note:** With **Amar (points only)** revival, nobody goes out, so there are no all outs or super tackles.

## Do-or-die raids
With Pro rules on, after a team makes two empty raids in a row, its next raid is do-or-die. The form warns "⚠ DO-OR-DIE raid". If that raid scores nothing, the raider is out and the defending team gets 1 point even without a tackle. The timeline shows "Do-or-die stop +1".

## Reading the board and the stats
The scoreboard shows each team's total and a column for each half (H1, H2, then ET1 and ET2 in extra time). Raid, tackle and all-out points sit with the team that scored them, so the halves add up to the total.

On the **Score** tab, the timeline lists every raid by minute. Under "Player stats", each team has RAID, TCKL and PTS columns. From the second half, tap **Overall**, **1st half** or **2nd half** to filter.

## Fix a mistake
1. On the **Scoring** tab, under "Correct the timeline", tap **Edit**.
2. On the wrong raid, tap **✎ Edit**. The raid form reopens, filled in as you entered it.
3. Fix it and tap **✓ Save raid**. The score, outs and stats recalculate.
4. To delete a raid, tap **✕**. The whole raid goes: raid points, tackle and any all out it caused.

Missed a raid? Under "Backfill an earlier moment", type the minute, tap **Backfill**, record the raid, then tap **Back to live scoring**.

## End the match
- At half-time tap **End 1st Half →**, then **▶ Start 2nd half** when play resumes.
- After the second half, tap **🏁 End Match**.
- If scores are level, you see the tie-breakers this match allows: extra time, **⚡ Golden Raid — sudden death** or **🎯 5-Raid Shootout**. **End as a tie** is always there.
- In a Golden Raid, the next raid that scores for either team ends the match.
- In a shootout, tap **Empty**, **+1**, **+2** or **+3** for each team's five raids. It ends once a winner is clear, with sudden death if still level. Shootout points don't change the match score or player stats.
- With **Gaminee (all-out ends)** revival, the first all out ends the match.

To stop early for rain or a team leaving, see [End a match early or record a walkover](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## Common questions

### Why did a raid score nothing for the raider?
It was empty, or the bonus was void. Check the "Scores:" line before you record.

### Can I make substitutions?
Yes. Under "Substitutions", tap the team's button, then who comes off and who comes on. A player who comes off can't return.
