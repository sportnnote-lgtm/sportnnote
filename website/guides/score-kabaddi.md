In kabaddi, one raid can score for both teams. You record each raid once, and the app works out the points, who is out, all outs, do-or-die and a full match centre.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Rule set** when scheduling. **Standard / Pro (7 · 2×20)** turns on do-or-die and super tackles; **Circle style** and **School** leave them off; **Custom** sets each rule.
- Add both squads first. Only named players appear in the player stats.

## Step by step: score a raid
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start match**. The clock starts.
2. Tap the raiding team's button, for example "🤼 Red House raiding".
3. Under **Raider (optional)**, tap the raider.
4. Under "Defenders touched (they go out)", tap 0 to 5. Each touch is a point, and each touched defender goes out. You can't pick more than are on the mat.
5. If the raider crossed the bonus line, tap the chip so it reads **Bonus point: Yes**.
6. If the raider was caught, tap the chip so it reads **Raider tackled: Yes**, then tap the defender under "Who made the tackle?".
7. The "Scores:" line shows what will count.
8. Tap **✓ Record raid**, or **Cancel** to throw it away.

## What each raid scores
- Empty raid: no touches, bonus or tackle. Nothing scores.
- Touch points: one per defender touched, credited to the raider.
- Bonus: one point, only when 6 or more defenders are on the mat. Otherwise the "Scores:" line says the bonus is void.
- Tackle: a point to the defending team and the tackler.
- Super raid: a raid worth 3 or more points.
- Super tackle: with Pro rules on, a tackle made with 3 or fewer defenders on the mat is worth 2 instead of 1.
- All out: when a whole team is out, the other team gets 2 extra points ("All out +2"). With Sanjeevani revival, the whole team comes back on.

> **Note:** With **Amar (points only)** revival, nobody goes out, so there are no all outs or super tackles.

## Do-or-die raids
With Pro rules on, a team's raid after two empty raids in a row is do-or-die ("⚠ DO-OR-DIE raid"). If it scores nothing, the raider is out and the defence gets 1 point even without a tackle ("Do-or-die stop +1").

## Reading the board and the match centre
The scoreboard shows each team's total and a column per half (H1, H2, then ET1 and ET2). The halves add up to the total.

On the **Score** tab, the timeline lists every raid by minute, with super raids, super tackles, do-or-die raids and all outs labelled. Below it, and on the **Summary** tab, "Team stats" is the match centre:
- **Points split:** raid, tackle, all-out and extra points (a failed do-or-die raid's point).
- **Raiding:** raids, successful raids, **Raid strike rate** (successful ÷ raids), empty and unsuccessful raids, super raids, do-or-die raids and **Do-or-die success**.
- **Defending:** successful tackles, **Tackle strike rate**, super tackles and all outs inflicted.

Then each team has RAID, TKL and PTS per player. Tap **1st half** or **2nd half** to filter.

> **Note:** Matches scored before raids were recorded in full show the points split only. The raid and tackle counts weren't captured, so they aren't shown as 0.

## Career stats
When the match ends, each player's line is rebuilt from the raids. Their kabaddi profile shows **Raid strike rate**, **Not-out %** (raids where they weren't put out), super raids and tackles, and **Best match** (most total points in one match). Tackle success % isn't shown: one scorer can't record every missed tackle.

## Fix a mistake
1. On the **Scoring** tab, under "Correct the timeline", tap **Edit**.
2. On the wrong raid, tap **✎ Edit**, fix it and tap **✓ Save raid**. The score, outs and stats recalculate.
3. To delete a raid, tap **✕**. The sheet lists what goes with it; tap **Yes, remove**.

Missed a raid? Under "Backfill an earlier moment", type the minute, tap **Backfill** and record it. A bar at the top shows the minute until you tap **Back to live**.

## End the match
- At half-time tap **End 1st Half →**, then **▶ Start 2nd half** when play resumes.
- After the second half, tap **🏁 End Match**.
- If scores are level, you see the tie-breakers this match allows: extra time, **⚡ Golden Raid — sudden death** or **🎯 5-Raid Shootout**. **End as a tie** is always there.
- In a Golden Raid, the next raid that scores ends the match.
- In a shootout, tap **Empty**, **+1**, **+2** or **+3** for each team's five raids, then sudden death if still level. Shootout points don't count in the score or player stats.
- With **Gaminee (all-out ends)** revival, the first all out ends the match.

To stop early for rain or a team leaving, see [End a match early or record a walkover](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## Common questions

### Can I make substitutions?
Yes. Under "Substitutions", tap the team, then who comes off and on. A player who comes off can't return.
