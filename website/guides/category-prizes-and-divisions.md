School and open events play everyone together but give prizes by category: best under-11, best girl, best unrated player, or golf's Boys U-12 and Girls U-15. SportnNote filters the one ranking by category, so the places inside a category follow the event's own order, including tie-breaks and countback.

## Before you start
- You must be a host of the tournament to set the categories. Anyone can view them.
- Categories read each player's profile: gender, date of birth and, for chess, their rating on the event's list. Players without these can be added by hand.

## Step by step: chess category prizes
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** → **📊 Standings & leaders**, and scroll to the **♟️ Wall chart**.
2. Tap **🏷 Category prizes (U-age / girls / unrated…)**.
3. Tap the categories you award, for example **U-11**, **Best girl / woman** and **Best unrated**. Others include U-9 to U-17, **Rated below 1600**, **Rated below 1400** and **Veterans 60+**.
4. Set **Prizes in each category** (3 by default) and **Ages counted on**: **1 Jan (FIDE youth)**, **31 Dec** or **Event start**.
5. Tap **✓ Done**.

Tap a category chip to see its ranking. **🏅 Category prizes** lists the winners of each category.

## Step by step: golf divisions
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** → **⛳ Golf leaderboard & rounds**.
2. Under **🏆 Leaderboard**, tap **🏷 Divisions (U-12 / U-15 / girls…)**.
3. Tap your divisions, for example **Boys U-12**, **Boys U-15**, **Girls U-12** and **Girls U-15**. **Ladies** and **Seniors 50+** are there too.
4. Tap **✓ Done**, then tap a division chip. The leaderboard shows only that division, with places inside it (1, 2, T3 …). Players who missed the cut or withdrew keep their labels.

**🏅 Division prizes** lists each division's winners once scores are in.

## One prize per player
In chess, **One prize per player** is on by default (the usual rule at Indian opens and school events). A player who wins an overall prize, or a prize in an earlier category, is passed over, and the next player in the category moves up. Set the number of **Overall prizes** the categories skip (3 by default). Categories are awarded in the order shown.

In golf it is off by default, because each division is its own competition: a junior can win overall and their division. Turn it on under **⚙ Divisions** if your rules say otherwise.

## Players the profile can't place
If a category needs an age or gender that a player's profile doesn't have, open the category: they appear as **＋ name**. Tap to add them. A hand-added player shows as **✓ name ✕**; tap again to take them out.

## Common questions

### What does U-11 mean exactly?
Under 11 on the cut-off day: a player who is 10 on that day is in, one who has turned 11 is not.

### Does "Rated below 1600" include unrated players?
Yes, as at most opens. Add **Best unrated** as well to award unrated players separately.

### Does a category change the main standings?
No. Categories only filter the ranking and list prizes. The overall table and its tie-breaks stay the same.
