Score a football match on one phone. The app runs the clock, stamps each moment with the minute and keeps team and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the format (**11-a-side**, **7-a-side**, **5s turf**, **Futsal** or **Custom**) and **If level at full time**.
- Set both lineups on the **Lineups** tab with **Set lineup & formation**. Use **Arrange on pitch (optional)** to put your keeper in the GK spot.

## Step by step: kick off and score
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **Kick off**. Joined late? Type the minute under "Already underway?" first.
2. For a goal, tap **Goal — Red House** (your team's name), then the scorer. It is on the board straight away. Pick the goal type or an assister, or tap **Close**. Use **Own goal instead** for an own goal, or **Team goal — no scorer**.
3. For a shot, tap **Shot**, the team and the player, then **On target**, **Off target** or **Blocked**.
4. For a foul, tap **Foul**, the team and the player, then who was fouled (or **Skip victim**). The app then offers a card: **Yellow for …**, **Red**, or **No card**.
5. For a coach or staff card, tap **Card**, the team, then **👔 Team official (coach / staff)**. It is on no player's stats.
6. For a substitution, tap **Sub — Red House**, the player going off, then the player coming on.
7. Near the end of each half, tap the added time the fourth official shows (**+1** to **+8**). If the board goes up again, tap **Change**. Then **End 1st Half →**, and later **End Match**.

## How minutes are shown
Minutes count the FIFA way: 10 minutes 30 seconds in is 11', and added time is 45+2' or 90+3', never 47'. Older matches keep their stamped minutes.

## Team stats and the box score
- From the second half the scoreboard and the match card show the half-time score, such as "HT 1-0" (after extra time also "FT 2-2").
- The **Stats** and **Summary** tabs compare the teams ("Not tracked" lists what's switched off). Each box score lists starters (*), then substitutes, with a **Totals** row and a **#** shirt-number column when the squad has numbers. **Show bench** adds unused players.
- An **OG** column appears only in a match with an own goal, on the player's own team. **SO** (shootout kicks scored-taken) and **SOS** (shootout saves) appear after a shootout, with the kick-by-kick list underneath.

## Player stats at full time
- At full time each player's stats are set from the timeline: goals by type, headers, assists, shots, saves, fouls, cards and penalties. A later correction re-sets them, so nothing is counted twice.
- An own goal counts for the other team. It goes on the player's profile as an **Own goal** (under Discipline), never as a goal.
- Shootout kicks are kept apart from match goals and saves: **Shootout kicks taken**, **Shootout kicks scored** and the keeper's **Shootout saves**.

## Minutes played and sin-bins
- Every player who took the field gets minutes (added time doesn't count). Minutes come from the lineup, so set it before kick-off.
- For youth or grassroots rules, the organiser can set **Sin-bin (minutes)** under **⚙ Customize this format**. The card panel then also shows **⏱️ Sin-bin 10'**; the player comes back by himself.

## Fix a moment you got wrong
1. Scroll to **Correct the timeline** and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to re-pick the player. Removing a yellow also removes the second-yellow red it caused.
3. Started late? Under **Backfill an earlier moment**, type a minute such as `12` or `45+2`, tap **Backfill**, then **Back to live** when done.

## Level knockout: penalties
1. If a knockout is level, the end button reads **End 2nd Half →** instead of **End Match**.
2. Choose extra time if offered, or tap **Penalty shootout →**.
3. The referee tosses a coin and the winning captain chooses. Tap **Red House first** or the other team's button.
4. For each kick, optionally tap who takes it. Players who have already kicked are marked "kicked" and listed last. The keeper facing it is filled in; tap **Change** if another player went in goal.
5. Tap **✓ Scored**, **🧤 Saved** or **✗ Missed**. The kick list under each team grows ("Messi ✓ · Salah ✗ saved (Martinez)") and the app stops once it is decided.

## Clean sheets and the Golden Glove
- A clean sheet goes to the goalkeeper on the pitch longest for a team that let in no goal (penalty shootouts don't count). Defenders don't get one.
- The **Golden Glove** ranks goalkeepers: most clean sheets, then saves, then fewest conceded. See [Tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## Common questions
### We had no full lineup. Does the keeper still get a clean sheet?
Yes, if the app knows the keeper: put him in the GK spot, or mark him as a GK on his profile.

### Who is suspended for our next match?
See [Football discipline and suspensions](/guides/football-discipline-and-suspensions/).

### Our keeper was sent off. What happens?
His time stops at the red card; a bench keeper who comes on takes over.
