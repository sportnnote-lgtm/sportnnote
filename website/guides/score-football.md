Score a football match on one phone, one tap at a time. The app runs the clock, stamps each moment with the match minute and keeps team and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the format (**11-a-side**, **7-a-side**, **5s turf**, **Futsal** or **Custom**) and **If level at full time**.
- Set both lineups on the **Lineups** tab with **Set lineup & formation**. Use **Arrange on pitch (optional)** to put your keeper in the GK spot.

## Step by step: kick off and score
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **Kick off**. Joined late? Type the minute under "Already underway?" first.
2. For a goal, tap **Goal — Red House** (your team's name), then the scorer. The goal is on the board straight away. Pick the goal type (**Header** included) or an assister, or tap **Close**. Use **Own goal instead** for an own goal, or **Team goal — no scorer** if you don't know who scored.
3. For a shot, tap **Shot**, the team and the player, then **On target**, **Off target** or **Blocked**. On target asks **Goal**, **Saved** (the save goes to the other keeper) or **On target only**; Blocked asks who blocked it.
4. For a foul, tap **Foul**, the team and the player. Pick who was fouled, or **Skip victim**. The app then asks for a card: **Yellow for …**, **Red**, or **No card**.
5. For a card to a coach or other staff, tap **Card**, the team, then **👔 Team official (coach / staff)**. Type a name if you like, then **Yellow** or **Red**. It goes on the timeline but on no player's stats, and nobody leaves the pitch.
6. For a substitution, tap **Sub — Red House**, the player going off, then the player coming on.
7. Near the end of each half, tap the added time the fourth official shows (**+1** to **+8**). If the board goes up again, tap **Change**. Then **End 1st Half →**, and later **End Match**.

## How minutes are shown
- Minutes count the way FIFA match reports do: a goal 10 minutes 30 seconds in shows as 11', and a half-time substitution is 46'.
- Added time shows as 45+2' or 90+3', never as 47' or 93'. Older matches keep the minutes they were stamped with.

## Team stats and the box score
The **Stats** and **Summary** tabs compare the teams ("Not tracked" lists what's switched off). Each box score lists starters (*), then substitutes, with a **Totals** row. **Show bench** adds unused players.

## Player stats at full time
- At full time each player's stats are set from the timeline: goals by type, headers, assists, shots, saves, fouls, cards and penalties. A later correction re-sets them, so nothing is counted twice.
- An own goal counts for the other team. It goes on the player's profile as an **Own goal** (under Discipline), never as a goal.

## Minutes played and sin-bins
- Every player who took the field gets minutes, until going off or a red card. Added time doesn't count.
- Minutes come from the lineup, so set it before kick-off. A team with no lineup gets no minutes.
- For youth or grassroots rules, the organiser can set **Sin-bin (minutes)** under **⚙ Customize this format**. When you log a card, the card panel then also shows **⏱️ Sin-bin 10'**. The player leaves for that time and comes back by himself.

## Fix a moment you got wrong
1. Scroll to **Correct the timeline** and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to re-pick the player. It keeps its minute; Cancel leaves it unchanged. Removing a yellow also removes the second-yellow red it caused.
3. Started late? Under **Backfill an earlier moment**, type a minute such as `12` or `45+2` and tap **Backfill**. A bar at the top shows the minute until you tap **Back to live**.

## Level knockout: penalties
1. If a knockout is level, the end button reads **End 2nd Half →** instead of **End Match**.
2. Choose extra time if offered, or tap **Penalty shootout →**.
3. The referee tosses a coin and the winning captain chooses. Tap **Red House first** or the other team's button.
4. Tap **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed** for each kick. The app says who kicks next and stops once it is decided.

## Clean sheets and the Golden Glove
- A clean sheet goes to the goalkeeper on the pitch longest for a team that let in no goal (penalty shootouts don't count). Defenders don't get one.
- Goals conceded are counted for whichever keeper was in goal at the time.
- The tournament **Golden Glove** award ranks goalkeepers only: most clean sheets, then most saves, then fewest goals conceded. See [Tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## Common questions
### We had no full lineup. Does the keeper still get a clean sheet?
Yes, if the app knows the keeper: put him in the GK spot, or mark him as a GK on his profile.

### Our keeper was sent off. What happens?
His time stops at the red card. A bench keeper who comes on takes over from then.
