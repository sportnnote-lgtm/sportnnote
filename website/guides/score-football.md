You score a football match on one phone, one tap at a time. The app runs the clock, stamps every moment with the match minute the way official reports do, and keeps team and player stats. Minutes played and clean sheets are worked out at the end.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the format when scheduling: **11-a-side**, **7-a-side**, **5s turf**, **Futsal** or **Custom**, plus what happens **If level at full time** (draw stands, extra time then penalties, or penalties straightaway).
- Set both lineups on the **Lineups** tab with **Set lineup & formation**. The lineup is how the app knows who kept goal, so use **Arrange on pitch (optional)** to put your goalkeeper in the GK spot. If you skip it, the first starter fills the GK spot.

## Step by step: kick off and score
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **Kick off**. Joined late? Type the current minute under "Already underway?" first.
2. For a goal, tap **Goal — Red House** (your team's name), then the scorer. Pick the goal type or an assister, or tap **No assist**. Use **Own goal instead** for an own goal, or **Team goal — no scorer** if you don't know who scored.
3. For a shot, tap **Shot**, the team and the player, then where it went: **On target**, **Off target** or **Blocked**.
4. An on-target shot asks what happened: **Goal**, **Saved** (the save goes to the other team's keeper) or **On target only**.
5. A blocked shot asks who blocked it. Tap the defender, or **Not sure — skip**.
6. For a substitution, tap **Sub — Red House**, the player going off, then the player coming on.
7. Near the end of each half, tap the added time the fourth official shows (**+1** to **+8**). Then **End 1st Half →**, and later **End Match**.

> **Tip:** A blocked shot counts as a shot but not as a shot on target (shots = on target + off target + blocked).

## How minutes are shown
- Minutes count the way FIFA match reports do. A goal 10 minutes 30 seconds in is in the 11th minute, so it shows as 11'. The first half-minute is 1', and a half-time substitution is 46'.
- Added time shows as 45+2' or 90+3', never as 47' or 93'.
- Older matches keep the minutes they were stamped with.

## Minutes played and sin-bins
- At full time every player who took the field gets their minutes: starters from kick-off, substitutes from the minute they came on, until they went off or were sent off. Only normal time and extra time count (90, or 120), never added time.
- Minutes come from the lineup, so set it before kick-off. A team with no lineup gets no minutes.
- For youth or grassroots rules, the organiser can set **Sin-bin (minutes)** under **⚙ Customize this format**. When you log a card, the card panel then also shows **⏱️ Sin-bin 10'**. The player leaves for that time and comes back by himself.
- During a sin-bin the scoreboard shows "⏱️ Suspended: Ravi (4:12 left)" and, while a team is short, "10 v 11 on the pitch". The same count shows after a red card.

## Fix a moment you got wrong
1. Scroll to **Correct the timeline** and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment, or **✎ Edit** to re-pick the player. The new entry keeps the original minute, including added time.
3. Started late? Under **Backfill an earlier moment**, type a minute such as `12` or `45+2` and tap **Backfill**. Everything you log is stamped there until you tap **Back to live**.

## Level knockout: penalties
1. If a knockout is level, the end button reads **End 2nd Half →** instead of **End Match**.
2. Choose extra time if offered, or tap **Penalty shootout →**.
3. Tap **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed** for each kick. The app stops the shootout as soon as it is decided.

## Clean sheets and the Golden Glove
- A clean sheet goes to the goalkeeper who was on the pitch longest for a team that let in no goal. Defenders don't get one.
- Only goals in normal time and extra time count. A 0–0 decided on penalties still gives both keepers a clean sheet.
- If your keeper is replaced, the one who played more minutes gets it. Goals conceded are counted for whichever keeper was in goal at the time.
- Each keeper's line also records goals conceded.
- If you fix a goal after full time, clean sheets are worked out again. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).
- The tournament **Golden Glove** award ranks goalkeepers only: most clean sheets, then most saves, then fewest goals conceded. See [Tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## Common questions
### We had no full lineup. Does the keeper still get a clean sheet?
Yes, if the app knows the keeper: put him in the GK spot, or mark a squad player as a GK on their profile.

### Our keeper was sent off. What happens?
His time stops at the red card. A bench goalkeeper who then comes on takes over from that minute.
