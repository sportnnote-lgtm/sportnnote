You score a football match on one phone, one tap at a time. The app runs the clock, stamps every moment with the match minute the way official reports do, and keeps team and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the format (**11-a-side**, **7-a-side**, **5s turf**, **Futsal** or **Custom**) and what happens **If level at full time**.
- Set both lineups on the **Lineups** tab with **Set lineup & formation**. Use **Arrange on pitch (optional)** to put your goalkeeper in the GK spot; otherwise the first starter fills it.

## Step by step: kick off and score
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **Kick off**. Joined late? Type the minute under "Already underway?" first.
2. For a goal, tap **Goal — Red House** (your team's name), then the scorer. Pick the goal type (**Header** included) or an assister, or tap **No assist**. Use **Own goal instead** for an own goal, or **Team goal — no scorer** if you don't know who scored.
3. For a shot, tap **Shot**, the team and the player, then where it went: **On target**, **Off target** or **Blocked**.
4. An on-target shot asks what happened: **Goal**, **Saved** (the save goes to the other team's keeper) or **On target only**.
5. A blocked shot asks who blocked it: tap the defender, or **Not sure — skip**.
6. For a substitution, tap **Sub — Red House**, the player going off, then the player coming on.
7. Near the end of each half, tap the added time the fourth official shows (**+1** to **+8**). Then **End 1st Half →**, and later **End Match**.

> **Tip:** A blocked shot counts as a shot but not as a shot on target.

## How minutes are shown
- Minutes count the way FIFA match reports do: a goal 10 minutes 30 seconds in shows as 11', and a half-time substitution is 46'.
- Added time shows as 45+2' or 90+3', never as 47' or 93'. Older matches keep the minutes they were stamped with.

## Team stats and the box score
On the **Stats** and **Summary** tabs, "Team stats" compares shots, possession, fouls, cards, corners and the rest ("Not tracked" lists what's switched off). Each team's box score lists starters (*) then substitutes: MIN, G, A, SH (goals included), SOT, SV, GA (keepers), FC, YC, RC and a **Totals** row. **Show bench** adds unused players. From the 2nd half you can pick **1st half** or **2nd half**.

## Player stats at full time
- At full time each player's stats are set from the timeline: goals by type, headers, assists, shots, saves, fouls, cards and penalties. A later correction re-sets them, so nothing is counted twice.
- An own goal counts for the other team. It goes on the player's profile as an **Own goal** (under Discipline), never as a goal.

## Minutes played and sin-bins
- Every player who took the field gets their minutes, from kick-off or the minute they came on until they went off or were sent off. Only normal and extra time count, never added time.
- Minutes come from the lineup, so set it before kick-off. A team with no lineup gets no minutes.
- For youth or grassroots rules, the organiser can set **Sin-bin (minutes)** under **⚙ Customize this format**. When you log a card, the card panel then also shows **⏱️ Sin-bin 10'**. The player leaves for that time and comes back by himself.
- During a sin-bin the scoreboard shows "⏱️ Suspended: Ravi (4:12 left)" and "10 v 11 on the pitch".

## Fix a moment you got wrong
1. Scroll to **Correct the timeline** and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment, or **✎ Edit** to re-pick the player. The new entry keeps the original minute, including added time.
3. Started late? Under **Backfill an earlier moment**, type a minute such as `12` or `45+2` and tap **Backfill**. Everything you log is stamped there until you tap **Back to live**.

## Level knockout: penalties
1. If a knockout is level, the end button reads **End 2nd Half →** instead of **End Match**.
2. Choose extra time if offered, or tap **Penalty shootout →**.
3. Tap **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed** for each kick. The shootout stops once it is decided.

## Clean sheets and the Golden Glove
- A clean sheet goes to the goalkeeper on the pitch longest for a team that let in no goal (penalty shootouts don't count). Defenders don't get one.
- Goals conceded are counted for whichever keeper was in goal at the time.
- If you fix a goal after full time, clean sheets are worked out again. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).
- The tournament **Golden Glove** award ranks goalkeepers only: most clean sheets, then most saves, then fewest goals conceded. See [Tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## Common questions
### We had no full lineup. Does the keeper still get a clean sheet?
Yes, if the app knows the keeper: put him in the GK spot, or mark a squad player as a GK on their profile.

### Our keeper was sent off. What happens?
His time stops at the red card. A bench keeper who comes on takes over from then.
