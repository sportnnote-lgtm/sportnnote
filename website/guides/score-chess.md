Chess is scored once per game: who won, or a draw, and how it ended. The app writes the result the way an arbiter does, White first: 1-0 when White wins, ½-½ for a draw and 0-1 when Black wins. Players get 1, ½ or 0 points in the table.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- In a Swiss, the pairing already gives each game its colours. See [Run a chess tournament](/guides/run-a-chess-tournament/).

## Step by step
1. Open the game → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Check the colours. The panel says "♔ White pieces:" with a name, and under it who has Black.
3. Under **Result (White first)**, tap **1-0** (White wins), **½-½** (Draw) or **0-1** (Black wins). Each box shows the names under it, for example "Anand beat Carlsen", so you can check the side.
4. Optionally, under **How (optional)**, tap how it ended. For a win: **Checkmate**, **Resignation**, **On time** or **Forfeit**. For a draw: **Draw agreed**, **Stalemate**, **Threefold repetition**, **50-move rule** or **Insufficient material**. You can also type the number of moves.
5. Tap **✓ Record 1-0…** (the button shows the result you picked).
6. A sheet asks, for example, "Record 0-1: Carlsen beat Anand by Resignation?". Tap **Yes, record result** to save it, or **No, go back** to change something.

Once saved, the game closes, the table updates and followers get the result.

## If the colours were swapped at the board
The colour is locked so a stray tap can't change the pairing. If the players really did swap, tap **Colours wrong? Change…**, then tap the player who had White. The note "Saved with the result" means nothing changes until you record the result. The 1-0 and 0-1 boxes follow the new colours.

> **Important:** In a Swiss, changing colours changes each player's colour history, which the next rounds' pairings use. Only change it if the game really was played the other way round.

## If a player doesn't turn up
A host can tap **🏳 Award a walkover**, or record the result with the **Forfeit** method. A forfeit scores the full point but is not a game played.

## Fix a mistake
- Just recorded the wrong result? Tap **↶ Undo** on the Scoring tab.
- Later, use [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Common questions
### Why does it ask before saving?
Recording a result closes the game, updates the table and tells followers at once. One tap on the wrong box would do all of that, so the app shows the result in words first.

### Why is the result 0-1 when the home player won?
The home player had Black. Chess results are always written White first.
