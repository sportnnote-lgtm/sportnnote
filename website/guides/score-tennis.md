Tennis scoring has a lot to remember: 15-30-40, deuce, tiebreaks and who serves next. You only tap who won each point. The app does the rest.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** when scheduling: **Best of 3 sets**, **Best of 5 sets**, **Grand Slam (Bo5, 10-pt TB at 6-6 in set 5)**, **Fast4**, **Pro set (to 8)**, **Match tiebreak (to 10)** or **Custom**.
- **At deuce** offers **Advantage (classic)** or **No-ad (deciding point)**. With no-ad, the point at 40-40 wins the game.
- Tap **⚙ Customize this format** for the rest. **Deciding set** can be a full set or a **10-point match tiebreak**. **Deciding-set tiebreak** can be **10-point at 6-6 (Grand Slam)**: the last set is played in games, and at 6-6 a tiebreak to 10 settles it.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**, and add the players.

## Step by step: score a point
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under "Who serves first?", tap the side that won the toss to serve. You can change it until the first point.
3. In doubles, under "Serving order — set 1", tap who serves first in each pair. You pick again at the start of every set.
4. The serving line then names the server, for example "Serving: Asha Rao".
5. After each point, tap the winning player under "Point — Red House" (or the other side's row).
6. For an ace, tap **🎯 Ace by Asha Rao**. It scores the point and gives the server an ace.
7. For a double fault, tap **⚠️ Double fault by Asha Rao → point Blue House**. The point goes to the receivers. Both buttons always name the current server.

## How the app follows the serve
- Serve changes every game, across sets too. In a tiebreak, the first server serves one point, then the serve changes every two points.
- In doubles, partners take turns on their pair's service games, starting with the player picked for the set.
- In a doubles tiebreak players keep that order, so a pair's second turn goes to the partner (ITF rule).

## Reading the board
The scoreboard shows the current game's points under POINTS and one column of games per set, with the set in play highlighted and a 🎾 dot next to the server. The header says "Set 2 · Tiebreak" during a tiebreak.

A set won in a tiebreak shows the loser's tiebreak points small next to the games, for example 7 and 6⁴. A match tiebreak gets its own "TB" column. After the match, the board shows sets won, and the result reads like "6-4, 3-6, 7-6(4)".

## Match stats
The **Score** tab shows a "Match stats" panel once the first point is played. From the second set, the chips switch between **Match** and each set.

- Aces, and service, return and total points won.
- Service games held and return games won (breaks).
- Break points saved and converted. A break point is a point that would win the game for the receiver. Tiebreak points never count as break points.
- Deciding points won, when the match uses no-ad.
- Set points and match points saved, when there were any.
- Most points in a row, and the biggest lead in games within a set.

In doubles, "Service points won, by server" gives each player's own serve, for example "18/26 (69%) · 4/5 held".

## Fix a mistake
- Tapped the wrong player just now? Tap **Undo**.
- To fix an older point, tap **Edit** next to "Correct the timeline" on the **Scoring** tab. Every point is listed, newest first.
1. Tap ✎ on the point. Change who won it, the type ("Point", "Ace" or "Double fault") and the player. Tap **Save**. For a double fault, pick the server who made it; their double fault count follows the fix.
2. To delete a point, tap ✕. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it, or **＋ Insert a point at the very start**.
3. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match, so games, sets, the server and the stats all recalculate. After the match, use [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends by itself when a side wins enough sets. If a player stops through injury, tap **🏁 End match…** and choose **Retired**. For a player put out by the umpire, choose **Default**. The result keeps the games played, such as "6-4, 3-2 ret.". See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
When the match ends, each player's tennis page adds it to **Apps**, **W-L** and **Win %**. **Match play** gives W-L and won % for sets, games and points, plus deciders and tiebreaks. **Serve & return** has aces, double faults, holds, breaks and break points; serve figures are each player's own. **Scoring** has points scored. Players with both formats also get **Singles / doubles**, and doubles players get **Partners**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Why does a Fast4 set end at 4-2?
Fast4 sets are first to 4 games, with a tiebreak at 3-3 and no-ad games.
