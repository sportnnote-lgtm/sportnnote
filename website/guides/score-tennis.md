Tennis has a lot to remember: 15-30-40, deuce, tiebreaks, who serves. You tap who won each point; the app does the rest.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- **Format**: **Best of 3 sets**, **Best of 5 sets**, **Grand Slam (Bo5, 10-pt TB at 6-6 in set 5)**, **Fast4**, **Pro set (to 8)**, **Match tiebreak (to 10)** or **Custom**.
- **At deuce**: **Advantage (classic)** or **No-ad (deciding point)**.
- **⚙ Customize this format**: **Deciding set** (full set or **10-point match tiebreak**) and **Deciding-set tiebreak**, e.g. **10-point at 6-6 (Grand Slam)**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**.

## Step by step: score a point
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under "Who serves first?", tap the serving side (changeable until the first point).
3. In doubles, under "Serving order — set 1", tap who serves first in each pair. You pick again every set.
4. The serving line names the server, e.g. "Serving: Asha Rao".
5. After each point, tap the winner under "Point — Red House" (or the other row).
6. Ace: tap **🎯 Ace by Asha Rao**. Double fault: tap **⚠️ Double fault by Asha Rao → point Blue House**. Both name the current server.

## How the app follows the serve
- Serve changes every game, across sets. In a tiebreak, the first server serves one point, then it changes every two.
- In doubles, partners alternate their pair's service games, tiebreaks included (ITF rule).

## Reading the board
POINTS shows the current game, with a games column per set and a 🎾 dot by the server. The header says "Set 2 · Tiebreak" in a tiebreak. A tiebreak set shows the loser's points small (7 and 6⁴); a match tiebreak gets a "TB" column. The result reads like "6-4, 3-6, 7-6(4)".

## Match stats
The **Score** tab's "Match stats" (chips: **Match** or each set) show:
- Aces, and service, return and total points won.
- Holds and breaks; break points saved and converted (never in tiebreaks).
- Deciding points won (no-ad); set and match points saved.
- Most points in a row; biggest games lead in a set.

In doubles, "Service points won, by server" gives each player's own serve, e.g. "18/26 (69%) · 4/5 held".

## Point detail (optional)
Off by default, so a point stays one tap. Turn it on with **On** in the "🔎 Point detail (optional)" row under the scoring buttons, or the **Point detail** toggle under "Stats captured" in "⚙️ Scoring settings" (Info tab or ☰ Quick options; mid-match, **Apply** starts it from the next point).

After each point, "How was it won? · Asha Rao" appears. Tap a chip (again to clear) or **Skip**; nothing is required.
- **Service winner** (server won), or **Winner** + **Forehand**, **Backhand**, **FH volley**, **BH volley**, **Overhead**, **Drop**, **Lob**, **Return**, and **At net**.
- **Forced error**, or **Unforced error** + **Forehand**, **Backhand**, **Volley**, **Overhead**.
- **1st / 2nd serve** chip (beside **Off** / **On**): points count as 1st-serve unless you tap **2nd serve**. Double faults are always 2nd-serve.

Winners count for the point winner, errors against the opponent (doubles: pick who under "By:"). Match stats add a "Point detail" block (winners, errors, by stroke, 1st serve in, 1st and 2nd serve points won); the box score adds **W**, **UE**, **FE**. Older matches are unchanged.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? Tap **Undo**.
- Older point: tap **Edit** next to "Correct the timeline" on the **Scoring** tab.
1. Tap ✎ on the point to change the winner, type ("Point", "Ace" or "Double fault"), player or "How was it won? (optional)". Tap **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to delete. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it, or **＋ Insert a point at the very start**.
3. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match, so games, server and stats follow. After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends itself when a side wins enough sets. For an injury, tap **🏁 End match…** → **Retired**; for an umpire's default, **Default**. The result keeps the games played, e.g. "6-4, 3-2 ret.". See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
The player's tennis page adds **Apps**, **W-L** and **Win %**. **Match play** has sets, games, points, deciders and tiebreaks. **Serve & return** has aces, double faults, holds, breaks, break points and (when serves were tracked) 1st serve in %, 1st and 2nd serve points won %. **Shot making** has winners, errors and Winners / UE from point-detail matches (☁ when only some tracked it). **Scoring** has points. Players with both formats get **Singles / doubles**; doubles players get **Partners**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Why does a Fast4 set end at 4-2?
Fast4 sets are first to 4 games, with a tiebreak at 3-3 and no-ad games.
