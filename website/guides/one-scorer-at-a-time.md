At a school final, two teachers may both be allowed to score. If both tap the same goal, the score goes wrong. So SportnNote lets several people be scorers, but only one phone scores at any moment. Everyone else watches live. When the scoring phone's battery dies, another scorer takes over in two taps.

## Before you start
- You must be listed as one of the match's scorers. Scorers are added on the match's **Info** tab under **Match scorers**. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- The lock only applies while a match is about to start or is live. Once the match ends, it's released.

## How you get the lock
You don't need to do anything. When you open the **Scoring** tab and nobody else is scoring, your phone quietly becomes the scoring phone. The first person to open the match and start scoring holds the lock.

On the **Info** tab, the **Match scorers** card shows everyone allowed to score. The person scoring right now has a **SCORING NOW** tag. Your own row says "📱 Scoring from this device".

## When someone else is scoring
If another scorer holds the lock, your **Scoring** tab shows a card instead of the scoring buttons. It reads, for example, "Priya is scoring right now (last update 2 min ago). One person scores at a time." You have two choices:
- **Watch live** opens the score view so you can follow along.
- **Take over scoring** moves scoring to your phone.

Your phone checks who holds the lock about every 15 seconds while the Scoring tab is open.

## Step by step: take over scoring
Use this when the scoring phone has died, lost signal, or the scorer has left.
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Tap **Take over scoring**.
3. Read the message "Take over from Priya?" and tap **Take over**.
4. The scoring buttons appear. Carry on from where the score stands.

> **Important:** Taps that never reached the server from the other phone are lost when you take over. Before you take over, check the score with the other scorer if you can, and re-enter anything missing.

## Step by step: hand over to another scorer
Use this for a planned change, for example at half-time or between innings.
1. Make sure the new scorer is listed under **Match scorers** on the **Info** tab and has joined SportnNote.
2. On their row, tap **Hand over**. Only the person scoring now, or a host, sees this link.
3. Confirm with **Hand over**. Your phone stops scoring at once.
4. The new scorer gets an alert, "You're scoring now", and scores from their next tap.

## If scoring moved away from your phone
If someone takes over from you, a banner at the top says "Scoring moved to Priya". Your scoring buttons are replaced by the "is scoring right now" card.

If your phone had taps that were still waiting to upload, the banner also says how many weren't saved. Tap **Discard** to throw them away. Those taps are never merged into the score, because merging could count the same goal or run twice. Tell the new scorer what you'd recorded so they can add it.

> **Tip:** If you're scoring with a weak signal, check the sync banner before handing over. Once your taps have uploaded, nothing is lost in a hand-over.

## Common questions

### What if the same person opens the match on two phones?
The second phone shows "You are scoring on another device". Tap **Take over scoring** on the phone you want to use.

### On a computer, does each browser tab count separately?
Yes. Each browser tab counts as its own device, so two tabs can't both score. Use one tab.

### Can the organiser take over?
Yes. Any listed scorer can take over. A host who isn't a scorer yet first adds themselves on the **Info** tab: tap **＋ Add scorer**, then their own name ("This device"). After that, the **Take over scoring** button appears on the Scoring tab.

### What happens when the match ends?
The lock is released. After full time, scorers and hosts can fix mistakes from the Info tab. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Someone took over by mistake. How do I get it back?
Tap **Take over scoring** again on your phone, or ask them to tap **Hand over** on your row.
