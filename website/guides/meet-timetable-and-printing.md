Call rooms, lane judges and notice boards run on a timetable and on paper. Give each round a day, time and area (track, jumps pit, pool) when you add the event. The meet's **Timetable** shows the programme by day and session and prints A4 sheets.


> **Note:** Times show in the venue's time zone (the tournament's), on every phone and every printed sheet.
## Before you start
- Add events and change times as a host of the tournament or an organiser. Anyone can see the timetable.
- Set the tournament's start and end dates. Each date becomes a day (Day 1, Day 2 …) on the timetable.

## Step by step: give an event its time
1. On the tournament, open the sport's events page and tap **＋ Add an event** (or **＋ Add a race**, **＋ Add a shooting event** …).
2. Fill in the category, the event, the athletes and the rounds as usual.
3. In **When & where**, check the **Area** and the time of each round. The app suggests the first free slot at that area on today's meet day, or Day 1 if the meet hasn't started. A final follows the heats the same afternoon at 15:00 if the heats are in the morning, or the next day if they're in the afternoon.
4. Change any time by typing it, for example `930` or `09:30`. Use **‹** and **›** to move a round to another day. Times before 12:00 are the morning session, before 17:00 the afternoon, and later ones the evening.
5. If an athlete you picked is in another event less than 30 minutes away, a red line names them and the other event. You can still create the event.
6. Tap **Create event & start list**. Later rounds take their planned time when they're seeded.

## Step by step: run the day from the timetable
1. On the events page, tap **🗓 Timetable & print sheets**.
2. Pick a day from the tabs. **NOW** shows what's running and **NEXT** what's coming up. Tap a row to open it.
3. Running late? Under a session, tap **+10 min**, **+15 min** or **+30 min**. Every round in that session that hasn't started yet, from the next one on, moves back after you confirm. Other sessions and finished rounds stay where they are.
4. To move one round, tap **Change time / area** under it, pick the day, type the time and area, and tap **Save**. A final that hasn't been seeded yet can be moved too; it shows **PLANNED**.
5. Events added before the timetable existed are listed under **Time to be announced**. Tap **Set time** to put them on the timetable.

## Athletes doubling up
- **Doubling up** lists every athlete with two events too close together on the same day. The time counts from the estimated end of the first event (a 100 m heat about 5 minutes, a long jump about 50) to the start of the next.
- Organisers set the warning gap with **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min** or **60 min**. It's saved for the whole meet.
- A field-event referee can excuse an athlete to run a race, so a warning is a prompt to plan, not a block.

## Step by step: print the sheets
1. In **Print sheets** on the timetable, pick what you need. The choices are **Timetable (all days)**, **Start lists & heat sheets** for the day, **Field-event cards**, **Results** and the **House table**.
2. On a computer, the sheet opens in a new tab. Tap **Print / Save as PDF**, or choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
3. To print one event, open it and tap **🖨 Print start list** (or **🖨 Print results** once times are in). Jumps, throws and weightlifting also have **📝 Field card**.

What each sheet shows:
- Every sheet has the meet's name, host, dates and logo at the top, on A4.
- **Start lists and heat sheets:** each heat with its lanes or order, bibs (if you use them), names and houses. Blank columns are left for the time and place, with signature lines for the starter and the chief timekeeper.
- **Field cards:** a box for each trial, 3 plus 3 more for the top 8, and a wind box for long and triple jump. High jump and pole vault show every bar height with three try boxes for O, X or –. Weightlifting shows three snatch and three clean and jerk boxes. Officials fill these in by hand.
- **Results:** places, marks, wind and the trials of a field event. They also show the **Q** / **q**, PB, SB and record markers, and a list of the records set. A sheet printed before the round is locked says "unofficial".
- **House table:** gold, silver, bronze and points for each house.

> **Note:** Android printing needs the latest app version; older versions share the sheet as a message.

## Common questions
### Why does my old event show "Time to be announced"?
It was created before timetables existed, so it has no planned time. Tap **Set time**. Its results, records and points don't change.

### Does moving a time change the record date?
Yes. A record is dated by the day the round is scheduled, so set the right day before the final is locked.
