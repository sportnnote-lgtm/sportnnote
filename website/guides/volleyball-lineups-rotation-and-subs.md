The toss and the rotation are optional. Record them and the app knows who serves every rally: it shows "🏐 Rohan serves", makes an ace one tap on the server, and charges a missed serve to the player who missed. Substitutions keep the six on court right, so the point chips and each player's sets played stay correct.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [How to score a volleyball match](/guides/score-volleyball/).
- Both squads must be added. A lineup helps: its six are suggested for the rotation, and the player in the **L** slot is suggested as libero.

## Step by step: the toss
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab. Before the first point you see "🪙 Toss — who serves first?".
2. Tap the team that serves first (🏐 and its name).
3. You don't need to do this again in sets 2, 3 and 4: the team that did not serve first in the previous set serves first (FIVB rule 7.1). The deciding set asks for a new toss.
4. Already scoring? Tap **🏐 Track serve, rotation & subs** and say who served first in this set.
5. Picked the wrong team? Tap **Change** (or **Fix first server** once the set has started).

## Step by step: the rotation
1. Under each team, tap **↻ Set rotation (I–VI)**.
2. Tap the players in serving order. The first one is position I (back right), who serves first. Then II, III, IV, V and VI. Tap a picked player's chip to remove them.
3. Under **Libero**, tap up to two liberos. They are not in the rotation.
4. Tap **✓ Save rotation**.
5. A small court shows each team: IV, III and II at the net, V, VI and I behind. The server is marked 🏐.

When a team wins the serve back, it rotates one place clockwise, and the next player serves. You never tap anything for this. If you set the rotation in the middle of a set, enter where the players stand now. A new set keeps the last rotation; tap **↻ Rotation…** to change it.

## Step by step: a substitution
1. Tap **🔁 Sub** under the team. The heading shows how many substitutions it has used, for example "subs 2/6".
2. Tap the player going off, then the player coming on.
3. For an injured player, tap **🩹 Injury (exceptional sub)** before you pick who comes on.
4. If something looks against the rules, the app asks first. For example: "Kiran Rao can only come back for Sneha Rao, who replaced them". Tap **No, cancel** to go back, or **Record anyway** if the referee allowed it.

The checks follow the FIVB rules:
- Each team has 6 substitutions per set. A 7th is offered only as an exceptional substitution for injury, which does not count.
- A starting player may leave once per set and come back once, only in place of the player who replaced them.
- A substitute may come on once per set, and only the starter they replaced can take their place.

## Libero swaps
Pick the libero as the player coming on or going off in **🔁 Sub**. Libero swaps are not substitutions and don't count toward the 6. The app asks if the libero would replace a front-row player, or if two swaps have no rally between them. A note appears if the libero rotates to the front row or to the serve, telling you who should come back on. The libero may not serve, block or attack above the net.

## What you get
- The **Score** tab adds "Match stats": points won on serve, side-out % (points won when receiving), aces, serve errors, the longest run and set points saved, for the match or one set. It also lists points won on serve by each server.
- A "Substitutions" list shows each change with the set and score, for example "Set 2 · 20-22 · Red House: ⬇ Kiran Rao ⬆ Sneha Rao".
- **Serve errors** go on the server's profile. **Sets played** includes every substitute and libero who came on.

## Common questions
### Do I have to record the toss?
No. Without it the app still works out the serving team from the rallies, as before. You just don't get the server's name or the serve figures.

### What happens to subs if I fix an old point?
They stay. A substitution is kept with its set and the number of rallies played, so a timeline fix doesn't lose it.

### Can I undo a substitution?
Yes. Tap **Undo** straight after it.
