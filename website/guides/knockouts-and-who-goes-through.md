A group match can end level. A knockout match can't: someone has to go through. SportnNote sets each match's rule for a level score from its stage, so a group draw is never pushed into a shoot-out and a knockout is never left without a winner.

## Before you start
- You must be an organiser (host) of the tournament.
- Set the sport's format on the tournament, including **If level at full time**. That is the rule your knockout matches use.

## How the stage sets the rule
- **League and group matches:** the draw stands, whatever the tournament's **If level at full time** says. The points table scores it as a draw. Hockey's **FIH + shoot-out bonus** table is the exception: every level match there still has a shoot-out for the 2 / 1 points.
- **Knockout matches** (any knockout round, the third-place match, a play-in, a Qualifier / Eliminator, or a double-elimination or plate match): if you left the decider at "draw stands", the match uses the sport's standard one instead.
- Football: extra time, then penalties.
- Hockey: a shoot-out.
- Handball: extra time 2 × 5, again if level, then 7-metre throws (IHF).
- Kabaddi: extra time, then a golden raid.
- Basketball: overtime until there is a winner, even if your league allows draws.

> **Note:** The rule is fixed when a match is created (auto-generated fixtures, a scheduled match, an imported schedule or the bracket's next round). Matches created earlier keep the rule they were created with.

## Two-legged ties (aggregate)
Make one from the tournament page with **New series / tie** → **🔁 Two legs (aggregate)**. Leg 1 can end level; the tie isn't over. Once leg 1 is finished, leg 2 knows its score when the scorer opens it, and the leg-2 scoreboard shows the running total, for example "Aggregate 2–2".

1. If the aggregate is level when leg 2 ends, leg 2 goes to the decider even if leg 2 itself wasn't drawn. Football plays extra time, then penalties. Handball goes straight to 7-metre throws, as two-legged ties usually do. Hockey has a shoot-out.
2. If leg 2 is drawn but one team leads on aggregate, there is no extra time. Full time ends the tie.
3. **Away-goals tiebreak** is off unless you tick it. UEFA scrapped away goals in 2021.

> **Tip:** Score leg 1 before anyone opens leg 2. The leg-1 score is added to leg 2 only before leg 2 kicks off.

## Pick who advances
Sometimes scoring can't settle a knockout: a match was abandoned or called off, ended level with no decider, or a two-legged tie finished level without one. The bracket then shows "No winner from the scoring" under that tie instead of waiting forever.

1. Open the tournament → **🏆 Knockout bracket**.
2. Under the tie, tap **Advances: Red House** or the other side.
3. Type the reason, for example "Abandoned at 1–1 after a floodlight failure; Red House won the toss".
4. Tap **Confirm: Red House advance**, then **Yes, Red House advance**.

The bracket marks the tie "Organiser decision" with your reason, the next round unlocks, and the decision goes in the tournament's activity log. A result from the match itself always beats a pick. Picks work the same way in double-elimination brackets and plate draws.

For how the draw itself is seeded, see [Seed a knockout draw so the best meet last](/guides/seed-a-knockout-draw/).

## Common questions
### Our knockout ended level and the app didn't offer penalties. Why?
The match was probably created before this rule, with the decider at "draw stands". Once it is finished, pick who advances in the bracket as above.

### Can I change a pick?
Not yet. A pick is final once confirmed, so check the team and the reason before you tap **Yes**.

### Does basketball play overtime on aggregate?
Not yet. A level aggregate in basketball goes to the organiser's pick.
