Field events run on trials, not heats. The app shows who is up, ranks everyone as you go and settles ties the World Athletics way. Finals add house points like track finals.

## Before you start
- Set up the tournament as in [Run athletics track events](/guides/run-athletics-track-events/): **Athletics** as a sport, position points, and houses as teams.
- Only a tournament host (or organiser) can add events and enter results.

## Step by step: add a field event
1. Open the tournament, tap 🏃 Athletics, then **＋ Add an event**.
2. Pick the age group and Boys, Girls or Mixed. **Jumps** and **Throws** follow the track events. Pole vault and hammer are marked optional, as they need a vault bed or hammer cage.
3. Each throw shows its implement, e.g. **Shot put · 3 kg**: World Athletics weights for U18, U20 and Open, common school practice below that. Check with your federation and change it if yours differs. Personal bests are kept per weight.
4. Pick the athletes and optionally type an entry mark in metres (5.12) or tap **Fill from PB**.
5. **Rounds:** a straight final suits most school meets. With more than 12 athletes you can choose **Qualification → final of 12** with a qualifying standard. Everyone who reaches it goes through; the best of the rest fill any spare places.
6. **Event settings:** for the triple jump, pick the take-off board (7, 9, 11 or 13 m). For long and triple jump, turn off **Wind gauge at the pit** if you have no gauge.
7. **High jump and pole vault:** set the opening height, the raise, and where the raise gets smaller (say 5 cm up to 1.40, then 3 cm). The bar must go up by at least 2 cm (5 cm in pole vault), and the raise may never grow.
8. **Order of trials:** **Draw the order** (World Athletics) or **Best entry mark last**. Then tap **Create event & start list**.

## Step by step: long jump, triple jump and throws
1. Open the event and tap **Enter results**. The top card shows who is up, their trial number, their place and the leader.
2. Type the mark (4.85) and, for the jumps, the wind (+1.2). Tap **✓ Mark**, **X foul** or **– pass**, and the next athlete comes up.
3. Wrong button? Tap **Undo** on the bottom bar within 5 seconds. This also works for **O clear**, **X fail** and **– pass** at the bar.
4. A mark far outside the usual range, such as 512 for a 5.12 m long jump, or a bar height of 120 for 1.20 m, asks **Check this mark** before saving. Nothing is refused, but an unconfirmed mark never shows **PB** or **MR**.
5. After round 3 the best 8, plus anyone tied for 8th, are marked ★ and take 3 more trials in reverse order of standing, so the leader goes last (round 6 uses the order after round 5). With 8 athletes or fewer, everyone gets all 6.
6. To correct a trial, tap **Edit round**, pick the round number and fix it on that athlete's row.
7. When the card says **All trials taken**, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The check lists anyone with no trial and any new meet record. An organiser can later tap **↺ Reopen final** to correct it; any record it set returns to the previous holder until it is locked again.

> **Tip:** Tap the ⏱ chip to time a trial. Athletes normally get 1 minute; the clock turns red for the last 15 seconds.

## Step by step: high jump and pole vault
1. Tap **Enter results**. The card shows the bar, who is up and which of their 3 tries it is.
2. Tap **O clear**, **X fail** or **– pass**. First tries go round in order, then second tries, then third.
3. Athletes may pass any height. Three failures in a row, even across heights, and they are out. The grid shows every O, X and – per height.
4. When everyone is done at the last height, tap **＋ Next height**.
5. Ties are settled by fewer failures at the tied height, then fewer failures in total.

## Jump-off for 1st
If athletes are still level for 1st when everyone is out, the card shows **Tie for 1st**:
- Tap **They share 1st** if the athletes agree. Both get gold.
- Or tap **Start jump-off**: one try per height. If all fail, the bar comes down 2 cm (5 cm in pole vault); if more than one clears, it goes up. The last to clear wins.
- The jump-off decides 1st only; marks stay at the tied height.

## Wind, records and profiles
- A jump with wind over +2.0 m/s is wind-aided, shown with a "w". It counts for places but not for personal bests or meet records; the best legal jump is used instead.
- Without a gauge at the pit, jumps with no wind reading count for records.
- **Best by event** on the Athletics page shows the best mark per category. Athlete profiles show each personal best with its wind or implement.

## Common questions
### Can I share the results?
Yes. **📤 Share** sends the start list or results, with each jump's wind, to WhatsApp.
