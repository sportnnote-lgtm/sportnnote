Field events run on trials, not heats. The app tells you who is up next, ranks everyone as you go and settles ties the World Athletics way. Finals add points to the house table, just like track finals.

## Before you start
- Set up the tournament as in [Run athletics track events](/guides/run-athletics-track-events/): **Athletics** as a sport, the position points, and your houses as teams.
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter results.

## Step by step: add a field event
1. Open the tournament, tap 🏃 Athletics, then **＋ Add an event**.
2. Pick the age group and Boys, Girls or Mixed. **Jumps** and **Throws** appear under the track events. Pole vault and hammer are marked optional: they need a vault bed or a hammer cage.
3. Each throw shows its implement, for example **Shot put · 3 kg**. For U18, U20 and Open these are the World Athletics weights. For younger groups they follow common school practice, so check with your federation and change the weight if yours differs. Personal bests are kept separately for each weight.
4. Pick the athletes and, if you like, type an entry mark in metres (5.12) or tap **Fill from PB**.
5. **Rounds:** a straight final suits most school meets. With more than 12 athletes you can choose **Qualification → final of 12** and type a qualifying standard. Everyone who reaches it goes through; if fewer than 12 do, the best of the rest fill the final.
6. **Event settings:** for the triple jump, pick the take-off board (7, 9, 11 or 13 m from the sand). For the long and triple jump, turn off **Wind gauge at the pit** if you have no gauge.
7. **High jump and pole vault:** set the opening height, how much the bar goes up, and the height where the raise gets smaller (say 5 cm up to 1.40, then 3 cm). The bar must go up by at least 2 cm (5 cm in pole vault), and the raise may never grow.
8. **Order of trials:** **Draw the order** (World Athletics) or **Best entry mark last**. Then tap **Create event & start list**.

## Step by step: long jump, triple jump and throws
1. Open the event and tap **Enter results**. The card at the top shows who is up, their trial number, where they stand now and the leader.
2. Type the mark (4.85) and, for the jumps, the wind (+1.2). Tap **✓ Mark**, or tap **X foul** or **– pass**. The next athlete comes up at once.
3. Tapped **X foul** or **– pass** by mistake? Tap **Undo** on the bar at the bottom. It stays for 5 seconds. The same works for **O clear**, **X fail** and **– pass** at the bar.
4. A mark far outside the usual range, such as 512 typed for a 5.12 m long jump, asks **Check this mark** before it is saved. A bar height like 120 for 1.20 m asks too. Nothing is refused, but an unconfirmed mark never shows **PB** or **MR**.
5. After round 3 the best 8, plus anyone tied for 8th, are marked ★. They take 3 more trials, in reverse order of the standings, so the leader goes last. Round 6 uses the order after round 5. With 8 athletes or fewer, everyone gets all 6.
6. To correct a trial, tap **Edit round**, pick the round number and fix it on that athlete's row.
7. When the card says **All trials taken**, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The check lists anyone with no trial and any new meet record. An organiser can tap **↺ Reopen final** afterwards to correct it; any record it set goes back to the previous holder until it is locked again.

> **Tip:** Tap the ⏱ chip on the card to time a trial. Athletes normally have 1 minute; the clock turns red for the last 15 seconds.

## Step by step: high jump and pole vault
1. Tap **Enter results**. The card shows the bar, who is up and which of their 3 tries it is.
2. Tap **O clear**, **X fail** or **– pass**. First tries go round in order, then second tries, then third.
3. An athlete may pass any height. Three failures in a row, even across heights, and they are out. The grid shows every athlete's O, X and – at each height.
4. When everyone has finished at the last height, tap **＋ Next height** to add one.
5. Ties are settled by fewer failures at the tied height, then fewer failures in total.

## Jump-off for 1st
If two athletes are still level for 1st when everyone is out, the card shows **Tie for 1st**:
- Tap **They share 1st** if the athletes agree. Both get gold.
- Or tap **Start jump-off**. Each athlete has one try per height. If all fail, the bar comes down 2 cm (5 cm in pole vault). If more than one clears, it goes up. The last one to clear wins.
- The jump-off decides 1st only. The athletes' marks stay at the tied height.

## Wind, records and profiles
- A jump with wind over +2.0 m/s is wind-aided and shown with a "w". It counts for places but not for personal bests or meet records. The athlete's best legal jump is used instead.
- Without a gauge at the pit, jumps with no wind reading count for records.
- **Best by event** on the Athletics page shows the longest or highest mark per category. Athlete profiles show each personal best with its wind or implement.

## Common questions
### Can I share the results?
Yes. Tap **📤 Share** to send the start list or the results, with the wind for each jump, to a WhatsApp group.
