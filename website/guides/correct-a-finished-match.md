After full time, a scorer may notice a goal credited to the wrong player, or a point that never happened. Without a fix, the player's stats and the points table stay wrong. **✏️ Correct this match** lets you fix the record after the match. Every correction is published openly, so players and parents can see what changed and who changed it.

## Before you start
- Scorers and match hosts can correct a match for 24 hours after it ends. The button shows how long is left, for example "Open for 17 h 40 m more".
- Tournament hosts can correct their tournament's matches at any time. They own the points table.
- All taps from the scoring phone must have uploaded. While some are still waiting, the button is greyed out and says "Waiting for unsynced taps to upload."
- Cricket has its own ball-by-ball editor. See [Fix a past ball, bowler or batter](/guides/cricket-edit-a-past-ball/).

## Step by step
1. Open the finished match → **Info** tab.
2. Tap **✏️ Correct this match**.
3. Read the message "Fix a scoring mistake?" and tap **Continue**. It reminds you that every change you publish is listed publicly.
4. You'll see the match's entries, newest first, such as each goal or point with the team and player.
5. If an entry never happened, tap **✕ Remove** on it. Tap **↺ Keep** if you change your mind.
6. If an entry was credited to the wrong person, tap **✎ Player** and pick the right player. Tap **↺ Undo change** to go back.
7. Changed entries are marked "removed" or "changed". Nothing is saved yet. Tap **Preview** at the bottom. The button shows how many changes you've made.
8. Check the preview. **Before → After** shows the score and result before and after. **Player stats** shows each player's change, for example "R. Kumar · goals −1". **Changes** lists what you did, in plain words.
9. Tap **Publish**, or **Back to edit** to keep working.

You'll see "Corrections published". The score, player stats and points table now use the corrected record.

> **Important:** You can only remove an entry or move it to another player. You can't add an entry that was missed during the match, so record everything live and check the score at each break.

## What everyone sees
The match's **Info** tab gets a public **Score edits** card. Each correction shows the date and time, the name of the person who published it, and a line for every change. This keeps corrections honest. Nobody can quietly change a result.

> **Tip:** Write down what went wrong before you open the screen, such as "Goal at 23' was Arjun, not Rahul". It makes the list quick to fix.

## Rules behind the feature
- A correction rebuilds the match from its record. The score, the result and the Player of the Match suggestion are worked out again from the corrected entries.
- A Player of the Match picked by hand stays. If a scorer already changed the Player of the Match, a correction doesn't replace it.
- A result entered by hand stays. If the match was ended with **🏁 End match…** (for example "No result — Rain"), that result is kept and only the score is refreshed. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).
- The 24-hour window starts when the match ends.

## Common questions

### The Preview says the change "leaves the match unfinished". What does that mean?
Your changes would leave the match without a finished result, for example by removing the winning point in a set. **Publish** stays off so the result isn't lost. Change your edits so the match still ends.

### I don't see Correct this match. Why?
Check that:
- the match has finished
- you are one of its scorers or hosts, and it ended less than 24 hours ago
- your scoring phone has uploaded all its taps

Tournament hosts can always correct, so ask them if the window has closed.

### Does a correction change the points table?
Yes, if the result changes. The table always uses the published record.

### Can I correct a match I watched but didn't score?
No. Only the match's scorers and hosts, and the tournament's hosts, can correct it. Anyone else can raise the problem with the organiser.
