When your eyes need to stay on the game, you can say what happened instead of tapping. The app turns each call into the same action the buttons make, so the score, the timeline and every player's stats come out the same.

## Before you start
- You must be the match's scorer or host. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- Speaking needs Chrome or Edge on the web (open app.sportnnote.in on the phone). In the Android app, and in other browsers, type the same words in the box under the button and tap **Send**.
- Say players by surname (or shirt number) and teams by name, or "home" and "away".

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **🎤 Voice scoring**. Allow the microphone if the phone asks.
2. Say a call, for example "goal Rao". The panel shows what it heard and what it will do: "Heard 'goal Rao' → goal · Asha Rao — Red House".
3. Tap **✓ Apply** to log it, or **✕** to drop it.
4. Keep going. **Recent calls** lists the last six calls, and **↩ Undo last** takes back the last action.
5. Tap **🛑 Stop listening** when you're done.

> **Tip:** **✋ Confirm** is the default: every call waits for your tap. Switch to **⚡ Auto ON** once you trust it, and confident calls go in straight away. A call the phone wasn't sure about still waits.

## Ending a period or the match
Saying "end quarter", "end half" or "full time" never ends anything on its own. The same "End the …?" sheet as the buttons opens, and nothing happens until you tap **Yes**. This applies in Auto mode too.

The same goes for any call that would finish the match, such as the winning point in badminton, the hole that closes out a golf match, or a carrom board that wins a game. The panel says "ends the match" (or "ends game 1"), and the sheet asks before the call goes in.

Football has its own voice panel that talks back ("Goal for Red House — who scored?") and logs each answer straight away. Its "end half" and "full time" now ask on screen first too, as the buttons do. A level knockout goes on to its decision instead of ending.

## What to say, sport by sport
- **Football**: "goal", then the scorer and assist when asked; "yellow card Rao", "corner home", "substitution", "kick off", "end half", "full time".
- **Cricket**: "four", "six", "dot", "two runs", "wide", "no ball", "leg bye", "wicket caught".
- **Basketball**: "two Rao", "three Rao", "free throw Rao", "rebound Rao", "foul Rao".
- **Volleyball**: "attack Rao", "ace Rao", "block Rao", "point home".
- **Kabaddi**: "raid Rao", "tackle Rao", "green card Rao", "yellow card Rao", "technical point home". A red card stays on the **Card** button, which asks first and adds the technical point.
- **Hockey**: "goal Rao", "PC goal Rao assist Khan", "stroke goal home", "penalty corner home", "shot Rao" (off target), "shot saved Rao" (the other keeper gets the save), "green card Rao", "yellow 10 Rao", "red card Rao", "circle home", "end quarter", "full time".
- **Handball**: "goal Rao" (a 9 m goal unless you say "wing", "6 m", "7 m", "fast break" or "breakthrough"), "wing goal Rao assist Khan", "7 m saved Rao", "shot missed Rao", "yellow Rao", "two minutes Rao", "red Rao", "time-out home", "turnover Rao", "end half", "full time".
- **Tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball**: "point home", "Rao scores", "ace Rao", "double fault Rao" (tennis).
- **Carrom**: the board winner and the coins left, for example "board home 5", "Rao 3 queen" or "board away 9 slam". Say "penalty home" for a penalty board to that side. You must say the coins; there is no default.
- **Golf match play**: "hole home", "Rao wins the hole", "halved". With handicap strokes set up, say both scores, for example "Rao 4 Khan 5", and the hole goes to the better net score. "Strokes 4 5" means home first.

## Good to know
- Stats go to the player you name, the same way the buttons credit them.
- In hockey and handball, a call is stamped at the game-clock time you said it, not when you tap **Apply**.
- A house called "Red" or "Yellow" is never read as a card colour. Numbers like "two minutes", "7 m" or a carrom coin count are never read as shirt numbers.
- "Full time" in hockey and handball only works in the last period. Earlier, say "end quarter" or "end half".

## Common questions

### It keeps saying "didn't catch"
Add the team or the surname ("goal home", "goal Rao"). A bare "goal" doesn't say who scored. You can always type the call instead.

### I applied the wrong call
Tap **↩ Undo last**. Or fix it later with **✎ Correct the timeline**, or after the match with [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Can I use voice for chess or the event sports?
Not yet. Chess results and event results are entered on their own screens.
