Say what happened instead of tapping. Each call becomes the same action the buttons make, so the score, timeline and player stats come out the same.

## Before you start
- You must be the match's scorer or host. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- Speaking works in the Android app (the latest version from the install page) and in Chrome or Edge on the web. In older app versions and other browsers, type the same words in the box under the button and tap **Send**.
- Say players by surname (or shirt number) and teams by name, or "home" and "away".

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **🎤 Voice scoring**. The first time, the app says why it needs the microphone; tap **Continue**, then allow it.
2. Say a call, for example "goal Rao". The panel shows what it heard and what it will do: "Heard 'goal Rao' → goal · Asha Rao — Red House".
3. Tap **✓ Apply** to log it, or **✕** to drop it.
4. Keep going. **Recent calls** lists the last six calls, and **↩ Undo last** takes back the last action.
5. Tap **🛑 Stop listening** when you're done. The app also stops by itself after about 45 seconds of quiet. Tap **🎤** to start again.

> **Tip:** **✋ Confirm** is the default: every call waits for your tap. Switch to **⚡ Auto ON** once you trust it, and confident calls go in straight away. A call the phone wasn't sure about still waits.

## Ending a period or the match
Saying "end quarter", "end half" or "full time" never ends anything on its own. The same "End the …?" sheet as the buttons opens, and nothing happens until you tap **Yes**. This applies in Auto mode too.

The same goes for any call that would finish the match, such as a badminton match point. The panel says "ends the match" and the sheet asks first.

Football's own voice panel asks back ("who scored?"); its "end half" and "full time" ask on screen first too.

## What to say, sport by sport
- **Football**: "goal", then the scorer and assist when asked; "yellow card Rao", "corner home", "substitution", "kick off", "end half", "full time".
- **Cricket**: "four", "six", "dot", "two runs", "wide", "no ball", "leg bye", "wicket caught".
- **Basketball**: "two Rao", "three Rao", "free throw Rao", "rebound Rao", "foul Rao".
- **Volleyball**: "attack Rao", "ace Rao", "block Rao", "point home".
- **Kabaddi**: "raid Rao", "tackle Rao", "green card Rao", "yellow card Rao", "technical point home". A red card stays on the **Card** button, which asks first and adds the technical point.
- **Hockey**: "goal Rao", "PC goal Rao assist Khan", "penalty corner home", "shot saved Rao", "green card Rao", "yellow 10 Rao", "circle home", "end quarter".
- **Handball**: "goal Rao" (9 m unless you say "wing", "6 m", "7 m", "fast break" or "breakthrough"), "7 m saved Rao", "two minutes Rao", "red Rao", "time-out home", "end half".
- **Tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball**: "point home", "Rao scores", "ace Rao", "double fault Rao" (tennis).
- **Carrom**: the board winner and the coins left, for example "board home 5" or "Rao 3 queen". "Penalty home" gives a penalty board.
- **Golf match play**: "hole home", "halved", or with handicap strokes both scores ("Rao 4 Khan 5").

## Your voice and privacy
- SportnNote never records, stores or uploads your audio. Only the words the phone recognised come back to the app, and only the score action you apply is saved.
- Your phone's own speech service turns speech into text. On most Android phones that is Google's, and it may send the audio to Google's servers to do so. The app uses on-device recognition when the phone has the language pack for your English downloaded. In Chrome or Edge on the web, the browser's speech service does it.
- The app listens for the English of where your phone is set: British English in the UK, Australian English in Australia, US English in the US, and Indian English everywhere else. If the phone doesn't have that one, it falls back to US English.
- Turn the microphone off any time in the phone's **Settings → Apps → SportnNote → Permissions**. Typing still works.

## Good to know
- Stats go to the player you name, the same way the buttons credit them.
- In hockey and handball, a call is stamped at the game-clock time you said it, not when you tap **Apply**.
- "Full time" in hockey and handball only works in the last period. Earlier, say "end quarter" or "end half".

## Common questions

### It keeps saying "didn't catch"
Add the team or the surname ("goal home", "goal Rao"). A bare "goal" doesn't say who scored. You can always type the call instead.

### The 🎤 button says to type instead
The panel says why: the microphone is off for SportnNote (allow it in Settings), the phone has no speech service turned on (on Android, install or enable Google's speech services), or there's no connection. Type the call and tap **Send** meanwhile.

### I applied the wrong call
Tap **↩ Undo last**. Or fix it later with **✎ Correct the timeline**, or after the match with [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Can I use voice for chess or the event sports?
Not yet. Chess results and event results are entered on their own screens.
