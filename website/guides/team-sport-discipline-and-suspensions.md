Cards shown in one match can cost a player the next one. The app keeps a discipline table for every hockey, handball, basketball and kabaddi tournament from the cards scorers log. It works out who must sit out the team's next match and warns you before you pick that player. Football has its own guide: [Football discipline table and suspensions](/guides/football-discipline-and-suspensions/).

## Before you start
- You need a tournament with one of these sports. The table builds itself from finished matches.
- Only hosts of the tournament can change the suspension rule.

## What each sport counts
- **Hockey:** green (🟩), yellow (🟨) and red (🟥) cards. Green and yellow suspensions are served inside the match. By default a red card is 1 match out (FIH events suspend a red-carded player for at least the next match).
- **Handball:** warnings (🟨), 2-minute suspensions (2′), disqualifications (🟥) and blue cards (🟦, a disqualification with a written report). A third 2-minute suspension counts as a disqualification. By default a red card without a report ends with the match (IHF Rule 16). A blue card is 1 match out until your disciplinary committee decides.
- **Basketball:** unsportsmanlike (U), technical (T) and disqualifying (D) fouls, and ejections (🟥). By default a D foul, or an ejection you logged yourself (fighting, for example), is 1 game out. A disqualification for 2 U, 2 T or T + U costs nothing more. Fouling out with 5 personal fouls is never a suspension.
- **Kabaddi:** green (🟩), yellow (🟨) and red (🟥) cards. By default a red card is 1 match out.

> **Important:** These defaults are a starting point. Your competition's regulations have the final say, so check them and change the rule if they differ.

## Step by step: set your suspension rule
1. Open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → your sport's **format & points** row (for example **Hockey — format & points**).
2. Scroll to **Discipline & suspensions**. The line under the heading says the rule in plain words.
3. For each sending-off question (for example **A red card means**), pick **No ban**, **1 match out**, **2 matches out** or **3 matches out**. Handball asks about red and blue cards separately. Basketball asks about D fouls and ejections, and about 2 U / 2 T / T + U disqualifications.
4. Hockey and kabaddi can also add up yellow cards, and basketball unsportsmanlike fouls. Pick **Off**, **2**, **3**, **4** or **5**. Off is the default.
5. If they add up, choose when to wipe the count: **Never**, **When the knockouts start**, or **After the quarter-finals**.
6. Tap **Save**.

## How the rule works
- One match can only bring one ban: the longest of its sending-offs.
- When a player reaches the card total, he is out for 1 match and the count starts again.
- A ban is served in the team's next finished match or matches in this tournament, whether or not the player was picked. A walkover counts as a match served.
- Cards shown to coaches and team officials are listed apart. They never suspend a player.

## Step by step: read the discipline table
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** tab. In a multi-sport event, pick your sport first.
2. Scroll below the leaders to **🟥 Discipline**.
3. Each carded player has a row with his team and one count per card type. The column headings are the icons listed above.
4. A red line, **Suspended for next match**, shows who sits out and why.
5. Under the table you see the cards per team and any team officials' cards. Tap a player's name to open his profile.

## Step by step: pick the squad for the next match
1. Open the upcoming match → **Info** → **Set matchday squad**.
2. A suspended player is marked **🟥 Suspended for this match** with the reason.
3. If you pick him, the app asks first. Tap **No, leave out**, or **Yes, pick anyway** if the ban was lifted.
4. **Fill starters** and **Copy last match's squad** skip suspended players.

> **Tip:** The warning never blocks you. The app only knows the cards scorers logged.

## Common questions
### A card was logged against the wrong player. How do I fix it?
Correct the match's timeline. The table and the suspension are worked out again. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Why is there no table for volleyball or cricket?
Volleyball sanctions and cricket code-of-conduct offences aren't logged per player, so there is nothing to count yet.

### Does a suspension carry over to another tournament?
No. The table and bans are per tournament.
