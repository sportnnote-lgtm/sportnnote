Carrom is scored one board at a time. The player who pockets all their coins wins the board and scores one point for each of the opponent's coins still on it, plus 3 for the Queen if they covered it. You tap three things after each board, and the app adds up the games and the match to ICF rules.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** when scheduling: **ICF (to 25 · 8 boards · best of 3)**, **Single game to 25**, **Casual (to 29 · best of 3)** or **Custom**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**, and add the players.
- Tap **⚙ Customize this format** for **Points to win a game**, **Boards per game** and **Match length**.

## The rules the app follows
- The board winner scores 1 point per opponent's coin left, from 0 to 9.
- A Queen covered by the board winner adds 3, but only while their game score is 21 or less. From 22 it adds nothing. A Queen covered by the loser scores nobody anything.
- A game is won at 25. If the last board takes a player past 25, the game is still written as 25, for example 25-18, not 28-18.
- After 8 boards the leader wins the game. If the scores are level, extra boards are played until someone leads.
- A match is best of 3 games, or a single game.

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab. The line at the top shows the game, the board and the target, for example "Game 1 · board 3 of 8 · to 25".
2. Under **Board won by**, tap the winner.
3. Under **Opponent's coins left**, tap the number of the loser's coins still on the board. Nothing is picked for you: **✓ Record board** stays off until you tap a number, even 0.
4. If the winner covered the Queen, tap **👑 Winner covered the Queen**. The chip says whether it is worth +3 or no points at 22 and above.
5. If the board was finished in the first turn, tap **⚪ White slam · broke** (the player who broke finished it) or **⚫ Black slam · didn't break** (the other player finished it in their first turn). This is optional and doesn't change the score.
6. Tap **✓ Record board**. The button shows what the board adds to the game. If this board wins a game or the match, it says so, for example "✓ Record board · +4 · wins Game 2", so check it before you tap.

In doubles the board is won by the pair, so both partners get the board, its points and the Queen. If the team list has more players than the side, a **Played by** row appears: tap the players who played. Your choice is kept for the next boards.

## Reading the board
The scoreboard shows GAMES won and a column of points for each game, with the game in play highlighted. Under it, **Games** shows each finished game, and **Boards** lists every board, newest first, with the coins, the Queen and any slam. When the match ends, the result reads like "2–1 · 25-18, 12-25, 25-20".

The **Box score** compares the two sides on points, boards and Queens, per match or per game.

## Fix a mistake
- Tapped the wrong winner or coins just now? Tap **↶ Undo**. Tap it again to go back further.
- After the match, use [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/). The app replays every board, so the games, the result and each player's figures follow.

## Ending the match
The match ends by itself when a player wins enough games. If a player can't go on, tap **🏁 End match…** and choose **Conceded** or **Awarded**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
When the match ends, every player gets a line, including the loser and both doubles partners. The carrom page adds the match to **Apps**, **W-L** and **Win %**.

- **Match play** shows **Games W-L**, **Boards won %** (boards won out of boards played) and **25-0 games**.
- **Scoring** shows **Points**, **Points per match**, **Points per board won**, **Boards won**, **Queens**, **White slams**, **Black slams** and the **Best match**.

Points are counted the way the game is written, so a player's points add up to their game scores. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

> **Note:** Matches scored before this change may show a little more in Points, because a game-winning board used to count in full. Boards won % and points per board only use matches scored with the new lines.

## Common questions
### Why did the Queen add nothing?
The winner was already on 22 or more. Under ICF rules the Queen only counts up to 21.

### The last board was worth 9 but the game shows 25. Is that right?
Yes. A game is written at 25 even when the last board goes past it, and the players' points follow the game score.
