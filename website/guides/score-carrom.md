Carrom is scored one board at a time. The player who pockets all their coins wins the board and scores one point for each of the opponent's coins still on it, plus 3 for the Queen if they covered it. The app adds up the games and the match to ICF rules.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** when scheduling: **ICF (to 25 · 8 boards · best of 3)**, **Single game to 25**, **Casual (to 29 · best of 3)** or **Custom**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**, and add the players.

## The rules the app follows
- The board winner scores 1 point per opponent's coin left, from 0 to 9.
- A Queen covered by the board winner adds 3, but only while their game score is 21 or less. A Queen covered by the loser scores nobody anything.
- A game is won at 25. If the last board takes a player past 25, the game is still written as 25, for example 25-18, not 28-18.
- After 8 boards the leader wins the game. If level, extra boards are played.
- A match is best of 3 games, or a single game.

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab. The top line shows the game, the board and the target, for example "Game 1 · board 3 of 8 · to 25".
2. Before board 1, the app asks "Who breaks first? (the toss · then it alternates)". Tap the player who won the toss. After that a line shows who breaks next, for example "⚪ Aarav to break".
3. Under **Board won by**, tap the winner.
4. Under **Opponent's coins left**, tap the number of the loser's coins still on the board. **✓ Record board** stays off until you tap a number, even 0.
5. Optionally, under **👑 Queen covered by**, tap **Winner** (+3, or no points at 22+), **Loser (no points)** or **Not covered**.
6. If the board was finished in the first turn, tap the slam chip. Once the toss is recorded there is one chip, and it already says **White slam** (the winner broke) or **Black slam** (the winner didn't break). Without a toss you pick **⚪ White slam · broke** or **⚫ Black slam · didn't break**. A slam doesn't change the score.
7. Tap **✓ Record board**. The button shows what the board adds. If it wins a game or the match, it says so, for example "✓ Record board · +4 · wins Game 2", so check it before you tap.

The break alternates board by board, and each new game opens with the other side breaking. In doubles the board is won by the pair, so both partners get its points. If the team list has more players than the side, a **Played by** row appears: tap the players who played.

## If the toss was wrong or missed
Tap **Fix the toss** next to the break line and pick who really broke first. Only who broke changes; the score stays. If no toss was recorded, tap **Record who broke the first board** instead.

## Reading the board
The scoreboard shows games won and the points in each game. **Boards** lists every board, newest first, with the coins, the Queen, any slam and who broke, for example "· Aarav broke". A Queen covered by the loser shows as "👑 covered by the loser". The final result reads like "2–1 · 25-18, 12-25, 25-20". The **Box score** compares points, boards and Queens.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? Tap **↶ Undo**. Tap it again to go back further.
- An older board was wrong? Under the controls, find **✎ Correct a board** and tap **Edit**. Every board is listed, newest first. Tap ✎ to change the winner, the coins left or the Queen, then **Save…** and confirm. Tap ✕ to remove a board; the app asks first. The boards after it are replayed, so game scores, games won and player points re-adjust.
- After the match, use [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends by itself when a player wins enough games. If a player can't go on, tap **🏁 End match…** and choose **Conceded** or **Awarded**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
When the match ends, every player gets a line, including the loser and both doubles partners. The carrom page adds **Apps**, **W-L** and **Win %**.

- **Match play** shows **Games W-L**, **Boards won %**, **25-0 games**, **Boards broken** and **Own-break win %** (boards won on your own break; only matches where the toss was recorded count).
- **Scoring** shows **Points**, **Points per match**, **Points per board won**, **Boards won**, **Queens**, **Queens covered, board lost**, **White slams**, **Black slams** and the **Best match**.

Points add up to the game scores. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Why did the Queen add nothing?
The winner was already on 22 or more. Under ICF rules the Queen only counts up to 21.
