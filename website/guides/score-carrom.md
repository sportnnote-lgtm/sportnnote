Carrom is scored one board at a time. The board winner scores one point for each of the opponent's coins still on it, plus 3 for a covered Queen. The app adds up the games and the match to ICF rules.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** (**ICF (to 25 · 8 boards · best of 3)**, **Single game to 25**, **Casual (to 29 · best of 3)** or **Custom**) and **Singles** or **Doubles**.

## The rules the app follows
- A Queen covered by the board winner adds 3, but only while their game score is 21 or less. A Queen covered by the loser scores nobody anything.
- A game is won at 25. If the last board takes a player past 25, the game is still written as 25, for example 25-18, not 28-18.
- After 8 boards the leader wins the game. If level, extra boards are played.
- A penalty board gives 3 points to the opponent of the player penalised. It is a board played, but not a board won, a Queen or a slam.
- A match is best of 3 games, or a single game.

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab. The top line reads like "Game 1 · board 3 of 8 · to 25".
2. Before board 1, the app asks "Who breaks first? (the toss · then it alternates)". Tap the player who won the toss. After that a line shows who breaks next, for example "⚪ Aarav to break".
3. Under **Board won by**, tap the winner.
4. Under **Opponent's coins left**, tap how many of the loser's coins are still on the board, even 0.
5. Optionally, under **👑 Queen covered by**, tap **Winner** (+3, or no points at 22+), **Loser (no points)** or **Not covered**.
6. If the board was finished in the first turn, tap the slam chip. With the toss recorded it already says **White slam** (the winner broke) or **Black slam**; without it you pick one. A slam doesn't change the score.
7. Tap **✓ Record board**. The button shows what the board adds. If it wins a game or the match, it says so, for example "✓ Record board · +4 · wins Game 2", so check it before you tap.
8. For a penalty board, tap the player who gets the points under **Board won by**, then **⚠️ Penalty board · +3 to …** and **✓ Record penalty board**. No coins, Queen or slam are asked.

The break alternates board by board (penalty boards too), and each new game opens with the other side breaking. In doubles both partners get the board's points. If the team list has more players than the side, a **Played by** row appears: tap who played.

## If the toss was wrong or missed
Tap **Fix the toss** (or **Record who broke the first board**) and pick who really broke first. The score stays.

## Reading the score sheet
Below the scoreboard, the **Score sheet** follows the ICF referee's sheet: one table per game with the result on top ("Game 1 · 25-18 · Aarav won"), then a row per board: **#**, **Break**, **Won by**, **Coins**, **Queen**, **Pts** and the running total. **PEN** marks a penalty board, ⚪ / ⚫ a White or Black slam, and * a tie-break board. In the Queen column, 👑 +3 counted, 👑 0 means covered at 22 or more, and 👑 L covered by the loser. The **Box score** compares points, boards and Queens.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? Tap **↶ Undo**. Tap it again to go back further.
- An older board was wrong? Under the controls, find **✎ Correct a board** and tap **Edit**. Tap ✎ to change the winner, **Played out** or **⚠️ Penalty**, the coins left or the Queen, then **Save…** and confirm. Tap ✕ to remove a board; the app asks first. The boards after it are replayed, so game scores and player points re-adjust.
- After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends by itself when a player wins enough games. If a player can't go on, see [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
When the match ends, every player gets a line, including the loser and both doubles partners.

- **Match play** shows **Games W-L**, **Boards won %**, **25-0 games**, **Boards broken** and **Own-break win %** (only matches with a recorded toss).
- **Scoring** shows **Points**, **Points per match**, **Points per board won**, **Boards won**, **Queens**, **Queens covered, board lost**, **White slams**, **Black slams** and the **Best match**.

Points add up to the game scores (penalty points included). See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Why did the Queen add nothing?
The winner was already on 22 or more. Under ICF rules the Queen only counts up to 21.
