Not every kabaddi point comes from a touch or a tackle. A defender steps over the line, a raider breaks his cant, a coach shouts from the side, or the referee shows a card. This guide shows how to record each one so the score, the players on the mat and all outs stay right.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [How to score a kabaddi match](/guides/score-kabaddi/) for raids, tackles and do-or-die.
- The match must be started (**▶ Start match**). Technical points and cards are not offered during a Golden Raid or a shootout.

## Step by step: a line-out during a raid
A player who goes out of bounds without a struggle is out, and the other team gets a point. Record it on the raid itself.
1. Tap the raiding team's button and fill in the raid as usual.
2. If defenders stepped out, tap **1 stepped out** or **2 stepped out** under "Line-out". Each one is out and gives the raiding team 1 point. The raider is not credited with these points.
3. If the raider stepped out, tap the chip so it reads **Raider stepped out: Yes**. He is out, the defending team gets 1 point, and there is no tackle. His touches don't count.
4. Check the "Scores:" line and tap **✓ Record raid**.

A defender line-out also saves a do-or-die raid, because the raiding team scored.

## Step by step: a technical point
1. On the **Scoring** tab, tap **⚖️ Technical point**.
2. Under "Point to", tap the team that gets the point.
3. Under "Why?", pick the reason: **Cant late / not kept**, **Coaching from outside**, **Delay of game**, **Two raiders entered**, **Entered / left the court without leave** or **Other**.
4. If you know who was at fault, tap the player (optional).
5. Tap **✓ +1 to** the team. Nobody goes out.

## Step by step: a card
1. Tap **🟨 Card**, then the team.
2. Pick the card: **🟩 Green — warning**, **🟨 Yellow — 2 min off** or **🟥 Red — sent off**.
3. Tap the player, or **Coach / team official**. A card for an official changes nothing on the mat.
4. If the referee also gave the other team a technical point, set the chip to **Technical point to … : Yes**. It is on by default for a red card.
5. Tap the record button, for example **✓ Yellow card — Ravi**. For a red card the app asks first; tap **Yes, red card**.

## What each card does
- Green: a warning only. Two greens for the same player? The panel reminds you the next card is yellow.
- Yellow: the player is off the mat for 2 minutes of playing time and his team plays a player short. The line under the mat count shows "Suspended: Ravi (Red House) (1:24 left)". The 2 minutes stop when you pause the clock, and he comes back by himself.
- A yellow for a player who is already out starts when he would be revived. No one else is revived in his place.
- Red: off for the rest of the match, with no substitute. His team plays a player short until the end.

While a player is suspended or sent off, the raid form shows him as "· suspended" or "· sent off" and he can't be picked to raid, tackle or come off in a substitution.

## How the rules change on the mat
- "On mat" counts only players who can play: "6/7 (1 suspended)".
- The bonus line needs 6 defenders on the mat, and a super tackle needs 3 or fewer, so a suspension can change both.
- An all out happens when the mat is empty. A suspended or sent-off player does not stop it. After the all out, everyone comes back except players still suspended or sent off.

## Fix a mistake
Under "Correct the timeline", tap **Edit**, then **✕** on the card or technical point. A card's technical point goes with it. To change a line-out, tap **✎ Edit** on the raid.

## Common questions

### Where do cards show up?
On the timeline straight away. Each player's cards and technical points conceded are added to their profile when the match ends, under Discipline.

### My older matches show no cards. Why?
Cards and technical points are counted from matches scored with this version. Older matches show "not tracked", not zero.
