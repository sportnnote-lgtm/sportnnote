A canoe sprint regatta is a list of races, such as the K1 500 m Women or the C2 1000 m Men. Each race is a straight final, or heats, semi-finals and Finals A and B on a 9-lane course, as the ICF runs them. You type finish times on a phone. The app sends each boat to its next race, ranks the finals and credits every paddler. Slalom is not covered.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add races and enter times.
- Create the tournament with **Canoe sprint** as a sport. Under **Canoe sprint basics**, check the **Lanes** (9 by default), the position points and whether you use **Hand timing**.
- Add your houses or schools as teams. Boats are picked from their members and score for them.

## Step by step: add a race
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Canoe sprint — events**, and tap **＋ Add a race**.
2. Pick the boat: **K1**, **K2**, **K4** (kayak) or **C1**, **C2**, **C4** (canoe). Then pick the distance: 200, 500, 1000 m, or 5000 m for the long-distance race.
3. Pick the age group and **Men**, **Women** or **Mixed**.
4. A single (K1, C1): tap a house or search a name, then tap each paddler.
5. A crew boat (K2, K4, C2, C4): under **＋ Add a crew from**, tap a house. Then tap **Seat 1** (the front), **Seat 2** and so on, and pick a paddler for each. **Fill empty seats** fills the rest from that house.
6. **Lanes & progression:** pick a progression. The line below shows where every place goes.
7. Tap **Create race & draw lanes**. Heats and lanes are drawn.

## The progressions
- Up to 9 boats: a **Straight final**.
- **Heats → Finals A/B:** the top places of each heat go to Final A and the next ones to Final B. With 12 boats, places 1 to 4 of each heat go to Final A and 5 to 6 to Final B.
- **Top 3 to Final A → semi:** the first three of each heat go straight to Final A. The rest race a semi-final, which fills Final A, and the next ones go to Final B.
- **Heats → semis → Finals A/B:** for bigger entries. Heat places fill the semi-finals, and the semis fill Final A and Final B.
- **Custom rule:** choose the heats, how many go straight to Final A, and whether there is a Final B.
- From the second round on, the best-ranked boats get the centre lanes: lane 5, then 6, 4, 7, 3, 8, 2, 9, 1. Boats are ranked by heat place first, then by time.

## Step by step: enter times
1. Open the race and tap **Enter results**. Use the **Heat 1**, **Final A** or **Final B** chips to switch races.
2. Type each time with the number keys: 13802 is 1:38.02. Tap **Next ›** to save and move on.
3. Photo finish: tap **.000** on a lane, then type thousandths (135124 is 1:35.124). Two boats on 1:35.13 are separated by their thousandths, and the sheet shows "photo 1:35.124".
4. A time far outside the usual range for the boat and distance asks **Check this mark** before saving.
5. Tap **DNS**, **DNF** or **DQ**, and type the reason in **Rule** (for example a false start, or leaving the lane).
6. Each card shows where the boat goes: **→ Final A**, **→ Final B** or **out**. A dead heat across a qualifying line sends both boats on. Enter a **Re-row / draw place** to separate them.
7. Turn on **Intermediate times** when you create the race to record the 250 m times (100 m over 200 m).
8. Tap **Close heats → seed the final** (or the semi-finals), then enter the finals and tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. An organiser can reopen a round until the next one has a time, or reopen the final. Reopening the final rolls back its records and points.

## What everyone sees
- **Finals A/B:** Final A decides the medals and places run on into Final B. After a 9-boat Final A, Final B's winner is 10th.
- **House table, Fastest by event and Meet records** per boat, distance and category.
- **Paddler profiles:** every paddler in the boat is credited with the race, time, place and medal. Profiles show races, A finals, medals and the best time per boat and distance.

## Common questions
### Do K1 and C1 records mix?
No. Each boat and distance keeps its own records and bests. K1 500 m and C1 500 m are separate events.

### Is slalom supported?
Not yet. Only sprint racing in lanes is covered.
