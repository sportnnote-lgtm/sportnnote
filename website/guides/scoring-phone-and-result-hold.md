Scoring means looking at the game, not the phone. SportnNote keeps the screen on while you score, gives a short buzz when a tap goes in, and holds the final result for about a minute so a wrong last tap doesn't reach every follower.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match (or a host who is scoring it).
- In the phone app, the screen-on and buzz features need the latest app version. On an older version scoring works exactly as before, without them.

## The screen stays on
While you have a match open that you are scoring, and it has started and isn't finished, your phone won't lock. The same applies on the **Enter results** tab of an athletics or swimming event that is open, and on the **📝 Scorecard** of a golf round that is live.

When the match ends, or you leave the screen, the phone goes back to locking normally.

> **Note:** In a web browser this works where the browser supports it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari). If your browser doesn't, set a longer screen timeout on your phone while you score.

## A short buzz on each tap
Every scoring tap gives a light buzz, so you can feel that it went in without looking down. **↶ Undo** gives a different, double buzz. Golf strokes and athletics marks buzz too.

To turn it off on your phone: **Profile** → **Settings** → **Buzz on scoring taps**. Tap it to switch between **On** and **Off**. It only changes your phone.

> **Note:** iPhone browsers can't buzz, so on an iPhone the web app scores silently. The Android app and Android browsers buzz.

## The 60-second result hold
The tap that finishes a match (match point, the final whistle, the last wicket or a chess result) shows the final score on your screen at once. The result itself waits about 60 seconds before it is saved as final. That's when followers get the "Full time" alert and the points table, bracket and players' win records update.

## Step by step
1. Make the tap that ends the match. The board shows the final score.
2. A note appears above **↶ Undo**: "Result sent to followers in 0:58 · Undo · Send now". It counts down.
3. If that last tap was wrong, tap **Undo** in the note (or the normal **↶ Undo**). The match goes back to live, nothing is sent, and you carry on scoring.
4. If the result is right, do nothing. When the countdown ends, it is sent. Tap **Send now** if you don't want to wait.

## Common questions

### What if I close the app or lock the phone during the countdown?
The result is sent straight away. Leaving the match screen does the same. If the app is closed before it can send (the battery dies, say), it is sent the next time you open SportnNote. A result is never lost; at worst it arrives late.

### Can I undo after the result has gone out?
Yes, **↶ Undo** still works, but followers already have the "Full time" alert and won't get a second one. To change a finished result properly, see [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Do viewers see the final score during the hold?
Yes. The score on their screens updates as usual. Only the result alert and the table update wait.

### Does ending a match with End match also wait?
No. **🏁 End match…** and a walkover already ask "Are you sure?" first, so they are saved at once. See [End a match early or record a walkover](/guides/end-a-match-early/).
