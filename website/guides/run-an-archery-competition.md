An archery competition is a list of events, such as Recurve 70 m Senior Men. Each event is a ranking round of ends, then, if you choose, a seeded match-play bracket with a bronze medal match. You enter the arrows on a phone; the app applies the World Archery rules and updates medals, house points, records and profiles.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter scores.
- Create the tournament with **Archery** as a sport. Under **Archery basics**, pick the **Position points**.
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Archers score for them.

## Step by step: add an event
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Archery — events**, and tap **＋ Add an archery event**.
2. Pick the bow: **Recurve**, **Compound** or **Barebow**. Then pick **Outdoor (72 arrows)** or **Indoor 18 m (60 arrows)**.
3. Pick the age group (**Senior**, **U21**, **U18**, **U15** or **School**) and **Men** or **Women** (Boys or Girls for younger groups).
4. **Round:** the usual distance is chosen for you (70 m senior recurve, 60 m U18, 50 m compound). **(WA)** marks World Archery rounds; shorter school distances such as 30 m keep their own records and PBs.
5. **Archers:** tap a house or search any player, then tap each archer.
6. **Match play:** choose **Top 4**, **Top 8** and so on, or **No match play**. If you pick **All** with an odd number, the top seeds get byes.
7. Tap **Create event & start list**. Target order is drawn.

## Step by step: enter the ranking round
1. Open the event. The **End 1 of 12** card lists every archer in target order. The first archer is already selected.
2. Tap the arrows on the keypad: **X**, **10** to **1**, or **M** for a miss. When the end is full, it saves by itself and the next archer comes up. **Undo** takes back the last save for 5 seconds.
3. Or type the end, for example `X 10 9 9 8 7`. Add `= 53` to check the scorecard total; if the arrows add up to something else, nothing is saved.
4. Tap **E1**, **E2** and so on to go back to an end, then tap an archer to correct it.
5. With match play, tap **Close ranking round → seed match play**. Without it, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

> **Important:** An end is exactly 6 arrows outdoors and 3 indoors, so it can never score more than 60 or 30. The app refuses anything else.

## How the ranking round is decided
- The higher score wins. If scores are level, more 10s (X included) ranks higher, then more X.
- Still level for the last match-play place: a shoot-off. Fill in each archer's **Shoot-off place**; the round can't close until you do.
- Other equal ranks: a coin toss for the seed. Enter it in **Coin-toss place (seed)**, or target order decides.
- Without match play, a tie for a medal asks for a **Shoot-off place**.

## Step by step: run match play
1. The bracket is seeded from the ranking round: 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7 and 3 v 6 for eight archers.
2. The next match opens below the bracket; tap any match to open it.
3. For each end, tap three arrows for the first archer, then three for the second, and tap **Save end**.
4. **Recurve and barebow:** the higher end earns 2 set points, and a tie gives 1 each. The first archer to 6 wins.
5. **Compound:** five ends, and the higher total wins.
6. At 5–5 (or level compound totals) the card asks for one shoot-off arrow each. The higher arrow wins; if both score the same, tap the archer closer to the centre, or **Can't separate — shoot again**.
7. If an archer doesn't turn up, tap **… absent (w/o)** before any end is shot.
8. The semi-final losers shoot the **Bronze medal match**. When the gold medal match is done, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

> **Tip:** To correct a match, tap the end you want to change. If the correction changes who went through, the app asks first. Later scores that no longer fit the bracket are then cleared.

## What everyone sees
- **Places:** gold, silver, bronze and 4th come from the medal matches. Archers who lose in the same earlier round share a place (all quarter-final losers are 5th), and they share its points.
- **House table**, **Best archers (points)** and **Meet records** (complete ranking rounds only) on the Archery page.
- **Archer profiles:** matches won and lost, set points, average arrow, 10 + X rate, medals, and personal and season bests per round.

## Common questions
### Can we run team or mixed team events?
Not yet. Only individual events work for now.

### We locked the results and found a mistake.
An organiser can tap **↺ Reopen match play**. Its records and points are rolled back until you lock it again. The ranking round can be reopened only while match play has no scores.
