You score a hockey match on one phone. You start and stop the game clock, tap what happens, and the app keeps the score per quarter, times every suspension and builds the box score and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: **FIH 4 × 15**, **School 2 × 25**, **Junior 4 × 10**, **Indoor 6-a-side**, **Hockey5s** (no penalty corners) or **Custom**, and what happens **If level at full time**: **Draw stands** or **Shoot-out**.
- Set each team's matchday squad on the **Info** tab and place the players on the pitch, with your goalkeeper in the GK spot. Starters give everyone their minutes played, and the keeper gets the saves. No line-up? Pick the keeper later with **🧤 Set keeper**.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Push back**. The clock counts down the quarter.
2. Tap **⏸ Stop** when the umpires stop time and **▶ Resume** when play restarts.
3. With the FIH format, the clock stops by itself after a goal and when you award a penalty corner. A bar under the clock says "Clock stopped (goal)" until you tap **▶ Resume**.
4. When the hooter goes, tap **⏭ End Q1**, then **▶ Start Q2** after the break. **🏁 Full time**, at the bottom of the controls, stays off until the last period has started, so a slip at half-time can't end the match.
5. Clock wrong, or you joined late? Tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the time left (for example `8:30`) and tap **Set**.

> **Tip:** Minutes follow FIH match reports: a goal at 14:20 of the first quarter is 15', and the second quarter runs 16' to 30'.

## Step by step: log the play
1. **🏑 Goal**: choose **Field goal**, **From PC** or **Stroke**, then tap the scorer, then the assist or **No assist**. If you don't know who scored, tap **Team goal (no scorer)**.
2. **🚩 PC**: a penalty corner. A panel asks how it ended: **🏑 Goal**, **🧤 Saved** or **↗ Wide** (then the shooter), **🛡 Defended**, **⚪ Stroke awarded** (then the stroke) or **🔁 Re-awarded** (a new corner). **Later** closes it. The outcome shows on the timeline and PC conversion is worked out for you.
3. **🎯 Shot**: tap **On goal — saved** or **Off target / blocked**, then the shooter (or **Skip player**). A save goes to the other team's keeper.
4. **⚪ Stroke**: tap **Scored** (then the scorer), **Saved** or **Missed**.
5. **🔁 Sub**: tap who comes off, then who comes on. Subs are rolling. If the keeper comes off, the player coming on takes over in goal.
6. **Circle entries**: tap the team's button each time it carries the ball into the shooting circle; **−1** takes one back. Matches that never logged one show circle entries as "not tracked".

## Cards and suspensions
1. Tap **🟨 Card**, then **🟩 Green 2′**, **🟨 Yellow 5′**, **🟨 Yellow 10′** or **🟥 Red**, then the player.
2. Green and yellow cards are timed: "⏱️ Suspended: Name (1:24 left)" shows under the clock. The countdown pauses when the clock stops, and the player is back by himself.
3. A red card is permanent. The team plays a player short for the rest of the match.

A suspended or sent-off player is left out of the player lists until he is allowed back.

## Shoot-out
If the match is level at full time and the decider is a shoot-out, you see "Level — the match goes to a shoot-out".
1. Tap which team shoots first.
2. For each attempt, tap the taker (optional), then **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed / saved**. "Next:" tells you whose turn it is.
3. Still level after five each? Sudden death starts, with the other team shooting first, until one team leads after equal attempts.

The result keeps the level score and adds the shoot-out, for example "2–2" and "SO 4–3". Shoot-out goals aren't counted as goals in player stats.

## Fix a mistake
1. Tap **✎ Correct the timeline** under the buttons.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to enter it again. It keeps its time; Cancel leaves it as it was.
3. After the match, use [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## What you get
- **Score** tab: the timeline, team stats (circle entries, shots, penalty corners, PC conversion, strokes, saves, cards) and each player's line.
- Player profiles: goals per game, field, PC and stroke goals, keeper save %, clean sheets and minutes suspended.
- Tournament tables use **FIH 3-1-0**, or **FIH + shoot-out bonus** (a draw goes to a shoot-out: winner 2 points, loser 1). See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Common questions

### Does the app enforce a second green card?
No. If the umpire shows a yellow instead, record a yellow.

### Can I score without running the clock?
Mostly. Every moment is then stamped at the start of the quarter and suspensions can't be timed. Tap **▶ Push back** (or **▶ Start**) once per period anyway: **⏭ End** and **🏁 Full time** only unlock once the period has started.
