Your scores, stats and profile belong to you. **Your data & privacy** in Settings puts everything in one place: a copy of what we keep, your analytics choice, and, if an organiser added you before you joined, who did it and when.

## Before you start
- You need to be signed in. Open [the app](https://app.sportnnote.in) and go to **Settings**.
- It works the same on Android, iPhone and the web.

## Step by step
1. Open **Settings** and tap **Your data & privacy** (under "Privacy & legal").
2. Tap **⬇️ Download my data**. On the web a file downloads straight away. On a phone, a share sheet opens so you can save the file or send it to yourself.
3. Under **Analytics**, tap **Allow analytics** or **Essential only**. The highlighted button is your current choice.
4. To fix a detail you spot in the file, use **Edit profile**. To remove everything, use **Delete my account**, which is at the bottom of the same page.

## What the file contains
The file is in JSON, a plain-text format that other apps and services can read. It contains:
- your account: name, date of birth, mobile, country and role;
- your player profile, including your guardian's details if you're under 18;
- your match stats, one line per match;
- the messages you sent;
- who and what you follow;
- your team, club and school memberships;
- your consent choices, with dates: the terms you agreed to, your analytics choice and, for under-18s, the parent or guardian confirmation.

Below the button, a short summary shows how many of each there are, for example "Stat lines (matches): 42".

## Analytics, and why some people are asked first
Analytics means counting which screens are opened, so we can see what works and what confuses people. If you allow it, the app keeps a random ID on your device. We never record what you type, never sell data and never show ads.

- **UK and Europe:** on your first visit, a box asks you to choose. Nothing is counted and no ID is stored until you tap one of the buttons.
- **Everywhere else, including India:** analytics is on, and you can switch it off here at any time.
- **Under 18:** your activity is never linked to you, whatever you choose.

Crash reports still arrive when you choose **Essential only**, without any device ID, so we can fix bugs.

## If an organiser added you
Coaches and organisers often add a whole squad before the players join, using a name and sometimes a mobile number. If that happened to you, the page shows a card such as "Coach Rahul Verma added your player details on 3 Oct 2026, before you joined."

That record is now yours. You can download it, correct it, or delete it. The invite you received also had a **Not you?** link, which removes the record without needing an account.

## Common questions
### How often can I download my data?
Up to 10 times an hour.

### Can I get my child's data?
Yes. Link your own account as their parent or guardian, then use **Manage**. See [Parent or guardian consent](/guides/parent-guardian-consent/).

### Does deleting my account delete my match results?
Your name and details are erased. Scores of matches you played stay in other people's histories as "Deleted player", so league tables don't change.

### Where is my data stored?
On servers in Mumbai, India, for every country. The Privacy Policy in Settings explains how it's protected and lists the services we use.
