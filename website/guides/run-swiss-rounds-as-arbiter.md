In a big open, the arbiter's day is the same loop each round: post the pairings, collect every board's result, deal with players who leave, ask for a bye or turn up late, then pair the next round. SportnNote puts each step on one screen.

## Before you start
- You must be a host of the tournament.
- The chess event uses **🇨🇭 Swiss** (see [Run a chess tournament](/guides/run-a-chess-tournament/)).

## Step by step: post the pairings
1. Open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → **Auto-generate fixtures** → **⚡ Generate preview**. Boards are numbered: **Bd 1** is the top board.
2. Tap **✅ Create**. The app opens the **Round** screen.
3. Tap **📤 Share pairings** to send them on WhatsApp, or **🖨 Print pairings** for an A4 sheet for the notice board (on a phone without printing, it shares the text instead).

Each line names White first, with each player's score before the round in brackets. Byes and players not paired are listed underneath.

## Step by step: enter the round's results
1. On the **Round** screen, tap the result on each board: **1-0**, **½-½** or **0-1**.
2. For a no-show, tap **More: forfeits, 0-0**: **+/−** (White wins by forfeit), **−/+** (Black wins by forfeit) or **0-0** (neither came).
3. Tap **✓ Save … results…**. The confirm sheet lists every board. Check them, then tap **Yes, save**.

Each game closes exactly as if it had been recorded on the game itself: the table, the wall chart and both players' profiles update. A board that already has a result, or that someone is scoring on another phone, is skipped and named. To fix a saved result, tap **Open game ›** and use **Fix a mistake** there.

To reopen an earlier round, tap **📋 Round N — pairings & results sheet** on the **Auto-generate fixtures** screen, then pick **R1**, **R2** and so on. Once results are in, **📤 Share results** and **🖨 Print results** appear too.

## Step by step: who plays the next round
When a round is finished, **🧾 Who plays round N** appears on **Auto-generate fixtures**. Tap a player's name to change their status:
- **Absent (0)**: not paired this round, 0 points.
- **½ bye**: a requested half-point bye. The counter shows how many they have used.
- **0 bye**: a requested zero-point bye.
- **🚪 Withdraw…**: not paired from this round on. It asks first. Their results so far stay, and each round they miss scores 0. **↩ Bring back** undoes it.

Under **＋ Late entry**, pick what each missed round is worth (**0**, or **½ each**), then tap the player. They are paired from this round.

Under **Half-point byes a player may request**, set your limit (2 by default). A request over the limit is refused, but you can still give a 0-point bye.

Then tap **⚡ Generate preview**. The preview names everyone not paired, for example "½-point bye: Rohan Pillai · Withdrawn: Tara Bose".

## How these count
- A requested bye counts as soon as its round is drawn. It is not a game played, and it never uses up the player's pairing-allocated bye.
- In tie-breaks, FIDE treats absences, requested byes and late-entry rounds as voluntary unplayed rounds: Buchholz Cut-1 removes one of them first.
- A player who withdraws counts as a draw in their past opponents' Buchholz for the rounds they miss.
- On the wall chart, **bye½** or **bye0** is a requested bye (or a late entrant's missed round), and **–** is a round not played.

## Team events
If each fixture is a team match, the team sitting out a round gets the bye's match points. By default that is the points for a match won; change it under **Swiss bye** in **Chess — format & points**.

## Common questions

### I removed a pairing from the preview by mistake.
Both players would score 0 that round, and the preview warns you about it. Tap **⚡ Generate preview** again to pair them.

### One game was cancelled. Can I still pair the next round?
Yes. Cancelled games no longer hold up the next round.

### Is this FIDE-certified pairing?
No. For a FIDE-rated event, pair in FIDE-endorsed software such as Swiss-Manager.
