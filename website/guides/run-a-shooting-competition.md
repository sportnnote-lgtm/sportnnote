A shooting competition is a list of events, such as 10 m Air Rifle Junior Men. Each event is a match of series, and for 10 m air rifle, 10 m air pistol and 50 m rifle 3 positions the best shooters then shoot a final. You type each series or shot on a phone; the app ranks with the ISSF tie rules, runs the final's eliminations and shoot-offs, and updates medals, house points, records and profiles.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter scores.
- Create the tournament with **Shooting** as a sport. Under **Shooting basics**, pick the **Position points**.
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Shooters score for them.

## Step by step: add an event
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Shooting — events**, and tap **＋ Add a shooting event**.
2. **Event:** pick the event, for example **10 m Air Rifle** or **10 m Air Pistol**.
3. Pick **Senior**, **Junior**, **Youth** or **School**, then **Men** or **Women** (Boys or Girls for youth and school).
4. **Match:** the ISSF length is marked **(ISSF)**. Youth and school start on a shorter match, such as 40 shots, which keeps its own records and PBs.
5. **Shooters:** tap a house or search any player, then tap each shooter. For a mixed team, tap two shooters for each team, in order.
6. **Scoring:** choose **By series** (the range gives you each series total) or **Shot by shot**.
7. **Final:** choose **Final of 8**, **Final of 6**, **Final of 4** or **No final**.
8. Tap **Create event & start list**. Firing points are drawn and become the start list order.

## Step by step: enter the match
1. Open the event. The **Series 1 of 6** card lists every shooter by firing point.
2. Type each shooter's series total, for example `104.6` for air rifle. For integer events like air pistol, also type the inner tens in the **X** box.
3. With **Shot by shot**, type the series' shots in one line instead: `10.4 10.2 9.9 …`, or `10 9 X …` for integer scoring (X is an inner ten). The app adds them up.
4. When everyone has a series, the card moves on. Tap **S1**, **S2** … to correct a series; **Undo** takes back the last save.
5. With a final: tap **Close qualification → seed the final**. The best shooters (marked q) go through, and their firing points are drawn again.
6. Without a final: tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

> **Important:** A series can never be more than its maximum: 109.0 for 10 decimal shots, 100 for 10 integer shots. The app refuses it. A very low series asks **Check this series** before saving.

## Step by step: run the final
1. Finalists start from zero. The **FINAL** card shows the shot to fire, for example **Shot 11 of 24 · next elimination after shot 16**.
2. Type each finalist's shot as it appears on the monitor: `10.5`, or just `105`. Final shots are always decimal, for pistol too.
3. After an elimination shot, the lowest total is out automatically, for example **Out after shot 16: … — 6th**.
4. If the lowest totals are level, the card switches to **Shoot-off**. Each tied shooter fires one shot; the lowest goes out. If they are level again, they fire again. Shoot-off shots are not added to the total.
5. On the last shot (24 for 10 m), the higher total wins gold. A tie for gold is also shot off.
6. To fix a shot, tap the finalist under **Correct a shot**, tap the shot, then type the new value and tap **Set shot**. A shot fired before an elimination asks first, because the places may change.
7. When the card says **Final complete**, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

## How places are decided
- **Match:** the highest total. For integer events, more inner tens rank higher, then the higher last 10-shot series, counting back series by series. Decimal air rifle skips the inner tens. 3 positions compares standing, then kneeling, then prone. With shots entered, the last shots break what is still level.
- **Without a final:** a tie for a medal that nothing breaks asks for a shoot-off place.
- **Final:** the first out is last. With 8 finalists the 10 m final eliminates from shot 12 (later with fewer); in 50 m 3 positions two go out after shot 40.

## What everyone sees
- **House table** and **Best shooters (points)** on the Shooting page and the tournament's **Stats** tab.
- **Meet records** per event and match length. Records are match scores; a final score is never a record.
- **Shooter profiles:** competitions, finals reached, inner-ten rate, medals, personal and season bests per event, and the average per 10 shots.

## Common questions
### Are the 25 m and shotgun finals run here?
Not yet: 25 m, trap and skeet medals come from the match. Mixed teams rank on the team total, without medal matches.

### We locked the results and found a mistake.
An organiser can tap **↺ Reopen final**. Its records go back to the previous holders, and its points leave the house table until you lock it again.
