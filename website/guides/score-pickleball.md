In side-out pickleball only the serving team scores. You tap who won each rally; the app works out the call, such as 4-2-1, and names the server.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- **Format**, side-out: **Tournament (side-out · best of 3 to 11)**, **Tournament (side-out · 1 game to 15)**, **Tournament (side-out · 1 game to 21)**, **Medal match (side-out · best of 5 to 11)**. Rally: **MLP (rally · 1 game to 21)**, **Rec (rally · 11)**, **Rec quick (rally · 11 · win by 1)**. **Custom** sets everything.
- Add the players, or the server can't be named.

## Step by step: start a doubles game
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the team serving first.
3. Under **Who starts on the right?**, tap each team's right-court player at 0-0. Nothing is picked at the start of game 1, and the buttons stay grey until both questions are answered.
4. Check the serving line, e.g. "Serving: Bina Shah (right) · 0-0-2". The first team starts as server 2, and its button shows a **SERVING** tag.
5. After each rally, tap the winning team's big **Rally won** button, in its team colour. That's the only tap.

## How the app follows the serve
- Serving team wins: a point; the server swaps sides and serves again.
- Serving team loses: the partner becomes server 2 and serves from where they stand.
- Server 2 loses: side-out. The other team's right-court player serves.
- Players change sides only when their team scores.

The call is serving score, receiving score, server number, e.g. "Serving: Asha Rao (left) · 2-2-1".

## Singles and rally scoring
- Singles: serve from the right on an even score, left on odd. The call has two numbers, e.g. 3-1.
- Rally scoring: every rally is a point and its winner serves. The buttons read "Point". Singles credits the player automatically; in doubles, crediting is optional: tap **credit a player** or long-press a side, then pick the name (**Cancel** closes it).

## Points for players
In side-out scoring a point goes to its server automatically, so there are no credit chips. "Box score" on the **Score** tab shows each player's points.

## Reading the board
A chip under the board shows **GAME POINT** or **MATCH POINT** with the team's name. In side-out scoring only the serving team can have one; in rally scoring both can show one at once.

## Match stats
The **Score** tab's "Match stats" (per match or game) show side-outs, service turns and points per turn (side-out), points won on serve and receive (rally), streaks, and game or match points saved. Doubles adds "Service points won, by server".

## Point detail (optional)
Off by default. Turn it on with **On** in the "🔎 Point detail (optional)" row under the buttons, or the **Point detail** toggle in "⚙️ Scoring settings".

After each rally, "How was it won? · Asha Rao" appears, even for a side-out rally. Tap a chip or **Skip**; nothing is required.
- **Winner** + **Drive**, **Dink**, **Volley**, **Overhead**, **Erne**, **ATP**, **Lob**.
- **Forced error**, or **Unforced error** + **Net**, **Out**.
- **Service fault** (receiver won).

Errors count against the opponent (doubles: pick who under **By:**).

## Games and the end of the match
- A game ends at the target (11, 15 or 21) with a two-point lead, unless the format is win by 1.
- The game's winner serves first next game, as server 2.
- To stop early, use **🏁 End match…**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? The Undo bar names what it removes, e.g. "↶ Undo: point to Red House (4-2)", "Undo: rally to Blue House · side-out" or "Undo: first-server pick". Each tap undoes one step; score, server and call go back too.
- Wrong player on the right? Mid-game, tap **Fix who started on the right** and pick the right player. The score stays; only the server's name updates.
- Wrong first server in rally scoring? Tap **Fix who served first**. In side-out scoring there is no such link, because the server decides who can score; use **Correct the timeline** instead.
- Older rally: tap **Edit** next to **Correct the timeline** on the **Scoring** tab.
1. Tap ✎ to change the winner, player or detail. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to remove a rally; the app asks you to confirm first.
3. To add a missed rally, tap ＋ on the rally before it. **Insert a missed point** starts with no side picked, and **Save** stays off until you choose one.
4. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match; later serves follow any change.

> **Note:** Players named on later points stay as they were. If a fix changes who served, check them.

## Common questions
### What about matches scored before this change?
Scores and credited players are unchanged.
