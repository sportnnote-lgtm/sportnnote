Many US school meets still measure jumps and throws in feet and inches. SportnNote can show and accept marks that way. Every mark is still stored and ranked in metres, so results, records and rankings stay comparable with every other meet.

## Before you start
- You organise the meet, or you're a host or an event official.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament → **Manage** tab → **Athletics — points & timing**.
2. Set **Field marks in** to **Feet and inches**. Choose **Metres** to keep metric. **Each viewer's region** shows feet and inches to people whose region is the United States, and metres to everyone else.
3. Open a jump or throw event. A note at the top confirms marks are in feet and inches.
4. Type each mark like `16-09.25` (the usual US results style) or `16' 9¼"`. Metres such as `5.12` still work.
5. The mark shows as `16' 9¼"`. Bar heights in high jump and pole vault work the same way, for example `5-06`.

## How marks are converted
- A mark typed in feet and inches is converted to metres and rounded to the nearest centimetre.
- A mark is shown to the nearest quarter inch, so whatever you type reads back the same.
- Rankings, countback, qualifying and records use the metres value. Records always stay metric.

> **Tip:** On a phone keyboard, the dash in `16-09.25` is quicker than the ' and " marks.

## Pounds for lifting and miles for road races
- Weightlifting: under **Settings**, the lifting settings have **Weights shown in**. **Kilograms + pounds** shows pounds next to the bar weight and each lifter's total. Kilograms always count: declare and enter attempts in kg.
- Road and cross-country: **Road distances** set to **Kilometres + miles** shows miles next to each distance when you set up a race (5 km · 3.1 mi).

## Common questions
### Do US visitors see feet and inches at an Indian meet?
Only if the meet is set to **Each viewer's region**. A meet set to **Metres** shows metres to everyone.

### Why did 21-04.25 become 6.51 m?
21 feet 4¼ inches is 6.508 m. Marks are stored to the centimetre, so it rounds to 6.51 m. It still displays as 21' 4¼".
