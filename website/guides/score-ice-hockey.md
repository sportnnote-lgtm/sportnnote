Score an ice hockey game on one phone. You start and stop the clock at every whistle and tap what happens; the app runs the penalty box, works out power plays and builds the box score.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the game or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: **IIHF 3 × 20**, **NHL 3 × 20**, **US high school 3 × 17**, **Youth 3 × 15**, **Youth 3 × 12** or **Custom**. In the US the sport shows as "Hockey".
- In a tournament, group games play overtime and a shoot-out if tied, and knockout games play play-off overtime. In a friendly, choose under **If tied after regulation**.
- Put each team's goalie in the G spot of the line-up on the **Info** tab. No line-up? Tap **🧤 Set goalie** under each team.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the game → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Face-off**. The clock counts down from 20:00.
2. Tap **⏸ Stop** at each whistle and **▶ Resume** at the face-off. The clock stops by itself after a goal or a penalty.
3. At 0:00 tap **⏭ End P1** and answer **Yes**. Tap **▶ Start P2** after the break.
4. Clock wrong? Tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the time left (for example `12:30`) and tap **Set**.
5. In the 3rd period, **🏁 Final horn** ends the game. If the score is level and the game has overtime, you see **⏭ End P3** instead and play goes on to **OT**.

## Step by step: log the play
1. **🚨 Goal**: the panel already says **EV**, **PP** or **SH** from the penalties, and **EN** if the other net is empty. Tap a chip to change it. Then tap the scorer, the first assist (or **Unassisted**) and the second assist (or **No second assist**).
2. **🥅 Shot**: a shot on goal that was saved. Tap the shooter or **Skip shooter**. The save goes to the other team's goalie.
3. **⏱ Penalty**: pick the kind (**Minor 2′**, **Double minor 4′**, **Major 5′**, **Major 5′ + game misconduct**, **Misconduct 10′**, **Game misconduct**, **Match penalty** or **Bench minor 2′**), an infraction such as **Tripping** (or **Skip**), then the player.
4. **🎯 Penalty shot**: tap **✓ Scored**, **🧤 Saved** or **↗ Missed**, then the shooter.
5. **🥅 Pull goalie** for an extra skater; tap **🧤 Goalie back** when the goalie returns. A goal into the empty net is marked EN and isn't counted against the goalie.
6. **⏸ Time-out · 1**: each team has one time-out a game.

> **Tip:** Turn on **Track +/- (who was on the ice at each goal)** in **⚙️ Match settings** and, after each goal, tap the skaters who were on the ice. Power-play goals don't count for +/-.

## How the penalty box works
- Under the clock you see who is in the box and the time left, plus the strength, for example "5 v 4 · Lakeview Wolves power play".
- A power-play goal ends the earliest minor of the team that is short. A double minor loses only the 2 minutes being served. Majors run in full.
- No team drops below 3 skaters. A third penalty waits until one of the first two ends.
- One minor each at the same stoppage with both teams at full strength plays 4 on 4. Other matching penalties cancel out, so the teams stay at full strength.
- A misconduct sits the player for 10 minutes without leaving the team short. A game misconduct or match penalty puts the player out of the game. For a match penalty, a team-mate serves 5 minutes.

## Step by step: overtime and the shoot-out
1. Overtime is sudden death. The first goal ends the game. Round-robin overtime is 5 minutes, 3 on 3. A penalty there makes it 4 on 3.
2. Still level? Tap **🏁 Final horn**, then pick who shoots first, for example **NH first** (teams show by short name).
3. Tap a shooter (optional), then **✓ Scored** or **✗ Saved / missed**. The shoot-out stops as soon as one team can't catch up.
4. Level after the set number of shooters (IIHF 5, NHL 3)? The app asks who shoots first in sudden death.
5. The shoot-out winner gets one extra goal in the final score, for example 2–1. No player is credited with that goal.

## Mistakes
- Tap **↶ Undo** to remove the last thing you logged. It names what it will remove.
- Open **✎ Correct the timeline** to remove a moment with **✕** or re-enter it with **✎ Edit**. If you cancel an edit, the original stays.

## Common questions
### How do standings count overtime?
IIHF tables give 3 for a regulation win, 2 for an overtime or shoot-out win, 1 for an overtime or shoot-out loss. The table shows W, OTW, OTL and L. Organisers can switch to **NHL 2-1-0 (OT loss = 1)**, which breaks ties on regulation wins, then regulation plus overtime wins.

### What is the walkover score?
5-0 by default (IIHF). Check your competition's rules and change it in the tournament settings.
