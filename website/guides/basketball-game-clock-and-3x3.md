A basketball quarter of 10 minutes can take 20 real minutes, because the clock stops on every whistle. Run the app's game clock the way the table officials run theirs, and every play is stamped with the real game time. Minutes played, plus/minus and the "last 2 minutes" timeout limit then match the official scoresheet.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- New matches have **Stoppable game clock** switched on in the format (FIBA, NBA, NCAA, School and 3×3). Matches scheduled before it keep the old minute count from tip-off.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap "▶ Start Q1" at the tip-off. The clock starts counting down from 10:00.
2. A foul or a time-out stops the clock by itself. So does a made basket in the last 2 minutes of the 4th quarter and of overtime (FIBA).
3. For any other stoppage (out of bounds, a violation, an injury), tap **⏸ Stop** in the clock panel.
4. When play restarts, tap **▶ Start**. While it is stopped, the panel says so.
5. If your clock and the table's drift apart, tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the time left (for example `4:32`) and tap **Set**.
6. At 0:00 the clock stops and the panel shows "0:00 — end of Q1". Finish any free throws, then tap "End Q1 →". The next quarter starts at 10:00 when you tap "▶ Start Q2".

> **Tip:** Plays you re-enter with **✎ Edit** keep their original game time and never stop the clock.

## What the clock gives you
- The play-by-play reads "Q2 07:32" (time left in the quarter).
- **MIN** and **+/-** in the box score come from the game time each player was on court. Set the starting five under **🔀 Substitutions** first.
- The FIBA timeout rule allows at most 2 time-outs once the 4th-quarter clock shows 2:00 or less. The app checks this against the game clock, not the time since tip-off.

## 3×3 games
Pick **3×3 (first to 21)** as the format. The app follows the FIBA 3×3 rules:
- **One 10-minute period.** The first team to 21 points wins straight away. Otherwise, at 0:00 the higher score wins; tap **🏁 End Match**.
- **Overtime.** Level at 0:00? Tap "🏀 Start Overtime (OT) · first to 2". There's no clock in overtime: the first team to score 2 points in it wins, and the game ends by itself.
- **Team fouls.** From a team's 7th foul, every foul gives the other team 2 free throws. From the 10th, it's 2 free throws and the ball. The free-throw panel opens with the count filled in and says when the team keeps the ball.
- **Shooting fouls.** A foul on a shot inside the arc gives 1 free throw. Change it to 2 for a shot from behind the arc.

## The 3×3 points table
A new tournament whose basketball format is 3×3 starts on the **FIBA 3×3** table:
1. Teams are ranked by win % (teams may play different numbers of games).
2. If exactly two teams are level, the winner of their game goes higher.
3. Next comes the higher average of points scored per game.

You can switch it in the points settings like any other system. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Common questions
### I forgot to start the clock. What now?
Tap **▶ Start**, then **⏱ Set clock** to the time the table shows. Plays you logged while it was stopped keep the time they were logged at.

### Can I still score without the clock?
Yes. The organiser can switch off **Stoppable game clock** in the match format. Then each play is stamped with the minute since tip-off, as before.

### Does the app run the 12-second shot clock?
No. The shot clock is for the table officials; the app records the game.
