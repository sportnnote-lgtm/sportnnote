Padel uses tennis scoring: 15-30-40, games and sets. Most tour events add the golden point, where 40-40 is settled by one deciding point. You only tap who won each point; the app does the rest.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** when scheduling: **Premier Padel / WPT (golden pt)**, **Classic (advantage)**, **Short (to 4, match TB)** or **Custom**.
- **At deuce** offers **Advantage (classic)** or **Golden point**.
- Tap **⚙ Customize this format** for **Games per set**, **Match length** and **Deciding set**: a **Full set** or a **Match tiebreak (to 10)**.
- Under **Players**, **Doubles** is the default. Add both pairs, each listed in the order they will serve.

## Step by step: score a point
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under "Who serves first?", tap the pair that will serve first. You can change it until the first point.
3. The serving line then names the server and their pair, for example "Serving: Asha Rao · Red House".
4. After each point, tap the player who won it under "Point — Red House" (or the other pair's row).
5. At 40-40 with golden point, the app shows "Golden point — next point wins the game." Tap the winner as usual.

## Sets, tiebreaks and the match tiebreak
- A set is won at 6 games by two clear games. At 6-6 a tiebreak to 7 (win by two) decides it, and the set reads 7-6.
- Short sets work the same way at 4 games, with the tiebreak at 4-4.
- With a match tiebreak, the deciding set is replaced by one tiebreak to 10, win by two. The app shows "Match tiebreak — first to 10 (win by 2)."

## How the app follows the serve
- Serve changes every game, and each pair's two players take turns on their service games.
- In a tiebreak, the first server serves one point, then the serve changes every two points.
- Players keep their serving order in a tiebreak, so a pair's second turn goes to the partner. That is the FIP rule, and the serving line follows it.

## Reading the board
The scoreboard shows the current game's points under POINTS and one column of games per set, with the set in play highlighted and a 🟡 dot next to the serving pair. The header adds "golden pt" when that rule is on. A set won in a tiebreak shows the loser's tiebreak points small, for example 7 and 6⁴. The match tiebreak has its own "TB" column. After the match, the result reads like "6-4, 3-6, [10-7]".

## Match stats
The **Score** tab shows a "Match stats" panel once the first point is played. From the second set, the chips switch between **Match** and each set.

- Service, return and total points won.
- Service games held and return games won (breaks).
- Break points saved and converted. A break point is a point that would win the game for the receivers. Tiebreak points never count.
- Golden points won, when the rule is on.
- Set points and match points saved, when there were any.
- Most points in a row, and the biggest lead in games within a set.

"Service points won, by server" gives each player's own serve, for example "18/26 (69%) · 4/5 held". It all comes from the point log.

## Fix a mistake
- Tapped the wrong player just now? Tap **Undo**.
- To fix an older point, tap **Edit** next to "Correct the timeline" on the **Scoring** tab. Every point is listed, newest first.
1. Tap ✎ on the point. Change who won it and the player, or tap **Team (no player)**. Tap **Save**.
2. To delete a point, tap ✕. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it, or **＋ Insert a point at the very start**.
3. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match, so games, sets, the server and the stats all recalculate. After the match, use [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends by itself when a pair wins enough sets. If a pair can't go on, tap **🏁 End match…** and choose **Retired**. If the referee puts a pair out, choose **Default**. The result keeps the games played, such as "6-4, 3-2 ret.". See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## What goes on the player's profile
When the match ends, each player's padel page adds it to Apps and W-D-L. Under "Totals · this sport" you'll see the points credited to them, plus their pair's games and sets won and lost, deciding sets, tiebreaks, return points, breaks and break point chances. Each player also gets their own service points, service games held and break points faced and saved. A match tiebreak counts as one game. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Who of the pair serves first?
The player listed first for that pair. Their partner serves the pair's next service game.

### Can I score padel singles?
Yes. Pick **Singles** under **Players** when you schedule. The serving line then names the player, and the serve simply alternates every game.
