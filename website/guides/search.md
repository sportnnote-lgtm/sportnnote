A parent wants yesterday's under-14 final. A coach is looking for the "St. Mary's" team. A student wants this year's inter-house athletics meet. One search box finds all of these: players, teams, matches and tournaments.

## Before you start
- You don't need to follow anyone to find them. Search covers everything public on SportnNote.
- Type at least two letters. With one letter, you'll see "Type one more letter to search."

## Step by step
1. On **Home**, tap the **🔍** at the top. Or open the **Discover** tab and tap **🔍 Search**.
2. Type in the box, for example a name, a team, a tournament or a school.
3. Results appear as you type, grouped into **Players**, **Teams**, **Matches** and **Tournaments**.
4. To see only one type, tap its chip at the top. Each chip shows how many it found. Tap **All** to go back.
5. Tap a result to open it.

## What each type searches
- **Players**: by name. On the **Players** chip, tap **⚙ Filters** to narrow the list, for example by sport, city or age.
- **Teams**: by team name or short name, such as `RED`.
- **Tournaments**: by tournament name or by the host's name, such as your school.
- **Matches**: by either team's name, or by the tournament's name. The newest matches come first.

## Find one match: "Red vs Blue"
To find a match between two particular teams, type both names with "vs" between them:
- `Red vs Blue`
- `red v blue`

You'll get only matches between those two teams, newest first. This is the quickest way to find yesterday's final.

> **Tip:** You don't need full names. Part of each name is enough, so `Mary vs Bosco` finds "St. Mary's School vs Don Bosco".

## Searching by phone number or email
If you type a whole mobile number or email address, the search looks for that exact player. It only finds adults who have chosen to be findable this way. It never shows the number or email to you. Teams, matches and tournaments can't be found by phone number.

## Common questions

### The results say "Only Cricket · show all sports". What's that?
You have a sport filter turned on under **⚙ Filters** on the Players chip. It also limits team, match and tournament results. Tap **show all sports** to clear it.

### What does "Not on app yet" mean next to a player?
Someone added that player to a team, but the player hasn't joined SportnNote themselves yet. Their stats are still recorded.

### Why can't I find a tournament I know exists?
Check the spelling, or try a shorter word, for example `athletics` instead of the full name. A tournament the organiser deleted won't appear.

### Part of the results say they "couldn't load just now". What should I do?
Your connection dropped for one type of result. The others are still shown. Search again in a moment.

### Can I follow what I find?
Yes. Open a player, team or tournament and tap the follow button. See [Choose your alerts](/guides/choose-your-alerts/).
