A player's sport page shows their career in that sport: the record, then sections of totals, averages, rates and bests. A career mixes everything, though: a box-cricket match with a T20 final, a doubles win with a singles loss. Filter chips show one slice at a time, and every figure is worked out again for just those matches.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can see the player's profile can use the filters. Nothing is saved or changed.
- The chips only appear when they would split something. A player who has only ever played T20 sees no **Format** chip.

## Step by step
1. Open the player's profile and, under **By sport**, tap a sport.
2. Under **All**, **Official** and **Friendly** you'll see a row of chips such as **Format: All ▾**. Swipe the row sideways to see more.
3. Tap a chip to open its choices, then tap one. Each choice shows how many matches it covers, for example **T20 · 4**.
4. The record, every section, the partners and the **Match history** now cover only those matches.
5. Add more chips to narrow it further, for example **Format: T20** and **Season: 2026**.
6. Tap **✕ Clear** to go back to the whole career.

> **Tip:** **All / Official / Friendly** still works with the chips. Official means tournament matches.

## What the career shows
- **The record**: **Apps** (matches played), the result record and **Win %**. Sports that can't end in a draw (tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel, pickleball, volleyball, basketball) show **W-L**; the others show **W-D-L**. Win % counts only matches with a result.
- Under it, when they apply: **Starts**, **Ties**, **No result**, **Best win run** (most wins in a row), and **Titles** and **Finals** (knockout finals the player's side won and reached).
- **Sections** for the sport, for example:
- Basketball: **Per game** (points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, minutes), **Shooting** (free throws and FT %), **Totals**, **Career highs** and double-doubles.
- Football: goals per game and per 90 minutes, shots on target %, shot conversion %, minutes, **Bests** (hat-tricks), and **Goalkeeping** for a player who has kept goal (clean sheets, goals conceded, save %).
- Volleyball: points, aces and blocks per set (over the sets the player was on court), and sets won and lost.
- Kabaddi: total points, raid and tackle points per match, Super 10s and High 5s.
- Racket sports: **Match play** (sets, games, points, deciders and tiebreaks won and lost, with won %), and **Serve & return** (service and return points won %, holds, breaks and break points where the sport has them).
- Chess: games, **Score** (a win is 1, a draw ½) and **Score %**.
- Carrom: points, points per match, queens and best match.
- In racket sports and carrom, a player who has played both shows **Singles / doubles** W-L, and a doubles player sees **Partners**: the record with each partner.
- A figure never recorded for this player is left out, not shown as 0. A ☁ means it was tracked in only some games; tap it to see how many.
- Each **Match history** row shows the score line (sets or games) and up to three key stats from that match. Chess rows show the colour and time control.

> **Note:** Cricket's career (batting, bowling and fielding) is explained in [Read the scorecard and career stats](/guides/cricket-scorecard-and-stats/). Golf has its own page.

## Which chips each sport has
- **Cricket**: **Format** (T20, ODI, T10, Hundred, Box, Long), **Ball** (leather or tennis ball), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**. See [Read the scorecard and career stats](/guides/cricket-scorecard-and-stats/) for how a match's format is decided.
- **Tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, pickleball, padel**: **Singles/Doubles**, **Format** (best of 3, best of 5 and so on), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Carrom**: **Singles/Doubles**, **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Chess**: **Colour** (White or Black), **Time control** (classical, rapid, blitz, bullet, untimed), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Football, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi**: **Format** (for example 7-a-side, 3x3, Indoor or Beach), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- Golf has its own page and no filter chips yet.

## Where the details come from
Each filter reads the match the stats came from: its format, tournament, date and teams. Nothing extra has to be entered.

- **Season** is the calendar year the match was played in.
- **Tournament** lists each tournament by name, and **Friendlies** for matches outside one.
- **Colour** in chess comes from who had White in the match. If the scorer didn't change it, the home player had White.

## Common questions
### Why is a match missing from every choice?
Its match record couldn't be found, for example a very old entry. It still counts in the full career.

### Why is a partner missing?
It counts when the app knows who was on the player's side: the partner's stats from that match, or a two-player team. The match still counts in the career.

### Why don't I see the Singles/Doubles chip?
The player has played only singles, or only doubles. A chip needs at least two choices to appear.
