A career total mixes everything: a box-cricket match with a T20 final, a doubles win with a singles loss, last year with this year. Filter chips on a player's sport page let you look at one slice at a time. Every figure on the page is worked out again for just those matches.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can see the player's profile can use the filters. Nothing is saved or changed.
- The chips only appear when they would split something. A player who has only ever played T20 sees no **Format** chip.

## Step by step
1. Open the player's profile and, under **By sport**, tap a sport.
2. Under **All**, **Official** and **Friendly** you'll see a row of chips such as **Format: All ▾**. Swipe the row sideways to see more.
3. Tap a chip to open its choices, then tap one. Each choice shows how many matches it covers, for example **T20 · 4**.
4. The record (**Apps**, **W-D-L**, **Win %**), the stats and the **Match history** now cover only those matches.
5. Add more chips to narrow it further, for example **Format: T20** and **Season: 2026**.
6. Tap **✕ Clear** to go back to the whole career.

> **Tip:** The **All / Official / Friendly** choice still works with the chips. Official means tournament matches.

## Which chips each sport has
- **Cricket**: **Format** (T20, ODI, T10, Hundred, Box, Long), **Ball** (leather or tennis ball), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**. See [Read the scorecard and career stats](/guides/cricket-scorecard-and-stats/) for how a match's format is decided.
- **Tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, pickleball, padel**: **Singles/Doubles**, **Format** (best of 3, best of 5 and so on), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Carrom**: **Singles/Doubles**, **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Chess**: **Colour** (White or Black), **Time control** (classical, rapid, blitz, bullet, untimed), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- **Football, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi**: **Format** (for example 7-a-side, 3x3, Indoor or Beach), **Tournament**, **Season**, **Opponent**.
- Golf has its own page and no filter chips yet.

## Where the details come from
Each filter reads the match the stats came from: the format the organiser or scorer chose, the tournament it belonged to, the date and the teams. Nothing extra has to be entered.

- **Season** is the calendar year the match was played in.
- **Tournament** lists each tournament by name, and **Friendlies** for matches outside a tournament.
- **Opponent** is the other team or player in that match.
- **Colour** in chess comes from who had White in the match. If the scorer didn't change it, the home player had White.

## Common questions
### Why is a match missing from every choice?
Its match record couldn't be found, for example a very old entry. It still counts in the full career; it just can't be placed in a split.

### Why don't I see the Singles/Doubles chip?
The player has played only singles, or only doubles. A chip needs at least two choices to appear.
