On a sports day or an inter-school meet, every sport ends with places, and the houses want one table that adds them all up. SportnNote turns each sport's places into points (or medals) and sums them per house or school in the **🏅 Medal table**.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament.
- The tournament needs two or more sports.
- Enter the houses or schools as the teams in every team sport, using the same name each time ("Red House" in football and in kabaddi is one house).

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament, go to **Manage** and tap **Edit details**. When you create a tournament, the same options appear on the create screen.
2. Under **Overall scoring (multi-sport)**, tap **🏅 Medal / position points**.
3. Set **Ranked positions** and type the points for each place, for example 1st 10, 2nd 7, 3rd 5.
4. Under **Rank the medal table by**, tap **🔢 Points** or **🥇 Medals first**.
5. Under **Bronze in knockouts**, leave a sport on **3rd-place match**, or tap it to switch to **two bronzes**.
6. Optionally give a big sport a **Per-sport weight**, such as 2×.
7. Tap **Save changes**. The table is on the tournament page under **🏅 Medal table**.

## Where each sport's places come from
Places follow how the sport was actually played, not just who won the most games.
- **Knockout:** the final winner is 1st and the loser 2nd. If a 3rd-place match is scheduled, its winner is 3rd and its loser 4th. If there isn't one, both semi-final losers share 3rd and both get bronze.
- **Earlier rounds:** teams out in the same round share a place. The four quarter-final losers are all =5th, and round-of-16 losers are all =9th.
- **Groups, then a knockout:** knockout places come first. Teams that didn't reach the knockout come next, grouped by where they finished in their group: every group's 3rd-placed team shares the next place, then every 4th, and so on.
- **Double elimination:** the grand-final winner is 1st and the loser 2nd. The losers'-final loser is 3rd, the losers' semi-final loser 4th, and earlier losers' rounds share a place. A plate draw never gives medals.
- **IPL-style playoff:** the final gives 1st and 2nd, the Qualifier 2 loser is 3rd and the Eliminator loser is 4th. The rest follow the league table.
- **League only:** the league table order, using the sport's own points and tie-breaks.
- **Athletics, swimming and other timed or measured events:** places come from each event's results. For golf, see [Golf leaders, awards and the medal table](/guides/golf-leaders-awards-and-medal-table/).

> **Important:** A team that tops its group but loses the final takes silver, not gold. The final decides it.

## Shared places split the points
When teams share a place, they split the points for all the places they cover. With 1st 10, 2nd 7, 3rd 5 and 4th 3, two joint bronze medallists each get (5 + 3) ÷ 2 = 4. Four quarter-final losers split the 5th to 8th place points the same way.

## Points or medals first
- **🔢 Points:** most points first. If two houses are level, more golds, then silvers, then bronzes decide it.
- **🥇 Medals first:** most golds first, then silvers, then bronzes, as in the Olympic medal table. Points only split houses with the same medals.

## Team ties and singles
- A team tie (several rubbers between two teams) counts as one fixture. The tie winner goes through, and the tie's result sets the place. See [Run a team tie](/guides/run-team-ties/).
- In singles and doubles, the player scores for their house. A badminton singles final between two Red House players gives Red House gold and silver. The table shows the player's name next to the place.
- A doubles pair counts for a house when both partners are in that house.

> **Tip:** A player's house comes from their profile. Check that every player has one before the meet starts, or their places count under their own name instead.

## Common questions
### Why is a sport missing from the table halfway through?
Knockout places only count once they are decided. Quarter-final losers appear as soon as their match ends; the medals appear when the semi-finals, the 3rd-place match and the final are played. A sport set up as a league or groups followed by a knockout shows no places until the knockout starts, because the knockout decides the medals. If you never play the knockout, change that sport's format to a league.

### We played a 3rd-place match but want two bronzes.
Tap that sport under **Bronze in knockouts** so it shows **two bronzes**. The 3rd-place match result is then ignored for medals.

### Can I break a tie for 1st some other way?
Not on the medal table. Use **Per-sport weight** before the meet if one sport should count for more.
