A team page adds up every finished match the team has played. It shows the record and form, what the team scores and lets in per game, who leads the team in each stat, and its records: biggest win, longest winning run and more. Everything is worked out from the matches and player stats you have already scored, so there is nothing extra to enter.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can open the team can read its stats. Nothing is saved or changed.
- Stats need at least one finished match. Player figures (team leaders, per-game shots and so on) also need the scorer to have credited players during the match.

## Step by step
1. Open the team: tap its name on a match, in a table, or under **Teams**.
2. Tap **📊 Stats** to jump to the stats.
3. A team that plays more than one sport shows a chip for each sport, for example **⚽ Football** and **🏀 Basketball**. Tap one. Goals and runs never add up together, so every figure is for one sport.
4. If the team has played in more than one tournament, or across more than one season, a second row of chips appears: **All events**, then each tournament (and **Friendlies**) with its match count, then **All seasons** and each year. Tap one to see only those matches. Swipe the row sideways to see more.
5. Scroll down through the record, **Per game**, **Team leaders**, **Records** and **Head-to-head**. Tap a record or a form letter to open that match.

> **Tip:** The tournament and season choice stays in the page link, so a shared link opens the same view.

## What each part shows
- **Played, Won, Win rate** and the **Form** strip: the last five results, newest first. W is a win, D a draw, L a loss. Cricket shows T for a tie, and NR for a no result.
- **For, against and difference** in the sport's own unit: goals in football, hockey and handball, points in basketball and kabaddi, sets in volleyball, games in badminton or table tennis. The label says which, for example **Goal difference** or **Set difference**. Cricket shows **Net run rate** for the matches you picked instead of a run difference.
- **Per game**: the team's average for and against, and the **Margin** (the average difference). Below that are team figures per game from the sport's match stats: shots and passes in football, rebounds, steals and turnovers in basketball, raid and tackle points in kabaddi. A shooting percentage such as **FT%** counts every attempt across the matches.
- **Team leaders**: the team's best player in each of the sport's leader categories, counting only matches they played for this team. Averages need a minimum number of games, the same minimum as tournament leaders. **Most games** is the player with the most appearances.
- **Records**: **Biggest win** and **Heaviest defeat** with the score, opponent and date; the most goals or points in one match; **Longest winning run**; **Longest unbeaten run** (sports with draws); **Current run** (for example W3); and, in goal sports, **Clean sheets** and **Failed to score**. Cricket shows **Highest total** and **Lowest total** instead of the biggest win, because a chase is won by wickets, not runs.
- **Head-to-head**: the record against each opponent, with the latest meeting's score.

## Why some figures are missing
- A per-game stat counts only the matches where the scorer tracked it. If shots were tracked in 3 of 4 matches, the tile says **Shots · 3 of 4**, and the average is over those 3. A match that didn't track it is never counted as 0.
- A stat that averages 0 (no cards at all, for example) is left out to keep the card short.
- A walkover or a no result counts in **Played**, but it has no score, so it's left out of for, against, per game and the score records.
- Sets and games sports don't show "most sets in a match"; the biggest win (for example 3–0) says more.

## Common questions
### Who counts as playing for the team?
Players in the team's matchday squad for that match. If no squad was picked, it's the team's squad. A player who also played for another side in a friendly only counts here for this team's matches.

### Why is a player missing from Team leaders?
They may be below the minimum for an average, or nobody on the team has a figure for that stat yet. Totals such as goals have no minimum.

### Can I compare two teams?
Yes. Open a match between them and, on its info card, tap **⚔️ Head-to-head & form ›**. See [Head-to-head and form](/guides/head-to-head-and-form/).
