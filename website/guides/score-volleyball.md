Every rally ends with a point for one team. Record each rally once; the app keeps the serve, sets and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format**: **Indoor**, **Beach**, **9-a-side**, **Single set to 25** or **Custom** (which also sets **Timeouts per team per set**).
- Add both squads, and set the lineup if you can. The six on court then come first; the rest fold under **Bench**.

## Step by step: score a rally
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab. Each team has its own panel, for example "Point — Red House".
2. In the panel of the team that won the rally, check how it was won. **Attack** is picked for you.
3. For an attack, tap the player who scored it. That is one tap.
4. For a block or an ace, tap **Block** or **Ace** first, then tap the player.
5. If the other team served into the net or out, tap **🥅 Opp. missed serve**. That is one tap.
6. For any other mistake by the other team, tap **🚩 Opp. fault**. You can pick what went wrong (**Net touch**, **Foot fault**, **Rotation**, **Double / 4 hits**, **Attack out**, **Block out**), then tap the player who erred, or **Not named**.
7. Not sure who scored? Tap **No player**. The team still gets the point.

## Who is serving
The team that wins a rally serves the next one. The serving team gets a 🏐 on the scoreboard and a badge on its panel. Only that team is offered **Ace**, and only the other team is offered **Opp. missed serve**.

For the server's name, record the toss and the rotation. The app then shows "🏐 Rohan serves", turns Ace into one tap on the server and charges a missed serve to the player who missed. See [Volleyball toss, rotation and substitutions](/guides/volleyball-lineups-rotation-and-subs/).

## What each point gives a player
- Attack: 1 point and 1 attack point to the attacker.
- Block: 1 point and 1 block to the blocker.
- Ace: 1 point and 1 ace to the server.
- Opp. fault with a player named: the team's point, and 1 error for the player who erred.
- Opp. missed serve with the server known: the team's point, and 1 serve error for the server who missed.
- Opp. missed serve or a fault with nobody named: the team's point, but nobody's stat.

Points include aces and blocks. **Errors** and **Serve errors** count only in matches where a player was named; other matches read "not tracked", never 0.

For attack attempts, attack efficiency and reception grades, turn on **🔎 Detailed stats (optional)** under the point panels. See [Volleyball detailed stats](/guides/volleyball-detailed-stats/).

## Sets, timeouts and the end of the match
- A set ends by itself at the target with a two-point lead (unless the organiser chose first to the target). The decider is a shorter race, to 15.
- When the next point can win the set or the match, the scoreboard shows **SET POINT** or **MATCH POINT** with the team's name.
- Timeouts: indoor teams have two per set, beach teams one. Tap "Timeout — Red House (1 left)".
- A **Switch sides** note appears when teams change ends: beach every 7 points (5 in the decider), indoor at 8 in the decider. Beach sets 1–2 show a **Technical timeout** note at 21 points.
- To stop a match early, use **🏁 End match…**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## Reading the board and the stats
"Player stats" and "Team stats" are on the **Score** and **Summary** tabs; tap **Set 1**, **Set 2** … to filter. Columns for serve errors (**SE**) and errors (**ERR**) appear when players were named. The point log lists every rally with how it was won, the score and the player, for example "1-1 · Net touch · Rohan".

## Sets played
At the first point the app notes who is on court. **Sets played** counts each set a player was on court or scored in, substitutes and liberos included. Per-set figures divide by it, as the FIVB does.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? Tap **Undo**. It also takes the stats back off the player.
- To fix an older point, tap **Edit** next to "Correct the timeline".
1. Find the point and tap ✎. Change who won the rally, the point type or the player.
2. Tap **Save**. The score, the sets and every player's stats recalculate, even if the fix moves the end of a set.
3. To delete a point, tap ✕ and confirm. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it.
4. Tap **Done** to close the editor.

## Common questions
### Do I have to name the fault?
No. Naming the player builds each player's error count.

### Can I score by voice?
Yes. Open **🎤 Voice scoring** and say "attack Rohan" or "ace Rohan", then tap Apply.
