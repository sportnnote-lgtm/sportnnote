In a team event, one fixture is several individual matches called rubbers: five singles in table tennis, three singles and two doubles in the Thomas Cup, four boards in chess. SportnNote treats it as one tie. You score each rubber on its own, and the table counts the tie once.

## Before you start
- Tournament or tie hosts create ties. Each team's captain, vice-captain or team admin can enter their own lineup.
- Add each team's players first; the lineup picker lists them.
- Team ties work for table tennis, badminton, tennis, squash, chess, carrom, padel and pickleball.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament, go to **Manage** and tap **New series / tie**. For a whole group or league, use **Auto-generate fixtures** instead (see below).
2. Pick the sport, **Team A (named first)** and **Team B**. **👥 Team tie (rubbers)** is already selected for these sports.
3. Under **How the tie is played**, tap a format, or **✏️ Custom rubbers** to build your own list of singles, doubles and mixed rubbers.
4. Under **Once the tie is decided**, choose **⏹ Always stop** or **▶️ Always play them** (see below).
5. For an ABC v XYZ format (table tennis), choose **Who plays ABC?** or tap **🪙 Draw it**. A host can change it until the first lineup is saved.
6. Tap **Create tie**. The tie page opens.
7. Each captain opens the tie and taps **Enter our lineup**: a player for every position (A, B, C, No.1, No.2 or each board) and the players for each doubles rubber, then **Save lineup**. A host can enter either team's lineup instead.
8. Tap a rubber to score it like any match.

> **Tip:** Lineups are sealed. Until both are in, the other team sees only **✓ In**, never the names. Once both are in, both show to everyone and each rubber gets its players.

## The formats
- **Table tennis:** World Team Champs (five singles, ABC v XYZ), Corbillon (four singles and a doubles), Olympic (a doubles, then four singles).
- **Badminton:** Thomas / Uber Cup (three singles, two doubles) and Sudirman Cup (five events, mixed doubles first).
- **Tennis:** a Davis Cup tie with reverse singles, or the Davis Cup / BJK Cup Finals (two singles and a doubles).
- **Squash:** three rubbers by order of merit. **Chess:** four or six boards at once, half a point for a draw.
- **Carrom, padel, pickleball:** common club formats; use custom rubbers if yours differ.

## When the tie is decided
A team needs more than half the rubbers: 3 of 5, 2 of 3, or 2½ of 4 chess boards. The rubbers left after that are dead rubbers.
- **🔀 By stage (default)** in **Auto-generate fixtures**: group and league ties play every rubber, because group tie-breaks count rubbers, games and points. Knockout ties stop.
- **⏹ Always stop:** the rubbers still to play are set aside and show **Not played**. They don't count anywhere.
- **▶️ Always play them:** dead rubbers are played and counted, so a 3–0 can end 4–1.
- A single tie made with **New series / tie** stops unless you choose **Always play them**.
- The tie settles as soon as the deciding rubber's result goes out, whoever scored it. If a correction takes the win away, set-aside rubbers come back.

The tie page shows which rule the tie uses. Ties created before this choice existed keep their old setting.

## Every fixture as a tie
In **Auto-generate fixtures**, tap **👥 Each fixture is a team tie** and pick the format. Every pairing becomes a tie, and later knockout rounds created from the bracket are ties too, with the knockout rule for dead rubbers.

## How the table counts ties
Each tie counts once in the table, and the **R** column shows rubbers won and lost. When you first turn on **👥 Each fixture is a team tie**, the points system switches to the federation's team ranking:
- **Table tennis (ITTF):** 2 for a tie won, 1 for a tie lost; teams level go to results among themselves, then rubbers, games and points ratios.
- **Badminton (BWF), squash, carrom, pickleball:** ties won. Two level: head-to-head. Three or more: rubbers, games, then points difference.
- **Tennis (Davis Cup / BJK Cup):** ties won. Two level: head-to-head. Three or more: % of rubbers, sets, games won.
- **Chess:** match points (2 a win, 1 a draw), then game points, then the direct encounter.

You can change it in the sport's points settings.

## Alerts and careers
- Team and tournament followers get one alert with the tie result, not one per rubber. A player's followers still hear about that player's rubber.
- Every rubber counts on its players' profiles like any singles or doubles match.

## Common questions
### Can a captain change the lineup?
Yes, until the other team's lineup is in. After that, ask the tie host. Once a rubber has started, nobody can change it.

### Can one player play two singles?
In nominated formats you'll see a warning, because most team events allow one singles each, but you can still save.

### Where do I see the tie?
In the tournament's **Matches** tab the tie is one card, **👥 Team tie · 5 rubbers**. Tap it for the tie page.
