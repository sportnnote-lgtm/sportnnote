Each race is an event (say 100 m U14 Boys), run as heats and a final that adds points to the house table. You enter times on a phone; the app ranks heats, seeds the final and keeps records and personal bests. For jumps and throws, see [Run athletics field events](/guides/run-athletics-field-events/). For cross-country, road races and race walks, see [Run a cross-country, road race or race walk](/guides/run-a-cross-country-or-road-race/).

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add events and enter results.
- Create the tournament with **Athletics** as a sport. Under **Athletics basics**, pick the position points (8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 by default; change them later in **⚙ Points & timing**, where relays can also score double) and say whether it is a **Hand-timed meet**.
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Athletes and relay teams score for them.

## Step by step: add an event
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Athletics — events**, and tap **＋ Add an event**.
2. **Category** and **Event:** pick the age group (U10 to U20, or Open), Boys, Girls or Mixed, then the race. The list follows the category: 3000 m starts at U16, and hurdles vary by age and gender.
3. **Athletes:** tap a house or search any player, and tap each one to enter them. Players outside the category are hidden; tap the category chip to show everyone.
4. **Entry marks (optional):** type each best time, or tap **Fill from PB** or **Fill from SB**. Digits fill from the right: 1185 is 11.85, 25034 is 2:50.34.
5. **Rounds:** the app suggests rounds from the World Athletics tables, e.g. 12 sprinters make 2 heats, with the first 3 in each plus the 2 fastest of the rest in a final of 8. You can pick another option.
6. **Lanes:** **Draw lanes** draws lanes within each heat. **Fastest in the middle** puts the best seed in lane 4.
7. Tap **Create event & start list**.

## Relays
- Pick **4 × 100 m relay** or **4 × 400 m relay** and tap a house to add its team. A second team from one house is named "Red House B".
- Tap the team and pick 4 runners in leg order, plus up to 2 reserves.

## Step by step: enter results
1. Open the event and tap **Enter results**. The **Heat 1**, **Heat 2** chips switch heats.
2. Before the start, tap **✎ Change lanes**, an athlete, then the new lane. Whoever was there swaps with them.
3. Type each time with the number keys: 1205 shows = 12.05 before you save. Tap **Next ›** to save and move on.
4. For a photo-finish thousandth, tap **.000** first: 12053 reads as 12.053. It counts as 12.06 and breaks ties.
5. For the 100 m, 200 m and sprint hurdles, enter the **Wind (m/s)**. Over +2.0 counts for places but not PBs or records.
6. Tap **DNS**, **DNF**, **FS** or **DQ** for didn't start, didn't finish, false start or disqualified, optionally with the rule number.
7. When a heat is done, tap **Next heat → Heat 2**. The ranking shows **Q** (by place), **q** (fastest losers), **PB**, **SB** and **MR**.
8. With every heat in, tap **Close heats → seed the final**. The check counts athletes with no result; they drop out without a place, so mark them **DNS** if they didn't run.
9. After the final, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The check lists anyone with no result and any new meet record, with the old one. Medals, house points, records and profiles then update.

## Times that look wrong
Each event has a usual range. A time outside it, such as an 800 m typed as 2153 (21.53), is never refused; you get **Check this mark**. Tap **No, re-enter it** to fix it, or **Yes, save** if it's real. An unconfirmed out-of-range time never shows **PB**, **SB** or **MR** and can't set a record.

## Reopen a round or the final
- An organiser (a tournament manager or the event's creator) can tap **↺ Reopen heats** on a closed round until the next round has a result. The final is seeded again when you close the heats.
- **↺ Reopen final** puts a locked final back to live. Any meet record it set returns to the previous holder, and its points leave the house table until you lock it again.

## Hand timing
- Tap **Hand** on a stopwatch time. It is read to the tenth (11.12 becomes 11.2) and shown with an "h".
- With **Hand** on, the last digit is the tenth: 108 is 10.8, 1053 is 1:05.3. Turning it off brings back the full time you typed.
- Hand times never count for PBs or records unless your meet is all stopwatches and **Hand-timed meet** is on in **⚙ Points & timing**. Then they count, as do races without a wind reading; wind over +2.0 still doesn't.

> **Tip:** Tap **📤 Share** to send the start list or results, with a live-results link, to WhatsApp.

## What everyone sees
- **House table** (Athletics page and **Stats** tab; tied places share points), **Best by event**, **Best athletes (points)** (individual finals only) and **Meet records**. When a school hosts, **School records** show its earlier meets' best marks.
- **Athlete profiles:** every result, medals, and a PB and SB per event.
