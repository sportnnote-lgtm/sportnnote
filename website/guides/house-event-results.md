On sports day a house can win ten medals without playing a single match. Athletics, swimming and the other event sports have finals, not matches, so a team or house page now has an **Event results** section. It shows what the house won in each sport, using the same finals and points as the meet's medal table. A golf team's stroke-play rounds show there too.

Nothing extra needs entering. Everything comes from the results the officials have already locked.

## Before you start
- The house needs to be a team in SportnNote, and its athletes entered for it. That happens when the organiser picks the house for each athlete, relay or crew while entering them for an event.
- Only finals count. A heat or semi-final adds nothing until the final is locked with **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

## Step by step
1. Open the team or house page.
2. Scroll down to **Event results**. Each sport the house took part in has its own card, sorted by golds and then points.
3. The five figures at the top of a card are 🥇 Gold, 🥈 Silver, 🥉 Bronze, Points and Finals. Points are the meet's own position points for that sport, with any relay multiplier. Finals counts the finals where the house had someone placed.
4. **Latest results** lists the house's places in the most recent finals: the medal or place, the athlete or relay, the event and meet, and the mark (time, distance, height or score).
5. Tap a result to open that final's full result sheet.
6. **Records held** lists the records in each meet's record book that belong to the house, with the event, the holder and the mark.

> **Tip:** A house page with no matches doesn't say "No results yet" any more. If it still does, check that the house was picked when its athletes were entered.

## Golf
If the team's players have played stroke-play rounds, a **Golf · stroke play** card shows the rounds played, the rounds they won, and the best 18-hole score.

## The athlete's own profile
An athlete's profile now opens with figures that make sense for their sport. Wins, draws and losses don't fit a 100 m race.
- Someone who only does event sports sees **Events**, **Finals**, **Medals** and **PBs**. A heat and its final count as one event. PBs counts the events where they have a personal best on record.
- A golfer who only plays stroke-play rounds sees **Rounds**, **Wins** and **Best round**.
- A player who also plays match sports keeps **Apps**, **W-D-L** and **Win %** for their matches, with a **Medals** tile added once they have won one.

Tap a sport under **By sport** for the full career: PB and season best per event, medals and every result.

## Common questions

### Why does a medal show on the meet's table but not on the house page?
The house page looks at meets where the house, or one of its squad, has an entry. If the athlete was entered under a house name typed by hand, not the house team, the medal is matched by name. Check the spelling matches the team's name exactly.

### Do points from a relay count for the house?
Yes. A relay or crew scores for the house it was entered for, with the same relay multiplier the meet uses.

### A final was reopened. Has the house lost the medal?
Until it's locked again, yes. A reopened final drops out of the medal table and the house page, and comes back when the official taps **🏁 Finish & lock results** again.

### Will I get an alert when the house wins a final?
Yes, if you follow the team and keep **Result** on. See [Choose which alerts you get](/guides/choose-your-alerts/).
