Most schools have a record board going back years. If those records aren't in SportnNote, the first 100 m final of the day "sets a meet record" even with a slow time. The record board fixes that. You enter the records you already have, and a final then shows **MR** or **SR** only when it beats them.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament or an organiser. Everyone else can see the records but can't change them.
- Have your records ready: the event, the category (say U14 Boys), the mark, the holder, and if you know them, the house and the year.
- There are two kinds of record. **Meet records** belong to this meet. **School records** count across every meet your school hosts. You'll only see the school option when the tournament is hosted by your school's organisation.

## Step by step: add one record
1. Open the tournament and go to the **Athletics** page (or **Swimming**, and so on).
2. Tap **📖 Record board — add your school’s records**.
3. Pick **Meet records (this meet)** or **School records (every meet)**, then **One record**.
4. Pick the age group and **Boys**, **Girls** or **Mixed**, then the event. For swimming, pick the pool length first: 25 m and 50 m records are kept apart.
5. Type the mark the way you would on a result: `12.84`, `2:05.3` for a time, `5.62` for metres.
6. Type the holder's name and, if you have them, their house and the year.
7. Tap **Save U14 Boys record** (the button names your category). If that event already has a record, you're asked before it's replaced.

## Step by step: paste the whole board
1. On the record board, tap **Paste a list**.
2. Put one record per line in this order: event, category, mark, holder, house, year. For example, `100 m, U14 Boys, 12.84, Kavya Nair, Red House, 2009`. You can copy the columns straight from a spreadsheet, because tabs work as well as commas.
3. Every line is checked as you type. Good lines show a ✓ with the record as it will be saved. A line with a problem shows its line number and what's wrong, for example an event name the app doesn't know.
4. Tap **Add 12 records** (the button shows how many). Lines with a problem are skipped, so you can fix them and paste them again.

> **Tip:** The house and year are optional. The app only treats the last column as the year if it's a four-digit number.

## What happens at a final
- When an official taps **🏁 Finish & lock results**, the check says whether a record falls and shows the old holder.
- A mark that beats the board shows **MR** or **SR** on the result sheet and replaces the record. A mark that equals it shows **=MR**.
- **↺ Reopen final** gives the record back to whoever held it before, historic records included.
- To take a record off the board, tap **✕** next to it and confirm. The next final in that event and category then sets the record afresh.

## Two finals at once
On sports day the track and the long-jump pit often lock their finals at the same moment. Each final now sends only the records it set, and the server adds them one at a time. Both records are kept, and neither official's phone can overwrite the other's.

## If saving doesn't finish
Locking a final saves its results first. It then writes each athlete's line to their profile (place, mark, medal, points, PB) and any new record. If the connection drops partway through, the results and places are still safe. The event page shows a red notice saying what didn't save.
1. Tap **Retry saving results to profiles** once you have signal again. It sends the same lines and record again and is safe to repeat.
2. If the app was closed before you could retry, an organiser can open the locked final and tap **↻ Re-sync results to profiles**. The lines are rebuilt from the saved results.

## Common questions
### Can an event official save a record?
Yes. If an organiser made someone the official for an event, that person can lock its final, and any record it sets is saved. They can't edit the record board itself.

### Why does my school record list show marks from other years' meets?
**School records** combines the board you entered with the best marks from every other meet your school has run in SportnNote. For each event, whichever mark is better is shown.

### Do wind-aided or hand times count?
They follow the same rules as before. A mark with wind over +2.0, or a hand time at a meet that isn't hand-timed, never sets a record. Neither does a time someone typed outside the usual range without confirming it.
