Inter-school leagues usually play one round a week, for example every Wednesday afternoon from September to December, with no matches over half-term. The fixture generator can lay out a whole season like that, instead of spacing every match a few minutes apart on one day.

## Before you start
- You organise the tournament and its teams are entered.
- Times are in the tournament's time zone (set under **Edit details**), not your phone's.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament → **Manage** tab → **Auto-generate fixtures** (or **schedule**).
2. Pick the format (league, groups or knockout) and the teams, as usual.
3. Under **When are the matches?**, tap **Weekly across a term**.
4. Tap your **Match day**. Pick two days, such as Tue and Fri, and a round is played on each.
5. Type the **Kickoff time**, for example `15:30` or `3:30 pm`.
6. Set **Term starts** and, if you like, **Term ends**.
7. In **No matches on**, type holidays, one per line. A range works too, such as `26/10/2026 - 30/10/2026`. The app shows how many days it will skip.
8. Set **Gap inside a round**. Use 0 if all a round's matches start together on different pitches, or 60 to stagger them an hour apart.
9. Tap **Generate preview**. A line lists each round's date, such as "6 rounds: Wed 14 Oct, Wed 21 Oct, Wed 4 Nov …".
10. Adjust any single fixture's date if you need to, then save.

> **Tip:** In a groups format, every group plays its round 1 on the same day, its round 2 the next match day, and so on.

## If the term is too short
When there are more rounds than match days, the preview warns you. The rounds that don't fit keep the first kickoff time. Add a second match day, move the term end, or remove a holiday.

## Common questions
### Do the clocks changing matter?
No. 15:30 stays 15:30 venue time on both sides of the change.

### What about the old way?
**Minutes apart** is still there: the first kickoff, then each match a set number of minutes after the last. Its default first kickoff is tomorrow at 10:00 in the tournament's time zone.

### Can different groups play on different days?
Not in one go. Generate each group separately and pick its match day each time.
