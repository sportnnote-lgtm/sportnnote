On school grounds the ball doesn't always reach the rope. Batters run four on a big outfield, a wild throw gives away 4 more, or a wide beats the keeper and they run two. Each of these is scored differently in the book. The run pad gives you one tap for the common runs and a small panel for the rare ones, and it tells you how each one is credited.

## Before you start
- You can score the match (a listed scorer, or a host of the match or its tournament), on the **Scoring** tab.
- A striker, non-striker and bowler are picked.
- If the match uses local rules (for example, a wide worth 2), set them first. See [Set local rules](/guides/cricket-local-rules/).

## Runs off the bat
- Tap **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4** or **6**. The **4** and **6** keys are boundaries: they count in the batter's 4s and 6s.
- Odd runs swap strike for you. If the batters end up the other way round, tap **⇄ Swap**.

## Step by step: an all-run 4, a 5 or a 7
1. Tap the last key on the run pad, **5·7·+**.
2. Under "All run — not a boundary", tap **4 runs**, **5 runs** or **7 runs**.
3. For anything else, type the number under "Any other number" and tap **Add**.

An all-run 4 shows as "4r" in the over strip and doesn't count as a four for the batter.

## Step by step: runs plus overthrows
1. Tap **5·7·+**.
2. Under "Overthrows", tap how many runs the batters had completed next to **Ran** (0 to 3).
3. Tap the overthrows next to **Overthrows**: **1**, **2**, **3**, or **4 (boundary)** if the throw went to the rope.
4. Check the line that appears, for example "= 5 to Ravi · not a four".
5. Tap **Add 5 runs**.

So 1 run plus 4 overthrows is 5 runs to the batter, not a four. This follows the Laws: overthrows that reach the boundary add 4 runs, but they are never a boundary hit by the batter. The over strip shows "5ot".

> **Note:** Overthrows off a bye or leg bye are byes or leg byes, not the batter's runs. Add them to the bye count, for example 1 bye plus 4 overthrows is **B 5**.

## Byes and leg byes
1. Tap **Bye** or **Leg bye**.
2. Tap **B 1** to **B 5** (or **LB 1** to **LB 5**). For more, type the number and tap the **Add** button.

Byes and leg byes are team extras. They count as a ball faced by the striker, but they aren't the batter's runs and aren't charged to the bowler.

## Wides
1. Tap **Wide**.
2. Tap **Wd** for a plain wide, or the runs the batters ran (or 4 if it beat the keeper to the rope). The button shows the total, for example **Wd+2 (=3)**.
3. For a bigger number, type it and tap the add button.

A wide isn't a legal ball unless your local rules say so, and all its runs are wides charged to the bowler.

## No-balls
1. Tap **No ball**.
2. If the batter hit it, tap the runs off the bat: **Nb** for none, or **+1** to **+6**. A **+4** or **+6** counts as a boundary. For an all-run 4 off a no-ball, type 4 in the box below and tap **Add Nb+4**.
3. If the ball missed the bat and they ran, tap **Byes** or **Leg byes** (if leg byes are allowed), then **Nb+1b** to **Nb+5b** (or **Nb+1lb** to **Nb+5lb** for leg byes) instead.

Runs off the bat on a no-ball are the batter's runs, and the batter is credited with a ball faced. The no-ball penalty is an extra charged to the bowler. Byes or leg byes off a no-ball are team extras, not charged to the bowler, and the scorecard lists them under b or lb. If free hits are on, the line above the run buttons turns green and reads **FREE HIT**, and the run buttons get a green frame. The buttons don't move, so you won't tap the wrong one.

> **Tip:** A wicket can fall on a wide or a no-ball. Tap **🎯 …or a WICKET off the wide** (or off the no-ball) in the same panel. See [Record every kind of dismissal](/guides/cricket-dismissals/).

## Common questions
### Why does my 4 show as "4r" in the over?
It was entered as an all-run 4 from the **5·7·+** panel, so it isn't a boundary. If it was really a boundary, tap **↶ Undo** and tap the **4** key instead.

### Is there a limit on runs per ball?
The typed fields accept up to 99, as a guard against typos.

### I entered the wrong runs three overs ago. Do I have to undo everything?
No. Use **✎ Edit a past ball**. See [Fix a past ball, bowler or batter](/guides/cricket-edit-a-past-ball/).

### Where do penalty runs go?
They have their own button, **⚖️ Penalty**. See [Add penalty, bonus and minus runs](/guides/cricket-penalty-and-bonus-runs/).
