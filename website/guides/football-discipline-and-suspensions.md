In a football tournament, cards follow a player from match to match. The app keeps a discipline table from every finished match. It works out who must sit out the team's next match, and warns you if you pick that player.

## Before you start
- You need a tournament with football in it. The table builds itself from the cards scorers log during matches. See [How to score a football match](/guides/score-football/).
- Only hosts of the tournament can change the suspension rule.

## Step by step: set your suspension rule
1. Open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → **Football — format & points**.
2. Scroll to **Discipline & suspensions**. The line under the heading says the rule in plain words.
3. Under **A direct red card means**, pick **No ban**, **1 match out**, **2 matches out** or **3 matches out**.
4. Under **Yellow cards that add up to a 1-match ban**, pick **Off**, **2 yellows**, **3 yellows**, **4 yellows** or **5 yellows**.
5. If yellows add up, choose when to wipe the count: **Never**, **When the knockouts start**, or **After the quarter-finals**.
6. Tap **Save**.

> **Note:** If you never change it, the rule is: a red card is 1 match out, and 2 yellow cards in different matches are 1 match out. Many school and club competitions use this.

## How the rule works
- A red card for a second yellow in the same match is always 1 match out. Those two yellows don't count towards the yellow total.
- A direct red is the number of matches you chose. A yellow earlier in that match still counts.
- Each time a player reaches the yellow total, he is out for 1 match and his count starts again.
- A ban is served in the team's next finished match or matches in this tournament, whether or not the player was picked.
- Wiping the yellow count doesn't cancel a ban already earned.
- Cards shown to coaches and staff are listed apart. They never suspend a player.

## Step by step: read the discipline table
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** tab. In a multi-sport event, pick **Football** first.
2. Scroll below the leaders to **🟥 Discipline**.
3. Each carded player has a row with his team and three counts: yellow cards (🟨), reds for a second yellow (2🟨) and direct reds (🟥).
4. A red line under the team, **Suspended for next match**, shows who sits out and why. "One more yellow = ban" warns of a player one yellow from a ban.
5. Under the table you see the cards per team and any team officials' cards. Tap a player's name to open his profile.

## Step by step: pick the squad for the next match
1. Open the upcoming match → **Info** → **Set matchday squad** (or **Edit matchday squad**).
2. A suspended player is marked **🟥 Suspended for this match** with the reason.
3. If you tap **Start** or **Bench** for him, the app asks first. Tap **No, leave out** to keep him out, or **Yes, pick anyway** if you know better (for example, the ban was lifted).
4. **Fill starters** and **Copy last match's squad** skip suspended players.
5. On **Arrange on pitch**, suspended players show 🟥 and "(suspended)" and ask the same question.

> **Tip:** The warning never blocks you. The app only knows the cards scorers logged, so the referee's report and your competition rules have the final say.

## Common questions
### A card was logged against the wrong player. How do I fix it?
Correct the match's timeline. The table and the suspension are worked out again from the corrected match. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Two of our next fixtures are on the list. Which one is he banned for?
The earliest one. Matches still to be played before a fixture count as served for that fixture's warning.

### Does a suspension carry over to another tournament?
No. The table and bans are per tournament.

### Do friendlies or extra time count?
Friendlies outside the tournament never count. Cards shown in extra time count like any other card.
