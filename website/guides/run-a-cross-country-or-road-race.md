In a cross-country or road race everyone starts together, so the finish order decides the places. You tap each runner, or type their bib, as they finish. Times are optional. With team scoring on, the app adds up each house's places, and both the individual and the team results earn house points.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser).
- Create the tournament with **Athletics** as a sport, and add your houses or schools as teams with their runners.

## Step by step: set up the race
1. In the tournament's athletics programme, tap **🌳 ＋ Road race, race walk or cross-country**. You can also tap **＋ Add an event**, then **🌳 Road race, race walk or cross-country →**.
2. Pick **🌳 Cross-country**, **🏃 Road race** or **🚶 Race walk**.
3. Pick the distance. Cross-country suggests one for the age group (3 km for U14 boys, for example). Road races offer 5 km to the marathon. Walks can be **On the track** or **On the road**. For any other distance, type it in km.
4. For a road race or road walk, say whether it is a **Certified, measured course**. For a road race, also choose **Gun time (official)** or **Chip (net) time**.
5. Pick the age group and **Boys**, **Girls** or **Mixed**.
6. Add runners. **＋ All Red House** adds every eligible runner from that house, or pick them one by one. Bibs are numbered by house from **Bibs from** (101 by default).
7. Set up **Team scoring** (on by default for cross-country). Choose the scorers (**First 4** is common in schools), the team size (**Up to 6**) and **How places count**.
8. Tap **Create race & start list**.

## Step by step: record the finish
1. Open **Enter results**. Runners are listed under **STILL ON THE COURSE** with their bibs.
2. As each runner finishes, tap their name, or type their bib in the **Bib → Enter** box and press Enter. The box stays ready for the next bib, and a bib that has already finished is never placed twice.
3. Times are optional. Each runner under **FINISHED** has a time box: type `3412` for 34:12 or `13405` for 1:34:05. Time everyone, or only some runners. Places always come from the finish order.
4. To fix a mistake, use **↑** to move a runner up one place or **✕** to take them off the list.
5. Choose **DNF**, **DNS** or **DQ** and then tap a runner to set their status. Tap them under **NOT CLASSIFIED** to undo it.
6. Tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The confirm lists anyone still without a result and any new meet record.

> **Tip:** With two officials, one calls out bibs while the other types them. It's faster than finding names on a busy finish line.

## Times that look wrong
If a time looks too fast or too slow for the distance and age group, the app asks you to check it. It also asks when a time doesn't fit the finish order. Road times are rounded up to the whole second (World Athletics rule TR 19.24). Track walks keep hundredths.

## Race walks: red cards
Choose **🟥 Red card**, then tap a walker for each red card the chief judge gets for them. Three red cards from different judges is a disqualification (TR 54.7), and the app asks before marking **DQ**. With the **Penalty Zone rule** on, three cards mean time in the penalty zone and the fourth disqualifies. **−🟥** removes a card entered by mistake.

## How team scores work
- Each house's first four finishers (or the number you chose) score their places. The lowest total wins.
- If two houses have the same total, the one whose last scorer finished higher wins. The sheet says which way the tie went.
- A house with too few finishers is listed as incomplete and isn't ranked. Its runners keep their own places.
- **Complete teams only (displace)** gives places only to runners from complete teams. A house's fifth and sixth runners still take places and push other houses' scorers back. Most school cross-country meets use this rule. **Overall places** uses everyone's actual place, and **Scorers only** ignores non-scorers.
- The **🏆 Team score** card shows the sums, for example 2 + 4 + 9 + 10 = 25, with non-scorers in brackets.

## Records and personal bests
- Road and walk times count as personal bests on any course.
- Meet and school records count only on a certified course with gun timing.
- Cross-country keeps no records or time PBs, because courses differ. Profiles show places, team places and team medals instead.

## What everyone sees
The **Results sheet** shows place, bib, name, house and time, with the team table below. **Medals & points** lists the individual and team medals, and both count on the house table. **📤 Share** sends the results and team scores as a message. Athlete profiles show road and cross-country races, PBs per distance, the best cross-country place and team medals.

## Common questions
### Can I add times later?
Yes, at any time before you lock the results. An organiser can also reopen a locked race.

### Can a road race have team scores?
Yes. Turn on **Score teams by placings** when you set up the race.
