Every golf score in the app is worked out from the course: the par of each hole, its stroke index (SI), and each tee's course rating and slope. A typo there makes to-par, net scores, Stableford points and handicap differentials wrong. This guide shows how to add a course, fix it later, add more tees, and decide what happens to rounds already played on it.

## Before you start
- Anyone can add a course while setting up a round.
- Only the person who added a course can edit it. Everyone else can still pick it for their rounds, or add their own copy.
- Keep the scorecard handy. You need the par and stroke index of every hole, and the rating and slope of each tee if you use handicaps.

## Step by step: add a course
1. Start a golf round (**⛳ Set up round 1** in a tournament, or a round with friends).
2. Under **Course**, tap **＋ New course**.
3. Type the course name and, if you like, the city. Pick **18 holes** or **9 holes**.
4. For each hole, tap the par to change it (P3, P4, P5) and type the stroke index under it. Each SI from 1 to 18 (or 1 to 9 on a 9-hole course) must be used exactly once. Any number used twice or out of range turns red.
5. Under **Tees**, the first tee is called "Default". Tap a colour to rename it (for example **White**), and type its **Course rating** and **Slope** if you have them.
6. To add another tee, tap **＋ Add a tee**. Fill in its name, colour, rating and slope. If this tee's par is different (for example the forward tees play a par 5 as a par 4), type it under **Tee par**. Pick **Men** or **Women** if the tee is rated for one of them.
7. Tap **Save course**.

> **Note:** Rating and slope go together. Fill in both or leave both empty. Without them, a player's course handicap is simply their Handicap Index.

## Fix a course or add tees later
1. In round setup, pick the course. Below the course list, tap **✏️ Edit** followed by the course name.
2. Or, during a round you host, tap "Wrong par, stroke index or tee rating? Edit the course" under the scorecard.
3. Change what you need: a par, a stroke index, a tee's rating or slope. You can add tees, rename them and remove them. The number of holes can't change once a course is saved, because cards are already sized to it.
4. Tap **Save course**.

A course made before you had all the tees shows only one tee, so the round setup has no tee choice. Add the other tees here, and the tee buttons appear in round setup.

## What happens to rounds on the course
- Rounds still open (not started, or in play) use the corrected course straight away. To-par, net scores, Stableford points and strokes received are all worked out again. The strokes on each card stay the same.
- Finished rounds keep the scores as recorded. The app keeps a copy of the course as it was when the round finished, so a later edit never changes a result quietly.
- If the mistake affected finished rounds too, tap **Recompute finished rounds** on the edit screen. The app asks first. It then works out those rounds again with the corrected course, including each player's handicap differential. You can only recompute rounds you organise.

> **Important:** Recomputing can change positions in a finished round. Tell the players before you do it.

## Give each player their own tee
When a course has two or more tees, round setup shows a tee button for the round (for example **White tees**), and each player row gets a small tee chip. Tap the chip to move that player to another tee; a grey chip means they play the round's tee.

When players play tees with different pars, the app follows the World Handicap System: each player's course handicap uses their own tee's rating, slope and par, and a player on a higher-par tee gets the difference in par as extra strokes. With one tee for everyone, nothing changes.

## Common questions
### Someone else added our course with a wrong par. What can I do?
Ask them to fix it. Or tap **＋ New course** and add your own correct copy.

### Do players' strokes change when I fix a par?
No. Only what is worked out from them changes: to-par, net, points and differentials.

### What if I rename a tee?
Open rounds you organise that use that tee keep it under the new name.
