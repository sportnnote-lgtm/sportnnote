Timeouts, a level score at the final buzzer and "who led when" are easy to lose track of at a busy court. The app counts timeouts for you, never ends a game level by accident, and builds a game-flow summary from the plays you log.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [How to score a basketball match](/guides/score-basketball/).
- The organiser's **Format** sets the timeout rule. **FIBA (4×10)** and **School (4×8)** use FIBA timeouts, **NBA (4×12)** uses NBA timeouts, and **3×3 (first to 21)** gives each team 1. Under **Custom**, pick **Timeouts**: FIBA, NBA or a set number per game.

## Step by step: timeouts
1. On the **Scoring** tab, find "Timeouts left" above the two timeout buttons, for example "Timeouts left (2nd half): IND 3 · KOR 2".
2. Tap "Timeout — IND" (or the other team) when a timeout is granted. The number in brackets drops by one.
3. If a team has none left, the app asks "No timeouts left" first. Tap **No, keep scoring** to cancel, or **Log anyway** if the officials allowed it.

Under FIBA rules each team gets 2 timeouts in the first half, 3 in the second half and 1 in each overtime. Unused timeouts don't carry over. Once the 4th quarter clock shows 2:00 or less, a team can take at most 2 of its second-half timeouts; the line then adds "last-minutes limit". NBA format: 7 a game, at most 4 in the 4th quarter and 2 in its last 3 minutes, and 2 in each overtime.

> **Note:** The app's clock counts up from each tip-off, so the "last 2 minutes" is read from it. That's why going over asks you instead of blocking.

## Overtime or a draw
Basketball has no draws: a level game plays overtime periods (5 minutes under FIBA) until someone wins.

1. Level at the end of the 4th quarter (or an overtime)? The panel says the score is level and shows "Start Overtime".
2. Tap it, then "▶ Start OT" at the restart. The score carries over.
3. If the organiser turned on **Allow a draw (league / friendly)** in the format, an **End as a draw** button also shows.
4. Without that setting you see "End level, no overtime…". It asks "End the game level?" first; tap **No, go back** to play overtime.

## Game flow
On the **Score** and **Summary** tabs, under the box score, "Game flow" shows:
- **Biggest lead** for each team, with when it happened and the score then.
- **Largest run**: the most points a team scored in a row while the other scored none, such as "10-0".
- **Lead changes** and **Times tied**.
- **Bench points**: points by players who didn't start. It shows once the starting five is known, from **Set five** under **🔀 Substitutions** or the match lineup.

Everything is worked out from the play-by-play, so an edit or undo updates it straight away. Football, hockey, handball and kabaddi matches show the same block on the **Summary** tab. Goal sports show "Most goals in a row" instead of runs and need at least 2 goals.

## Common questions
### A timeout I logged was wrong. How do I remove it?
Tap **Undo**, or tap **Edit** next to **🗓 Correct the timeline** and **✕** on the timeout.

### Why is bench points missing?
The app needs to know who started. Set the five, or fill in the match lineup.
