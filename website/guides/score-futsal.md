Futsal is scored like football, with three differences from the Futsal Laws of the Game: the clock stops whenever the ball is out of play, each team gets one time-out a half, and fouls add up. This guide covers those three. Goals, cards, subs and stats work as in [How to score a football match](/guides/score-football/).

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- The organiser picks **Futsal** as the **Format** when scheduling. It sets 5 a side, 2 halves of 20 minutes, rolling subs, 2 × 5 minutes of extra time, **Stopped clock** and **Futsal time-outs and accumulated fouls**.
- Matches scheduled before this update keep the old running clock.

## Step by step: the stopped clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Kick off**. The clock starts.
2. Every time the ball goes out of play or the referee stops the game, tap **⏸ Stop clock**.
3. When play restarts, tap **▶ Start clock**. While it's stopped, a line says "Clock stopped".
4. If your clock and the timekeeper's differ, tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the match time (for example `13:20`, or `33:20` in the second half) and tap **Set**.
5. At 20:00 the panel says time is up. Finish any kick, then tap **End 1st Half →**. Start the second half with **▶ Start 2nd half**; it counts on from 20:00.

> **Tip:** Every goal, card and foul is stamped with the time on the stopped clock, so minutes played and the timeline match the official sheet even when a half takes 40 real minutes.

## Step by step: time-outs
1. In the clock panel, tap **⏸️ Time-out — Red House (1)** when that team's time-out starts. The number is how many it has left.
2. The clock stops and the time-out goes on the timeline.
3. Tap **▶ Start clock** when play restarts.

Each team gets one time-out in each half and none in extra time. If a team has used its time-out, the app asks before logging another, in case the referee allowed it.

## Step by step: accumulated fouls
1. Log fouls as usual: **🟫 Foul**, the team, the player, then the fouled player or skip.
2. The clock panel shows each team's accumulated fouls this half.
3. When a team commits its 6th foul of a half (and every foul after), a box says the other team gets a direct free kick from the 10 m mark, with no wall.
4. If it's scored, log it with **Goal** → the scorer → **Free kick**. Tap **Got it** to close the box.

Fouls start again from 0 in the second half. Fouls from the second half carry into extra time.

> **Note:** Every foul you log counts towards the total, so log only the fouls the referee gave a free kick or penalty for.

## Common questions
### Does a futsal shoot-out work the same way?
Yes. Pick who kicks first after the toss; sudden death keeps that order. See [Choose who goes first in a shoot-out](/guides/choose-shoot-out-order/).

### Is there added time?
No. With the stopped clock there's no added-time prompt: the half ends when the clock reaches 20:00.

### Can I use the stopped clock for 5s turf or school games?
Yes. The organiser can switch on **Stopped clock** (and the futsal time-outs and fouls) under the format's advanced options for any football match.
