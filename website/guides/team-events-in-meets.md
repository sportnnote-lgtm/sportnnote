Championships have team events next to the individual ones: three archers shooting for their house, a mixed pair in air rifle, four riders in a team pursuit. In SportnNote a team event is its own event with its own medals and records. Every member gets the result on their profile, and the house gets the points.

## Before you start
- You need to be an organiser of the meet, or an official of the event.
- Add your houses as the meet's teams, with their players.
- For archery and shooting teams, add and enter the individual event first. The team score is made from those scores.
- Check **📜 Competition rules** for the sport.

## Step by step
1. Open the meet, tap the sport, then **＋ Add an archery event** (or the shooting, cycling or athletics button).
2. Archery: under **1 · Bow & round**, tap **Team (3)** or **Mixed team (1 + 1)**, then choose the bow, age group and round, the same as the individual event.
3. Shooting: tap the event, then **Team of 3**. For a mixed pair, pick **10 m Air Rifle Mixed Team** or **10 m Air Pistol Mixed Team**.
4. Cycling: tap **Track (velodrome)**, then **Team pursuit** or **Team sprint**.
5. Under **2 · Teams**, check that the individual event is ticked under **Scores from the ranking round** (or **the individual match**). Tap **Fill every house’s team with its best 3** to make a team for each house, or **＋ Add a team from** a house and pick the members seat by seat.
6. Choose the match play or medals option, then tap **Create event & start list**.
7. On the team ranking, the **Team scores** card lists each member's score. Tap **↻ Take the members’ scores from the individual event** after a correction, or tap a member to type a score.
8. Tap **Close team ranking → seed team match play**, score the matches, then **🏁 Finish & lock results**.

## Archery team and mixed team
A team's ranking score is its members' ranking-round scores added together; ties go to more 10s, then more X. The best teams are seeded into a bracket.
- Team of 3: sets of 6 arrows, 2 per archer. The higher set earns 2 set points, a tie 1 each, first to 5. At 4–4 each archer shoots one arrow; the higher total wins, then the arrow closest to the centre.
- Mixed team: a man and a woman, 4 arrows a set, first to 5, a 2-arrow shoot-off.
- Compound teams add up 4 ends instead.

## Shooting teams
- Team of 3: the three shooters' match scores added together, with no final. Ties go to inner tens, then the last series of all three.
- Mixed team: a man and a woman, 30 shots each, entered by series. With 4 or more teams the best 4 go to the **Medal matches**: 1st v 2nd for gold, 3rd v 4th for bronze.
- In a medal match each athlete fires one shot a series. Type both shots per team, such as `10.4 10.2`. The higher total earns 2 points, a tie 1 each, first to 16. At 16–16, a shoot-off of one shot each.

## Cycling team pursuit and team sprint
- Team pursuit: 4 riders, 4000 m (3000 m for school and youth), timed on the third rider. The two fastest teams ride for gold, 3rd and 4th for bronze. A team that is caught loses: tap **Caught**.
- Team sprint: 3 riders over 3 laps, timed on the last rider.
- With fewer than 4 teams there is one round and the times decide.

## Relays
- Athletics: **4 × 200 m relay** is in the relay list as an optional event.
- Mixed relays: choose **Mixed** under **1 · Category**, then the 4 × 400 m or 4 × 100 m relay. A team must be 2 boys and 2 girls in any order, unless your competition rules fix boy, girl, boy, girl.
- Swimming mixed freestyle and medley relays are also 2 boys and 2 girls.

> **Note:** A team's score sets the team record for that event, such as Recurve 30 m team School Boys. It is kept apart from the individual record and is never counted as a member's personal best.

## Common questions
### Where do the team medals show on a profile?
On the sport's page of each member's profile, a 👥 line shows the team events, team medals and team matches won and lost. Team medals are also counted in the medal totals, the same as relay medals.

### A member's individual score was corrected after the team event was made. What now?
Open the team ranking and tap **↻ Take the members’ scores from the individual event**. A score you typed by hand is kept.

### Which rules can I change?
In **📜 Competition rules**: the team bracket size, whether teams are named by each house or filled with each house's best scorers, mixed team medal matches or the total, cycling team rounds, and the mixed relay order. See [Choose the competition rules for a meet](/guides/competition-rules-for-meets/).
