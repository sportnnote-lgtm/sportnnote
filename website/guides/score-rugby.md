Score a rugby union match on one phone: you run the clock and tap what happens; the app adds the points, times sin-bins and builds the stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** (15s, U18, U16, U14, tens, sevens, sevens final or **Custom**). It sets the half length, the sin-bin (10 minutes; 2 in sevens and tens) and the replacements allowed.
- Put each team's starting players on the **Info** tab so everyone gets minutes.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Kick-off**. The clock counts up; the second half starts at 40:00 (7:00 in sevens).
2. Tap **⏸ Stop** and **▶ Resume** whenever the referee stops time.
3. When time is up the clock turns red and keeps going (`40:00 +1:12`): the half ends when the ball is next dead.
4. At half-time tap **⏭ End H1**, then **▶ Start H2** after the break. **🏁 Full time**, at the bottom of the controls, stays off until the second half has started.
5. Clock wrong? Tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the match time (`52:30`) and tap **Set**.

## Step by step: log the scores
1. **🏉 Try** (5 points): tap the scorer (or **Team try (no scorer)**), then the player who gave the scoring pass, or **No assist**.
2. The conversion comes up straight away: tap **✓ Good** (2 points) or **✗ Missed**, then the kicker. If no kick happens, tap **Not taken**.
3. **🎯 Kick at goal**: choose **🎯 Penalty goal (3)** or **🦶 Drop goal (3)**, then **✓ Good** or **✗ Missed**, then the kicker.
4. **🏉 Penalty try**: one tap, 7 points, no conversion. It belongs to the team, not a player.

> **Tip:** Name the kicker every time, misses too: that's what makes goal-kicking % right.

## Cards and the sin-bin
1. Tap **🟨 Card**, then **🟨 Yellow** or **🟥 Red**, then the player.
2. A yellow is the sin-bin, timed on playing time (across half-time too). The banner counts down and shows "Playing 1 short"; the player is back by themselves.
3. A red is off for the rest of the match. A second yellow becomes a red automatically.
4. With **20-minute red card (World Rugby trial)** on, you also get **🟥 20-minute red**: the player can't return, but a replacement may fill the place after 20 minutes. Two yellows then make a 20-minute red.

## Replacements
1. Tap **🔁 Sub** and choose **🔁 Replacement**, **🩺 HIA (temporary)** or **🩸 Blood (temporary)**, then who comes off and who comes on.
2. The panel shows replacements used ("3 of 8"); HIA and blood ones don't count. When the player is cleared, tap **↩ Back on**.
3. With **Rolling replacements (age-grade)**, a replaced player may come back on.

## Extra time, sudden death and kicks
- Leagues keep **Draw stands (league)**. Tournament knockouts get the **Standard knockout**: 15s play 2 × 10 extra time, 10 minutes of sudden death, then a kicking competition; sevens and tens play 5-minute sudden-death periods until someone scores. The organiser can pick another option.
- In sudden death the first score ends the match.
- Kicking competition: pick who kicks first, then tap **✓ Over** or **✗ Missed**: five each, then one each.

## Optional detail
Turn on **Track tackles, carries and set pieces** for a **📋 Detail** button: tackles, missed tackles, carries, line breaks, turnovers won, penalties conceded, lineouts and scrums. Untracked matches never show these as 0.

## Fix a mistake
- **↶ Undo** says what it will remove, for example "Undo Try · Asha Rao (12-7)".
- **✎ Correct the timeline** lists every moment. **✕** removes one after asking. **✎ Edit** re-enters it at its original time, and **Cancel** keeps the original.

## What you get
- A box score (T, C, PG, DG, PTS, YC, RC) per half or match, a timeline and the score by half.
- Careers and leaderboards: points, tries, try assists, conversion % and goal-kicking %, plus Top try scorer and Top points scorer awards.
- Tables use bonus points by default (4 a win, 2 a draw, +1 for 4+ tries, +1 for losing by 7 or fewer; ties on points difference, then tries). Organisers can switch to **Rugby World Cup**, **4-2-0 (no bonus points)** or **Sevens 3-2-1**.
- A walkover counts 28-0 with the try bonus; change it if your competition differs.

## Common questions
### Can I score without running the clock?
Yes, but sin-bins can't time out and minutes played won't be right.

### Does a penalty try count towards the try bonus?
Yes. Penalty tries count as tries in the table, but they don't go on any player's stats.
