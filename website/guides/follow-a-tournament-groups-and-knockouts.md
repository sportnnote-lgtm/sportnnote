In a tournament with groups and a knockout, people ask "How is our group going right now?" and, weeks later, "Where did we finish?" SportnNote keeps every group table and knockout round on the tournament. They update while matches are played and stay there afterwards. Each team also has a journey page: group finish, each knockout round, final place.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can open the tournament can see all of this. You don't need to be an organiser.
- The group tables fill in from match results. Knockout rounds appear once the organiser creates them (see [Settle a level knockout and pick who goes through](/guides/knockouts-and-who-goes-through/)).

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament and go to the **Stats** tab.
2. Tap **📊 Standings & leaders**. Each group has its own table, and the heading tells you where it stands: **Group A · 3 of 6 played** while it is under way, **Group A · live** while a match is being played, and **Group A — final** once the group is done.
3. While a match is live, a red dot appears next to both teams, and a **● Live** line under the table shows the current score. The table refreshes every 30 seconds while you keep the screen open.
4. When a group is final, each team gets a tag: **Q** went through, `Q*` went through as one of the best-placed teams, **E** is out.
5. Tap any team in a table to open its **Tournament journey**.
6. To see the knockout, go back and tap **🏆 Knockout bracket**. Each round shows its progress, such as **2 of 4 decided · 1 live**, and every match shows its score.
7. Tap a name in the bracket to open that team's journey.

## What a group table tells you
- **During the group stage**, positions are provisional, and every team is listed, even before its first result.
- **After the group stage**, the table stays as it finished, headed **Group A — final**. The Q and E tags come from who actually reached the next stage. If the organiser picked a different team to go through, the tags follow that choice.
- **When every group is done but the knockout hasn't been drawn yet**, the tags follow the tournament's saved rule (for example, top two plus the best third-placed team). A note says so, because the organiser still makes the draw.
- A **Super Four** (or other Super round) gets its own table that works the same way.

## What the knockout view tells you
- Every round to the final, plus the third-place playoff, the double-chance playoff, double-elimination rounds and the plate.
- **Live** matches have a red outline. Decided matches show the winner with a tick.
- **How a level match was settled** appears in orange under the score, in the sport's own words. For example: "Riverside win 4–3 on pens", "Hilltop win 3–2 in the shoot-out", "Owls won the Super Over by 4 runs", "1½–½ · Bina through (Armageddon)", "After overtime (OT1)", or "Golden Raid".
- **Two-legged ties** show the aggregate and each leg. **Team ties** (table tennis, badminton, chess and others) show rubbers won and each rubber.
- An **organiser decision** shows who advanced and the reason.

## A team's journey
The **Tournament journey** page shows, from top to bottom:
- The team's final place: **Champions**, **Runners-up**, **3rd**, **Joint 3rd**, **=5th** and so on, or **Still in it** while it's playing. **🍽️ Plate winner** is shown separately because the plate gives no medal.
- A one-line summary, such as "Group A 1st → QF won → SF won → Final lost → Runners-up".
- The group card, for example "1st of 4 · 7 pts from 3", with the Q or E tag. Tap it to open the table.
- One card per knockout round: the opponent, **Won**, **Lost**, **● Live** or **Next** (with the date), the score from this team's side, each leg or rubber, and how it was decided. Tap a card to open the match. Tap the opponent's name to see their journey.

In a multi-sport tournament, chips at the top switch between sports.

You can also reach journeys from two other places:
- **Team page**: a **🧭 Tournaments** section lists each tournament the team played in and how far it got.
- **Player profile**: open a sport on the profile. The **🧭 Tournaments** section shows how far the player's side went in each tournament.

> **Note:** The medal table on the tournament stays empty for a sport with groups and a knockout until the knockout starts, because the knockout decides the medals. Group exits are placed after that. See [Run a sports-day medal table](/guides/multi-sport-medal-table/).

## Common questions
### Do I need to refresh?
No. Standings, the bracket and the journey page update every 30 seconds while a match in that sport is live and the screen is open.

### Our group is finished but there are no Q or E tags. Why?
The tags appear once the knockout has started, or once every group is done and the tournament has a saved "top N go through" rule. Until then, nobody's place in the knockout is certain.

### Why does a team show E when it was in the top two?
The organiser decided who went through (after a withdrawal, say). The tags follow who actually reached the next stage.
