Most school and club golf team events work the same way: each team sends a few players, everyone plays their own ball, and only the team's best scores count. For example, four players play and the best three scores make the team total. The worst score is dropped. This guide shows how to set that up, how the team leaderboard works, and how ties are split. Players still appear on the normal individual leaderboard too.

## Before you start
- You need to be the organiser of the round (or a host of the tournament).
- Decide how many scores count, for example best 3 of 4.
- Decide whether scores count per round (the usual school format) or per hole (best ball).
- If you play net, enter each player's Handicap Index when you add them.

## Step by step: set up a team round
1. Set up the round as usual: **⛳ Set up round 1** in a tournament, or a new golf round. Pick the course, tees and format, and add the players.
2. Tap **👥 Team competition (best N of M)**. It turns into **✓ Team competition**.
3. Under **Best**, tap how many scores count (1 to 5).
4. Tap **Per round** or **Per hole (best ball)**.
5. In stroke play, tap **Gross** or **Net**. Stableford teams always add points.
6. Under **Teams in this round**, tap the teams taking part. To add one, type its name, for example `Green Valley School`, and tap **＋ Add**.
7. Each player now has a team chip next to their Handicap Index. Tap it to cycle through the teams until it shows the right one. **No team** means the player plays for themselves only.
8. Tap **⛳ Create round**. If a team has fewer players than the scores that count, the app tells you before it creates the round.

> **Tip:** The setup for the next round carries the team rule and every player's team over, so you only set it up once.

## Reading the team leaderboard
In a round, open **🏆 Leaderboard** and tap **👥 Teams** (tap **🏌️ Players** to go back). In a tournament, the **👥 Team leaderboard** sits under the individual leaderboard and adds up every round.

- The line at the top repeats the rule, for example "Best 3 of 4 scores each round · gross".
- **Total** is the team's score to par (or points in Stableford), **Thru** shows **F** when the counting cards are finished, and **R1**, **R2** show each round in a tournament.
- Tap a team to see its players. A tick means the score counts. A struck-through score was dropped this round.
- In best ball, the best scores on every hole count, so no whole card is dropped.

While cards are still being played, the team total counts the holes played so far, just like the individual leaderboard.

## How ties are split
When two teams finish level, the app compares:
1. The non-counting score of the final round (the better dropped score wins), then the rounds before it.
2. Then the counting cards over the last 9, 6, 3 and 1 holes of the final round.
3. If they are still level, they share the place, shown as T2.

While any of the tied cards is still on the course, the teams stay tied.

## When a team has no score
A team needs at least the number of scores that count. If a player withdraws, is disqualified or picks up in stroke play, and the team is left with too few scores, it is listed below the ranked teams with "Fewer than 3 scores". In best ball, a hole counts only when the team has enough scores on that hole.

## Common questions
### Can a player be in a team and still win the individual prize?
Yes. Team scoring never changes the individual leaderboard, Best Gross or Best Net.

### Can I change a player's team after the round is created?
Not yet. Set teams before you tap **⛳ Create round**. For the next round, change them in that round's setup.
