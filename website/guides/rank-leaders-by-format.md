A school's football season can mix 11-a-side league games, 7-a-side cup ties and a futsal week. Adding all those goals together on one leaderboard isn't fair to anyone. Format chips let you rank one kind of match at a time: every leader, every rate and every award suggestion is worked out again for just those matches.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can open the tournament can use the chips. Nothing is saved; switching only changes what you see.
- The chips only appear when the matches really differ. If every match was 11-a-side, there is no **Format** chip.

## Which chips each sport has
- **Cricket**: **Format** (T20, ODI, T10, Hundred, Box, Long) and **Ball** (Leather or Tennis ball).
- **Football**: **Format**: 11-a-side, 7-a-side, 5-a-side and **Futsal** (a match played with futsal rules).
- **Hockey**: **Format**: 11-a-side, **Hockey5s** or **Indoor 6-a-side**.
- **Handball**: **Format** by the length of a half, such as 2 × 30 min or 2 × 25 min.
- **Basketball**: **Format**: 5-on-5 or **3x3**.
- **Volleyball**: **Format**: **Indoor**, **Beach** or **9-a-side**.
- **Kabaddi**: **Format** by the rule set the organiser chose.
- **Tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, padel and pickleball**: **Singles/Doubles** and **Format** (best of 3, best of 5 and so on).
- **Carrom**: **Singles/Doubles**.
- **Chess**: **Time control**: classical, rapid, blitz or bullet.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** tab, or one sport's own page inside the tournament.
2. Above the leaderboards, tap a chip such as **Format: All ▾**. Its values appear underneath, each with how many player lines it covers, for example **7-a-side · 24**.
3. Tap a value. The leaderboards now rank only those matches, and a line says "Leaders from 7-a-side matches only."
4. To go back, tap **All** under the chip, or **✕ Clear**.

On the tournament's **Awards** tab the same chips sit above the suggestions. Pick a format and the suggestions and rankings use only those matches; the award line names the format, for example "Futsal".

> **Tip:** In a multi-sport meet, the chips only filter the sport you are looking at. The other sports' leaders stay as they are.

## Common questions
### Why don't I see any chips?
All the matches so far share one format, so there is nothing to split. The chips appear on their own once a second format is played.

### Does this change the points table?
No. Tables, results and medals are never filtered. Only the leaderboards and the award rankings follow the chips.

### Can I filter one player's career the same way?
Yes, with more choices such as season and opponent. See [Read and filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).
