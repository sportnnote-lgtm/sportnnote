Leaders add things up over a tournament: most runs, most goals. Records are different. They are the best things that happened in a single match, such as the highest team total, the biggest partnership, the fastest goal or the longest tiebreak. SportnNote saves each match's records when the match finishes, and the tournament's **Stats** tab ranks them across every finished match.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can open the tournament can read its records. There is nothing to set up.
- Records come from finished matches that were scored live in the app. A match closed by hand (a walkover, an abandoned or conceded match) sets no records, because it has no full scoring to read them from.
- Golf, athletics, swimming and the other timed or measured events keep their own records on the sport's results page, not here.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament and tap the **Stats** tab.
2. If the tournament has more than one sport, tap the sport's chip under **📊 By sport**, for example **🏏 Cricket**.
3. Scroll past the table and the leaders to **📜 Cricket records** (the heading names the sport you picked).
4. Tap a record type in the chip row, for example **Highest partnerships**. Swipe the row sideways to see all of them.
5. Read the top 5. Each row shows who set it, their team and opponent, and the figure on the right.
6. Tap a row to open that match and see the full scorecard or timeline.

> **Tip:** When two records are level, the one set first stays above. The later match has to beat it, not just equal it.

## What each sport records
- **Cricket**: **Highest team totals**, such as 187/5 (20 ov). **Lowest team totals**, which only counts an innings that ran its course: the side batting first, or a chase that fell short. A winning chase isn't a low total. **Highest partnerships**, with both batters, the balls and the wicket, for example 64 (41), 3rd wkt; an asterisk means unbroken. **Best individual innings**, such as 72* (48), and **Best bowling figures**, such as 4/21. **Biggest wins (runs)** and **Biggest wins (wickets)**, with the balls left. A Super Over never counts.
- **Football and hockey**: **Biggest wins**, **Most goals in a match**, **Fastest goal** (the first goal of each match, by the minute; own goals don't count as anyone's) and **Hat-tricks**. Shoot-out goals don't count.
- **Handball**: as football, but the player record is **Most goals by a player**, because three goals is routine in handball.
- **Basketball**: **Highest team scores**, **Biggest wins**, **Most points by a player**, **30-point games** (40+ is marked) and **Triple-doubles**: 10 or more in three of points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, from what the scorer logged.
- **Kabaddi**: **Highest team scores**, **Biggest wins**, **Most raid points in a match**, **Super 10s** (10+ raid points) and **High 5s** (5+ tackle points). A shoot-out decides the match, but it isn't counted in the margin.
- **Volleyball and the racket sports**: **Longest matches**, in sets or games and then by points or games inside them. **Longest matches (time)**, only for matches whose points were timed on the scorer's phone. **Comebacks**, where the winner came from behind, such as 3–2 from 0-2 down. **Longest tiebreaks** in tennis and padel. **Longest sets** or **Longest games** by points in the other sports, such as 30-28.
- **Chess**: **Fastest wins (moves)**, when the scorer entered the number of moves. A forfeit has no moves, so it doesn't count.

## Example
At the Greenfield Inter-House Meet, Kavya Rao and Arjun Menon put on 58 for the 2nd wicket for Teal House. That sits at the top of **Highest partnerships**. In the next round, Rohan Das and Ishita Nair add 58 for Coral House. Their stand goes second, because Kavya and Arjun got there first. Tapping either row opens that match's scorecard.

## Common questions
### A record changed after the match. Why?
Records are worked out again whenever the finished match is corrected. If the scorer fixes a ball or a goal, the records follow. If the scorer undoes the last action after full time, the match is open again and its records are removed until it finishes.

### Why don't I see a partnership from an older match?
Records are saved at the end of each match. For matches finished before records existed, most sports work them out from the saved result. Cricket partnerships need the ball-by-ball log, so they appear once SportnNote has refreshed those older matches.

### Why is a record type missing?
The chip row only shows types that have at least one record. If no one has scored a hat-trick yet, **Hat-tricks** isn't listed.

### Where are a single team's records?
On the team page. See [Read a team's stats and records](/guides/team-stats-and-records/).
