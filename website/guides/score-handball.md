You score a handball match on one phone. You run the clock and tap what happens; the app keeps the score per half, times suspensions, counts time-outs and builds the box score and player stats.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** (**Senior 2 × 30**, **Youth 2 × 25 (12–16)**, **Youth 2 × 20 (8–12)** or **Custom**) and **If level at full time**: **Draw stands (league)**, or one of the extra-time / 7 m throw options a knockout needs.
- Place each team's players on the court on the **Info** tab, keeper in the GK spot, so everyone gets minutes played and the keeper gets saves. No line-up? Use **🧤 Set keeper**.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Throw-off**. The clock counts up, like a handball scoreboard: the second half starts at 30:00.
2. Tap **⏸ Stop** and **▶ Resume** when the referees stop and restart time.
3. At half-time tap **⏭ End H1**, then **▶ Start H2** after the break. **🏁 Full time**, at the bottom of the controls, stays off until the second half has started.
4. Clock wrong, or you joined late? Tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the match time (for example `41:30`) and tap **Set**.

> **Tip:** With extra time on and the score level, the second half ends with **⏭ End H2**, not Full time, and play goes on to **ET1** and **ET2**.

## Step by step: log the play
1. **🤾 Goal**: choose **6 m**, **9 m**, **Wing**, **Breakthrough** or **Fast break**, tap the scorer (or **Team goal (no scorer)**), then the assist or **No assist**.
2. **🎯 7 m**: a 7-metre throw. Tap **✓ Scored** (then the scorer), **🧤 Saved** or **↗ Missed** (then the thrower). A save goes to the other team's keeper as a 7 m save.
3. **↗ Shot**: an attempt that didn't score. Choose the type, then **🧤 Saved**, **↗ Missed** or **🛡 Blocked**, then the shooter (and for a block, the defender).
4. **⚠️ Turnover**: tap **⚠️ Technical fault** or **🖐 Ball stolen**, then the player. For a stolen ball, also tap the defender who stole it.
5. **🔁 Sub**: tap who comes off, then who comes on. If the keeper comes off, the player coming on goes in goal.

## Time-outs
Tap **⏸ Time-out** for the team that asks. The clock stops and the button shows how many are left. The IHF limits apply: 3 a match, at most 2 a half, only 1 in the last 5 minutes, none in extra time. When a team can't take one, the button is off and a line says why.

## Sanctions
1. Tap **🟨 Card**, then **🟨 Warning**, **⏱ 2 minutes**, **🟥 Disqualify** or **🟥🟦 + report** (a disqualification with a written report, the blue card), then the player. Bench players can be sanctioned too.
2. 2 minutes stops the clock and shows "⏱️ Suspended: Name (1:24 left)". The team plays a player short; the countdown runs on playing time and the player is back by himself. A player's third suspension is also a disqualification.
3. A disqualified player is out for the match and the team plays short for 2 minutes. Then tap **🔁 Sub** → **No one — fill the empty place** and pick who comes on.
4. One warning per player: a second one is recorded as 2 minutes.
5. For a team official, type the name under **Or a team official** (optional) and tap **Record**. An official's 2 minutes or disqualification also leaves the team short for 2 minutes.

## Extra time and 7-metre throws
When it is still level and there is no more extra time, you see "Level — the match goes to 7-metre throws".
1. Tap which team throws first.
2. For each throw, tap the thrower (optional), then **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed / saved**. "Next:" tells you whose turn it is.
3. Still level after five each? The teams keep taking one throw each until one team leads.

The result keeps the score after playing time and adds the throws, for example "28–28" and "SO 4–3". Shoot-out goals aren't counted as goals in player stats.

## Fix a mistake
1. Tap **✎ Correct the timeline** under the buttons.
2. Tap **✕** to remove a moment (it asks first), or **✎ Edit** to enter it again at its time. **▶ Back to live** or Cancel keeps it as it was.
3. After the match, use [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## What you get
- **Score** tab: the timeline, team stats (shooting %, goals by type, 7 m, saves, steals, blocks, technical faults, time-outs, sanctions) and each player's line.
- Player profiles: goals per game, shooting %, 7 m %, goals by type, keeper save %, discipline.
- Tournament tables use **IHF 2-1-0** (ties: head-to-head points, then head-to-head goal difference and goals, then overall), or **Simple 2-1-0**. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Common questions

### Can I score without running the clock?
Mostly, but moments are stamped at the start of the half and suspensions can't be timed. Tap **▶ Throw-off** (or **▶ Start H2**) once per half anyway: **⏭ End** and **🏁 Full time** unlock only then.
