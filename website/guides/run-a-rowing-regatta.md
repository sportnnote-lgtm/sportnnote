A regatta is a list of races, such as the 1x 2000 m Senior Men. Each race is a straight final, or heats, a repechage and Finals A and B, as World Rowing runs it. You type finish times on a phone, and the app sends each crew to its next race and credits every rower and the cox.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser) to add races and enter times.
- Create the tournament with **Rowing** as a sport. Under **Rowing basics**, pick the **Lanes** on your course (6 by default), the position points and whether you use **Hand timing** (stopwatches, no photo finish).
- Add your houses or schools as teams (**Manage** → **Participating teams**). Crews are picked from their members and score for them.

## Step by step: add a race
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Rowing — events**, and tap **＋ Add a race**.
2. **Boat & distance:** pick the boat class (1x, 2x, 2-, 4x, 4-, 4+, 8+) and the distance (2000 m for championships; 1500, 1000 or 500 m for school or masters racing).
3. Pick the age group, **Men** or **Women** (crew boats can be **Mixed**), and the class: **Open**, **Lightweight** or para **PR1**, **PR2**, **PR3**. Their records stay apart.
4. **Scullers** (a single): tap a house or search a name, then tap each sculler.
5. **Crews** (any other boat): under **＋ Add a crew from**, tap a house. The crew is named after it (Red House A, Red House B). Tap a seat (**Bow**, **2**, **3**, **Stroke**, **Cox**), then a player. **Fill empty seats** fills the rest from that house. Tap a filled seat to clear it.
6. **Lanes & progression:** check the lanes, then pick a progression. The line below spells out where every place goes.
7. Tap **Create race & draw lanes**. Heats and their lanes are drawn.

## The progressions
- Up to one course's worth of crews: a **Straight final**.
- 7 to 12 crews on 6 lanes, **Repechage → Finals A/B**: each heat winner goes straight to Final A. Everyone else races a repechage. The best of the repechage fill Final A and the next go to Final B. With 9 crews that is 2 heats, then 2 repechages (1st and 2nd to Final A, 3rd and 4th to Final B).
- 13 or more crews, **Repechage → semis → Finals**: heat winners go to the semi-finals, the repechages fill them, and the rest race Final C. Semi-final places 1 to 3 go to Final A and 4 to 6 to Final B.
- **Finals A/B, no repechage**: top places of each heat to Final A, the next to Final B.
- **Custom rule:** choose the number of heats, how many per heat go straight to Final A, and whether the rest race a repechage and a Final B.
- From the second round on, the best-ranked crews get the centre lanes: lane 3, then 4, 2, 5, 1, 6.

## Step by step: enter times
1. Open the race and tap **Enter results**. Use the **Heat 1**, **Repechage 2** or **Final B** chips to switch races.
2. Type each time with the number keys: 72345 is 7:23.45. Tap **Next ›** to save and move to the next lane.
3. A time far outside the usual range, such as 1:23.45 for a 1x 2000 m, asks **Check this mark** first.
4. Photo finish: tap **.000** and type thousandths (722104 is 7:22.104). The time shows to 1/100, and the thousandths decide the order.
5. Tap **DNS**, **DNF** or **DQ** for a crew that didn't start, didn't finish or was excluded. Type the reason in **Rule**, for example "Excluded — 2nd false start".
6. Each crew's card shows where it goes, such as **→ Final A**, **→ Repechage** or **out**.
7. A dead heat across a qualifying line sends both crews on. To separate them after a re-row (or a draw), enter each crew's **Re-row / draw place**.
8. Turn on **500 m splits** when you create the race to record each crew's time at 500, 1000 and 1500 m.
9. Tap **Close heats → seed the repechage**. Heat winners show as **Already through** on the repechage.
10. Enter Final A and Final B, then tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. An organiser can reopen a round until the next one has a time, or reopen the final (its records roll back).

## What everyone sees
- **Finals A/B:** places run on. Final A decides the medals and Final B's winner is 7th after a six-boat Final A, even with a faster time.
- **House table, Fastest by event and Meet records:** one record per boat class, distance and category, set by the first final of each.
- **Rower profiles:** races, A finals, medals, the best time per boat class and distance, and the seat raced.

## Common questions
### Why did a faster Final B crew finish behind Final A?
World Rowing ranks the finals in order. A crew can only place within the final it rowed in.

### Can a cox set a best time?
Yes. The cox is credited with the crew's time in that boat class, and the profile shows "Coxed 1 race".
