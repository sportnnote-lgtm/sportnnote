When a team doesn't turn up, the other side wins. But goal or point difference decides many tables, so the score of that walkover matters too. Sports federations award a fixed score for a walkover. The app counts that score for the team, and you choose it for your tournament when you set it up.

## Before you start
- Only hosts of the tournament can change the walkover score or recalculate past walkovers.
- A friendly (a match outside any tournament) always uses the federation score below. There is nothing to set.
- To record a walkover or a conceded match, see [End a match early or record a walkover](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## The scores the app uses
- **Football:** 3-0 (FIFA).
- **Basketball:** 20-0 (FIBA rules, Art. 20). A team that concedes during play loses by default: the score stands if the winner was ahead, otherwise it is 2-0 (Art. 21).
- **Volleyball:** the winner gets every set it needed at the set target to 0. For a best-of-5 match that is 3-0 in sets and 25-0 in each set (FIVB rules 6.4). A team that concedes during play keeps the sets and points it won. The other side is given the points it needed to finish the set and the remaining sets.
- **Hockey:** 5-0. **Handball:** 10-0. These are common in competition regulations but check yours.
- **Kabaddi** and other sports: no score by default. You can set one.

For football, hockey, handball and basketball, a match conceded or awarded during play keeps its score if the winner's margin was already bigger. For example, a team leading 4-1 when the other side walks off keeps 4-1, not 3-0.

> **Note:** The match card still shows **W/O**, or the score that was played. The walkover score counts for the team only: the points table, the team page (goals for and against, records, head-to-head) and two-legged ties. It never counts for any player.

## Step by step: set it when you create a tournament
1. Tap **🏆 New tournament** and pick your sports under **Sports**.
2. Under the sport basics, the **Walkover scores — confirm for each sport** card lists each team sport with its federation score already picked.
3. Keep it, tap another score, or tap **No score**.
4. Finish the form and tap **Create tournament**.

If you add a team sport to a tournament later, the same card shows for that sport: open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → **Edit details**.

## Step by step: change the walkover score later
1. Open the tournament → **⚙ Manage** → your sport's **format & points** row.
2. Scroll to **Walkover / forfeit score**. The line under the heading says the score in use.
3. Pick the federation chip (for example **3-0 (FIFA)**), another score, or **No score** to add nothing.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Step by step: recalculate past walkovers
Walkovers recorded before this feature counted 0-0, and they stay that way until you choose to update them.
1. Save the score you want first (see above).
2. On the same page, tap **Recalculate past walkovers**.
3. The check sheet explains what changes. Tap **Yes, recalculate**.
4. A message says how many results now count the new score.

> **Tip:** Results and winners never change, only the score the table counts. You can switch the setting back and recalculate again.

## Points for the losing side
In the FIBA points system a team that forfeits gets 0 points instead of the usual 1 for a loss. The FIVB volleyball preset also stores 0 for a forfeit. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Common questions
### Does the walkover score count in a tennis or badminton round robin?
Not by default. A racket walkover is recorded as "w/o" with no score, as in ITF and BWF tournaments.

### Where do I see walkovers on a team page?
In the team's stats, next to goals for and against: **Walkovers won** and **Walkovers given**. They follow the tournament and season chips. See [Read a team's stats and records](/guides/team-stats-and-records/).

### Does the walkover score count in a two-legged tie?
Yes. A leg won by walkover adds its score to the aggregate, for example 3-0 in football.

### What about chess?
Chess forfeits already follow FIDE rules: the point counts, but the game is unplayed for tie-breaks.

### We play best of 3 sets in volleyball. Is the walkover still 3-0?
No. The app reads your format, so a best-of-3 walkover is 2-0 in sets and 25-0 in each set (21-0 in beach volleyball).
