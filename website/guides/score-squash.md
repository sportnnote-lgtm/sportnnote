In PAR (point-a-rally) squash every rally is a point. In English scoring only the server scores; a lost serve is a hand-out. You tap each rally's winner.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- **Format**: **PSA (PAR 11 · best of 5)**, **American (PARS 15)**, **Club English (to 9)**, **Short (PAR 11 · best of 3)** or **Custom**.
- PAR 11 games need a two-point lead (12-10). **Club English (to 9)** is first to 9.
- **⚙ Customize this format** sets **Scoring** (**PAR (point-a-rally)** or **English (hand-out)**), **Points to win** and **Win by**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**.

## Step by step: PAR scoring
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the side that won the spin. Nothing is picked at the start; the point buttons stay grey with "Pick who serves first to start scoring." until you choose.
3. The serving line names the server, and their button shows a **SERVING** tag.
4. After each rally, tap the big "Point" button of the side that won it, in its team colour (singles: named for the player).
5. Singles credits the player automatically. In doubles the tap scores for the pair; to credit one player (optional), tap **credit a player** or long-press a side, then pick the name (**Cancel** closes it).
6. The rally winner serves next; a game's winner serves first in the next.

Wrong first server? After the first rally, tap **Fix who served first** and pick the right side. Only the serve changes; the score stays, and serve stats re-work themselves.

## Step by step: English scoring
1. On the **Scoring** tab, the top box says "English scoring" and shows the server.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the first server. The buttons wait until you do.
3. After each rally, tap the winning side's **Rally won** button.
4. Server won: a point, credited to the server automatically, and they keep serving. Receiver won: a hand-out; the serve passes and nobody scores.

The header gives the call, server first, e.g. "Game 1 · English · 5-3". There is no **Fix who served first** link here, because the server decides who can score; fix a wrong first server through **Correct the timeline**.

## Reading the board
GAMES won and a points column per game, with a dot by the serving side. The result reads like "3–1 · 11-7, 9-11, 11-5, 11-8".

A chip under the board shows **GAME POINT** or **MATCH POINT** with the side's name. In English scoring only the server can have one; in PAR both sides can show one at once.

## Match stats
The **Score** tab's "Match stats" show points won on serve and receive (PAR), or hand-outs won and points per service turn (English), plus streaks and game or match points saved.

## Point detail (optional)
Off by default. Turn it on with **On** in the "🔎 Point detail (optional)" row under the buttons, or the **Point detail** toggle in "⚙️ Scoring settings".

After each rally, "How was it won? · Asha Rao" appears. Tap a chip or **Skip**; nothing is required.
- **Winner** + **Drive**, **Drop**, **Boast**, **Volley**, **Nick**.
- **Forced error**, **Unforced error** + **Tin**, **Out**, or **Service fault**.
- Referee decisions: **Stroke** and **No let** (appeal refused).

Winners and strokes count for the rally winner; errors and no lets against the opponent (doubles: pick who under **By:**).

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? The Undo bar names what it removes, e.g. "↶ Undo: point to Asha Rao (7-5)", "Undo: rally to Asha Rao · hand-out" or "Undo: first-server pick". Each tap undoes one step.
- Older rally: tap **Edit** next to **Correct the timeline** on the **Scoring** tab. English scoring lists hand-outs too.
1. Tap ✎ to change the winner, player or detail. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to remove a rally; the app asks you to confirm first.
3. To add a missed rally, tap ＋ on the rally before it. **Insert a missed point** starts with no side picked, and **Save** stays off until you choose one.
4. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match; in English scoring a fix can turn a point into a hand-out, and later serves follow. After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends itself when a player wins enough games. Otherwise tap **🏁 End match…** → **Retired** or **Default**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/). Conduct penalties, the **🔁 Let** button and match time: see [Code violations, cards and timeouts](/guides/racket-conduct-and-timeouts/).

## What goes on the player's profile
The player's squash page adds **W-L**, **Win %**, **Match play**, **Serve & return** (singles only) and **Shot making** (from point-detail matches). Doubles players get **Partners**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Why didn't the score change?
In English scoring the receiver won the rally: a hand-out. Only the server scores.
