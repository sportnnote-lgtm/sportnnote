When the umpire gives a warning, a card or a penalty, or a player calls a timeout, the app records it. When a penalty costs a point or a game, it changes the score for you, and it suggests the next penalty from the sport's own rules.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- Everything here is in **☰ Quick options** on the **Scoring** tab, not next to the point buttons, so you can't tap it by accident.

## Step by step: give a code violation or card
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab → **☰ Quick options**.
2. Tap the conduct tile. It is **Code violation** in tennis and padel, **Cards** in table tennis, **Misconduct** in badminton, **Conduct** in squash and **Technical** in pickleball.
3. Tap the side that offended. In doubles you can also tap the player (optional).
4. The app shows its suggestion in bold, for example "2nd code violation → point penalty (1 so far)". That level is already picked; tap another one if the umpire gave something else.
5. Tap a reason if you like, for example **Racket abuse**.
6. Read the line above the button, for example "Point to Rafael Nadal.", then tap **Apply: Point penalty**.

The violation goes into the point log, for example "Code violation · point penalty · Racket abuse". The Undo bar names it too: "↶ Undo: code violation · point penalty · Roger Federer (30-0)".

## What each sport's schedule suggests
- **Tennis** (ITF Point Penalty Schedule): **Warning**, then **Point penalty**, then **Game penalty** for the 3rd violation and every one after that. A **Default** is the Referee's call.
- **Padel** (FIP): **Warning**, then **Point penalty**, then **Game penalty**. **Disqualification** is also available.
- **Table tennis** (ITTF 3.5.2): **Yellow card · warning**, then **Yellow + red · 1 point**, then **Yellow + red · 2 points**, then **Referred to the referee**. Cards are counted per player.
- **Badminton** (BWF Law 16): **Warning · yellow card**, then **Fault · red card**, which gives the rally and the serve to the opponent. **Disqualified · black card** is the Referee's decision.
- **Squash** (WSF Rule 15): **Conduct warning**, **Conduct stroke**, **Conduct game** and **Conduct match**. The referee can give any of them, and the suggestion steps up one level each time.
- **Pickleball** (USA Pickleball 13.G): **Technical warning**, then **Technical foul · point**, then **Forfeit · ejection**.

A point penalty is scored for the opponent like a normal point, so games, sets and the match follow. In pickleball side-out scoring, the point goes on the opponent's score without a side-out. A game penalty awards the game in play; if it happens during a tiebreak, the opponent wins the tiebreak and the set.

## Tennis time violations
Tap **Time violation** in Quick options. The first is a **Warning**. After that, the app suggests **Fault · loss of serve** if the offender is serving, or **Point penalty** if they are receiving. A fault doesn't change the score. If it came on the 2nd serve, also tap the **Double fault** button. Time violations are counted separately from code violations.

## Default or disqualification
Pick **Default** (or **Disqualified**, **Conduct match**, **Forfeit**) and tap the red **Apply** button. The app asks "Default — Roger Federer?" with a green **No, keep playing** and a red **Yes, end the match**. Tap Yes and the match closes with the other side winning by default, the same as **🏁 End match…** → **Default**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/).

## Timeouts and breaks
1. In **☰ Quick options**, tap **Timeout**, **Medical timeout** or **Toilet break**.
2. Tap the side. For a medical timeout, you can also tap the player.
3. Tap **Start timeout** (or **Start medical timeout**, **Start toilet break**).

- **Table tennis**: 1 timeout per player or pair per match (ITTF 3.4.4.3). The chip shows "1 left" or "none left".
- **Pickleball**: 2 timeouts per game, or 3 in a game to 21, and 1 medical timeout per match.
- **Tennis**: medical timeout and toilet break markers.
- **Badminton** has no timeouts. The only breaks are the intervals at 11 and between games, which the app already shows.

Timeouts never change the score.

## Squash: a let
When the referee calls a let, tap **🔁 Let — replay the rally (no point)** under the point buttons. The log shows "Let · rally replayed · no point". For a stroke or no let, turn on point detail and tap **Stroke** or **No let** after the point.

## How long the match took
On the **Score** tab, a line above the box score shows the time, for example "⏱ 1 h 05 min · G1 21 min · G2 18 min" (sets in tennis and padel). When the match ends, the time is added to the result line. It is worked out from when you tapped each point, so it shows only for matches scored with this version.

## Common questions
### I gave the wrong penalty. How do I fix it?
Tap Undo straight away. If it was a while ago, open **Correct the timeline** and remove the penalty point there. Warnings and timeouts stay where they were in the log.

### Do penalty points count in serve stats?
No. They count in the score but not as served points, aces or breaks.
