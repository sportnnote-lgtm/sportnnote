In a combined event each athlete does several events, such as hurdles, high jump, shot, long jump and 800 m. Every mark becomes points on the World Athletics Scoring Tables for Combined Events, and the highest total wins. You enter each event on the usual race or jump screen; the app adds the points, keeps a running total with places and seeds the next event.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser).
- Create the tournament with **Athletics** as a sport, and add your houses or schools as teams with their athletes.
- Each combined event is for one age group, boys or girls. Boys use the men's tables and girls the women's, at every age.

## Step by step: set up the event
1. In the tournament's athletics programme, tap **🏅 ＋ Combined event (decathlon, heptathlon, pentathlon)**. You can also tap **＋ Add an event**, then **🏅 Combined event (decathlon, heptathlon, pentathlon) →**.
2. Pick the age group, then **Boys / Men** or **Girls / Women**.
3. Pick the event: **Decathlon** (10 events over two days; women's decathlon has its own order), **Heptathlon** (girls, 7 events over two days), **Indoor heptathlon** or **Indoor pentathlon**, or a school preset: **Pentathlon (school)** or **Tetrathlon (school)**.
4. Check the order. To use your board's or your house's own list, tap events under **Add / remove events**, or tap **✎ House's own list** and start from nothing. Use **↑** to move an event earlier and **✕** to drop it. Only events that the scoring tables cover can be picked.
5. Under **Days**, keep **One day** or choose where day 2 starts.
6. Check the throws' implements (World Athletics weights from U18; school weights below, which you can change). Turn on **Hand-timed meet (stopwatches)** or **No wind gauge** if that is your meet.
7. Pick the athletes and tap **Create … & first start list**.

> **Note:** A non-standard list keeps its own records and personal bests.

## Step by step: run each event
1. The first event opens on the usual results screen. Races have heats and lanes, jumps and throws have attempt cards, and the high jump has the bar.
2. Enter marks as you normally would. Each athlete's points show under their place, and **Standings after …** below the live ranking shows this event's points and everyone's total.
3. When the event is done, tap **Close 1. 100 m hurdles → start 2. High jump** (the names follow your list). Everyone still in goes on, seeded by their total so the leaders compete together.
4. After the last event, tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The totals give the places, medals and house points, and a meet record is set if the total beats it.
5. **Results sheet** shows every athlete's marks, points and total. **📤 Share** sends the event's results and the standings.

## The rules the app follows
- **Three trials only** in the jumps and throws.
- **Bar raises:** 3 cm each time in the high jump, 10 cm in the pole vault. The heights are filled in for you.
- **False starts:** each race may have one false start. The first time you tap **FS**, the app asks you to warn the athlete and restart the race. Any further false start in that race disqualifies the athlete who makes it, and that event scores 0 points.
- **Didn't start (DNS):** an athlete who doesn't start an event is out of the competition. They don't appear in later events and finish as **DNF** with no place.
- **DNF, no mark or DQ in one event:** that event scores 0 (for example no height cleared, or three fouls) and the athlete carries on.
- **Hand times** are converted before scoring: add 0.24 s for races up to 200 m and the sprint hurdles, and 0.14 s for the 400 m.
- **Ties on total:** the athlete who scored more points than the other in more events is ahead. If that is level, the higher single-event score decides. If everything is equal, they share the place.

## Common questions

### Do the marks count for my individual personal bests?
Yes. A 13.45 m shot inside a pentathlon counts as a shot put PB, the same as at an ordinary meet. The combined event's medals and places only come from the total.

### Can an event inside a combined event set a meet record?
No. Only the total can set a meet or school record. To count as a record, the total must be fully timed (or from a hand-timed meet), and either no wind reading may be over +4.0 m/s or the average wind must be +2.0 m/s or less.

### What shows on an athlete's profile?
The best total per combined event and age group, under **Personal bests** (for example "Pentathlon (U16 Girls) 2530 pts"), and each event's mark and points in the results history.

### I closed an event too early. Can I fix it?
Yes. An organiser can reopen an event until the next one has results; the next start list is seeded again when you close it. The last event can always be reopened, and its medals, points and record come off until you lock it again.
