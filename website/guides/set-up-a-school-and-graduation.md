Every school names its classes differently: Grade 10, Year 13, Secondary 4, JC2. SportnNote asks which school system you follow when you create a school, so class names, the graduating class, age groups and the age cut-off all start right. You can change any of them later.

## Before you start
- You need to be signed in. You become the community's first owner.
- Know which class your students leave after (for example Year 13 in a school with a sixth form, Year 11 without one).

## Step by step
1. Open the **Organize** tab and tap **🏛️ New community**.
2. Type the school's name and tap **School** under **Type**.
3. Under **School system**, check the one shown. It comes from your region, for example **🇬🇧 United Kingdom**. Tap **Change…** to pick another: India, Gulf — Indian curriculum, Gulf — British curriculum, United Kingdom, Singapore, Malaysia, Australia or United States.
4. Check **Graduating class (students leave after this)**. It is filled in for your system, for example Year 13. Tap a suggestion such as **Year 11**, or type your own.
5. Tap **Create community**.

> **Tip:** The school system also sets the age groups and the age cut-off for meets your school hosts. A UK school's meets count ages on 31 August, for example.

## What each school system sets
- **India**: Grade 1 to 12, graduating after Grade 10 unless you change it, ages on 31 December.
- **Gulf — Indian curriculum**: KG1, KG2, Grade 1 to 12, graduating after Grade 12, April school year.
- **Gulf — British curriculum**: FS1, FS2, Year 1 to 13, graduating after Year 13, ages on 31 August.
- **United Kingdom**: Reception, Year 1 to 13, graduating after Year 13 or Year 11, ages on 31 August.
- **Singapore**: Primary 1 to 6, Secondary 1 to 5, JC1, JC2, with A, B and C Divisions. The school year starts in January.
- **Malaysia**: Year 1 to 6, Form 1 to 5, Lower 6, Upper 6.
- **Australia**: Prep, Year 1 to 12, with bands like 12 & Under. "Sports day" reads "Carnival".
- **United States**: Kindergarten, Grade 1 to 12, grade events like Grades 9–12. You'll see "schedule" instead of "fixtures", and "Soccer" and "Field hockey" as sport names.

## Change it later
1. Open your school's page and tap **✎ Edit**.
2. Under **School system**, tap a different one.
3. Fix **Graduating class** if needed, then tap **Save**.

## Move everyone up a year
1. On the school's page, find **🎓 Promote year**.
2. Each student shows their class now and their next class, for example Year 9 → Year 10. Where the name changes, so does the class: Primary 6 → Secondary 1, Reception → Year 1.
3. Students in the graduating class show **🎓 Graduate**. So do students in the last class there is, such as Year 13 or Grade 12. Nobody is ever moved up to a class that doesn't exist, like "Year 14".
4. To change one student, type a different class, type the same class to repeat the year, or clear it to skip them. For someone staying on, tap **Keep on**.
5. Tap the button at the bottom, for example **Promote 120 · Graduate 34**.

Graduating students leave the school, and any Admin or Organizer rights they had end.

> **Important:** If no graduating class is set, or no student is in it, **Promote year** says so in red. Fix the class under **✎ Edit** before you apply, or nobody will graduate.

## Common questions
### We write "Class X-A", not "Grade 10". Will students still graduate?
Yes. Classes are matched by their number and kind, not the exact words. Class X, Class 10-A, Std 10 and 10th all count as Grade 10. Year 11 doesn't count as Grade 11, because Year and Grade are different systems.

### We're an Indian school. Did anything change?
No. Existing schools stay on the India system, and still graduate after Grade 10 unless you set another class.

### Our school runs to Grade 12, but students graduated after Grade 10.
Tap **✎ Edit**, set **Graduating class** to Grade 12, and tap **Save**.

### Where do age groups and cut-offs come in?
When your school hosts a meet. See [Champions, age cut-offs and event officials](/guides/sports-day-champions-and-age-groups/). Your own region setting is separate: see [Set your region, time zone and date format](/guides/region-and-time-zone/).
