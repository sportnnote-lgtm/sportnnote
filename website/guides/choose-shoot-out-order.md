A level knockout match ends in a shoot-out. Who goes first is decided at the ground, usually by a coin toss, so the app never assumes it. You tap the team the officials send first. Where the rules allow a new order for sudden death, the app asks again. Both choices are shown on the match timeline for everyone following.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- A shoot-out only comes up in a knockout (or leg 2 of a two-legged tie) that is level at the end. Group games never go to one. See [Knockouts and who goes through](/guides/knockouts-and-who-goes-through/).

## Step by step: football penalties
1. At full time (or after extra time) the panel says the match is level. Tap **Penalty shootout →**.
2. Under "🪙 Who kicks first?", tap the team that kicks first after the referee's toss, for example **Red House first**.
3. Log each kick with **✓ Scored**, **🧤 Saved** or **✗ Missed**. "Next" always shows whose kick it is.
4. Level after five each? Kicks go on in the same order until one team scores and the other doesn't (Law 10). The panel says "Sudden death — kicks continue in the same order".

## Step by step: hockey shoot-out
1. Level at full time, the panel shows "🪙 Who shoots first?". Tap the team the umpires' toss sends first.
2. Log each attempt with **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed / saved**.
3. Still level after five each? The app asks "Who shoots first?" again for sudden death. Under FIH rules the team that went second in the first five now goes first; the panel names that team so you can check. Tap the team going first.
4. Sudden death runs until one team leads after the same number of attempts.

## Step by step: handball 7-metre throws
1. When extra time is over (or straight away, if the format says so), the panel shows "🪙 Who throws first?". Tap the team that throws first after the referees' toss.
2. Log each throw with **✓ Scored** or **✗ Missed / saved**.
3. Still level after five each? The app asks who throws first in sudden death. Tap the team the referees give; the order can stay the same or change.
4. Then one throw each until one team leads.

> **Tip:** Picked the wrong team? Tap **Undo** straight away. It takes back the choice, and you can pick again.

## What everyone sees
- The timeline gets a row such as "🪙 Penalty shoot-out — Blue House kick first", "🪙 Shoot-out — India shoot first" or "🪙 7-metre throws — Kiel throw first".
- A sudden-death pick adds a second row, for example "🪙 Sudden death — Spain shoot first".
- The score line shows the shoot-out next to the match score, for example "2–2" and "SO 4–3".

## Common questions
### Matches finished before this update: do they change?
No. Their shoot-outs keep the order they were scored in. A hockey shoot-out with no sudden-death pick follows the FIH order; a handball one keeps the same order.

### Do shoot-out goals count in player stats?
No. They are kept apart from match goals: shoot-out attempts and goals have their own lines.
