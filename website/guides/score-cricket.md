Score a whole cricket match on one phone, ball by ball. You tap what happened on each delivery and the app keeps the score, the overs, strike, the batting and bowling cards, and the result. This guide walks through a match from the toss to the last ball, and links to the detailed guides for each part.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser sets the format: overs, players per side, and options like the powerplay, Impact Player and DLS.
- Both squads should be set. Players not in the squad can't be picked.

## Step by step: before the first ball
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Under **🪙 Toss**, tap who won it, then **⏏ Chose to bat** or **◎ Chose to bowl**. This sets who bats first.
3. Under **🧢 Match setup**, tap a player and choose **Captain (c)** or **Keeper †**. Each side needs both before scoring starts.
4. Tap the two openers. The first name you tap is the striker, the second the non-striker.
5. Under "Over 1 — pick … bowler", tap the bowler.

> **Tip:** Want a wide worth 2 or no byes? Set it before the first ball. See [Cricket local rules](/guides/cricket-local-rules/).

## Step by step: scoring each ball
1. Tap the runs off the bat: **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, **4** or **6**. A 4 or 6 is a boundary. Strike changes by itself on odd runs and at the end of the over.
2. For an all-run 4, a 5, a 7 or overthrows, tap **5·7·+**.
3. For a wide or no-ball, tap **Wide** or **No ball**. The panel opens right under the button, so pick the runs there.
4. For byes or leg byes, tap **Bye** or **Leg bye**, then the runs.
5. At the end of each over, "Over 3 complete — new bowler needed" appears. Tap the next bowler.

The line just above the run buttons shows who's on strike. After a no-ball it turns green and reads **FREE HIT**. It stays the same height, so the buttons never jump under your thumb.

Once the bowler is picked, the bowler names fold into one line, "Bowling: Kabir · 1.2-0-8-0", with a **Change** button. Mid-over, **Change** replaces an injured or suspended bowler. See [Bowling limits and injured bowlers](/guides/cricket-bowling-rules/) and [Score 5s, 7s, overthrows and extras](/guides/cricket-runs-and-extras/).

## Step by step: a wicket
1. Tap **WICKET**, then how they were out, for example **Bowled** or **Caught**.
2. Answer what the panel asks: the catcher for a catch (**c & b** is first), or the fielder and "who's out, and at which end?" for a run out.
3. Tap the next batter, check the line at the top, and tap **Confirm wicket**.

If nobody is left to bat, for example in a short squad, tap **Confirm wicket — innings closed**. Every kind of dismissal, including run outs off a wide and retired hurt, is in [Record every kind of dismissal](/guides/cricket-dismissals/).

## Mistakes and odd moments
- Wrong tap? **↶ Undo** takes back the last entry.
- Noticed it later? Use **✎ Edit a past ball**. See [Fix a past ball, bowler or batter](/guides/cricket-edit-a-past-ball/).
- Penalty runs, bonus runs and dropped catches go through **⚖️ Penalty**, **± Runs** and **🧤 Fielding**. A 5-run penalty that happened on a delivery can ride on that ball. See [Penalty, bonus and minus runs](/guides/cricket-penalty-and-bonus-runs/).
- Drinks, rain breaks, a new keeper, a concussion sub or the Impact Player are under **☰ Quick options**. See [Match quick options](/guides/match-quick-options/).
- Rain cut the overs? Tap **⏱ Overs & target**. See [Rain, fewer overs and DLS targets](/guides/cricket-rain-and-dls/).

## Step by step: the innings break and the chase
1. The innings ends by itself when the overs run out or the side is all out.
2. To end it early (a declaration, or the organiser's call), tap **End innings →** and confirm. The sheet shows the score and the target.
3. Pick the openers and the bowler for the chase, as before. The scoreboard shows the target and what's needed.
4. The match ends by itself when the target is reached or the chase runs out of overs or wickets.

If the scores finish level, the app offers a Super Over (or ends it as a tie if the format says so). To stop a match early, use **End match**.

## After the match
The result, the scorecard (with fall of wickets, partnerships and extras) and every player's figures are saved. Players' career stats update. See [Read the scorecard and career stats](/guides/cricket-scorecard-and-stats/).

## Common questions
### The wrong batter is on strike. How do I fix it?
Tap **⇄ Swap** next to "Batsmen".

### Can two people score the same match?
Only one phone scores at a time; another scorer can take over. See [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
