When you follow your child, you probably want to know about every goal they score. When you follow a whole tournament, you may only want the results. The 🔔 next to **✓ Following** lets you choose, separately for each player, team and tournament you follow.

## Before you start
- You need to be signed in to follow anything.
- When you first follow something, every alert is on. You only need the bell to turn some off.
- Alerts need notifications to be allowed for SportnNote. See "Getting alerts on your phone" below.

## Step by step
1. Open a player, team or tournament.
2. Tap **+ Follow player**, **+ Follow team** or **+ Follow tournament**. The button changes to **✓ Following**, and a 🔔 appears next to it.
3. Tap the **🔔**. A sheet called "Which alerts?" opens.
4. Untick what you don't need. Tick it again to turn it back on. **Turn all off** and **Turn all on** switch everything at once.
5. Tap **Save**.

The bell shows your choice at a glance:
- 🔔 means every alert is on.
- 🔔 with a small dot means some alerts are off.
- 🔕 means all alerts are off.

## What each switch does
### For a player you follow
- **Before they play**: a reminder before their match, at your own reminder times. The app says "At your reminder times (Settings → Match reminders)". You set those times under **Settings** → **Match reminders** (by default a day, an hour and 15 minutes before).
- **Match starts**: when their match goes live.
- **Result**: who won, when the match ends.
- **Goals, wickets & big moments**: each time they score, in any sport.

### For a team you follow
- **Match starts**: when the team's match goes live.
- **Result**: who won, when the match ends.

### For a tournament you follow
- **Match starts**: when any match in it goes live.
- **Result**: who won, for every match in it.

> **Tip:** For a big tournament with many matches a day, keep only **Result** on. For your own child, keep everything on.

If you untick everything, the sheet says you'll get nothing for them. They stay in your feed until you unfollow. To unfollow, tap **✓ Following**.

## See everything you follow
1. Go to **Profile** and tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **Following**.
3. Each player, team and tournament has its own 🔔, so you can change alerts from one place.

**Settings** → **Match reminders** also has a card, **⭐ Alerts for things you follow**, with an **Open Following** button.

> **Note:** At the moment the Following list may not show every team and tournament you follow. If one is missing, open its page and use the 🔔 there.

## Getting alerts on your phone
- Android app: allow notifications for SportnNote when asked. If you said no, turn them on in your phone's Settings → Apps → SportnNote → Notifications.
- Web app (app.sportnnote.in): open **Settings** → **Match reminders** and tap **Turn on notifications**, then allow them in the browser.
- iPhone: alerts from the web app only work once SportnNote is on your Home Screen. In Safari, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen", open SportnNote from the new icon, then tap **Turn on notifications**.

## Common questions

### Will a corrected score send me the alert again?
Usually not. After full time, an alert only goes out when a player's score goes up. Removing a goal that never happened sends nothing. If a goal is moved to the right player, that player's followers may get an alert for it.

### I follow a team and a player in it. Will I get two alerts?
You may get one for the team and one for the player, because they're separate follows. Turn off **Match starts** or **Result** on one of them if that's too many.

### Where do I set my own match reminder times?
Under **Settings** → **Match reminders**, in the **🔔 Reminder timers** card. The same times are used for matches you play or score in, and for players you follow.
