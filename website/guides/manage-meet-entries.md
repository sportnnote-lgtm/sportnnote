Houses send lists on paper, a runner is injured in the call room, someone turns up late. The entries screen puts every athlete of an age group against every event on one grid, warns at the school's event limit, and takes a pasted list. On each event you can add a late entry, scratch an athlete or delete the event.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser).
- Add your houses or schools as teams of the tournament, so pasted athletes score for them.
- Open the tournament's sport page (for example **Athletics**) and tap **📋 Entries (athletes × events)**.

## Step by step: set the event limit
1. Tap the limit line at the top (**No limit on events per athlete** at first).
2. Pick the most individual events and the most relays each athlete may enter, or tap **School rule: 3 individual + 1 relay**.
3. Tap **Done**.

> **Note:** The limit warns; it never blocks. Going over asks "Enter anyway as an organiser override?", and the athlete is then listed with "organiser override" in the warning box.

## Step by step: tick entries on the grid
1. Pick the age group (U10 to Open) and **Boys / Men**, **Girls / Women** or **Mixed**.
2. Under each name is their count, for example "2/3 + 1/1R": two of three individual events, one of one relay.
3. Columns are the group's events (athletics and swimming also list programme events not yet set up).
4. Tap a box to enter the athlete. ☑ means entered on an event with no start list yet. Tap it again to untick.
5. When the ticks are in, use **Make the start lists**. Each button opens event setup for that event with the entered athletes already picked, so you only choose seeds, rounds and lanes.

For events with a start list: ✓ is on it, R is in a relay team, S is scratched. Tapping an empty box there is a late entry.

## Step by step: paste a school's list
1. Tap **📥 Bulk add — paste a list**.
2. Paste one athlete per line: name, house, gender, date of birth or age group, bib. You can add events at the end, for example `Aarav Nair, Red House, B, 05/03/2012, 101, 100m; LJ`. Rows copied from a spreadsheet (with tabs) work too, and a header row is skipped.
3. Tap **Check the list**, then **Add**, and confirm.

Some rules behind the check:
- Dates are read day first, the Indian way: 05/03/2012 is 5 March 2012.
- The same name and date of birth as a player already on the app is the same person, and that player is used. A same-named player whose age fits is shown as "Maybe …". Tap **Create a new player instead** if it's someone else.
- New athletes are created for this meet only and marked **event-only**. They have no account and are on no team roster.
- Events named on a line are ticked. Where the start list already exists, they become late entries.

## Step by step: add a late entry
1. Open the event and find the **🗂 Entries** card under the start list.
2. Tap **＋ Late entry**.
3. Pick an athlete from the meet, or tap **＋ New (not on a team)** and type their name, gender, date of birth (or age group) and house. For a relay or a crew, pick the team and its members.
4. Leave the heat on **Auto**, or pick a heat or **New heat**. The line underneath shows where they'll go, for example "Goes into heat 2, lane 8".
5. Tap **Add late entry** and confirm.

On **Auto**, the athlete takes an outside free lane in the heat with the fewest athletes; nobody else moves. If every heat is full, a new heat is made, with its own qualifying places. Events without lanes put them last in the smallest group.

> **Important:** Late entries go into the first round only, while it is open. Once a round is closed, an organiser has to reopen it first.

## Step by step: scratch an athlete
1. On the event, tap **✕ Scratch**, then **Scratch** next to the athlete.
2. Pick a reason (Injured, Absent, Unwell, Event clash, Withdrawn by school, Wrong entry, or Other) and confirm.

A scratched athlete is listed as DNS with the reason. Their lane stays empty and they never go on to the next round. **Reinstate** puts them back in the same lane until the round is closed. Choose **Wrong entry** to take someone off the start list completely instead of listing them as DNS.

Someone who already has a result can't be scratched; mark DNF, WD or DQ instead.

## Step by step: delete an event
1. On the event, tap **🗑 Delete event**.
2. If nothing has been scored, confirm **Yes, delete event**. The event and its start list are removed.
3. If the event has results, the app says so. Confirm **Yes, delete event and results** to remove every round, its marks and its medals and points. Any record it set goes back to the previous holder.

## Common questions
### Who can see the entries screen?
Only the meet's organisers. Everyone else sees start lists and results as usual.
