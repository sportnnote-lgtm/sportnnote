When a golf event ends, people want more than the winner. They want to know who shot the low round, who putted best and who made the most birdies. In a school sports day, golf places also need to count for the houses. This guide shows where the golf leaders are, which golf awards you can give, and how golf places reach the medal table. For scoring rounds, see [Run a golf event](/guides/golf-scoring-and-leaderboard/).

## Before you start
- Leaders and awards come from finished rounds. A round counts once a host taps **🏁 Finish the round**. A round still in play doesn't count yet.
- To pick and publish awards, you must be a host of the tournament.
- Golf counts on the medal table in a tournament that ranks sports by places (a medal meet), or a meet that also has athletics or swimming events.

## Step by step: see the golf leaders
1. Open the tournament and tap the golf card (⛳ Golf).
2. Scroll past the leaderboard to **📊 Leaders**.
3. Swipe sideways through the cards. Tap a player to open their profile.

You can also open the tournament → **Stats** tab and pick golf. Tap **⛳ Golf leaderboard & rounds** for the full leaderboard. The golf leader cards are under it.

## The golf leader cards
- **Low round:** the lowest score in one complete 18-hole round, with the score to par, for example 68 (−4).
- **Low round (9 holes):** the same for 9-hole rounds. A 36 for 9 holes is never compared with a 72 for 18.
- **Scoring average:** strokes per complete round, worked out per 18 holes. The lowest is best. A player needs at least 2 complete rounds to rank.
- **Birdies** and **Eagles+:** the totals over the event.
- **Fewest putts per round:** putts per 18 holes, counting only the holes where putts were entered. A player needs at least 18 holes with putts.
- **Greens in regulation:** the share of greens hit, from holes where greens were tracked. A player needs at least 18 greens tracked.

A complete round means every hole has a score and the player did not pick up. A card with a pick-up (NR) still counts for birdies, but not for the low round or the scoring average.

Match play is different: its leaders show **Birdies** (when strokes are entered) and **Holes won**. Stroke play and Stableford never show **Holes won**.

> **Note:** Putts and greens show only for players whose putts or greens were entered. Nobody is shown with 0 putts just because putts weren't tracked.

## Step by step: change the minimums
1. Open the tournament → **Stats** tab and pick golf.
2. Under the leaders, tap **Minimums**.
3. Use **−** and **+** to change the rounds, holes putted or greens tracked. 0 means no minimum.
4. Tap **Save**.

> **Tip:** For a one-day, one-round event, set the scoring average minimum to 1, or just use the low round.

## Step by step: golf awards
1. Open the tournament → **Awards** tab and pick golf.
2. The golf award cards are **Player of the Tournament**, **Most birdies**, **Low round** and **Best scoring average**. Each shows a suggested winner from the finished rounds, such as "68 (−4) · 1 round".
3. Tap **Change** to pick someone else, or **How is this ranked?** to see the rule.
4. Tap **Publish awards**, then **Publish**.

For custom awards and editing after publishing, see [Publish tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).

## How golf places reach the medal table
When every round of the golf event is finished, the final leaderboard places score for each player's team. The team is the one picked for team stroke play. If there isn't one, it's the player's house.

- 1st, 2nd and 3rd win gold, silver and bronze.
- Points use the meet's points for places, or 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 if none are set.
- Tied players share the points of the places they cover.
- Players who missed the cut (MC), withdrew, were disqualified, did not start or had no return (NR) score nothing.
- If a playoff for first is still to be played, golf waits until you record the playoff winner.

See the totals on the tournament → **Stats** tab under **🏅 Medal table**.

## Common questions
### Why is a player missing from the low round card?
They have no complete 18-hole round yet. Either a hole has no score, they picked up, or their round isn't finished.

### Does a player without a house score on the medal table?
No. Their place still shows on the golf leaderboard, but it adds points to no team.
