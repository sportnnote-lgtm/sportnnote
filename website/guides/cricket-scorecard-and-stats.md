The scorecard shows who scored, when each wicket fell and which over turned the match. After the match, figures go to each player's profile and the tournament's leaders.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can see the match can read it. Open the match and tap the **Scorecard** tab. Tap a team's name to open or close their innings.

## The batting table
- **R** runs, **B** balls faced, **4s** and **6s** boundaries, **SR** strike rate.
- Under each name is how they got out, for example "c Arjun b Kabir". A * means not out. Tap a name to open the profile.

## Extras, total and fall of wickets
- **Extras** shows the make-up, e.g. "12 (b 2, lb 1, wd 6, nb 2, pen 5)". **Total** shows runs/wickets, overs and run rate.
- **Fall of wickets**: "3-45 (Asha, 7.2 ov)" means the 3rd wicket fell at 45, Asha was out, on the 2nd ball of the 8th over.

## The bowling table
- **O** overs, **M** maidens, **R** runs, **W** wickets, **0s** dots, **Eco** economy.
- A maiden is an over with no runs charged to the bowler (byes and leg byes don't count).
- Fielding notes follow as "Fielding: Ravi 1 drop · Asha 6 saved"; see [fielding notes](/guides/cricket-penalty-and-bonus-runs/).

## Partnerships and the overs chart
- **Partnerships**: each pair's runs and balls, and each batter's share. A * means still batting.
- **Overs**: a bar per over; a mark on top means a wicket. Tap a bar for that over ball by ball.

## Career stats on a profile
1. Open a player's profile and, under **By sport**, tap **Cricket**.
2. You'll see **Apps**, the **W-D-L** record and **Win %**, then three sections: **Batting**, **Bowling** and **Fielding**.

**Apps** counts every match in the playing XI. A no result isn't part of Win %, which is wins out of the matches that had a result.

What the short names mean:
- **Avg** under Batting: runs per dismissal (120 runs, 3 outs = 40.00). **SR**: runs per 100 balls.
- **Highest**: the best score (* = not out). **Econ**: runs per 6-ball over.
- **Avg** and **SR** under Bowling: runs and balls per wicket. Lower is better.
- **Best**: the best bowling in one match, for example "3/12": most wickets, then fewest runs.
- **BF** balls faced; **Ducks** times out for 0 (retired hurt on 0 isn't one).
- **Inns** under Bowling: matches they bowled in; **4w** four-wicket hauls, **5w** five or more.
- Under **Fielding**: Catches, Stumpings, Run outs and the fielding notes. A wicket-keeper also sees **Ct as keeper** and **Keeper dismissals** (keeper catches plus stumpings).

A "–" means there's nothing to work it out from yet. A ☁ on a figure means older matches didn't record it; tap it to see over how many.

## Captaincy
A player who has captained gets a **Captaincy** section: **Mat** as captain, the **W-L** record (**W-L-T** with a tie) and **Win %**. The captain and keeper come from the ones picked at the toss.

> **Note:** Matches finished before the captaincy record arrived count once the organiser's stats refresh has run.

> **Tip:** Super Over runs and wickets aren't counted in careers. They're a tie-breaker, not part of the match.

## Career by format
A box-cricket 50 off 15 balls shouldn't inflate a T20 strike rate. When a player has played more than one kind of match, chips appear above the figures:
1. Tap **Format: All** and choose **T20**, **ODI**, **T10**, **Hundred**, **Box** or **Long**.
2. Every section and the match history now cover only those matches. **✕ Clear** shows the whole career again.

Sixes and Box cricket count as **Box**, Test / timeless as **Long**. A custom format goes by its numbers: 10-ball overs is Hundred, fewer than 11 a side Box, then up to 10 overs T10, 20 T20, 50 ODI, more Long. No format saved shows as **Other**.

**Ball** splits leather and tennis-ball matches. **Tournament**, **Season** and **Opponent** work the same way; see [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Tournament records leaders
1. Open the tournament and tap the **Stats** tab.
2. Tap **Cricket**.
3. Swipe the leader cards sideways.

After **Runs**, **Wickets** and **Catches** come the records: **Highest score**, **Best bowling**, **Best batting average**, **Best strike rate**, **Best economy**, **Most 50s**, **Most 100s**, **Most 4s**, **Most 6s**, **Most maidens**, **Most dot balls**, **Most ducks** and **Most keeper dismissals**.

When the matches span more than one format or ball, **Format** and **Ball** chips appear above the cards, on a sport page too. Pick **T20** to rank T20 matches only.

A rate means little after a few balls, so these cards need a minimum, shown under the card's title. Organisers can change it with **Minimums**:
- **Best batting average**: at least 3 innings, and out at least once.
- **Best strike rate**: at least 30 balls faced.
- **Best economy**: at least 10 overs bowled.

A card appears once somebody qualifies.

## Common questions
### When do career figures update?
The full figures are saved when the match ends. If the match is later corrected, the profile is updated too. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/) and [Fix a past ball](/guides/cricket-edit-a-past-ball/).

### Why does an older player's average look odd?
Older matches only saved runs and wickets. They count in the totals, but averages, rates, ducks and 4w use only matches with full figures.
