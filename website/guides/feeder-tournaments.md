Many school competitions run in stages, such as county rounds that feed a national final, zonal rounds that feed a national round, or conference seasons that feed state playoffs. Each stage is its own tournament in SportnNote. A link between them carries the qualifiers forward, so nobody has to type the teams in again.

## Before you start
- Create both tournaments: the qualifying round (the feeder) and the next round.
- You need to organise the feeder to link it, and the next round to add its qualifiers.

## Step by step
1. Open the feeder tournament → **Manage** tab.
2. On **Qualifies to another tournament**, tap **Set up**.
3. Under **Find the next-round tournament**, type its name and tap it.
4. Choose **Top of each group** or **Top overall / final places**. Then tap **Top 1**, **Top 2**, **Top 3**, **Top 4** or **Top 8**.
5. Tap **Save**. The card now reads, for example, "Top 2 of each group go through to National Finals".
6. Later, open the next-round tournament → **Manage** tab → **Participating teams**. A **Qualifiers** card lists each feeder and who is through so far.
7. Tap **Add N qualifiers**. They join as confirmed entries, each showing "Qualified from County Cup".

## Who goes through
- **Top of each group:** each group's table, using that tournament's own tie-breakers.
- **Top overall:** the knockout's final places when the final is finished (winner, runner-up, then the third-place match or both beaten semi-finalists). Otherwise it's the league table.
- While matches are still to play, the card says "not final yet". You can wait, or add the current leaders and adjust later.

> **Note:** Only the next round's organisers can give a team the "Qualified from" badge. A team asking to join can't claim it.

## Common questions
### Can several tournaments feed one final?
Yes. Every feeder shows on the final's **Qualifiers** card with its own **Add** button.

### What if a qualifier withdraws?
Withdraw it on the teams page as usual, then add the next team by hand.

### Do the teams have to be the same team records?
Yes. Qualifiers are the same teams that played the feeder, so their history and players carry on.
