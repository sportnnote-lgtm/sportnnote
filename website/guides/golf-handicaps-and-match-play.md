Your Handicap Index decides how many shots you get. The app never issues an index (only your club or the Indian Golf Union can), but it uses the one you enter. It fills in round setup, gives the right shots in match play, and works out each round's score differential so you can follow your form.

## Before you start
- Know your Handicap Index, for example 12.4. A plus handicap is written with a plus sign, for example `+1.2`.
- For differentials, the course needs a Course Rating and Slope for the tees you play. Add them when you create the course ("Course rating" and "Slope"), copied from the scorecard.

## Step by step: add your Handicap Index
1. Open your profile and tap **✎ Edit profile**.
2. Make sure **⛳ Golf** is selected under your sports.
3. In the golf box, type your index in **Handicap Index (WHS)**.
4. Tap **Save profile**.

From now on, when an organiser adds you to a golf round, your index is already filled in. They can still change it for that round.

## Your handicap on your profile
Open your golf profile. The **Handicap** box shows:
- **Handicap Index**: the one you entered.
- **Score differentials**: one bar per finished 18-hole round on rated tees, the last 20 at most. Tap a bar to see its date, course, adjusted gross and differential.
- **Unofficial index estimate**: from 3 differentials up. With 20 it averages your best 8, and the counting bars are highlighted.

How a differential is worked out (World Handicap System):
- Each hole is capped at net double bogey: par plus 2 plus the shots you get on that hole (Rule 3.1). Without an index the cap is par plus 5. A picked-up hole takes the cap.
- Differential = 113 ÷ Slope × (adjusted gross − Course Rating) (Rule 5.1).

> **Important:** The estimate is not an official Handicap Index. It skips the playing conditions adjustment, the caps on how fast an index can rise, and the reduction for an exceptional score. 9-hole rounds and unfinished cards get no differential.

## Step by step: match play with strokes
1. Start a golf friendly or a bracket match with the format **Match play**. Pick **Back 9** for a nine-hole match on holes 10 to 18.
2. Tap **▶ Start the match**, then **⛳ Enter strokes · handicap shots**.
3. Pick the course (or **Standard par 72**) and the tees. Both players' Handicap Indexes fill in from their profiles; change them if needed.
4. The line underneath says who gets shots, for example "Asha Rao gets 6 shots". Tap **Save**.
5. On each hole, the box shows par, stroke index and who gets a shot (●). Use − and + for each player's strokes, then tap the **Record hole** button. It works out the hole on net strokes and says who wins before you tap.
6. If you'd rather not enter strokes on a hole, tap a result button such as **Hole 12 halved**.

The higher handicap gets the difference between the two playing handicaps, at 100% (the standard for singles match play), on the hardest holes by stroke index. On nine holes, the nine are ranked among themselves. Extra holes give shots the same way as the holes they replay.

To change the course or handicaps later, tap **⚙ Change strokes / course**. **Stop entering strokes** goes back to tapping results only. Holes already played keep their result.

## Common questions
### Do strokes in match play count on my profile?
Yes. Each hole you enter strokes for adds a birdie, par, bogey and so on to your golf stats, alongside holes won.

### Why does a back-nine match start at hole 10?
It is played on holes 10 to 18, so the app numbers them that way. A first extra hole is the 19th.
