Most US school and college pools are 25 yards long, not 25 metres. Times in a yards pool can't be compared with metre times, so SportnNote treats a yards pool as its own course: its own events, its own records and its own personal bests. No time is ever converted between yards and metres.

## Before you start
- You organise the meet, or you're one of its hosts.
- Everything else works as in [Run a swim meet](/guides/run-a-swim-meet/): heats, lanes, manual timing, splits, house points.

## Step by step
1. Create the tournament with **Swimming** as a sport. Under the swimming settings, set **Pool** to **25 yd (short course yards, US)**. If your phone is set to the United States, a new swim meet starts on yards already.
2. To change the pool later, open the tournament → **Manage** tab → **Swimming — pool, points & timing**.
3. Add an event from the **Swimming** page. In **Category**, the pool chips show **25 yd pool (short course yards)** selected; one event can still be switched to another pool.
4. Pick the race. The yards programme lists 50, 100 and 200 yd of every stroke, the 100, 200 and 400 yd individual medley, the 500, 1000 and 1650 yd freestyle, and the relays. Longer races appear for older age groups.
5. Seed, enter times and finish the event exactly as in a metres pool.

## What's different in yards
- Event names read in yards everywhere: "100 yd butterfly", "4 × 50 yd medley relay".
- Splits read every 50 yd.
- The 400, 800 and 1500 m freestyle aren't offered in a yards pool; the 500, 1000 and 1650 yd freestyle take their place. They're never offered in a metres pool.
- The "Check this mark" warning uses ranges for yards, which are faster than 25 m times.
- Records and bests are marked **SCY** next to **LC** (50 m) and **SC** (25 m), and kept separately. A swimmer's profile shows a separate personal best for each pool.

> **Note:** A meet record set in yards is only compared with other yards times. Your 25 m and 50 m records are untouched.

## Common questions
### We swim some meets in yards and some in metres. Is that a problem?
No. Each meet, or each event, records its pool. Profiles and record books keep the three pools apart.

### Can I convert a yards time to metres?
No. Yards-to-metres conversion factors are estimates and aren't valid for records, so the app never converts.

### Do the US high-school events appear in order?
The app lists the events by stroke. You choose which events to run and in what order on the meet timetable.
