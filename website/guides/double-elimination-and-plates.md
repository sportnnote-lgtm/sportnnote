In a straight knockout, one bad match and you're out. Pickleball events often play pools and then double elimination, where a team is only out after its second loss. School badminton, table tennis and tennis events often run a plate instead: the first-round losers play their own small knockout, so nobody goes home after one match. SportnNote runs both, for any sport.

## Before you start
- You must be an organiser (host) of the tournament.
- Double elimination needs at least 3 teams (or players). A plate needs at least 2 first-round losers.

## Set up double elimination
1. Open the tournament and tap **Auto-generate fixtures**.
2. Tap **🏆 Knockout (round 1)**. After pools, tap **🏅 Advance groups** instead, then **🏆 To knockout**.
3. Under **Bracket**, tap **🔁 Double elimination**.
4. Leave **Grand-final reset** on, or tap it to turn it off (see below).
5. Under **Seeding**, choose **🥇 Seeded (list order)** or **🎲 Random draw**.
6. Tap **⚡ Generate preview**, then **✅ Create … matches** to create the first round of the winners' bracket.

## How double elimination runs
- **Winners' bracket:** a normal seeded draw. Top seeds get byes if the numbers don't fit.
- **Losers' bracket:** your first loss drops you here. Losers' round 1 pairs the winners' round 1 losers. Then the losers' bracket alternates: survivors play the teams who have just dropped from the winners' bracket, then survivors play each other.
- **No quick rematches:** dropped teams are placed in the other part of the draw from where they came. A team rarely meets someone it has already played until the late rounds.
- **Grand final:** the winners'-bracket champion plays the losers'-bracket champion.
- **Grand-final reset:** if the losers'-bracket team wins the grand final, both teams have now lost once, so one more match decides the title. Turn the reset off if you want one grand final only.

To play it, open the tournament and tap **🏆 Knockout bracket**. The winners' bracket, losers' bracket and grand final each show as columns of rounds. On a phone, swipe sideways inside a bracket to see every round. Whenever results make new matches possible, tap **▶ Create … ready to play**. A bye never creates a match.

## Set up a plate
1. Open the sport's settings and turn on **Plate for first-round losers**. You can also tap it on the **Auto-generate fixtures** screen, under **Bracket**, when you create a single-elimination knockout.
2. Play the main draw's first round as normal.
3. Once every first-round match has a winner, open **🏆 Knockout bracket** and tap **🍽️ Create the plate**.
4. Tap **Yes, create the plate**. The first-round losers are drawn in bracket order. If the numbers don't fit, the first ones get byes.
5. As plate results come in, tap **▶ Create … plate matches** for the next round.

The plate shows under the main bracket. When the plate final is played, the bracket names the **Plate winner**.

## Who places where
- **Double elimination:** the grand-final winner is 1st and the loser is 2nd. The losers'-final loser is 3rd and the losers' semi-final loser is 4th. Teams out in the same earlier losers' round share a place.
- **Plate:** it never changes a place or gives a medal. First-round losers keep their place from the main draw, and the plate winner is shown as an award only.

## Ties, levels and walkovers
Both draws work with team ties: a tie counts once in the bracket. If a match in either draw can't be settled by scoring (abandoned, called off, or level with no decider), the bracket lists it with **No winner from the scoring**. You pick who advances, as in [Settle a level knockout and pick who goes through](/guides/knockouts-and-who-goes-through/). Knockout deciders, such as extra time or penalties, apply to every match in both draws.

## Common questions
### Can I switch a running knockout to double elimination?
No. Choose it when you create the first round. A knockout already in progress stays as it was created.

### Does a plate work with double elimination?
No. In double elimination the losers' bracket already gives every team a second chance.

### How are teams seeded after pools?
Pool winners come first (best record first), then runners-up, as in [Seed a knockout draw so the best meet last](/guides/seed-a-knockout-draw/).
