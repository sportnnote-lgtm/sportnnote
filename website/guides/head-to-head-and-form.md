Before a match, the first question is usually "who won last time?". Head-to-head answers it: the wins, draws and losses between two players or two teams, each side's last five results, and the score of every meeting. In singles sports such as tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, pickleball, padel, chess and carrom it compares the two players. In team sports, and doubles, it compares the two teams.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can open the match or the player's profile can use it. Nothing is saved or changed.
- It counts finished matches scored in SportnNote. A match that is still being played doesn't count yet.

## Step by step
1. To check before a match: open the match and find the info card with the date, venue and tournament.
2. Tap **⚔️ Head-to-head & form ›**. A singles match opens the two players; any other match opens the two teams.
3. To compare a player with anyone: open their profile, tap a sport under **By sport**, and tap **⚔️ Compare with…** next to the **Form** strip.
4. The list under **Played against** shows everyone they have met in that sport, most often first, with the record. Tap a name, or type at least two letters in the search box to find another player.
5. Read the result: the two players' or teams' wins and draws, each side's form, and the meetings.
6. Tap a meeting to open that match. Tap a name at the top to open that player's or team's page.
7. Tap **Compare with someone else** to pick a different opponent.

> **Tip:** The **Form** strip on a player's sport page shows their last five results, newest first. Tap a letter to open that match.

## What it shows
- **Wins and draws**: for example **Aarav won 3**, **Drawn 1**, **Riya won 2**. Cricket says **Tied** instead of drawn; sports that can't draw (tennis, badminton and the like) show only the two win counts. No results are listed under the tiles.
- **Form**: each side's last five results in this sport against anyone, newest first: W win, D draw, L loss, T tie, NR no result.
- **Meetings**: up to ten, newest first, read from the first side, with the score and the set or game line, for example **2–1 · 6-4, 3-6, 7-5**, and the date. A walkover reads **w/o**.

## How players are matched up
- A meeting is a finished match where the two players were on opposite sides. Doubles partners are on the same side, so they are never counted against each other.
- Each player's side comes from their match stats. When one side won and the other lost, they were opponents. For older matches without a player's stats, a singles entry with just that player still counts.
- Doubles: a player's own head-to-head counts every meeting, singles or doubles, where the two were on opposite sides.

## Common questions
### Why does it say "They haven't met yet"?
There's no finished match between them in that sport in SportnNote. Matches played elsewhere don't count.

### Why is the team head-to-head different from the team page?
It isn't: both read the same finished matches. The team page lists every opponent; this page shows one opponent's meetings in full.

### Does it use only one tournament?
No. It covers every finished meeting, in tournaments and friendlies. Use the team page's tournament chips to see one event.
