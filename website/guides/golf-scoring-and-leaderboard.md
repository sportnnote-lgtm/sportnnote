A golf event is one leaderboard across one or more rounds. Each group marks its own card on a phone, the leaderboard updates as holes are entered, and after a round the organiser can keep only the leaders for the next round, the way a 72-hole open keeps the top players after 36 holes. This guide covers stroke play and Stableford. Match play uses the normal bracket.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament to set up and finish rounds.
- Players can mark the card for their own group. Everyone else sees it view only.
- Add the course once (pars and stroke indexes). After that, every round can pick it.

## Step by step: set up and score a round
1. Open the tournament and tap the golf card (⛳ Golf).
2. Tap **⛳ Set up round 1**. Pick the course and tees, the format, the players and, if you use handicaps, each player's Handicap Index.
3. Choose the group size and tee interval, then tap **⛳ Create round**. The app makes the groups and tee times.
4. On the day, tap **▶ Start the round**.
5. On the **📝 Scorecard** tab, use − and + for each player's strokes on the hole, or tap **Par**. Tap **Pick up** if a player picks up. Then tap the "Next: hole 2 ▶" button and carry on.
6. To record putts, tap **Track putts**. A row of putt counts appears under each score.
7. When every group is in, tap **🏁 Finish the round**. Scores are locked and each player's round goes to their profile.

> **Tip:** No signal on the course? Keep scoring. Cards are saved on the phone and sync when you are back online.

## Reading the leaderboard
The **🏆 Leaderboard** tab shows the position, the total (to par, or points for Stableford), today's score and holes played (thru). Tap a player to see their card hole by hole. In a tournament, the leaderboard on the golf page adds up every round.

- Ties: Players on the same total share the place, shown with a T, for example T3.
- Countback: If the format uses countback, ties on finished cards are split on the last 9, 6, 3 and then the last hole of the final round. While any of the tied players is still out on the course, they stay tied (T). Countback is only applied once all of their cards are complete.
- NR, WD and DQ: A player who picks up in stroke play has no return (NR) and is listed at the bottom with anyone who withdrew or was disqualified.

## Making a cut
1. After round 2 is finished, open the golf page and tap "＋ Set up round 3". You can also tap **＋ Set up the next round** on the finished round.
2. The players, course and format are carried over. The leaders tee off last.
3. Under **✂️ Cut (optional)**, enter a number in **Top N (and ties)**, for example `50`. Anyone tied with the 50th player also makes it.
4. Tap **⛳ Create round**. Only the players who made the cut are entered in round 3.

Leave the box blank for no cut.

## Players who missed the cut
As soon as the next round is set up, players who missed the cut drop below a "cut" line on the leaderboard. Each one is marked MC and shows their total for the rounds they played. They are not ranked among the players still in the event, even if their 36-hole total is lower than someone's 54-hole total. If there is a second cut, players who went further are listed above those cut earlier.

Players marked MC are never offered again when you set up a later round.

## Golf stats on a player's profile
Open a player's profile and choose golf to see their rounds, scoring average and totals.

- **Best round** only compares complete 18-hole rounds with no pick-ups. A 9-hole round is shown on its own as **Best 9 holes**.
- **Putts/round** only counts the holes where putts were entered, scaled to 18 holes. A round played without **Track putts** doesn't pull the average down.

## Common questions
### Why did a player's best round change?
Before, a complete 9-hole round could show as the best round because its total was lower. It is now listed as the best 9 instead, and the best round is the lowest complete 18-hole score.

### Why is putts per round higher than before?
Rounds without putts used to count as zero putts. Now only rounds (and holes) where putts were entered count.

### Two players are level and one is still playing. Who is ahead?
Neither. They share the place with a T until both cards are complete. Then countback decides, if the format uses it.
