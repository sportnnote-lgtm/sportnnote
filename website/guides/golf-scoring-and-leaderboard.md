A golf event is one leaderboard across one or more rounds. Each group marks its own card on a phone, the leaderboard updates as holes are entered, and after a round the organiser can keep only the leaders for the next round. This guide covers stroke play and Stableford. Match play uses the normal bracket: tap the "wins hole" button for the winner of each hole, or "Hole 3 halved" (the number follows the hole). If a player gives up the match, tap **🏳 Concede match…** at the bottom, pick who concedes, and confirm. The result keeps where the match stood, for example "Final · conceded, 3 down thru 12", and the hole-by-hole list shows it too.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament to set up and finish rounds.
- Players can mark the card for their own group. Everyone else sees it view only.
- Add the course once (pars and stroke indexes). After that, every round can pick it.

## Step by step: set up and score a round
1. Open the tournament and tap the golf card (⛳ Golf).
2. Tap **⛳ Set up round 1**. Pick the course and tees, the format, the players and, if you use handicaps, each player's Handicap Index.
3. Set the group size and tee interval, and tap **⛳ Create round**. The app makes the groups and tee times.
4. On the day, tap **▶ Start the round**.
5. On the **📝 Scorecard** tab, use − and + for each player's strokes on the hole (up to 20), or tap **Par**. Then tap the "Next: hole 2 ▶" button and carry on.
6. If a player picks up in stroke play, tap **Pick up (NR)**. It asks first, because one pick-up makes the whole card a no return. In Stableford **Pick up** just scores 0 points on the hole. **Clear…** wipes a hole and also asks first.
7. To record putts, tap **Track putts**. Under each score, tap 0 to 4 putts, never more than the strokes. For more, tap **5+**, and tap it again to step up to the stroke count. If you lower the strokes, the putts come down with them.
8. Once a card has every hole scored, it shows **✍ Sign the card** with two chips. The marker taps **Marker** and the player taps **Player**; they turn into **Marker ✓** and **Player ✓**, and the card reads **✍ Card signed**. Changing any hole score un-signs the card, so both sign again.
9. When every group is in, tap **🏁 Finish the round**. The confirm lists any cards not signed by both marker and player, so you can check them first. Scores are then locked and each player's round goes to their profile.

> **Tip:** No signal on the course? Keep scoring. Cards are saved on the phone and sync when you are back online.

## Reading the leaderboard
The **🏆 Leaderboard** tab shows the position, the total (to par, or points for Stableford), today's score and holes played (thru). Tap a player to see their card hole by hole. In a tournament, the leaderboard on the golf page adds up every round.

- Ties: Players on the same total share the place, shown with a T, for example T3.
- Countback: If the format uses countback, ties on finished cards are split on the last 9, 6, 3 and then the last hole of the final round. While any of them is still on the course, they stay tied (T).
- NR, WD and DQ: A player who picks up in stroke play has no return (NR) and is listed at the bottom with anyone who withdrew or was disqualified.

## Making a cut
1. After round 2 is finished, open the golf page and tap "＋ Set up round 3". You can also tap **＋ Set up the next round** on the finished round.
2. The players, course and format carry over. The leaders tee off last.
3. Under **✂️ Cut (optional)**, enter a number in **Top N (and ties)**, for example `50`. Anyone tied with the 50th player also makes it.
4. Tap **⛳ Create round**. Only the players who made the cut are entered in round 3.

Leave the box blank for no cut.

## Players who missed the cut
As soon as the next round is set up, players who missed the cut drop below a "cut" line on the leaderboard. Each one is marked MC and shows their total for the rounds they played. They are never ranked among players still in the event. If there is a second cut, players who went further are listed above those cut earlier.

MC players are never offered for a later round.

## Golf stats on a player's profile
Open a player's profile and choose golf to see their rounds, scoring average and totals.

- **Best round** only compares complete 18-hole rounds with no pick-ups. A 9-hole round is shown on its own as **Best 9 holes**.
- **Putts/round** only counts the holes where putts were entered, scaled to 18 holes. A round played without **Track putts** doesn't pull the average down.

## Common questions
### Do both signatures have to be there to finish?
No. You can still finish the round, but the confirm names every card not signed by both.
