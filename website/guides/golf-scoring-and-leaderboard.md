A golf event is one leaderboard across one or more rounds. Each group marks its own card on a phone, the leaderboard updates as holes are entered, and after a round the organiser can keep only the leaders for the next round. This guide covers stroke play and Stableford. Match play uses the normal bracket; see [Golf handicaps and match play with strokes](/guides/golf-handicaps-and-match-play/). Withdrawals, handicap changes, the stats row and playoffs are in [Golf entry admin, stats and prizes](/guides/golf-entry-admin-and-stats/).

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament to set up and finish rounds.
- Players can mark the card for their own group. Everyone else sees it view only.
- Add the course once (pars and stroke indexes). After that, every round can pick it.

## Step by step: set up and score a round
1. Open the tournament and tap the golf card (⛳ Golf).
2. Tap **⛳ Set up round 1**. Pick the course and tees, the format, the players and, if you use handicaps, each player's Handicap Index.
3. Set the group size and tee interval, and tap **⛳ Create round**. The app makes the groups and tee times.
4. On the day, tap **▶ Start the round**.
5. On the **📝 Scorecard** tab, use − and + for each player's strokes on the hole (up to 20), or tap **Par**. Then tap the "Next: hole 2 ▶" button and carry on.
6. If a player picks up in stroke play, tap **Pick up (NR)**. It asks first, because one pick-up makes the whole card a no return. In Stableford **Pick up** just scores 0 points on the hole. **Clear…** wipes a hole and also asks first.
7. To record putts, tap **Track putts**. Under each score, tap 0 to 4 putts (never more than the strokes), or **5+** and tap again to step up.
8. Once every hole is scored, the card shows **✍ Sign the card**. The marker taps **Marker** and the player taps **Player**, and it reads **✍ Card signed**. Changing a score un-signs the card.
9. When every group is in, tap **🏁 Finish the round**. The confirm lists any cards not signed by both marker and player, so you can check them first. Scores are then locked and each player's round goes to their profile.

> **Tip:** No signal on the course? Keep scoring. Cards are saved on the phone and sync when you are back online.

## Reading the leaderboard
The **🏆 Leaderboard** tab shows the position, the total (to par, or points for Stableford), **Thru** and **Strk** (total strokes). Thru shows **F** when the player has finished the round, the number of holes while they play, and – before they tee off. Tap a player to see their scorecard: Out, In and Tot, par and stroke index rows, a circle for a birdie and a square for a bogey, a Net row when they get shots, and putts when entered. **Share card** sends it as text. On the **📝 Scorecard** tab, **📋 Full card** shows your whole group's card.

In a tournament, the leaderboard on the golf page adds up every round. It also shows **Today** and a column per round (**R1**, **R2**, …): a finished round shows its strokes, a round in play shows its score to par. On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see every column. To see a player's card from an earlier round, tap the round under **Cards:** (for example **R2**), then tap the player.

- Ties: Players on the same total share the place, shown with a T, for example T3.
- Countback: If the format uses countback, ties on finished cards are split on the last 9, 6, 3 and then the last hole of the final round. While any of them is still on the course, they stay tied (T).
- NR, WD, DQ and DNS: A player who picks up in stroke play has no return (NR) and is listed at the bottom with anyone who withdrew, was disqualified or did not start.

## Making a cut
1. After round 2 is finished, open the golf page and tap "＋ Set up round 3". You can also tap **＋ Set up the next round** on the finished round.
2. The players, course and format carry over. The leaders tee off last.
3. Under **✂️ Cut (optional)**, enter a number in **Top N (and ties)**, for example `50`. Anyone tied with the 50th player also makes it.
4. Tap **⛳ Create round**. Only the players who made the cut are entered in round 3.

Leave the box blank for no cut.

## Players who missed the cut
Once the next round is set up, players who missed the cut drop below a "cut" line, marked MC, with their total for the rounds they played. They are never ranked among players still in the event or offered for a later round. After a second cut, players who went further are listed above those cut earlier.

## Common questions
### Do both signatures have to be there to finish?
No. You can still finish the round, but the confirm names every card not signed by both.
