A wagon wheel shows where a batter scored their runs: lines from the pitch to each part of the field, with fours and sixes in their own colours. Turn it on for a match, and after each scoring shot the scorer can tap where the ball went. The taps are optional. Skip any ball and scoring carries on as normal.

## Before you start
- You score the match (a listed scorer or a host).
- The wagon wheel is off by default. Turn it on for one match under **Match rules**: **Wagon wheel (where each shot went)** → **On**. Organisers can switch it on for a whole tournament under **⚙ Customize this format**.
- It works in every format. Shots in a Super Over are saved but don't count in careers.

## Step by step
1. Score the ball as usual, for example **4**.
2. Under the scoring buttons, a card asks "Asha Rao's 4 (6.2) — where did it go?".
3. Tap the part of the field. The inner ring is inside the 30-yard circle; the outer ring is deep. A four or six is always counted as deep.
4. Optional: tap the shot, for example **Drive**, **Cut**, **Pull**, **Sweep** or **Lofted**.
5. The card shows "✓ Cover (deep) · Drive". Tap **Done**, or just bowl the next ball.

Tapped the wrong area? Tap the right one; the newest tap wins. **↶ Undo** also takes it back ("Undo: wagon-wheel tag").

> **Tip:** For a left-hander, tap **Left-hander** first. The field flips, so Cover is on the left. The app remembers it for that batter until you leave the screen. If the player's profile says "Left-handed", it's already set.

## Where the drawing appears
- **Scorecard** tab: open an innings, then **Wagon wheel**. Pick a batter. You see their lines, runs per area, 4s and 6s, the off-side and leg-side split, and "5 of 8 scoring shots tagged".
- The batter's cricket profile shows a **Wagon wheel** of runs by area from every tagged match.

## The field areas
For a right-hander, from straight down the ground, clockwise: Mid-off, Cover, Point, Third man, Fine leg, Square leg, Mid-wicket and Mid-on. A left-hander's field is the mirror image. Careers combine them correctly.

## Common questions
### Do I have to tag every shot?
No. Untagged shots still count in the score and the batter's figures. The wagon wheel says how many shots it covers, so viewers know when it's partial.

### Can I tag a shot after the next ball?
No, only the most recent scoring shot. The last ball of an innings can be tagged until the next innings starts.

### What about dot balls, byes and wides?
Only runs off the bat are tagged. A no-ball hit for runs can be tagged too.

### Does editing a past ball move its tag?
If you delete or insert a ball with **✎ Edit a past ball**, check the wagon wheel. Tags are kept by over and ball number.
