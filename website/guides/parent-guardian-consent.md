Every SportnNote account for someone under 18 needs a parent or guardian. In India, the parent ticks the consent box at sign-up, as before. In other countries, the parent confirms from their own email. Once linked, a parent can manage the child's data from their own account.

## Before you start
- The child signs up with their own details and their parent's or guardian's name and email.
- In the US, children under 13 can't create their own account. A parent adds them as a player from the parent's own account instead.
- A parent who wants to manage the child's data needs their own SportnNote account.

## Step by step: confirming a child's account (outside India)
1. The child signs up and gives your email in **Guardian email**.
2. When they first sign in, SportnNote emails you. The subject reads, for example, "Please confirm Aisha's SportnNote account".
3. Open the link in the email. It works for 7 days. You can also open the guardian consent page and type the code from the email.
4. Read what SportnNote keeps and who can see it, then tap **I agree** or **I don't agree**.
5. If you're signed in to your own account when you agree, it is linked as the child's parent/guardian straight away.

Until you confirm, the child's account is limited:
- nobody can follow them;
- nobody can send messages about them;
- only their own teams and tournaments see their full profile.

If you tap **I don't agree**, the account is locked and deleted after 30 days, unless you confirm later from a new link.

> **Tip:** No email? The child can send it again from **Settings → Your data & privacy → Send the email again**, up to 3 times a day. If the address was wrong, correct it first in **Edit profile**.

## Step by step: managing your child's data
1. Sign in with **your own** account, not your child's.
2. Go to **Settings → Link as a parent/guardian**. If you aren't linked yet, enter the code from the guardian email and tap **Link**.
3. Next to your child's name, tap **Manage**.
4. Choose what you need from the list below.

The **Manage** page has:
- **👁️ View**, a summary of what we keep;
- **⬇️ Download**, the full file (JSON);
- **✅ Confirm consent**, which turns a waiting or locked account fully on;
- **Withdraw consent**, which locks the account straight away;
- **Delete their data**: type DELETE, then confirm.

## Common questions
### What does a locked account mean?
The child can still sign in, but their profile is hidden, and following and messages are off. If nobody confirms within 30 days of the withdrawal, the account and its personal data are deleted.

### Does deleting remove my child's match results?
Their name, details, photo and ID proofs are erased. Scores of matches they played stay in their teams' histories as "Deleted player", so standings stay correct.

### Who receives messages about my child?
You do, once you're linked. Coaches and organisers can never message an under-18 player directly.

### Why doesn't India use the email step?
India keeps the sign-up consent box that families already use. The email confirmation is how we meet the rules in other countries.
