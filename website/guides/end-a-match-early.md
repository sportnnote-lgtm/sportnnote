Rain, bad light, a team walking off or a slot running out can stop a school-meet match. When that happens, the scorer needs to close the match honestly and say why. SportnNote lets you end a match by hand with a result and a reason. The reason is shown to everyone, and the points table updates the right way.

## Before you start
- To end a match that has started, you must be one of its scorers. The button is on the **Scoring** tab.
- To record a walkover before a ball is bowled or a whistle blown, you must be a host of the match or the tournament.
- Every result needs a reason of at least a few letters. It's the public record of what happened.

## Step by step: end a match that has started
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Tap **🏁 End match…** below the scoring buttons.
3. Under "How did it end?", pick **Win**, **Conceded**, **Draw** (**Tie** in cricket), **No result** or **Abandoned**. The list below explains each one.
4. For **Win** or **Conceded**, tap the team that wins.
5. Type a reason, or tap a quick reason: **Rain**, **Bad light**, **Ground unfit**, **Time up**, **Injury** or **Team left**.
6. Read the preview line. In a tournament, it shows the result and what the points table will do, for example "Points table: both teams +1 (no result)".
7. Tap **End match**.

The match is marked finished. A banner with the result and the reason, such as "No result — Rain", shows on the match page, on the match card and in shared links.

The five choices mean:
- **Win**: one side is awarded the match, for example because they were clearly ahead when play had to stop.
- **Conceded**: one side gave up or walked off. The other side wins.
- **Draw** or **Tie**: both sides finish level.
- **No result**: play started but the match couldn't produce a result.
- **Abandoned**: the match was called off part-way and won't count as a normal game.

> **Note:** Knockout matches (quarter-finals, semis, finals and so on) only offer **Win** and **Conceded**, because someone has to go through. The panel says "Knockout: pick who goes through."

## Cricket: net run rate
In cricket, a **Win** or **Tie** shows an extra chip, **Count in NRR (all overs)**. It's on by default, so both teams are charged their full overs for net run rate. Turn it off if your tournament rules say a shortened match shouldn't affect NRR. The points are still awarded either way. For overs cut by rain, see [Rain, fewer overs and DLS targets](/guides/cricket-rain-and-dls/).

## What it does to the points table
- **Win** or **Conceded**: the winner gets the normal win points.
- **Draw** or **Tie**: both teams get the draw points.
- **No result** or **Abandoned**: the match counts as played for both teams, and each gets the "no result" points. In cricket that's 1 point each by default. In other sports it's 0, because those matches are usually replayed. Goals, runs and run rate from the match don't count.
- The table shows an **NR** column once any team has a no result.

Organisers can change the "no result" points for a sport under **⚙ Manage** → the sport's **format & points** row → **No result**. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Step by step: record a walkover
Use this when a team doesn't turn up and the match never starts.
1. Open the match → **Info** tab.
2. In the **Match scorers** card, tap **🏳 Award a walkover**.
3. Under "Who takes the walkover win?", tap the team that turned up.

The match is marked finished with the other side conceded, and the card shows **Walkover**. The winner gets the normal win points.

## Common questions

### I ended the match by mistake. Can I undo it?
Not with **↶ Undo**. A host can reset a tournament fixture to not started, or delete a friendly, from the **Danger zone** on the Info tab. This is only open for 30 minutes after the last scoring tap. See [Match quick options](/guides/match-quick-options/). To fix a wrongly scored goal or point after the end, see [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Why is the End match button greyed out?
Pick how the match ended, pick a winner if needed, and type a reason. The button turns on when all three are done.

### What's the difference between No result and Abandoned?
They count the same in the table. Use **No result** when play happened but couldn't finish with a result. Use **Abandoned** when the match was called off. The words show on the public result.

### Does a player's stats from the match still count?
Yes. Goals, runs and wickets already scored stay on players' profiles.
