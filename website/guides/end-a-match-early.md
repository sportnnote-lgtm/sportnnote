Rain, bad light or a team walking off can stop a match. End it by hand with a result and a reason; the points table updates the right way.

## Before you start
- To end a match that has started, you must be one of its scorers, or a host of the match or its tournament. Hosts don't need to add themselves as a scorer first.
- To record a walkover, you must be a host of the match or the tournament.
- Every result needs a short reason, shown publicly.

## Where the button is, and the "Are you sure?" check
**🏁 End match…** and **↺ Restart match** sit in the **Match controls** box at the very bottom of the **Scoring** tab, below every scoring button and **☰ Quick options**.

Anything that ends, restarts, resets or deletes a match opens a check sheet first, such as "End this match?", saying what will happen ("Final score 6-4, 3-2 will be saved and the match closes"). Then pick:
- **No, keep scoring** (green): nothing changes.
- **Yes, end match** (red): it goes ahead.

The sport's own end buttons ask the same way: **End 1st Half →**, **⏭ End Q1**, **End innings →**, **🏁 End Match**, **🏁 Full time** and "End as a draw / tie". Ending a half or quarter uses an amber **Yes**, because **↶ Undo** can bring it back.

## Step by step: end a match that has started
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **🏁 End match…** in **Match controls**.
3. Under "How did it end?", pick **Awarded**, **Conceded**, **Draw** (**Tie** in cricket), **No result** or **Abandoned**. Racket sports show **Retired** and **Default** instead. Hosts also see **🏳 Walkover**.
4. For **Awarded** or **Conceded**, tap the team that wins.
5. Type a reason, or tap one such as **Rain**, **Bad light** or **Injury**.
6. Check the preview line (result and, in a tournament, the points-table effect).
7. Tap **End match**, then **Yes, end match** on the check sheet.

A banner with the result and reason, such as "No result — Rain", shows on the match page, card and shared links.

The choices mean:
- **Awarded**: one side is awarded the match, for example because they were clearly ahead when play stopped.
- **Conceded**: one side gave up; the other wins.
- **Draw** or **Tie**: level.
- **No result**: play couldn't produce a result.
- **Abandoned**: the match was called off part-way.
- **🏳 Walkover** (hosts only): see below.

> **Note:** Knockout matches only offer **Awarded** and **Conceded** (plus **🏳 Walkover**), because someone has to go through.

## Racket sports: retired, default and the score line
In tennis, padel, badminton, table tennis, squash and pickleball, **Retired** means a player or pair stopped, for example through injury. **Default** means one side was put out, for example for conduct. The score line keeps the games played plus a mark: "6-4, 3-2 ret.", "21-15, 8-3 def." or "11-7 abandoned". A walkover, or a retirement before the first point, shows "w/o".

## Cricket: net run rate
In cricket, **Awarded**, **Conceded** or **Tie** shows a **Count in NRR (all overs)** chip. It's on by default, so both teams are charged their full overs. Turn it off if your rules say a shortened match shouldn't affect NRR. The points are awarded either way. Rain-cut overs: see [Rain, fewer overs and DLS targets](/guides/cricket-rain-and-dls/).

## What it does to the points table
- **Awarded**, **Conceded** or a walkover: the winner gets the normal win points.
- **Draw** or **Tie**: both teams get the draw points.
- **No result** or **Abandoned**: the match counts as played, and each team gets the "no result" points (1 each in cricket by default, 0 in other sports). Goals, runs and run rate from the match don't count. The table shows an **NR** column.

Organisers can change the "no result" points under **⚙ Manage** → the sport's **format & points** row. See [Points table and adjustments](/guides/points-table-and-adjustments/).

## Step by step: record a walkover
For a team that doesn't turn up or can't play.
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **🏁 End match…** at the bottom.
2. Tap **🏳 Walkover**.
3. Under "Who takes the walkover win?", tap the team that turned up.
4. The check sheet names the winner ("Walkover to Red House?"). Tap **Yes, walkover to …**.

Before the match has started, you can also do it from the **Info** tab: in the **Match scorers** card, tap **🏳 Award a walkover**, tap the team, then confirm.

The card shows **W/O** and no score; the winner gets the normal win points. If play had started, use **Conceded** instead so the score stays on the record.

## Common questions

### I ended the match by mistake. Can I undo it?
Not with **↶ Undo**. A host can reset a tournament fixture, or delete a friendly, from the **Danger zone** at the bottom of the Info tab, for 30 minutes after the last scoring tap. See [Match quick options](/guides/match-quick-options/) and [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### I don't see Restart match any more
It's only there in the first 5 minutes after the first score (with nothing scored it reads **↺ Not started? Cancel**). Both ask first.

### Do players' stats from the match still count?
Yes. Goals, runs and wickets already scored stay on their profiles.
