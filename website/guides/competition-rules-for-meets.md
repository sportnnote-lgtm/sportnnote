Every meet runs on a rule book, and school meets often bend the federation's rules a little. One meet may allow one false start per race. Another may score cross-country by the first four runners, or use its own weight categories. The Competition rules page lists each of these choices for a sport. Each one starts on the standard rule, with a short note on what it changes. You check them once, before the meet starts, and every event of that sport is then scored the same way.

## Before you start
- You need to be an organiser of the tournament (the host, a co-host, or an admin of the host organisation).
- The sport must be added to the tournament. Athletics, swimming, weightlifting, shooting, archery, rowing, canoe sprint and cycling each have their own page.
- Do this before anyone enters a result. Once any result exists for that sport in the tournament, its rules lock.

## Step by step
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → the sport's events row (for example **Athletics — events**).
2. Find the **📜 Competition rules** card at the top and tap **Check**. The same card is shown when you add an event.
3. Read each rule. The standard choice is marked **· standard**, and the line under it says where the rule comes from.
4. Tap a different choice to change a rule. Nothing is saved yet.
5. Tap **Save rules**. If you agree with every standard choice, tap **Use these rules** instead.
6. If events are already on the programme with no results yet, the app asks **Apply to the events already set up?** Tap **Yes, save rules**.

> **Tip:** Changed your mind? **Back to standard** puts every choice back to its default. It isn't saved until you tap **Save rules**.

## What you can choose
Weightlifting:
- **Calling order**: the IWF order (bar, attempt number, bigger jump, who lifted first, lot) or a simpler bar, attempt, lot order.
- **Increase after a good lift**: +1 kg (IWF), +2 kg or +5 kg. The automatic next declaration uses the same jump.
- **After a no lift**: repeat the same weight (IWF) or go up.
- **Bodyweight categories**: by age group (IWF 2025, IWF youth or the school list), one list for everyone, or your own list of kg limits.
- **Medals** for the snatch and the clean & jerk, and **Sinclair** totals with the coefficients you choose.

Athletics:
- **False starts**: zero tolerance, where the athlete is disqualified, or one per race, where the next false start disqualifies. Combined events have their own choice.
- **Wind limit** for records and PBs: +2.0 m/s, or no wind rule.
- **Hand times** for records, and whether to show the ≈ electronic conversion.
- **Age cut-off** day, **events per athlete**, and the lane a late entry gets.
- **Cross-country and road teams**: how many score, how many run for the team, how places are counted, and the tie-break. The tie-break is either the better last scorer or the better next runner, the "6th runner" rule. You can also say whether the road course is certified.
- **Combined events**: how equal totals are split, and whether missing an event puts the athlete out or just scores 0.

Swimming: false starts (disqualified, or the first one recalled), 50 m splits, and whether a 10-lane pool is numbered 0–9 or 1–10.

Archery: whether 10s or Xs break a tie, how a level shoot-off arrow is settled, and the bracket size.

Shooting: the tie-break, the final size, and whether equal medal places with no final are shot off or shared.

Cycling: the gap that starts a new time group, the GC tie-break, and the points per sprint.

Rowing and canoe sprint: the progression table offered first, and how lanes are given after the first round.

Some choices, like the cross-country team rule or the shooting final size, only set what a new event starts with. You can still change them on that event when you add it.

## Team sports in the same tournament
If the tournament also has team sports, the page ends with **Bans and walkover scores**. Tap a sport to check its suspension rules and the score a walkover counts as.

## Common questions
### Why can't I change a rule any more?
A result has been entered for that sport, so the page shows **🔒 Locked**. Changing a rule halfway would score some events one way and some another. To change one, clear those results first, or set up a new tournament.

### Some rules say "verify". What does that mean?
The standard was set from the rule book as we remember it. Check it against your federation's rules or your school board's circular. If yours differs, pick the matching choice.

### Do older events change?
No. Events that already have results keep the rules they were scored with. Saving only updates events that have no results yet.

### Example
Greenfield School runs its sports day with one false start allowed per race. Its cross-country is scored on the first four runners per house. The organiser sets both on the Athletics page the week before. Every heat and the cross-country race then follow those rules.
