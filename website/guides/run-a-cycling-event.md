A cycling day is a list of races, such as the U16 Boys time trial 10 km. Time trials go by the clock, road races by the order on the line, and track sprints run as a bracket. You enter results on a phone, and the app works out places, medals, house points and records.

## Before you start
- You need to be a host of the tournament (or an organiser).
- Create the tournament with **Cycling** as a sport, and pick the position points and **Hand timing** under **Cycling basics**.
- Add your houses or schools as teams. Riders score for them.

## Step by step: add a race
1. Open the tournament, then **Manage** → **Cycling — events**, and tap **＋ Add a race**.
2. Pick **Road** or **Track (velodrome)**, then the race.
3. Set the race up: a time trial's distance and **Start interval**, a stage race's stages, a points or scratch race's laps (a points race also needs **Sprint every … laps**).
4. Pick the age group (U12 to Elite and Masters) and **Boys / Men**, **Girls / Women** or **Mixed**.
5. Tap each rider, then **Create race & start list**. The start order is drawn.

## Road: time trial
1. The start list shows each rider's start, such as **+2:00**.
2. Type each time with the number keys (131811 is 13:18.11) and tap **Next ›**.
3. A time far outside the usual range asks **Check this mark** first. Tap **.000** to add thousandths that split equal times.
4. Tap **DNS**, **DNF**, **OTL** (outside the time limit) or **DQ** when needed.

## Road: road race with a bunch finish
1. Tap riders in the order they cross the line. Fix mistakes with **↑** (move up) or **✕** (take off the line).
2. Type the winner's time, for example 1:45:12.
3. After that, type a time only where a gap opens. Riders in a group get the group's time and show **s.t.**. A rider at least one second behind the rider ahead starts a new group, shown as **+0:28**.
4. Switch the mode chip to **DNF**, **OTL**, **DQ** or **DNS** and tap a rider. Tap them under **Not classified** to undo it.

## Road: stage race
1. Each stage is its own round. On a road stage, tap the order on the line as above, plus any **Bonus s** (time bonus), **Points** and **KOM**.
2. On a time-trial stage, type each time to 1/100. The overall leader starts last.
3. The sheet shows **GC after stage n** (total time in whole seconds, bonuses taken off), the stage result and the points and mountains standings.
4. Tap **Close stage 1 → start stage 2**. Everyone who finished carries their overall time into the next stage. DNF, OTL, DQ and DNS riders are out.
5. Equal overall times are split by the time-trial hundredths, then the sum of stage places, then the last stage. The final standings decide the medals.

## Track: pursuit and time trial
1. With 4 or more riders, the pursuit has qualifying rounds, then two finals: **Final for gold** (the two fastest) and **Final for bronze** (3rd and 4th).
2. Track times go to 1/1000: 345432 is 3:45.432.
3. In a final, tap **Caught** for a rider caught by the other. That rider loses the race.

## Track: sprint
1. Type each rider's flying 200 m time, such as 10401 for 10.401.
2. Tap **Close qualifying (flying 200 m) → seed the match play**. The best riders are seeded 1 v 4, 2 v 3 in a bracket of 4, 8 or 16.
3. In **Sprint — match play**, tap a match, then the winner of each heat. Best of three: the first rider to win two heats goes through. Tap a winner again to clear that heat.
4. A correction that changes who went through asks first, then clears later heats that no longer fit. The semi-final losers race for bronze.

## Track: keirin, scratch, points and elimination
- **Keirin** and **Scratch**: tap riders in finish order. In a scratch race, **+ lap** puts a rider who gained a lap ahead of the bunch.
- **Points race**: tap **S1**, **S2** and so on, then the first four in each sprint for 5, 3, 2 and 1 points. **Finish** is the last sprint and scores double (10, 6, 4, 2). Tap riders in finish order there, because it also settles ties. Use **+** or **−** for laps gained or lost, worth plus or minus 20 points.
- **Elimination**: tap each rider as they are eliminated. Places fill from the back, and the last two settle 1st and 2nd.

## Finish and correct
1. Tap **🏁 Finish & lock results**. The confirm lists any new meet record.
2. An organiser can reopen a round until the next has results, or reopen the final (its records and points roll back).

## What everyone sees
- The **House table** and **Meet records** (time trials, pursuits and the flying 200 m, per distance).
- **Rider profiles:** races, wins, medals, and personal and season bests per distance.

## Common questions
### Why does the 10th rider show s.t.?
They finished in the winner’s group, so they share its time; the order on the line sets the place.

### Can I run a team pursuit or madison?
Not yet. Team pursuit, team sprint, madison and omnium are coming later.
