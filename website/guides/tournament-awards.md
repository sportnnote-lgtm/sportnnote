At the closing ceremony, you need to name a Player of the Tournament, a top scorer and a best bowler. The **Awards** tab suggests winners from the stats already recorded. You change any you disagree with, add your own awards, and publish. The winners get an alert, and everyone can see and share the list.

## Before you start
- You must be a host of the tournament to prepare and publish awards.
- Suggestions come from player stats, so matches need to have been scored with players picked for each goal, run or point.
- Before you publish, only hosts see the **Awards** tab. After publishing, everyone sees it.

## Step by step: prepare and publish awards
1. Open the tournament → **Awards** tab.
2. In a multi-sport meet, pick the sport at the top. Each sport has its own awards.
3. Each award card shows a suggested winner marked **Auto-suggested**. The first card is always **Player of the Tournament**. Other cards depend on the sport, for example **Best batter** and **Best bowler** in cricket.
4. To pick someone else, tap **Change** on the card. Tap a player in the list, or search by name or team. To see how the suggestion was worked out, tap **How is this ranked?**.
5. To leave an award empty, tap its **✕**. To fill an empty one, tap **Pick**.
6. To add your own award, tap **＋ Custom award**. Type an **Award name**, or tap a suggestion such as **Best fielder**, **Emerging player** or **Fair play**. Then tap **Choose player**.
7. When you're happy, tap **Publish awards**, then **Publish** to confirm.

Each winner gets an alert, "You won" plus the award name. The Awards tab now shows the winners to everyone.

> **Important:** Your choices aren't saved until you tap **Publish awards**. If you leave the tab halfway, you'll start again from the suggestions.

> **Tip:** Publishing saves the awards for every sport in the tournament at once. Check each sport's awards before you publish.

## Step by step: share the awards
1. Open the **Awards** tab after publishing.
2. Tap **📤 Share awards** to send the list on WhatsApp or anywhere else.

## Step by step: edit published awards
1. On the **Awards** tab, tap **✏️ Edit awards**.
2. Change, clear or add awards as before.
3. Tap **Update awards**, then **Update**. Anyone newly named gets an alert.

## How suggestions are worked out
- **Player of the Tournament** adds up a points score across every match in the tournament. The score weights the sport's important stats, such as goals, runs, wickets and catches.
- Other awards use the total of one stat, such as runs or wickets, across the tournament.
- If players are tied, the list goes by name. You always choose the final winner.

## Change Player of the Match (once)
After each match, SportnNote suggests a Player of the Match from the stats. If the umpires or officials choose someone else, a scorer or host can change it, but only once.
1. Open the finished match → **Summary** tab.
2. Tap **Change Player of the Match**.
3. Pick the officials' choice from either team.
4. Confirm with **Change**. The message shows who it's changing from and to.

After that, the button disappears for everyone.

> **Note:** The once-only rule stops a Player of the Match being changed back and forth after the result is shared. Make sure you have the officials' final decision before you change it.

## Common questions

### The Awards tab says suggestions will appear once matches have player stats. Why?
No player stats have been recorded yet, usually because matches were scored without choosing who scored. Award cards fill in as stats come in. You can still add custom awards once any player has stats.

### Can I give an award to someone with no stats?
Custom awards are chosen from players who have stats in this tournament.

### Does correcting a match change the Player of the Match?
A correction can change the suggestion. It never replaces a Player of the Match that a scorer picked by hand. See [Correct a finished match](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

### Can parents see draft awards?
No. Until you publish, the Awards tab is only visible to hosts.
